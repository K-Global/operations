# D03 — Differences and Familiarisation Training · OM D

Training on variant differences within a rating group — where two or more K Global types share a common rating but differ in systems or handling.

**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-25 · **Status** Stub — to build

## Scope

- Differences training triggers — when a variant within a rating group requires dedicated training rather than familiarisation alone.
- Familiarisation training — lighter-touch awareness training for minor variant differences.
- Worked reference: the A330neo/A350 rating group and the A340→A330/A350 family relationship (OM A §A05 §2).
- Recording of differences/familiarisation completion against the pilot's qualification record (§D13).
