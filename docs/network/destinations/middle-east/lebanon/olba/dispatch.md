## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** The main passenger terminal is a U-shaped building of two wings connected by a central terminal — an **East Wing** (opened 1998) and a **West Wing** (opened 2002) — plus a separate **General Aviation Terminal** (opened 2005) on the northwest corner of the field for business/charter traffic. The passenger terminal is reported to handle both domestic and international flights with separated arrivals/departures flows. `[Wikipedia]`
- **Our operation uses:** Main passenger terminal complex; exact concourse/gate assignment for scheduled commercial service not confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear passport control and customs in the main terminal; this is the country's principal port of entry alongside the seaport. 🟧 Desk hours not independently confirmed.
- **Notes:** Terminal infrastructure has undergone periodic renovation and expansion over time; confirm current gate/concourse assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan. 🟧
