## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single unified **Passenger Terminal Building**, opened 28 January 2021 at a reported cost of ~US$1.1 billion, ~210,000 m² (2.3M sq ft), designed for 14 million passengers and 130,000 air-traffic movements per year with a stated 4,700-bag-per-peak-hour handling capacity; 24 departure gates, check-in halls, E-gates, security lanes and a 9,000 m² duty-free retail area. 🟧 Individual pier/concourse letter designations for this terminal were not confirmed in reachable public sources.
- **Our operation uses:** The single terminal handles all scheduled commercial traffic; a separate **Awal Private Terminal** (~4,000 m², operated by Hala Bahrain, a Bahrain Airport Company subsidiary) serves VIP/government/private-aviation clients. 🟧 Awal's apron/stand linkage (likely the Executive apron, stands E1–E4) is not cross-confirmed against the AIP.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Single-terminal international arrivals hall; no split domestic/international building identified.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Individual concourse/pier structure within the 2021 terminal, and any post-2021 modification, not independently confirmed this pass.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** **Main Apron A** (stands 7–10, Code F except stand 7 = Code C), **Main Apron B** (11–22, Code F except stand 14 = Code E) and **Main Apron C** (23–28, Code F except stand 28 = Code C) are the principal Code E/F clusters, all concrete, PCN 86–120 (Apron B stands 20–22 and Apron C at PCN 120/R/B/W/T). 🟩
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Stand 7 (Apron A) and stand 28 (Apron C) are Code C-limited; stand 14 (Apron B) is Code E-limited — do not plan a Code F aircraft onto these three.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Western A (61–63), Western B (50–58 + 52A), Middle (1–6), Executive (E1–E4), Cargo (C1–C5), Northern (70–75) and Eastern (81–88) aprons provide additional GA/executive/cargo/remote capacity — surfaces and PCN vary by apron (AD 2.8).
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact Main Apron A/B/C gate for a scheduled Code E/F turn; remote/hardstand use is atypical for scheduled service.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** A **Code F landing/take-off on the main runway closes Taxiway Alpha between A1 and A2 to all movement**, including vehicles — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None identified — no IATA Level 1/2/3 coordination regime found in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No movement curfew found. The genuine night-window restrictions are **reverse-thrust-above-idle prohibited on landing 2100–0300** (except emergency, ATC-cleared) and **engine run-ups limited to ground-idle power 2100–0300** — commercial/noise restrictions, not a movement ban. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** No ATFM/CTOT regime identified for OBBI in reachable sources; en-route clearance is via radio or **PreFANS data-link DCL** (ACARS, addr. OBBI), requested TOBT‑30 to TOBT‑10. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** Absent a formal slot/curfew regime, the dominant schedule-integrity driver at OBBI is the **regional conflict-zone/airspace-availability picture** (§10 below and the [Middle East airspace brief](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md)) rather than local ATC capacity — Bahraini airspace closed outright for six weeks in early 2026 amid regional tensions.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** **Bahrain Airport Services (BAS)** is the field's ground handler, H24; aircraft repair for visiting aircraft is via **Gulf Air**; no hangar space is available for visiting aircraft.
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 12 min / taxi-out 15 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody service times.
- **Services:** Ground power (FEGP where serviceable, else GPU) and PCA are provided at pop-up-pit-equipped stands, with APU use limited to ≤10 min before off-block and ≤5 min after on-block; catering/cabin services expected as standard for a full-service Gulf hub of this size.
- **Turnaround risk items:** APU/GPU compliance windows at pop-up-pit stands, the TWY Alpha (A1–A2) closure during any Code F movement on the main runway, and the current single-effective-runway-pair situation (RWY 12R/30L unavailable, §Briefing §3.5) as a capacity/resilience risk during peak or irregular operations.

## 7. Fuel

- **Supplier / into-plane:** **Bahrain Aviation Fuelling Company (BAFCO)** — Jet A-1 available to **contract customers or on a cash basis** (cash requires a pre-arranged deposit with BAFCO); **AVGAS 100LL is not available** (not relevant to our jet fleet). 🟧
- **Uplift availability & hours:** Fuelling published H24 (AD 2.3); hydrant stands serve 07–26A, E1–E4, C1–C4, with bowser coverage across the remaining stands (1–6, 50–58, 61–63, 71–75, 26B, 27–28, 80–88).
- **Price / tankering angle:** Not independently sourced this pass — assess price differential against home-base tankering policy at planning. See [`OM E — Fuel Policy`](../../../../../flight-ops/fuel-policy.md).
- **Quality / freeze notes:** Standard Jet A-1; no cold-soak/fuel-freeze consideration at this Gulf field — the extreme end of the climate range here is heat, not cold (§Briefing §14).

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** The **regional conflict-zone/airspace-availability picture** is the dominant reliability driver at OBBI in the current environment — Bahraini airspace closed outright 28 February–8 April 2026 and again briefly on 3 June 2026 amid regional military tensions, with Gulf Air rerouting via Dammam (OEDF) during the closures. This is a durable, re-check-live planning input — see the [Middle East airspace brief](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) §9. 🟥
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** No formal ATFM/CTOT program identified for OBBI in reachable sources; the practical exposure is instead **RWY 12R/30L's current unavailability**, concentrating all traffic onto the single 12L/30R pair. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Shamal dust events and CAT II LVP activations (350 m RVR hard floor) are the routine weather-driven reliability factors; the conflict-zone/airspace picture is the outsized, low-frequency-but-high-impact one. 🟧
