## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A passenger terminal exists; it has been directly damaged by strikes on multiple occasions (2015, 2017, 2024, 2025, 2026 per public reporting) — no current condition/layout confirmed. 🟥
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable — no scheduled K Global operation; field carries no VAMSYS record.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Tagged as a Port of Entry in public data; hall location/hours not published. 🟧
- **Notes:** The **6 May 2025** Israeli airstrike was described by the airport's own director as leaving the facility "completely destroyed," with several aircraft destroyed on the ground; a further strike on 28 May 2025 destroyed the last Yemenia aircraft operating from the field. Treat any pre-2024 facility description as historical, not current. 🟥
