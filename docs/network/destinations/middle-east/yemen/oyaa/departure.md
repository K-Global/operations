## 10. Gotchas

- 🟥 This is a **joint civil/military field** — expect Yemeni Air Force helicopter traffic and activity on and around the field at any hypothetical operation.
- 🟥 The field has been the direct site of armed conflict and a targeted ground attack (30 Dec 2020) — ground-security assumptions from a peacetime field do not apply.
- 🟧 No declared-distance, SID, or navaid data was obtained from a primary source — any tactical departure planning here is currently unsupported by verified data.
