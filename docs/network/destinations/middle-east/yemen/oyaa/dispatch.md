## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A passenger terminal exists (new terminal built 1983–85, capacity ~1M pax/year at the time); no current concourse/pier breakdown obtained from a reachable public source. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable — no scheduled K Global operation under the standing caution.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Field is tagged as a Port of Entry in public data; hall location/hours not published. 🟧
- **Notes:** A **25 July 2023 severe thunderstorm blew through the terminal's glass facade**, wounding 11 passengers and damaging two aircraft — reflects real structural exposure to weather, and a still-recovering facility profile more broadly given the field's conflict history. 🟥
