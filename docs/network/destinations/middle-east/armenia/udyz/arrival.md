## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 **The field's defining hazard.** Mt. Aragats (NW, ~13,419 ft) and Mt. Ararat (SW across the Turkish border, ~16,854 ft) dominate the surrounding terrain; area MSA sectors around the ZVR VOR reach as high as 18,100 ft in places — cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md). Fly every published altitude constraint precisely; do not improvise a shortcut in this terrain environment.
- **Specific threats:** RWY 09 ILS CAT II requires special aircrew and aircraft certification — confirm currency before planning a low-vis approach. RWY 27 has no confirmed precision approach in reachable sources — a visual/RNP-only profile should be expected and briefed accordingly.
- **Airspace / traffic:** Single-runway field; traffic density not independently confirmed but expected to be moderate as Armenia's principal gateway — cross-ref the [Middle East airspace brief](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md).
