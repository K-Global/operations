## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
Tabuk sits on the northern Hejaz interior plateau at 2,551 ft — the immediate terminal environment is low-relief; no close-in high terrain was identified in reachable sources. Confirm the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice; no primary AIP terrain data was reached this pass.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟥
OETB shares its site and boundary with **King Faisal Air Base**, home to Royal Saudi Air Force **7 Wing** — multiple active fighter and trainer squadrons (No. 2 Sqn F-15C/D, No. 29 Sqn F-15SA, No. 21/37/79/88 Sqn Hawk 65/165, including the "Saudi Falcons" display team) operate from a **separate runway** on the same field. This is a materially higher military-traffic-density co-location than a typical joint-use regional field — expect fighter/trainer circuit activity, formation departures/arrivals and possible short-notice airspace restrictions around the base's flying program. No specific deconfliction procedure was confirmed in reachable public sources. The field lies within the **Jeddah (OEJD) FIR** — see [Middle East / Gulf Airspace Briefing](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) for the regional structure. Tabuk's northwestern location places it within range of navaids across the Jordan/Israel border (Aqaba, Eilat VORs visible from the field, §9) — a reminder that this corner of Saudi Arabia sits close to the Levant, where the regional brief's conflict-zone and GNSS-jamming cautions (§9/§11 of that brief) are most acute; confirm the live picture at planning.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
Two civil runways (06/24, 13/31) identified; no displaced threshold or declared-distance detail was found in reachable sources for either — not independently confirmed. 13/31's surface type is unconfirmed in the sources reached.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Interior-plateau desert climate — expect **shamal winds and blowing dust** seasonally (regional pattern, see [Middle East Airspace Briefing](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) §11) and high summer temperatures; at 2,551 ft this field does not meet the density-altitude "hot-and-high" trigger the way Taif (OETF) does. No OETB-specific climatology statistic was found this pass — see §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟥
The standout item is the **active co-located fighter base** — a real traffic/airspace-management consideration distinct from a typical civil regional field, and a discriminator versus OESK/OEYN/OETF in this pack. The remaining gaps (approach minima, RFF, hours, fuel, exact runway-pair confirmation) are confirmation items rather than known hard restrictions, but should be closed before treating this brief as flight-planning-ready.
