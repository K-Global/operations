## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None formal, but **PPR (3-hour prior notice) is required for any non-scheduled flight** — functionally the field's own coordination gate. 🟥
- **Curfew / night restriction:** 🟥 **Published operating hours 04:00–20:00 local (≈01:00–17:00 UTC)** — the field is effectively closed overnight, a hard scheduling constraint even though not formally labelled a curfew.
- **CTOT / flow control:** No ATFM/CTOT programme identified — consistent with an uncontrolled field.
- **Commercial impact:** **The PPR requirement and the 04:00–20:00 local operating window are the dominant commercial constraints** at this field — any schedule must fit inside the published hours and clear the 3-hour prior-notice requirement.
