## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single terminal opened July 2015 (156,940 m², three storeys, 31 passenger boarding bridges, 18 departure gates with air-bridges, 16 contact + 20 remote apron stands), designed by **GMW Architects/Mimarlık**, inspired by Hejazi date-palm architecture. Operated by the **TIBAH** consortium under a 25-year BOT concession — Saudi Arabia's first privatised airport. A **2024-announced expansion** (Scott Brownrigg) proposes converting the existing terminal to dedicated international use and building a new domestic terminal, targeting 17M passengers/year by 2028 — status as of today not independently re-verified. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** The single existing terminal — not independently confirmed for our specific gate/pier. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear customs/immigration in the main terminal; H24 coverage confirmed at the airport level. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 Field is **not in VAMSYS** — terminal/gate assignment for our operation cannot be confirmed against a live category/base record.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** East Apron carries the highest PCN rating (85/R/A/W/T); specific Code E/F designation not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not individually confirmed.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** 20 remote apron stands alongside 16 contact stands; West/Remote/Royal/Military/GA/Isolation aprons rated PCN 59/F/A/W/T.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect either, pending confirmation; the field's own remote-stand count (20, versus 16 contact) suggests remote handling is a routine, not exceptional, occurrence here.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** **West Apron is explicitly restricted to Hajj/Umrah and normal traffic**, with a 2-hour parking limit before repositioning to the remote apron — see [Briefing §13](index.md).
