## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** Published for all four runway ends (RNAV 1/GNSS-based) — exact designators not extracted this pass. 🟧
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** Madinah Approach assigns the STAR-to-runway transition, with **17/35 preferred**.
- **Transition to approach:** Expect an RNAV-STAR-to-ILS transition (17/35) or RNAV-to-VOR/RNP (18/36) with radar vectors onto final; verify the charted transition.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** Published gates are pointers only — verify chart; 250 KIAS below FL100 is the norm.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 Elevated volcanic-plateau setting (Harrat Rahat to the south); the AIP itself documents solar-marked obstruction beacons on nearby high ground and a charted VSS infringement on the VOR RWY 36 procedure — moderate terrain awareness warranted, though this is not a high-mountain profile. See [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 **Wind shear** is an AIP-flagged, actively studied local hazard with a standing pilot-report request — brief accordingly. **Bird strike** is also explicitly flagged for takeoff/landing.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 Hajj/Umrah gateway traffic density — expect schedule-density pressure during peak season, less so on routine days.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Two runways, not one** — 17/35 preferential, 18/36 secondary with expected delay if assigned; RWY 18 has no ILS at all.
- **Charted VSS infringement on VOR RWY 36** — a building 1.3 NM before the threshold at 2,290 ft, right of centreline.
- **AIP-documented VHF terrain coverage gap** — expect a complementary Approach frequency to be assigned.
- **Wind shear** is an AIP-flagged, actively studied local hazard — brief for it.
- **Bird strike caution** is explicitly flagged in the AIP.
- **Hajj/Umrah surge traffic density** — expect denser sequencing during peak weeks.
