## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 Flat coastal (Tihamah) plain at 20 ft AMSL; no close-in high terrain — cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **AIP-published bird hazard** — large flocks cross the aerodrome in all seasons, highest concentration at dawn and dusk; brief accordingly for arrivals in those windows.
- **Airspace / traffic:** Class C TMA / Class D CTR with field surveillance radar; moderate traffic, standard procedural/radar mix — cross-ref [Briefing §3.2](index.md) for the region's overriding security/closure consideration.
