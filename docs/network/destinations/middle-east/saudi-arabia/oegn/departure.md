## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** Via TWY A (main, 45 m) with A1/A2 spurs; TWY B/C also serve the field. Confirm the day's routing with Ground.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 **Aprons 2 and 3, and TWY N, are not visible from the Tower** (AIP local aerodrome regulation) — aircraft taxiing from Apron 2/3 must hold short of TWY A and advise Tower before proceeding; TWY N is treated as **non-manoeuvring area**, not a routine taxi route.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** Not independently confirmed beyond the above. 🟧
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** Not confirmed. 🟧

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** No preferential-runway rule published; runway in use follows wind/traffic, with RWY 33 favoured when an ILS approach is operationally desirable (single ILS runway, §Briefing §3.5).
- **By departure direction:** Both runways route outbound via a common turn logic toward waypoints PEKAR/LUBAL (§4).
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** None published.
- **Interaction with arrivals:** Single-runway field — arrivals and departures share the one strip; sequencing is by Tower/Approach.

## 10. Gotchas

- 🟥 **Bird activity at dawn/dusk** — AIP-published extreme-caution note for large flocks crossing the aerodrome; brief accordingly for early/late departures.
- 🟥 **Aprons 2/3 and TWY N are Tower blind spots** — hold short of TWY A and advise Tower when taxiing from those aprons.
- 🟧 **RWY 33 is the field's only ILS runway** — a RWY 15 departure/arrival pairing in low weather has no precision-approach reciprocal.
- 🟥 **Field carries an active regional NOTAM-closure/security watch-item** — confirm current status before planning any departure from this field (Briefing §2/§5).
