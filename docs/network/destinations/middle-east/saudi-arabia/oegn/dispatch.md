## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Not confirmed. Apron 4 is the highest-strength surface (concrete, PCN 90/R/B/W/T) and the most plausible position for a larger type; Aprons 1–3 (concrete/asphalt, PCN 51/R/B/W/T) are lower-strength. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not confirmed — no fleet assigned.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** AIP notes aircraft parked at Apron 1 over one hour, or overnight, must shift to Apron 2 or 3 (except large aircraft) — implies Apron 1 is a short-stay/contact area and 2/3 are longer-stay.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not confirmed. 🟧
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Aprons 2 and 3 are **not visible from the Tower**; aircraft taxiing from those aprons must hold short of TWY A and advise Tower — cross-ref [Briefing §13](index.md) and [Departure §2](departure.md).
