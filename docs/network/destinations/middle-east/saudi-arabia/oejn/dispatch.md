## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** ("New Terminal," 810,000 m², one of the largest airport terminals in the world) opened in phases 2018–2019; 46 boarding gates (8 dual-purpose widebody/narrowbody); features a central garden, aquarium, mosque, automated people mover, and a direct Haramain High Speed Railway station link. The former **South Terminal** (1981) closed 26 June 2020, its operations absorbed into Terminal 1. The **Hajj Terminal** (SOM design, tent-roof, Aga Khan Award 1983) is a separate, dedicated pilgrim-surge facility with an 80,000-simultaneous-pilgrim capacity, mostly open/naturally-ventilated except for climate-controlled customs/baggage areas.
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal 1 for scheduled international service — not independently confirmed for our specific gate/pier. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear customs/immigration per the assigned terminal; H24 coverage confirmed at the airport level. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 During Hajj season, an additional 1.5–2 million passengers are processed over roughly six weeks via the Hajj Terminal — a genuine capacity/scheduling consideration even for operators not directly carrying pilgrim charters, given the field-wide surge in movements.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** **Apron 7** rated up to Code F (A380/747-8-capable), splittable into Code-C double stands; standard aprons (1–6) carry Code E as the common ceiling. `[GACA eAIP AD 2.8/2.20]`
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrowbody-oriented stands on the smaller aprons — confirm locally.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Numbered aprons 1–16 plus lettered A/B/C/G; **Apron 8 reserved for Royal/VIP**, **Apron 12 RSAF-only** (with engine test area), **Apron 14 Royal maintenance**, **Apron 15 isolated/run-up**, **Apron 16 dedicated GA**.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate at Terminal 1 for scheduled service.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** No specific hot spot flagged in the reachable AIP extract beyond the general parallel-ILS-selection caution — see [Briefing §3.2](index.md).
