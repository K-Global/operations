## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Three terminal buildings — the main **Passenger Terminal** (327,000 m², 66 check-in counters shared Saudia/Flynas, 44 for foreign carriers), the **Aramco Terminal** (exclusive to Saudi Aramco Aviation employee flights), and the **Royal Terminal** (16,400 m², 4 boarding bridges — rarely used, royals typically prefer the King Abdulaziz Air Base special terminal instead).
- **Our operation uses:** Main Passenger Terminal expected for scheduled international service — not independently confirmed for our specific gate/pier. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear customs/immigration in the main terminal; H24 coverage confirmed at the airport level. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 Universal Aviation was awarded the sole GA-terminal management/ground-handling mandate in a reported Feb 2026 development — a recent change worth confirming if the K Global operation ever touches the GA side.

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Field broadly regarded as **Code E-capable** (747-400/A340-600-class) throughout; **Apron 2 Stands 1 and 3 are individually Code F-rated** (A380-class), each requiring exclusive use of an adjacent taxiway (P1/P2) while occupied — Stand 2 on the same ramp remains usable by Code C/D concurrently. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not individually confirmed; smaller aprons/stands likely narrowbody-oriented.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Six numbered aprons (1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 11); nine dedicated cargo stands support the field's substantial freight-carrier roster.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate at the main Passenger Terminal.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** 🟥 **Stands 19, 20, 21 are not visible from the tower** — extra vigilance required on ground movement in that area. See [Briefing §13](index.md).
