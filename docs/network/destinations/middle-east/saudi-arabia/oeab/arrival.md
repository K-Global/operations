## 3. Descent & energy plan

- 🟥 **Top-of-descent trigger:** Plan descent with the field's very high elevation (6,858 ft) and surrounding terrain in mind — MSA/holding altitudes here (11,000–12,000 ft at the ABH DVORTAC) sit only 4,100–5,150 ft above field elevation, a much tighter terrain-clearance margin than a sea-level field. Do not apply a standard sea-level descent-planning rule of thumb without adjustment.
- **Speed control:** STAR speed gates and 250 KIAS below FL100 — confirm current chart.
- **Altitude constraints:** Key STAR crossing constraints are pointers — fly the charted altitudes; treat every constraint as terrain-driven until the chart says otherwise.
- **Energy traps:** 🟥 The escarpment/mountain-plateau setting means a late, high, fast arrival carries a genuine terrain-margin cost, not just a stabilised-approach cost — plan conservatively.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 **The defining hazard at this field.** OEAB sits atop the Sarawat/Asir mountains with a dramatic western escarpment toward the Tihamah coastal plain; nearby peaks (Jabal Sawda/Soudah area, ~3,015 m official) rise roughly 900–1,000 m above the field. The AIP's own mandatory departure climb gradients and elevated holding altitudes (§Briefing §3.1) are the clearest evidence of how seriously this terrain constrains procedure design — treat every approach here with a heightened terrain-awareness mindset, not a routine-field mindset.
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 **"The City of Fog"** — routine afternoon mountain cloud/mist; the AIP's low-vis procedure is the standing mitigation. **Monsoon-influenced convective weather** is plausible in the spring/mid-summer wet season.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Circuit coordination required with neighbouring **Khamis Mushait (OEKM)** military field (~9 NM) — confirm circuit direction assignment.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- 🟥 **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Missed-approach/holding altitudes at the ABH DVORTAC are **11,000–12,000 ft** — well above field elevation, reflecting the genuinely high terrain-clearance requirement here. Fly the published MAP precisely; do not shortcut a go-around track near this terrain.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** Coordinate with Abha Approach for re-sequencing; be aware of the OEKM circuit-coordination requirement on any non-standard track.
- **Go-around traps:** A rushed or non-standard go-around in mountainous terrain is a genuine CFIT risk — fly the charted procedure exactly.

## 10. Gotchas

- 🟥 **This is the highest-elevation, most terrain-critical field in the K Global Saudi network** — brief accordingly, every time, regardless of how many times you've flown it before.
- **Runway is 13/31, not 16/34** — use 13/31 throughout.
- **RWY 31 has no ILS** — VOR only; plan RWY 13 as the precision-approach runway.
- **"City of Fog"** — routine mountain cloud/mist; expect the low-vis one-movement-at-a-time procedure in season.
- **Circuit coordination required with Khamis Mushait (OEKM)** for any non-standard direction.
- 🟥 **Conflict-zone NOTAM closure risk supersedes all other planning** — verify live status before arrival planning; do not treat this document's snapshot as current clearance to operate.
