## 9. De-icing provisioning & season

- **Availability:** **NIL** — not provided at this field, despite a genuine winter-cold/possible-frost profile at 6,858 ft elevation (nighttime temperatures 3–7°C, Dec–Feb, with possible frost near Al-Soudah). 🟥 This is a real safety/ops-planning gap — do not assume any on-field mitigation exists.
- **Season:** Winter (Dec–Feb) is the relevant risk window; not otherwise quantified.
- **Provisioning:** Not applicable — no facility exists.
- **Commercial impact:** Any winter operation into OEAB should independently assess icing risk against current METAR/TAF, given the absence of on-field de-icing.
