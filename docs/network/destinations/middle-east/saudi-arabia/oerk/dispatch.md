## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Five terminals. T1–T4 (1983, triangular plan) linked by connector buildings; **T5** (2016, ~106,500 m²) added widebody-capable capacity, historically able to serve 16 narrowbody or 8 widebody aircraft simultaneously. A **Royal Terminal/Pavilion** serves government/state traffic. A reported **Feb 2026 phased reallocation** split terminals by carrier type (T1 Flynas/Flyadeal international, T2 Saudia/Riyadh Air international, T3/T4 domestic, T5 all foreign carriers) — recent and potentially volatile; verify current assignment. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Most likely **T5** (foreign-carrier, widebody-capable) under the reported 2026 reallocation — not independently confirmed for our specific operation. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear customs/immigration per the assigned terminal; H24 coverage confirmed at the airport level. 🟧 Terminal-specific desk hours not confirmed.
- **Notes:** 🟧 The reported Feb 2026 terminal reallocation is recent — confirm current gate/terminal assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** T5's stated 8-widebody simultaneous capacity is the strongest indicator of Code E/F infrastructure; no explicit ICAO reference-code designation was found in the AIP text reviewed. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrowbody-oriented gates in T1–T4 are not expected to take Code E/F — confirm locally.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Numbered apron system (Apron 1–5, 6A–6E, 7/7B/8/9/9B Royal, Cargo, GA) — several stands (12, 17, 22, 27, 302, 401, 407) require engine shutdown + towing if stopped; stands 5E–8W require full tow-out to TWY E. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate at a major-hub field; confirm with handling.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Taxiway H3 obstruction flagged in the retrieved AIP cycle — see [Briefing §13](index.md).
