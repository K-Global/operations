## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
Yanbu sits on the flat Red Sea coastal plain in Medina Province — no close-in high terrain was identified in reachable sources. This is not expected to be a significant CFIT driver; confirm the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
OEYN is a regional field on the Red Sea coast within the **Jeddah (OEJD) FIR** — see [Middle East / Gulf Airspace Briefing](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md). The regional brief notes the **Red Sea / Bab-el-Mandeb corridor** as a live conflict-zone/Houthi-threat area further south (§9 of that brief); Yanbu itself is well north of the acute closure zone (the SW-Saudi fields NOTAM-closed after Houthi attacks are OEAB/Abha, OEGN/Jizan, OENG/Najran, OESH/Sharurah — all further south), but the general Red Sea GNSS-jamming and overflight-risk caution applies network-wide and should be checked live at planning.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
**Single runway (10/28)** — no redundancy if the runway is closed (disabled aircraft, contamination, obstruction); plan diversion contingency accordingly (§16). No displaced threshold or declared-distance detail was found in reachable sources.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Red Sea coastal climate — expect **high heat and humidity**, with the Red Sea's typical convective/haze patterns; unlike the interior desert fields in this pack, Yanbu's proximity to the sea means humidity (not just dry heat) is a factor. No OEYN-specific climatology statistic was found this pass — see §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
Yanbu is a significant industrial/petrochemical port city (refining and export hub) and a growing Red Sea tourism/diving gateway — international carriers (Qatar Airways reopened Yanbu service per public reporting) have shown renewed interest in the field. No hard operational restriction was identified in reachable sources; the open items are chiefly confirmation gaps (approach minima, RFF, hours, fuel) rather than known hazards, alongside the unresolved **runway-designator and elevation discrepancies** (§1, §18).
