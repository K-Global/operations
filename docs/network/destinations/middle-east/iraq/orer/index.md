## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟧
ORER sits at ~1,341 ft AMSL on the Kurdistan plain. No chart-confirmed close-in high terrain was found in reachable sources for the immediate arrival/departure area, but the wider Kurdistan Region terrain rises toward the Zagros foothills to the north and east — a lower-order consideration on wider arrivals than the close-in terrain documented at Sulaymaniyah (ORSJ, ~78 NM SE). Verify the current MSA ring on the AIRAC chart; treat this as a secondary rather than primary CFIT concern for the field itself.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟥
> **STANDING CAUTION — read before every flight.** Erbil lies within the **Baghdad (ORBB) FIR**, documented in the [Middle East regional airspace brief](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) as part of the region's **conflict-affected core**, currently under an **active EASA CZIB advising against operation in FIR Baghdad at any altitude or flight level** (CZIB 2026-05, issued 08 Jul 2026, valid to 31 Aug 2026); a current Iraqi NOTAM (**ORBB A0279/26**) additionally warns of **GPS jamming/spoofing in the northern part of the country**, which directly covers the Kurdistan Region including Erbil. **This picture is live and perishable — re-verify at every planning cycle.**
>
> **Erbil-specific history:** the airport's military section (hosting US-led coalition forces) has been the **direct target of multiple explosive-laden drone attacks** — 15 Apr 2021, 6 Jul 2021, and 11 Sep 2021 (two drones, one intercepted by C-RAM air defence, one crashed) — with no casualties reported in any incident. A regional summary additionally records **drone/missile strikes reported near Erbil and Sulaymaniyah in April 2023**. The stated primary risk is **militia drone/rocket activity and the potential for misidentification by air-defence systems**. This note takes no position on any party or cause — it states only that the status is variable, materially elevated for this specific field, and must be checked. Cross-ref [Middle East airspace brief](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) §9.

Day-to-day terminal traffic is handled by Erbil Tower/Arrival/Departure on VHF radar (with Baghdad Approach also listed at range); no unusual civil VFR-mix issue was identified in reachable sources.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟩
The single active runway (18/36) is a long, modern concrete surface (4,800 × 75 m) with no reported contamination or displaced-threshold issue in reachable sources. The former 15/33 runway pair is reported **closed** — do not plan any use of it pending independent confirmation.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Continental Kurdistan-plateau climate: hot, dry summers and cooler, wetter winters, with occasional fog possible on the plain during the cooler months. No AIP-sourced seasonal frequency statistic was found — treat as regional climatology pending a primary source. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟥
Plan around three durable constraints: (1) the **conflict-zone/security status of the ORBB FIR and the field's own history of direct drone attacks** (§3.2) is the dominant planning driver; (2) the field has a documented precedent of **abrupt, non-weather-related suspension of international commercial service** (29 Sep 2017 – 14 Mar 2018, following the 2017 Kurdistan independence referendum) — a reminder that political/security developments, not just NOTAMs, can remove this field from the network with limited notice; (3) **GPS jamming/spoofing is specifically flagged for the northern part of Iraq** (Briefing §3.2), directly relevant to Erbil given its Kurdistan Region location.

## 4. Cautions & Warnings

- 🟥 **STANDING CAUTION — conflict-zone/airspace status.** Baghdad (ORBB) FIR is under an active EASA CZIB advising against operation at any altitude. Check the current CZIB, state NOTAMs and company routing policy before every flight — see [Middle East airspace brief](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) §9.
- 🟥 **The airport's military section has been directly targeted by explosive-laden drone attacks on at least three documented occasions (2021)** — ground security posture is dynamic, not routine.
- 🟧 **GPS jamming/spoofing is specifically flagged for northern Iraq**, directly covering the Kurdistan Region — brief the conventional-navaid cross-check and notify ATC promptly if position uncertainty is suspected.
- 🟧 **International commercial service was suspended entirely for ~5.5 months in 2017–2018** following the Kurdistan independence referendum — a documented precedent for non-weather-related service disruption; confirm current political/operational status before planning.
- 🟩 **The former 15/33 runway pair is closed** — plan on 18/36 only, pending independent confirmation.
- 🟧 **Field elevation (~1,341–1,363 ft, sources disagree) combined with regional summer heat** is a secondary performance consideration — confirm current-day performance planning.
