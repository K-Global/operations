## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat Mesopotamian plain at 114 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **STANDING CAUTION.** Baghdad (ORBB) FIR is under an active EASA CZIB ("avoid at all levels") and the field itself has a documented history of rocket/drone attacks (Briefing §3.2/§3.5) — the operative arrival-phase threat is **security/conflict-related, not aerodynamic or terrain-related**. - 🟥 **STANDING CAUTION.** Confirm the current EASA CZIB and Iraqi NOTAM status for FIR Baghdad (ORBB) before every arrival — this is not a routine navigational brief item (Briefing §3.2/§4).

- 🟧 **Extreme summer heat can affect approach/landing performance margins** despite the field's low elevation.
