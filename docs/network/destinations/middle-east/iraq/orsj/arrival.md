## 10. Gotchas

- 🟥 **STANDING CAUTION.** Confirm the current EASA CZIB and Iraqi NOTAM status for FIR Baghdad (ORBB) before every arrival — this field has additionally had reported strikes nearby in 2023 (Briefing §3.2/§4).
- 🟥 **No ILS at this field** — a winter fog event is a real go/no-go consideration, not a minor caution; verify current wx and a genuinely capable alternate before committing to an arrival in marginal conditions.
- 🟧 **This field sits closer to significant terrain than Baghdad, Basrah or Erbil** — do not treat the arrival environment as terrain-benign without checking the current AIRAC.
- 🟧 **This is a single-runway field with no reciprocal/crosswind alternative** — verify current serviceability before planning.
- 🟧 **The airport was renamed to "Jalal Talabani International Airport" (Mar 2026)** and some tools/charts may still reference the legacy code **ORSU** or the prior name — confirm you and ATC are referencing the same field.
