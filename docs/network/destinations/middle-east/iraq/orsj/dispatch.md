## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** The passenger terminal has a two-level layout — arrivals (passport control, baggage claim, customs, currency exchange, car rental) on the ground floor and departures (check-in, security, passport control, duty-free, boarding gates) on the first floor, per public sourcing. A separate VIP terminal serves private jets, government officials and high-profile passengers with dedicated check-in and expedited processing. Combined terminal capacity is reported at ~1.5 million passengers/year (expandable to ~3 million); actual traffic has historically run well below this capacity. 🟧 (tier-4 sourced, not primary-confirmed)
- **Our operation uses:** Not published / verify. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** One of two international gateways in the Kurdistan Region (the other being Erbil/ORER); exact hall assignment not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟥 **The field sits within the standing ORBB FIR conflict-zone advisory and has no ILS** (Briefing §3.2/§3.4) — treat any terminal/handling assumption as provisional and re-confirm through current operator/handling-agent channels.
