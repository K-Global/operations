## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 3** is the field's primary international passenger terminal; **Terminal 1** handles domestic and low-cost/charter operations. 🟧 Tier-4 sourced (industry/travel-guide material), not independently AIP-confirmed.
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal 3, consistent with scheduled international service — confirm concourse/gate assignment with handling. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals process through Terminal 3; passport control/immigration operates H24 `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.3]`.
- **Notes:** A public tender for a Terminal 3 eastern-expansion project (additional ~50,000 m² terminal plus underground baggage/security facility) has been reported — confirm current terminal-programme status before finalising a stand/gate plan. 🟧

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Category L (our) stands:** Available across the main aprons (N, J, L) and the Terminal 3 apron/concourses; Terminal 3 concourse stands (B/C/D/E) use an Advanced Visual Docking Guidance System (AVDGS) `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.20]`. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** None identified as excluding Category L equipment; **Taxilane H is wingspan-restricted to 36 m** — relevant only for a larger-than-Category-L type, not our current operation.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Apron V (V1/V2) uses assigned start-up positions rather than gate-connected engine start `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.20]` — availability for a scheduled turn not confirmed. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact Terminal 3 gate for a scheduled destination turn; confirm with handling.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** See [Briefing §13](index.md) for named rapid-exit taxiways and the Taxilane H wingspan restriction.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **Fully coordinated** — applications required 48 h in advance (Mon–Thu) or 72 h in advance (Fri–Sun), submitted to the airport slot/parking coordination address `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.20]`. 🟧 Exact IATA Level 1/2/3 designation not stated in the reachable extract.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No single hard curfew; instead a **layered set of night-time movement restrictions**: RWY 30 closed for takeoff 23:00–06:00 LT (unless approved for operational reasons); other runways closed for takeoff roughly 01:40–05:00 LT (summer) / 01:40–05:30 LT (winter); the **field is closed for all landings daily 01:00–02:00 LT**; and an annual **Yom Kippur** closure applies (last ARR/DEP 14:00 LT on the eve; first ARR 22:30 LT / first DEP 23:30 LT on the day) `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.20/2.21]`. 🟥
- **Commercial impact:** The daily 01:00–02:00 LT landing closure and the runway-specific takeoff windows both constrain late-night/early-morning scheduling — build buffer on any rotation approaching these windows. The fully coordinated slot regime makes on-time performance a real network consideration.
- **CTOT / flow control:** Pushback/taxi clearance is issued not later than 10 minutes before CTOT `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.20]`; broader regional ATFM/flow-control exposure should be assessed against the current airspace-status picture (see [Briefing §3.2](index.md)). 🟧
- **Standing NOTAM reminder:** confirm current airspace-status bulletins before finalising any schedule change that affects routing or alternates (see Briefing §3.2/§4).

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** A Noise Monitoring Terminal (NMT) network enforces per-location maximum departure noise levels (roughly 85–93 dB(A) depending on aircraft weight and location) `[Israel AIP AD 2 LLBG 2.21]`; a stricter "reduced noise level" (−3 dB(A)) applies for takeoffs in the 05:00/05:30–06:00 LT shoulder window. Specific financial noise-charge figures not confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** The runway-specific takeoff restrictions and the daily 01:00–02:00 LT landing closure are **hard movement restrictions** — plan schedule buffer rather than relying on an exception. 🟥
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Avoid planning a rotation that relies on a landing between 01:00–02:00 LT, or a takeoff on RWY 30 between 23:00–06:00 LT, or on the other runways in the 01:40–05:00/05:30 LT window — treat any schedule slippage into these windows as a real operational-risk trigger.
