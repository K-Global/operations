## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat, low (204 ft AMSL) reclaimed coastal desert — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 The field sits inside an **active regional conflict-zone/security picture** — the OKAC FIR is overflight-prohibited (arrivals operable only with prior approval), the field is under an EASA CZIB "avoid at all levels" advisory, and **GNSS jamming/spoofing is pervasive** across the Gulf. Brief the conventional-navaid (DME/DME/IRU) fallback for any FMS position anomaly and expect ATC to compensate with radar vectors/heading assignments. See the [Middle East Airspace Briefing](../../../../airspace/middle-east.md) §9/§11 for the live, re-check-at-planning detail.
- **Airspace / traffic:** Kuwait Approach is understood to be a radar facility as one of the developed continental Gulf FIRs; exact sector/traffic-density detail not confirmed this pass. 🟧

## 10. Gotchas

- **OKAC FIR overflight is prohibited** — arrivals require prior approval under the current security regime; re-verify at planning, every time.
- **No CAT III chart found in the current AIP index** — do not plan on deep low-visibility arrival capability without a fresh check.
- **GNSS jamming/spoofing is pervasive regionally** — expect FMS position drift and be ready for ATC-issued radar vectors as the fallback.
- **Terminal 1, the normal international terminal, is currently closed for repairs** — confirm current terminal/stand assignment before planning any arrival here.
- **The new 16/34 runway is oriented independently of the 15/33 parallel pair** and sits west of the main terminal complex — expect a longer/different taxi-in if assigned this runway.
- **Extreme summer heat (routinely >50°C)** is a genuine performance consideration on approach/landing planning, not just departure — confirm current-day performance figures.
