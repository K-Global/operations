## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** All three runways (3,400–4,580 m) are non-limiting for any K Global type.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** 🟥 **The dominant performance consideration at this field.** Kuwait's summer temperatures regularly exceed 50°C per the airport's own design documentation — plan derated/assumed-temperature thrust, V-speeds and tyre-speed limits with genuine hot-day margins in mind, despite the field's low (204 ft) elevation. See [`OM E — High-Elevation Aerodrome Operations`](../../../../../flight-ops/high-elevation-aerodrome-operations.md) (its "hot lower field" framework applies directly here).
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Not a normal consideration at this climate; occasional heavy-rain/flash-flood events are a documented design consideration for the airfield's drainage systems, but no braking-action data was found. 🟧
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — no field-length penalty for any K Global type at OKKK; the hot-weather derate consideration applies fleet-wide rather than to one airframe.

## 10. Gotchas

- **OKAC FIR overflight is prohibited** — departures require prior approval under the current security regime; re-verify at planning, every time.
- **No CAT III chart found in the current AIP index** — do not plan on deep low-visibility departure capability without a fresh check.
- **Extreme summer heat (routinely >50°C)** demands genuine hot-day performance planning despite the field's low elevation.
- **The new 16/34 runway is oriented independently of the 15/33 parallel pair** and sits west of the main terminal complex — do not assume a shared taxi/config relationship with 15/33.
- **De-icing is NIL** — the seasonal caution here is heat, not cold.
- **GNSS jamming/spoofing is pervasive regionally** — brief the conventional-navaid fallback and expect vectors/heading assignments from ATC as compensation.
