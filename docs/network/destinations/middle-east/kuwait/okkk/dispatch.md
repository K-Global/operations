## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Five numbered terminals. **Terminal 1** (opened 1979, designed by Kenzo Tange) is the original main international building and, under normal conditions, serves all international airlines other than Kuwait Airways and Jazeera Airways. **Terminal 2** (Foster + Partners design, ~700,000 m², targeted for 25–50M pax/year capacity) is under construction, targeted for opening in late 2026. **Terminal 3** is the general-aviation terminal, currently closed for reasons connected to the T2 construction programme (predates the 2026 conflict). **Terminal 4** (opened 2018) is Kuwait Airways' dedicated terminal. **Terminal 5** (opened 2018) is Jazeera Airways' dedicated terminal.
- **Our operation uses:** 🟥 Under normal conditions a K Global international call would route through **Terminal 1** — but **T1 has been closed for repairs since mid-2026** following drone-strike damage (a subsequent strike in June 2026 caused further structural damage per public reporting). As of this build, **foreign/international carriers are progressively resuming service** using available capacity while T1 remains closed; confirm the *current* terminal assignment directly with handling/DGCA before planning any K Global call to OKKK.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Port-of-Entry status confirmed; hall/desk assignment depends on which terminal is currently handling international traffic — see above.
- **Notes:** 🟥 The terminal picture at OKKK is **actively in flux** in 2026 — driven by both the pre-existing T2 construction programme *and* conflict-related damage/closure of T1. Treat any terminal assignment in this document as provisional and re-verify close to the planning date.
