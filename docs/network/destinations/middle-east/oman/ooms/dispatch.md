## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** OOMS operates a single, large integrated passenger terminal north of the original runway, opened **18 March 2018**, covering roughly 580,000 m², with 118 check-in counters, 10 baggage-reclaim belts and 82 immigration counters `[Wikipedia, retrieved 2026-07-26]`. The pre-2018 terminal (opened 1973, expanded through the 1980s–90s) has since been retired from routine passenger service.
- **Our operation uses:** The current single terminal for all scheduled passenger service — no separate low-cost or remote terminal split confirmed for our operation. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear immigration in the single terminal; 82 immigration counters reported terminal-wide `[Wikipedia, retrieved 2026-07-26]`.
- **Notes:** Terminal capacity is planned in phases — Phase 1 ~20M pax/year, with Phase 2/3 expansion toward 24M and 48M respectively `[Wikipedia, retrieved 2026-07-26]`; current build-out stage relative to these phases not independently confirmed. 🟧
