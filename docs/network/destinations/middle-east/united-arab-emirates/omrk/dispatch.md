## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Two small existing buildings — a **Departure terminal (4,933 m²)** and an **Arrival terminal (3,134 m²)** — supplemented by a VIP terminal (capacity ~60 pax) and a Business/Premiere lounge (capacity ~30 pax). A **new 30,000 m² terminal** is under construction, aimed at raising capacity toward ~3 million annual passengers (from roughly 1M+ in 2025); construction is reported as targeted for completion around 2028. 🟧 Figures are media-sourced (Khaleej Times, Skift), not an official ACI/ICAO statistic register.
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable — OMRK carries no K Global scheduled service (not in VAMSYS).
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Standard Port-of-Entry processing through the existing Arrival terminal; the new terminal is reported to add upgraded customs/passport-control infrastructure and electronic gates. 🟧
- **Notes:** A **separate private-aviation terminal** (dedicated hangars/aircraft parking) has been tendered, aimed at high-net-worth/private-jet traffic linked to the emirate's tourism development (incl. the Al Marjan Island resort/casino project) — operates independently of the main passenger terminal programme. 🟧

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Not independently confirmed by code letter; **Stands 4–19** (concrete, PCN 120/F/B/W/T, 487 × 210 m apron) are the larger-aircraft cluster, with maximum accommodated size **up to Boeing 747** for positioning/overhaul. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** **Stands 1–3** (asphalt, PCN 58/F/B/W/U, 130 × 81 m) are the smaller cluster — confirm suitability before assigning a larger type.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** A dedicated **General Aviation parking apron** (concrete, PCN 111/R/B/W/T, ~32,678 m²) exists separately from the numbered stands.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not applicable — no K Global operation at this field.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** See [Briefing §13](index.md) — no separate aircraft Ground frequency is published; taxi guidance between stands and the runway comes from Tower.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None published — this is not an IATA-coordinated field.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** 🟥 **PPR required for any flight operating between 2200–0800 local**, submitted via the standard Landing Permission process (Briefing §5) to the airport Duty Officer, forwarded to RAK DCA for approval. PPR is also required **at any time** for instrument-approach training, circuit work, or qualifying cross-country flights.
- **CTOT / flow control:** No ATFM/CTOT regime independently found; given proximity to the busier Dubai/Abu Dhabi TMA flow-management environment, confirm at planning whether any wider-FIR flow restriction could apply on a given day. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The PPR regime — rather than a slot file — is the scheduling guardrail here: any planned overnight or late-night rotation (2200–0800 local) needs the Landing Permission Request lodged and approved in advance, not arranged same-day.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** The airport's **own ground-handling FBO**, office located in the passenger terminal; contact via Duty Officer or handling@rakairport.com. 🟩
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not confirmed — not in VAMSYS. 🟧
- **Services:** Pushback/tugs, air-start unit, GPU, passenger stairs (rated up to Boeing 747-size aircraft), toilet-service units, baggage vehicles, apron service vehicles, a runway-friction tester, passenger/crew minibus, and FOD sweeper — all listed in the operator's own published equipment inventory.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Terminal-programme construction disruption during the 2025–2028 build-out is a plausible risk item; the mixed radar/procedural control regime (Briefing §3.2) could also extend approach sequencing outside the Sun–Thu 0800–1400-local radar window. Neither independently quantified this pass. 🟧

## 7. Fuel

- **Supplier / into-plane:** Airport company's own bowser service; Jet A-1 and Avtur sourced from **ADNOC**. 🟩
- **Uplift availability & hours:** H24, per the operator's own published fuel-service statement. 🟩
- **Price / tankering angle:** Not applicable — no K Global operation currently plans fuel through this field. The operator notes bulk-order discounts may be available; not independently priced. 🟧
- **Quality / freeze notes:** 🟧 The Jet A-1 supplied **does not contain an anti-icing (FSII) additive** — a genuine consideration for any type/operator that relies on supplier-added FSII rather than onboard injection. See [`OM E — Fuel Policy`](../../../../../flight-ops/fuel-policy.md).

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** None published — the operator states "No Restrictions are in place" for aircraft noise. 🟧 Not independently cross-checked against a primary AIP source.
