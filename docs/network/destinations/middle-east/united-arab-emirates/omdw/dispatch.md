## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** OMDW opened in 2010 as a cargo-focused facility, adding passenger service from 2013 at historically low volumes with a limited roster of budget/charter carriers. A **128 billion AED (~US$34.85 billion) new passenger terminal** was approved in April 2024 as part of a masterplan targeting eventual capacity of up to 260 million passengers/year across five parallel runways and roughly 400 gates — a **multi-year/multi-decade construction program**, not a current-day facility.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — current-day (2026) passenger terminal capability at this field should be independently verified before planning a scheduled operation here. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Assumed present for international cargo/passenger clearance; exact facility/hours not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 This is an **active construction site as much as an operating airport** — confirm current terminal/gate availability and any construction-related NOTAMs before finalising a stand plan.
