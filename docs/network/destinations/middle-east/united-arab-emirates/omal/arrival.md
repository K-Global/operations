## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 **Jebel Hafeet** (summit ~1,249 m / 4,098 ft AMSL) rises roughly **15 NM south-east of the field**, representing ~3,230 ft of vertical relief above field elevation — a genuine standalone terrain feature in an otherwise flat desert setting. Respect the current-chart MSA on any south/south-easterly arrival or missed-approach segment — see [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** No wake, closely-spaced-parallel or offset-approach considerations apply — single runway. Winter radiation fog and shamal dust are the main weather-driven approach threats (Briefing §14).
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Approach control is **remoted to Abu Dhabi Radar East**, not worked by an on-field radar unit; the field carries a very high volume of resident flight-training traffic (600+ movements/day reported) sharing the single runway — brief for possible sequencing behind circuit/training activity.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Jebel Hafeet** (~4,098 ft AMSL, ~15 NM SE) is a real terrain feature — do not assume the area is uniformly flat desert on a south-easterly arrival.
- **Approach control is remoted to Abu Dhabi Radar East** — this is not an on-field radar unit; confirm the correct approach frequency.
- **Single runway shared with a very high volume of resident flight-training traffic** — expect possible extended vectoring/sequencing behind circuit activity.
- **Code F/A380 aircraft:** taxiways A, E, F, L, M, O only on taxi-in — **no backtracking or 180° turn on the runway**.
- **Winter radiation fog (Nov–May, worst Jan–Feb) and shamal dust** can reduce visibility with limited warning — brief a diversion contingency in season.
- **OMAL is not in VAMSYS** — do not assume any company-confirmed stand, handling or turnaround arrangement exists at this field.
