## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None found — no IATA slot-coordination level confirmed for OMAL. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No formal curfew found in reachable sources. Operator material characterises the field's traffic as heavily daylight-hours given its training-flight role, and a separate operational note states that **unscheduled aircraft (other than VVIP/VIP) should plan arrival times preferably between 1300–0300 UTC**, subject to prior approval from Al Ain OPS Management — treat as a scheduling preference to confirm, not a proven hard curfew. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** No ATFM/CTOT regime confirmed; the field's own **"Al Ain Flow Control System"** governs training-sortie approval (dual sign-off from ATS Al Ain and Abu Dhabi Radar East) rather than a commercial slot programme. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** With no VAMSYS role, there is no schedulable-window analysis to perform; if the field is ever brought into the network, the flow-control/training-approval regime and the unscheduled-arrival preferred window would need to be reconciled with any proposed schedule.
