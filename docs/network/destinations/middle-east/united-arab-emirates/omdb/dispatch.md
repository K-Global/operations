## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** connects via automated people-mover to a large satellite concourse used by international carriers. **Terminal 2** serves budget/regional carriers via a bus-boarding gate layout with no jetbridges. **Terminal 3** is the field's largest facility, comprising three concourses and historically home to the dominant widebody long-haul operation, including dedicated A380-capable gates. A separate **cargo terminal** (low-millions-of-tonnes/year capacity) and a **General Aviation Terminal / Executive Flights Terminal** (premium check-in with buggy transfer) sit nearby.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — expect a Code E/F assignment in Terminal 3 consistent with its widebody-oriented layout, but this has not been independently verified for our operation. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals process through T1 or T3 depending on gate assignment.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Confirm current gate/concourse assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan; the long-term network-transition context (OMDW expansion) may affect terminal allocation over time (see [Briefing §18](index.md)).
