## 10. Gotchas

- **Summer heat is the dominant performance planning item** — routine 45°C+ days can be weight-limiting even though the field itself is near sea level; do not assume a "non-issue" field on elevation class alone.
- **Shamal dust/haboob events** can reduce visibility rapidly — confirm current LVP status before a departure in season.
- **Terminal A is a relatively new, still-maturing single-terminal operation** — confirm current taxi routing/stand assignment rather than assuming a static picture.
- **ATC frequency table for this field is thin in public sources** — confirm the full Delivery/Ground/Tower/Approach sequence against the current AIRAC before relying on any single frequency listed here.
