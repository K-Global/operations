## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 AIP states plainly **"high terrain in all quadrants."** Charted antenna obstacle **837 ft MSL, 0.86 NM from THR 08T, bearing 173° GEO**. The Greenland icecap dome (~10,500 ft) is the wider driftdown constraint — see [Briefing §3.1](index.md) and the [Arctic airspace brief](../../../../airspace/arctic.md) §7.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Field operates on TRUE north, no magnetic correction applied** (mag var 46°W published but unused) — confirm FMS/heading-reference setup before any approach; a magnetic-reference error here is a live CFIT threat. 🟥 Surface wind >30 kt from 125°–185° GEO → severe downdrafts + moderate turbulence to 5,000 ft on final RWY 08T. 🟧 PAPI noted "not coincident" between the two ends.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Thule CTR (Class D, 5.2 NM radius, 2,800 ft MSL–GND), combined Thule TWR/APP, DASR referenced. **ATS only Mon–Fri 1200–2000 UTC, closed weekends/US holidays** — outside those windows there is no controlled service at all. See the [Arctic airspace brief](../../../../airspace/arctic.md) and the [North America airspace brief](../../../../airspace/north-america.md) for the broader regional network context.

- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟥 Severe downdrafts/turbulence on RWY 08T final combined with seasonal runway contamination make excursion risk a standing consideration on this field, not an edge case.

## 10. Gotchas

- **AIP §3.12: field cannot be used for planned emergency divert or weather alternate** — any arrival here follows a genuine unplanned emergency, not a scheduled or plannable diversion.
- **Field operates on TRUE north, no magnetic correction** — verify FMS/heading reference before flying any procedure; runway designators (08T/26T) are TRUE, not magnetic.
- **Surface wind >30 kt from 125°–185° GEO → severe downdrafts + moderate turbulence to 5,000 ft on final RWY 08T** — a standing, AIP-documented hazard, not a rare event.
- **RCR 08 or lower expected 15 Sep–15 May** (~8 months/yr) — plan for contaminated-runway braking action across most of the year.
- **ATS only Mon–Fri 1200–2000 UTC; AD closed Sat/Sun/US federal holidays** — an emergency arrival outside those windows meets an unstaffed or minimally staffed field.
- **Confirmed quarters mandatory before arrival; hangar space "extremely limited"; no repair facilities** — plan for very limited on-field recovery support.
- **PPR/military access gate governs everything** — coordinate through HAF/A30 or the named FAA Flight Standards District Office/Principal Operations Inspector channel; this is not a normal ATC/handling arrangement.
