## 5. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None — access is governed by the **PPR process** (30-day advance via HAF/A30, or 72-hour PPR under blanket approval), not a slot/CTOT mechanism (Briefing §5). 🟥
- **Curfew / night restriction:** AD open Mon–Thu 1000–2100 (0900–2000), Fri 1000–2000 (0900–1900) local; **closed Sat/Sun/US federal holidays**; PN (prior notice) 6 HR to the AD even within open hours. 🟥
- **CTOT / flow control:** No civil slot regime — ATS is provided only Mon–Fri 1200–2000 UTC; outside that window there is no controlled service at all (Briefing §3.2). 🟥
- **Commercial impact:** For K Global, the only realistic "commercial impact" scenario is a genuine emergency diversion landing outside PPR/staffed hours — plan crew/ops actions for that contingency, not for a schedulable use of this field.
