## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 BGSF sits at the head of Søndre Strømfjord, roughly 190 km inland, with rising terrain on both sides of the approach/departure track — there is **no easy circling option**. See [Briefing §3.1](index.md) and the [Arctic airspace brief](../../../../airspace/arctic.md) §7 (icecap terrain/MORA).
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **RWY 27 has no published instrument approach** — a forced RWY 27 arrival in IMC requires visual/circling only inside fjord walls, at night or in marginal weather. 🟧 Katabatic/foehn winds off the icecap can produce rapid, hard-to-forecast wind and turbulence changes channelled along the fjord (09/27) axis.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Procedural (non-radar) control since Sondrestrom's radar service ended ~Nov 2025 — workload is position-report driven, not conflict driven. See the [Arctic airspace brief](../../../../airspace/arctic.md) §3/§10 (Nuuk FIR procedural framework) and the [North America airspace brief](../../../../airspace/north-america.md) for the broader regional network context.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain rises on both sides of the fjord; assume the missed-approach climb must clear valley walls, not open terrain — verify the published MAP against the current chart before relying on it. 🟥
- **Re-sequencing environment:** Low-traffic procedural field; a go-around would be handled via position reports, not radar vectoring — expect self-sequencing guidance from Sondrestrom Approach/FIS.
- **Go-around traps:** A go-around off a forced RWY 27 visual/circling attempt is the most sensitive case at this field — brief the terrain-clearance path before ever accepting that runway in marginal weather.

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 27 has no published instrument approach** — expect RWY 09 to be the planned instrument runway; do not accept a forced RWY 27 IMC arrival without a clear terrain-clearance plan.
- **No easy circling option** in the fjord — terrain both sides means a late runway change is a real threat, not a preference item.
- **Procedural (non-radar) control** — maintain compulsory position reports; do not expect radar vectoring or radar-derived separation on arrival.
- **RFF may be limited to CAT 7 (vs CAT 8 certified) and may require 4h prior notice** — confirm before diverting unannounced with our largest types.
- **Fuel cold-soak** — an aircraft arriving off a long high-latitude sector may already be close to the Jet A-1 freeze margin (−47 °C) before touchdown — see [`OM E — Polar & High-Latitude Operations`](../../../../../flight-ops/polar.md).
