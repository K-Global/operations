## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 No close-in high terrain identified for either approach path in reachable sources; St. Croix's interior hills (~1,088 ft max) lie clear of the field's southwestern coastal location, but no MSA-sector figure was independently confirmed this pass — cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 The **RWY 28 approach is published as sometimes obscured by smoke from a landfill east of the airport** — brief a go-around/RWY 10 contingency when winds otherwise favour 28. A charted **217 ft building obstacle sits 5,330 ft from the runway, 800 ft right of centreline** on the RWY 28 side. **Birds & wildlife** are noted on and around the airport (published AIP remark) — a standing threat on either end.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟩 Low-density field (historically ~99 movements/day); San Juan CERAP provides approach control — no high-density traffic concern identified.

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** RWY 10 — **9,003 ft**; RWY 28 — **8,998 ft** (1,000 ft displaced threshold). Both figures reflect the same shared displaced-threshold geometry (see [Briefing §7](index.md)) — neither runway offers the full 10,004 ft physical length for landing. Both distances are ample for any K Global type at typical landing weights.

## 9. Arrival frequency sequence

- **Sequence:** **San Juan CERAP 128.65/279.6 (approach) → Saint Croix Tower 118.6/239.3 (0700–2200) → Saint Croix Ground 121.7 (0700–2200).** Outside Tower hours, San Juan CERAP provides IFR clearance/coordination by phone (787-253-8664/8665) and CTAF (118.6) covers the traffic-advisory function; runway lighting is pilot-activated via CTAF 2300–0700. Take the assigned frequency and confirm current chart. (Frequencies from [Briefing §8](index.md).)
- **Approach/Departure control:** **San Juan CERAP (TJZS)** — see [North America airspace brief](../../../../airspace/north-america.md) 🟧 (does not currently cover the Caribbean/San Juan CERAP area — see [Briefing §18](index.md)).

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 28 is never the precision end** — no ILS is published for it; if wind forces a RWY 28 arrival, expect RNAV(GPS)-only minima and brief the landfill-smoke/obstacle items (§5).
- **Tower closes at 2200** — after that, expect San Juan CERAP by phone/frequency and pilot-activated lighting via CTAF.
- **ARFF unavailable 2300–0500** — a real risk-acceptance item for any arrival inside that window.
- **Birds & wildlife on/around the airport** — standing consideration on both ends.
- **Hurricane season (Jun–Nov, peak Aug–Oct)** — the field's dominant seasonal arrival-planning risk; watch tropical-cyclone advisories.
