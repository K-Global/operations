## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single two-story terminal, ~181,000 sq ft, 10 gates (Virgin Islands Port Authority). A multi-phase expansion/modernisation programme has been under way since 2018 (Phase 1 — lounge/restroom/concessions upgrade — completed December 2021); Phases 2–4 (second level, jet bridges, additional hold room, baggage-handling upgrade) remain unfunded/planned as of the operator's 2026 published status. In 2024 VIPA selected a private partner for a longer-term redevelopment agreement.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — gate/concourse assignment for a K Global service not published. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** CBP/PoE presence indicated (see [Briefing §5/§6](index.md)); hall location and connection implications not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** The terminal programme is **mid-modernisation** (Phase 1 of 4 complete as of Dec 2021; Phases 2–4, including jet bridges, still unfunded per the operator's own 2026 published status) — confirm current gate/jet-bridge availability before planning a contact-gate turn. 🟧

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None found — not a slot-coordinated field.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No formal curfew; the practical overnight constraints are **ARFF unavailable 2300–0500** and Tower closed 2200–0700 (San Juan CERAP provides phone/frequency IFR clearance outside Tower hours). 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** None identified — low-density field, no ATFM/EDCT-style program found.
- **Commercial impact:** The overnight ARFF/Tower gap is the field's main scheduling guardrail — avoid planning a scheduled arrival/departure inside the 2300–0500 ARFF gap without an operational risk review.
