## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** Not confirmed in reachable research pass — pull the current-AIRAC STAR list at planning. 🟧
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** **RWY 12L is the usual landing runway.** Arrivals through the **Havana FIR** are typically descended to **FL250–FL200** before transfer to **Mérida Centre** — crews may need to call Mérida Centre approximately 10 minutes prior to that boundary. During quiet periods, expect a **direct clearance to the CUN or NCP VOR**, then radar vectors toward a point at **D20 on the extended centreline for RWY 12L, at 4,000 ft** — be prepared for an early turn and a reduced track distance to touchdown. Crews may also be vectored to the **CUN VOR** and asked to fly a westerly radial outbound to ~20 NM at low level before turning back to the field.
- **Transition to approach:** Radar vectors from Mérida Centre/Approach onto the procedural VOR/DME or ILS as assigned; verify the charted transition.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** Not independently confirmed for MMUN specifically — apply standard Mexico FIR speed constraints pending verification. 🟧

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 **None.** No significant terrain in the vicinity of MMUN — a materially different and lower-workload profile than MMMX.
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 **PAPIs on RWY 30L/30R are reported difficult to see**, becoming visible only around 1,000 ft on the approach — brief this specifically whenever those runways are assigned (winter/heavy-rain configuration). **Single-runway operations** (12L closed) shift landing traffic to 12R, typically for very early/late arrivals.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Seasonal peak-period congestion (winter high season, spring break, Jul/Aug); Mérida ACC (SENEAM) provides area control within the countrywide Mexico FIR (MMFR), with a Havana FIR boundary handoff on eastbound-adjacent routings.

## 10. Gotchas

- **PAPIs on RWY 30L/30R are reported difficult to see**, only becoming visible around 1,000 ft — brief this specifically whenever those runways are in use.
- **Single-runway operations** (12L closed) shift landing traffic to 12R for very early/late arrivals — confirm current runway status before planning the approach.
- **Mérida Centre may offer a direct-to-VOR clearance with an early turn** during quiet periods — be ready for a compressed descent/energy profile versus the full STAR.
- **Confirm which VOR (NCP vs CUN) is active for the outbound vs inbound leg** of the procedural VOR/DME approach.
- **Hurricane season (Jun–Nov)** — check the current tropical-cyclone forecast track before descent into the terminal area on any rotation transiting the region in season; the airport has a history of short-notice operational suspension.
- **No significant terrain** — a materially lower CFIT-workload arrival than the network's high-elevation/mountain-ringed fields; do not, however, relax standard MSA discipline.
