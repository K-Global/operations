## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Two terminals — **Terminal 1** (the larger, older facility, majority of both domestic and international traffic) and **Terminal 2** (used by a subset of carriers, including long-haul/partner international services). An inter-terminal connection exists (bus/APM per public sources; not independently confirmed). 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — terminal assignment depends on handling-agreement/slot allocation; confirm with the local handler ahead of each rotation. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear immigration in the assigned terminal; reported average immigration processing time ~23 minutes in one public source — not independently verified. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 Confirm current-year terminal/gate assignment before finalising a stand plan; both terminals have separate operating-hours windows (T1 05:00–23:59, T2 06:00–23:00 — see §4).

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** No formal IATA Level confirmed; the **61-movements/hour saturation declaration** (reaffirmed 2022) is the operative capacity constraint, functioning similarly to a slot cap in practice. 🟥
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard curfew found. Terminal-specific operating windows are published: **Terminal 1 05:00–23:59**, **Terminal 2 06:00–23:00** local — outside these windows, expect no scheduled service at the respective terminal. A **night noise restriction on heavier aircraft** is separately reported without confirmed specifics. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not independently confirmed as a formal ATFM/CTOT program specific to MMMX, but the saturation cap almost certainly drives peak-hour flow management. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The **61-movements/hour ceiling** at one of the world's busiest dual-runway airports, combined with the closely-spaced dependent-parallel geometry (Briefing §1/§3.2), makes **on-time pushback and schedule discipline** a real network-reliability lever here — treat MMMX as a field where recovered delay is hard to claw back intraday.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** **Menzies** reported among ground handlers serving MMMX; not exhaustively confirmed as our contracted agent. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 16 min / taxi-out 20 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody/narrowbody service times for the assigned type.
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav (uplift permitted per operational cross-check), catering and cabin services expected at a major-hub stand.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Saturation-cap-driven ATC sequencing delays, closely-spaced dependent-parallel throughput limits, and the reported Terminal-1 push-back/taxiway-blocking constraint (§3) can each add unplanned turn time. High-elevation engine-start technique (hot/hung-start risk, Briefing §3.5) is a minor additional turnaround-planning factor.

## 9. De-icing provisioning & season

- **Availability:** Not typically required given the two-season (dry Nov–Apr / wet May–Oct) climate profile — treat as **NIL by default**. 🟧
- **Season:** No recognised de-icing season confirmed; occasional early-morning cold at 7,316 ft elevation in the dry season is a theoretical consideration, not a confirmed operational requirement. 🟧
- **Provisioning:** Not applicable absent a confirmed requirement.
- **Commercial impact:** Negligible expected; the dominant seasonal commercial-impact driver at MMMX is the **hot-and-high payload/fuel trade on warm-day departures**, not de-icing (§7/§16 of the Briefing).

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** 🟥 The **61-movements/hour saturation cap** combined with the **closely-spaced dependent-parallel runway geometry** — this is a structural capacity constraint at one of the busiest dual-runway airports globally, not a weather-driven one.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** High at peak hours given the saturation declaration; expect ATC-driven sequencing delay independent of weather. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Dry-season early-morning fog and wet-season afternoon thunderstorms are secondary reliability risks layered on top of the structural capacity constraint. 🟧

---

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** No hard curfew found; the operative constraint is each **terminal's published operating window** (T1 05:00–23:59, T2 06:00–23:00) rather than a movement ban — confirm consequence of an off-window arrival with handling. 🟧
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Build schedule buffer around the **61-movements/hour saturation cap** at peak banks, and treat the closely-spaced dependent-parallel geometry as a standing throughput limiter when planning connection banks through this destination.
