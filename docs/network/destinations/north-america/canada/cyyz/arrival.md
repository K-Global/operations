## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat southern-Ontario lakeshore plain at 569 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 The **five-runway dual-parallel-pair-plus-crosswind (CSPO) geometry** sustains continuous high traffic density; Transport Canada's 2025 safety alert on Closely Spaced Parallel Operations at CYYZ is direct evidence of this complexity. Winter lake-effect/blowing-snow events can force reduced-rate or single-stream arrivals. Wake from an adjacent parallel is a standing consideration during simultaneous/dependent operations.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 Very high density under **Toronto Terminal** — Canada's busiest terminal airspace; brief for re-sequencing and extended vectoring, especially in marginal weather.

---

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat lakeshore plain) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around drops you back into a very high-density, five-runway CSPO radar environment — expect extended vectoring and possible holding before re-sequencing, especially during a winter lake-effect event.
- **Go-around traps:** Traffic/config awareness across the parallel-pair-plus-crosswind system on the miss; multiple simultaneous runway operations increase re-sequencing complexity.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Five-runway CSPO geometry** sustains continuous high traffic density — expect vectoring/holding, especially in marginal weather or during a bank; this is the subject of a dedicated 2025 Transport Canada safety alert.
- **Winter lake-effect snow/blowing-snow events can drop the field below CAT I minima quickly** — brief the CAT III (RWY 05) plan and a holding/diversion contingency in season.
- **Displaced thresholds on RWY 05/23, 24R, 15R/33L** reduce usable LDA below the full paved length — check the runway-specific figure.
- **Config change across the five-runway system driven by a wind shift** can bring a late runway change — watch the ATIS wind trend.
- **Taxiway K is uncontrolled and wingspan/ALR-restricted** — a ground-workload factor on taxi-in for Code E/F types.
- **Restricted Hours Operating Policy 00:30–06:29** — a delayed arrival approaching the restricted window has direct noise-compliance consequences; do not plan a schedule that relies on a post-00:30 landing without confirming exemption criteria.
