## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 Benign boreal lake-district terrain, flat to gently rolling, no significant high ground close to the field (Briefing §3.1). Confirm MSA sectors against the current-AIRAC area chart before any real use.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Cold Lake Air Weapons Range (CLAWR)** restricted/danger airspace lies ~35 km north and is in continuous/near-continuous use for fighter training, including the annual **Maple Flag** exercise — expect high-density non-standard traffic and mandatory coordination through military ATC (Briefing §3.2). 🟧 Winter contamination (snow/ice/blowing snow) is the seasonal excursion risk on landing (Briefing §3.3).
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 Active RCAF fighter operational base (4 Wing, CF-18 Hornet) and NORAD western-air-defence node — cross-ref [Briefing §3.2](index.md) and the [North America airspace brief](../../../../airspace/north-america.md) for regional context; no dedicated Edmonton Centre FIR brief has yet been built in this network.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Military PPR and real-time ATC coordination are mandatory** — this is never a planned arrival; treat any use as an unbriefed emergency event requiring full command-level coordination (Briefing §3.5).
- **CLAWR restricted airspace ~35 km north, high-density fighter traffic including annual Maple Flag exercise** — expect non-standard sequencing.
- **Sharp (>135°) taxiway turns at A1-A/A2-A** — hazard for large-aircraft ground manoeuvring.
- **No civil fuel uplift on arrival** — do not plan to refuel here even in a diversion scenario.
- **Do not confuse CYOD with Cold Lake Regional Airport (CEN5)**, a separate uncontrolled civil field ~4 NM NW to which civil/unscheduled traffic is normally redirected (Briefing §2/§4).
- **Winter contamination risk** — confirm braking action/runway condition via NOTAM before any winter-season use.
