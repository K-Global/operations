## 10. Gotchas

- **Military PPR is the binding constraint** — never plan a departure from CYOD as anything other than an emergency/military-coordinated event.
- **Sharp (>135°) taxiway turns at A1-A/A2-A** — briefed for large aircraft; a genuine ground-manoeuvring hazard, not a paperwork item.
- **No civil fuel uplift** — do not plan to depart from here after uplifting fuel; fuel is military-supplied only.
- **High-density fighter traffic and CLAWR restricted airspace ~35 km north** — expect non-standard procedural loads and coordinate closely with military ATC (Briefing §3.2).
- **Noise-abatement straight-in/no-overhead-break restriction outside core hours** — verify current AIRAC before assuming any departure/arrival profile.
- **Prior tier-4 frequency figures (Wing Ops "34.02", PMSV "34.46", ATIS "26 MHz") were decimal-shift errors** — use the CFS-confirmed values only (Briefing §8).
