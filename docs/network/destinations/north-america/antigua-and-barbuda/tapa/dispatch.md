## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single modern passenger terminal (Terminal A), opened August 2015, ~23,000 m² with four jet bridges, described at opening as the region's most modern terminal. The former terminal building was renamed **Terminal B** and is being redeveloped to support cruise-ship home-porting ground transport, not passenger flight processing.
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal A — the sole active passenger-flight terminal.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals process through Terminal A; specific hall/desk configuration not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 Confirm current terminal capacity/gate assignment against the primary AIP or airport-operator source before finalising a stand plan.
