## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** One passenger terminal (new building completed and opened December 2020, replacing the prior terminal) and one dedicated cargo terminal. No domestic/international split relevant here — all scheduled traffic is international by definition (island territory).
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable in the normal course — TXKF is not a scheduled K Global stop. In a contingency diversion, the single passenger terminal and its arrivals hall would be used.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** The terminal houses the **U.S. CBP preclearance facility** for US-bound passengers, who clear US customs/immigration in Bermuda and land in the US as a domestic arrival. 🟧 exact hall/desk layout not confirmed.
- **Notes:** No construction or terminal-change items identified beyond the completed 2020 rebuild. 🟧

## 8. Customs / immigration & PoE for pax

- **Port of Entry:** **Yes** — US CBP preclearance operates from the terminal for US-bound passengers.
- **CBP / immigration hours:** **US CBP preclearance: 0700–1600 daily.** Bermuda customs/immigration hours for non-US-bound flows not confirmed. 🟧
- **Pre-clearance / visa-transit notes:** US-bound passengers clear US Customs and Border Protection in Bermuda and are treated as a domestic arrival on landing in the US — relevant if TXKF is ever used as a technical stop ahead of a US destination, though this is not the field's normal role for K Global (see §1).
- **Late/overnight/diversion caveat:** CBP preclearance operates within its published 0700–1600 window only — a late-night contingency diversion would **not** have US preclearance available and would need to be handled as a standard international arrival into Bermuda instead. 🟧

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Hurricane-season closure** (1 June–30 November, Bermuda's own historical peak late August–mid-October) is the field's defining reliability driver — a 1–2 day full closure is a real, recurring, recently-documented event (Hurricane Imelda, October 2025).
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** No ATFM/flow-program exposure identified outside hurricane season.
- **On-time reliability picture:** Not applicable in the normal course (non-destination); if used as a diversion field, plan around the tower/RFF-hours caveat (§4/Briefing §6) and hurricane-season closure risk rather than routine delay statistics. 🟧
