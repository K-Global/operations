## 8. Clearance / CTOT & departure frequency sequence

- **Clearance delivery:** Delivered on **Ground (124.5)** when the Tower is staffed; when the Tower is closed, clearance delivery is provided by **New York ARTCC on 128.5** — see [Briefing §8](index.md).
- **Frequency sequence:** Ground/Clearance (124.5) → Tower (118.1, when staffed) → Approach/Departure ("Bermuda Radar," a remote FAA New York position, 119.1) → New York Oceanic (KZWY) once clear of the terminal area. Outside tower hours, CTAF/UNICOM is 122.8 (also the pilot-controlled-lighting frequency).
- **CTOT / slot handling:** None identified — no ATFM/EDCT-style program found for TXKF.

## 10. Gotchas

- **180° turns on RWY 12/30 are prohibited unless ATC instructs** — do not plan a self-initiated back-taxi turnaround.
- **Aircraft movements on the apron are uncontrolled** — maintain own-separation awareness taxiing to/from the ramp.
- **APU use is limited to 45 minutes before scheduled take-off**, with shutdown as soon as possible when parked on Apron IV.
- **Wildlife control service is unavailable 0300–1100Z** — factor this into any departure planned in that window. 🟧
- **Flight is prohibited within 3 NM of restricted area R-3017** — verify current location/status.
- Tower is **not H24** (~0700–2300 LT) — a departure outside those hours proceeds under pilot-controlled lighting and uncontrolled-field procedures on 122.8.
