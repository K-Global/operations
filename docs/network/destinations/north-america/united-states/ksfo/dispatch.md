## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Four terminals in a horseshoe — Terminal 1 (Harvey Milk), Terminal 2, Terminal 3, and the **International Terminal** (Boarding Areas A and G). Domestic mainline is split across T1/T2/T3; all international widebody arrivals/departures work the International Terminal.
- **Our operation uses:** **International Terminal, Boarding Area A or G** for the A330-900 international service. Federal inspection (CBP) hall is in the International Terminal.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** International arrivals clear CBP in the International Terminal; onward domestic connections re-clear security after the FIS hall — build connection time accordingly.
- **Notes:** SFO runs long-cycle terminal redevelopment (T1/T3 harmonization) — confirm current gate/boarding-area assignment with handling on the day. 🟧

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** International Terminal Boarding Areas A and G carry the widebody international gates; at least six (incl. A9/G101 clusters) are A380-capable with dual/triple jetways — the A330-900 (Code E, ≈64 m span) is comfortably accommodated. 🟩
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Domestic T1/T2/T3 gates are narrowbody-oriented; do not plan a Code E onto a domestic contact gate.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Remote/hardstand use is atypical for scheduled widebody international; confirm with handling if a contact gate is unavailable during a bank. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact International Terminal gate for a scheduled arrival.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** The **Twy Zulu ≥171 ft wingspan restriction captures the A330-900** and the Tower obstructed-vision areas on Twy A affect ground routing to/from these stands — see Briefing §13 and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** No formal IATA Level 2/3 designation found in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard curfew; a **voluntary Nighttime Preferential Runway Use Program** (since 1988) governs night runway choice — night arrivals 28L/R, night departures 10L/R, core hours 0100–0600 (broader "night" often cited 2200–0700). 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** SFO is a frequent origin of **FAA Ground Delay Programs and EDCTs**, driven by marine-layer capacity loss and the reduced-throughput 28L/28R staggered-approach regime (Briefing §3.2). Expect EDCT-style ground holds on the inbound/outbound leg during marginal weather.
- **Commercial impact:** Marine-layer-season mornings (Jun–Sep, worst Jul–Aug) are the principal schedule-risk window; build arrival/turn buffers accordingly.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Full major-hub handling available; specific ramp handler for our operation not confirmed in reachable public sources (Signature Aviation confirmed on the GA/FBO fuel side). 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not confirmed; plan a standard widebody international turn and confirm minimum ground time with handling. 🟧
- **Services:** Pushback, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services all available at International Terminal Code E stands (standard major-hub provision).
- **Turnaround risk items:** Gate availability during international arrival/departure banks; CBP hall throughput at peak; ground-routing constraints (Twy Zulu wingspan rule) can add taxi time.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** Strong year-round trans-Pacific and transatlantic/tech-corridor demand; summer leisure peak overlaps the marine-layer delay season.
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Business-heavy Mon/Thu/Fri skew on the long-haul corridors; leisure fill on weekends. Silicon Valley demand keeps premium-cabin load firm year-round. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** Significant belly-cargo demand on trans-Pacific/transatlantic widebody services; SFO is a substantial belly-freight gateway. Confirm belly uplift against payload at planning. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** International arrivals feed domestic connection banks (re-clear after CBP) — inbound timing into a connection wave affects minimum connect times.

## 8. Customs / immigration & PoE for pax

- **Port of Entry:** Yes — Federal Inspection Services in the **International Terminal**.
- **CBP / immigration hours:** Reported **0600–2400 local**; not confirmed 24h in any reachable public source. 🟧
- **Pre-clearance / visa-transit notes:** Standard US entry; ESTA/visa per nationality. No pre-clearance at SFO (arrivals clear on arrival).
- **Late/overnight/diversion caveat:** If CBP is genuinely 0600–2400, a **late-night or diverted international arrival could land outside staffed hours** — verify CBP availability before planning a late/overnight slot. This is a live 🟧 in the Briefing (§18). 🟧

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Marine-layer stratus/fog (Jun–Sep, worst Jul–Aug)** collapsing arrival capacity on the closely-spaced 28L/28R pair — the airport's defining reliability threat. Winter frontal passages are a secondary driver.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** High and seasonal — SFO GDPs cascade nationally. The **permanent 2026 staggered-approach mandate** has structurally lowered the arrival rate (mid-50s/hr → ~36/hr), tightening reliability even in good weather.
- **On-time reliability picture:** Summer-morning arrivals into SFO carry the highest delay/holding exposure in the network; hold fuel and buffers should reflect this. 🟧

---

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** SFO is a **noise-sensitive airport** with an active Noise Abatement Office (Mon–Fri 0800–1700, 650-821-5100); no specific published dB penalty schedule found in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** No hard curfew to infringe; the nighttime program is voluntary (runway-preference, not a movement ban). 🟧
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Prefer the voluntary night configuration (arrivals 28L/R, departures 10L/R) for 0100–0600 movements; use Quiet Bridge Visual RWY 28R and related noise routings where offered (see Arrival page).
