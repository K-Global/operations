## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From International Terminal (Boarding Areas A/G), Ground routes toward the departure end of the 01s (01L/01R) in the west plan; confirm the exact taxi with Ground on the day. High-speed Twy T runs grooved full-width between 28R and 28L.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 **Twy Zulu — outbound heavy aircraft with wingspan ≥171 ft under power are prohibited from the westbound Twy Zulu via Twy Zulu 2; the A330-900neo (≈210 ft/64 m) is captured** — confirm alternate routing with Ground. Twy S between Twy Z and Twy S2 is closed to aircraft >215 ft span (does not affect us). Two **Tower obstructed-vision areas** on Twy A (between Gates 88/89 and between Gates F20/F21) — Tower cannot confirm those areas are clear.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** Departing the 01s in the west plan involves crossing/interacting with the 28 arrival stream — expect crossing instructions and hold-short clearances. Several **runway hold-position signs are mounted on the right** of the taxiway, not the left — do not assume left placement.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** SMGCS/low-vis taxi routing applies in marine-layer conditions; strict adherence to painted nose-gear lines required, no oversteering. Follow-me availability not confirmed. 🟧

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** Prevailing afternoon westerlies drive the "West" plan — **arrive 28L/28R, depart 01L/01R**. This is the standard daytime configuration (vZOA publishes it as "SFOW").
- **By departure direction:** Northeast/east departures fit the 01 departure naturally; westbound/oceanic departures are radar-vectored off the 01s onto the appropriate SID transition. Note the closely-spaced parallel geometry (Briefing §3.2) constrains simultaneous ops.
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** Night core hours 0100–0600 prefer **departures on 10L/10R** (over the bay, away from noise-sensitive areas).
- **Interaction with arrivals:** Departures off the 01s are threaded through/across the 28 arrival flow — expect wake and crossing management; departure rate is coupled to arrival config.

## 10. Gotchas

- **01-departure runways are the short pair** — verify take-off performance for the assigned 01L/01R, don't assume the 11,000+ ft figures.
- **Twy Zulu ≥171 ft wingspan rule captures the A330-900** — plan the ground route out with Ground before pushback.
- **Hold-position signs mounted on the right** at several locations — do not assume left-side placement.
- **Departing the 01s crosses the 28 arrival flow** — expect hold-short/crossing instructions and wake considerations.
- **EDCT/GDP is routine in marine-layer weather** — a clean METAR at the field does not mean no flow program; check before pushback.
- **1L is closed to departing tri-jets >155 ft span** (not our type, but note the rule exists).
