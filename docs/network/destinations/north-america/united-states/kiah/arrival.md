## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat Texas Gulf Coastal Plain at ~97 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **RWY 09/27 is closed to wingspan ≥215 ft** — the A388 (A380) and B748 (747-8) must be assigned a different runway; a late runway reassignment for these types is a real energy/sequencing consideration. Gulf-of-Mexico convective activity in season can force holding, re-routing or diversion. Wake-turbulence consideration applies during simultaneous/dependent operations across the close-spaced east–west parallels.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 Houston Class B with five runways in two crossing parallel groups sustains continuous multi-stream traffic under Houston Approach — brief for re-sequencing and extended vectoring, especially in convective weather or during a wingspan-driven runway reassignment.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat coastal plain) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around drops the aircraft back into a busy Houston Class B, five-runway radar environment — expect vectoring and possible holding before re-sequencing, especially during convective weather.
- **Go-around traps:** Traffic/configuration awareness across the two crossing runway groups; awareness of the RWY 09/27 wingspan restriction if a runway change is issued during the miss.

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 09/27 is closed to any aircraft with a wingspan of 215 ft or more** — do not expect this runway as an arrival option for the A388 or B748.
- **No published preferential-runway rule** — brief for either the east–west parallel set or the 15/33 pair depending on wind/ATIS, not a fixed default.
- **Convective weather in season (summer afternoons) and the Atlantic hurricane season (Jun–Nov)** can force late re-routing, holding or diversion — brief a weather contingency, especially in the warm months.
- **RWY 15L/33R and taxiways WA/WB carry known magnetic anomalies** — be aware even on arrival taxi-in.
- **Portions of several taxiways are not visible from the tower** — expect extra vigilance on taxi-in.
- **Bird and bat wildlife hazard** on and around the airport — maintain a heightened visual scan on approach and rollout.
