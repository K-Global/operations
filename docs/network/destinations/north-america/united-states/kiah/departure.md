## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From Terminal D/E, Ground/Ramp routes toward the assigned departure runway (one of the three east–west parallels most commonly, or the 15/33 pair in a crosswind/overflow configuration); confirm the exact taxi with Ground on the day.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 **TWY WD** (closed to wingspan >171 ft between TWY NR and TWY WB); **TWY NR** (closed to wingspan >125 ft between TWY WD and TWY WB; a separate segment between TWY WW and TWY WB closed to wingspan >214 ft); **TWY WW** (RWY 15L run-up pad, closed to wingspan ≥135 ft); **TWY NK** (dual-taxiway operations near the North Ramp/Terminal D ramp — west centreline ≤125 ft, east centreline ≤214 ft; simultaneous ops prohibited when the middle taxilane is in use); **TWY NJ** (similar dual-taxiway restriction near the Terminal C ramp); **TWY RC** (closed to wingspan >118 ft); **TWY SF** (between RWY 09/27 up to the east bridge, closed to wingspan ≥215 ft); **TWY WC** (west of RWY 15R/33L, restricted to wingspan ≤118 ft).
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** A departure crossing between the parallel runway sets should expect explicit crossing/hold-short instructions; several movement areas (portions of TWY WA, WB, WD, NR) are **not visible from the tower**, so read-back discipline is especially important there.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** SMGCS/low-vis taxi routing applies in fog conditions consistent with the field's CAT II/III environment; follow-me availability not confirmed. 🟧

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** No single published preferential-runway rule was found in reachable sources — the active configuration (east–west parallel set vs the 15/33 pair) is wind/traffic driven; confirm via ATIS. 🟧
- **By departure direction:** SID selection is fix/direction-driven per the current chart (e.g. northbound RNAV departures vs southbound/eastbound families) — pull the current-AIRAC SID applicable to the assigned runway and filed routing.
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** A general "noise sensitive area" north, east and west of the airport is noted in FAA remarks; no detailed noise-abatement departure procedure (NADP) preference was found. 🟧
- **Interaction with arrivals:** The three east–west parallels and the crossing 15/33 pair create dependent-configuration interactions; a departure off one runway set may cross or be sequenced against arrival traffic on the other — see [Briefing §3.2](index.md).

## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** All five runways (9,000–12,001 ft) are non-limiting for any K Global type. **RWY 09/27 is closed to wingspan ≥215 ft**, excluding the A388 (A380) and B748 (747-8) from that runway specifically — plan an alternate runway assignment for these two types.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Non-issue at ~97 ft, though high Gulf Coast heat/humidity in summer can still reduce engine/aerodynamic margin on hot days — confirm performance per type/OAT, not a hot-and-high field but not immune to high-temperature derate considerations.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Gulf Coast convective-season heavy-rain contamination is the relevant seasonal consideration; winter ice/snow contamination is rare. No specific braking-action data confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — no field-length or field-elevation performance penalty for any K Global type at KIAH; the operative constraint is the RWY 09/27 wingspan restriction for the A388/B748.

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 09/27 is closed to any aircraft with a wingspan of 215 ft or more** — never plan an A388 (A380) or B748 (747-8) departure from this runway.
- **Multiple taxiways carry hard wingspan limits** (TWY WD >171 ft, TWY NR >125 ft/>214 ft by segment, TWY WW ≥135 ft, TWY RC/WC >118 ft) — confirm the assigned taxi route accommodates the aircraft before pushback.
- **RWY 15L/33R and taxiways WA/WB carry known magnetic anomalies** that may affect compass heading for takeoff — cross-check FMS/compass alignment.
- **Portions of several taxiways are not visible from the tower** (WA, WB, WD, NR per FAA remarks) — expect extra vigilance and explicit read-back on crossings.
- **No published preferential-runway rule** — confirm the active configuration via ATIS rather than assuming a default.
- **Bird and bat wildlife hazard** noted on and around the airport.
