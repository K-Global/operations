## 8. Customs / immigration & PoE for pax

- **Port of Entry:** Yes — **Customs Landing Rights Airport**, not a staffed 24h Port of Entry.
- **CBP / immigration hours:** PPR required (775-784-5585); no walk-up customs hall. 🟥
- **Pre-clearance / visa-transit notes:** Domestic US field for a US-registered/US-operating carrier — no overflight permit required; the PPR requirement is the operative constraint for any international routing through KRNO.
- **Late/overnight/diversion caveat:** **After-hours landing without prior approval is NOT authorised** — this materially limits KRNO's value as an unplanned late-night/overnight international diversion. 🟥
