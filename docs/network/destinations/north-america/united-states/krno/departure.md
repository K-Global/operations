- **By wind / config:** No formal preferential-runway program published; **17R/35L (longest, ILS + MALSR) is the primary jet runway when wind allows** (Briefing §1). 08/26 is the crosswind option.

- **Interaction with arrivals:** Radar Class C environment (NORCAL); no published dependent-configuration constraint, but expect glider/soaring VFR traffic in the vicinity and up to 30–50 NM south during mountain-wave conditions (Briefing §3.2) to affect sequencing.

## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** 17R/35L (11,001 ft) is generous in absolute terms, but hot-and-high density altitude materially erodes climb/accelerate-stop margins, especially on the shorter 17L/35R (9,000 ft) and 08/26 (6,102 ft) — see Briefing §2/§3.5.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** **Hot-and-high is the dominant performance driver** — NOAA July mean daily max ≈93.9°F (34.4°C) at 4,415 ft field elevation drives density altitude well above field elevation on summer afternoons. Compute actual density altitude and takeoff/climb performance at dispatch; do not use a generic long-runway assumption. See [High-Elevation Aerodrome Operations](../../../../../flight-ops/high-elevation-aerodrome-operations.md) (OM E) — KRNO is named there as an illustrative hot-and-high field.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Winter mountain weather brings snow/icing; "Cold Temperature Airport" altimetry correction applies at or below -13°C.
- **Link to OM B:** Widebody-class types should treat KRNO's density-altitude penalty as thrust/climb-gradient-limiting, not field-length-limiting, on hot afternoons — confirm against type performance data before any operational (non-alternate) use.

## 10. Gotchas

- **SPARKS ONE (OBSTACLE) exists for a reason** — do not assume a standard-gradient SID clears the surrounding terrain; confirm the actual gradient on the current chart.
- **Density altitude, not runway length, is the real limit** on hot summer afternoons — 11,001 ft feels generous but thrust/climb margin is the binding constraint.
- **Taxiway restrictions bite before you even reach the runway** — Twy C (≤100,000 lb), Twy A (>149 ft wingspan closed), Twy M/Twy J (air carrier closed) — confirm routing avoids these.
- **Glider/soaring traffic up to 18,000 ft and 30–50 NM south** during mountain-wave conditions — an unusual VFR conflict layer for a Class C field.
- **"Cold Temperature Airport" below -13°C** — apply the altimetry correction; do not assume a mild-climate default.
