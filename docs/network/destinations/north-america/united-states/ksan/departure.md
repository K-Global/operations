## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From a Terminal 2 gate, Ground/Ramp routes toward the assigned end of the single runway (09 or 27 depending on wind); confirm the exact taxi with Ground (123.9, 0000–0600) or Ramp Control (129.775/131.975, 0600–2400) on the day.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 Aircraft exiting **RWY 09 at TWY D and turning right onto TWY Bravo are limited to a maximum wingspan of 136 ft** (B757-300W-equivalent) — this excludes every K Global widebody type from that exit/turn combination; plan an alternative routing. Aircraft crossing **RWY 09/27 on TWY C6** must hold short of TWY C6 facing west on TWY C, parallel to the runway. Taxiing aircraft are prohibited from passing to the south of aircraft located on TWY B into the alley between Gates 7 and 14. Jet-blast impact is noted at the north end of TWY F — engine start is restricted until 800 ft from the north end of TWY F, abeam the second parking pad.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** The TWY C6 crossing of RWY 09/27 (hold-short-facing-west procedure above) is the field's principal published crossing caution point — expect explicit ATC instructions.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** No dedicated SMGCS low-vis taxi routing identified in reachable sources; follow-me availability not confirmed. Marine-layer fog can reduce surface visibility for taxi as well as approach — brief accordingly. 🟧

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** The prevailing onshore westerly wind drives **RWY 27** as the field's dominant departure runway; **RWY 09** is used in the reciprocal/offshore-wind case (locally referred to as "the flip"). There is no independent noise-preference override — the single runway means wind is the primary selector.
- **By departure direction:** Not runway-independent — departure direction follows directly from the wind-selected runway end (27 = southeast/east-bound initial track over the bay/Coronado area reversed from the RWY 27 landing direction context; 09 = westbound initial track out over the coast). Verify the charted initial track for the assigned SID.
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** No separate noise-preferential runway program exists beyond the physical wind-based single-runway choice; the nighttime noise-restriction program (Briefing §12) constrains *when* departures may occur rather than *which* runway is used.
- **Interaction with arrivals:** Single-runway field — departures and arrivals share the same physical runway with no parallel/crossing runway to decouple them; expect sequencing gaps between departure releases and inbound traffic, especially during a busy bank or a marine-layer single-approach-stream event.

## 5. Initial climb, speed & noise-abatement departure

- **Initial climb / speed:** Per SID/ATC; standard 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft MSL (14 CFR 91.117) — confirm current chart.
- **Noise-abatement departure procedure (NADP):** No single mandated fleet-wide NADP confirmed in reachable sources; the field's noise-restriction program (Briefing §12) is time-window-based rather than track/procedure-based — comply with the published SID track precisely regardless of NADP variant chosen.
- **Early turn / altitude constraints:** Published per the BORDER SEVEN departure procedure — verify constraints on the current chart, particularly given the close-in obstacle environment on the RWY 27 (downtown/Balboa Park) side.

## 6. Engine-out / emergency-turn & obstacle considerations

- **Engine-out / emergency-turn procedure:** No published EO/emergency-turn specifics confirmed in reachable sources — apply the operator/type EOSID for the assigned runway, giving particular weight to the close-in obstacle environment described below. 🟧
- **Close-in / distant obstacles:** FAA obstruction data for the runway environment records a **31 ft tree at 674 ft from the runway** (385 ft left of centreline, 15:1 slope to clear), a published **approach-surface ratio of 44:1 to the displaced threshold**, a **61 ft sign at 250 ft from the runway** (500 ft right of centreline), and a **+40 ft light pole 110 ft from the runway end** (260 ft right) — a tightly constrained obstacle environment close to the runway ends. On departure from RWY 27, downtown San Diego high-rises and the close-in parking-structure environment described in Briefing §3.1 lie under the initial climb-out path in the reciprocal sense to the arrival case — brief accordingly.
- **Special DP / obstacle DP:** Special takeoff minimums and departure procedures (BORDER SEVEN, §4) are published specifically because of this obstacle environment — never plan a standard/default departure track without checking the current chart. 🟧

## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** Single runway 9,401 ft physical length; TORA for the assigned end is **8,280 ft (RWY 09)** or **9,401 ft (RWY 27)** depending on wind/config (Briefing §7) — non-limiting for typical K Global widebody departure weights but confirm performance for each leg/weight given the reduced TORA on the RWY 09 end.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Non-issue at 17 ft field elevation, coastal Southern California climate — no hot-and-high penalty.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Rare — dry coastal climate with no meaningful winter icing season; occasional Pacific storm-system rain is the only realistic wet-runway consideration. 🟧
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) and [`OM E — Short and Limited Runway Operations`](../../../../../flight-ops/short-and-limited-runway-operations.md) — no field-elevation performance penalty at KSAN, but the single-runway/reduced-declared-distance profile is a real planning input for our largest types.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Single runway — no reciprocal/parallel option.** A rejected takeoff, disabled aircraft or closure on 09/27 closes the entire field; there is no alternate runway to fall back on.
- **RWY 09 exit via TWY D onto TWY Bravo is wingspan-limited to 136 ft** — every K Global widebody type is excluded from this specific taxi combination; brief the alternative routing in advance.
- **Special takeoff minimums/departure procedures apply** — never assume a standard departure track or standard minima at this field; verify the current BORDER SEVEN chart.
- **Close-in obstacle environment near both runway ends** (tree, sign, light pole per FAA obstruction data) — respect the charted departure procedure precisely, especially off RWY 27 toward downtown.
- **No hard curfew, but a real financial-penalty exposure** for a late-night departure infringing the 22:00–23:30/23:30–06:30 noise-restriction windows — build buffer into the last rotation of the day.
- **Practice approaches and touch-and-go operations are prohibited** at this field — not a departure-page item directly, but relevant if a rejected-landing/go-around scenario is being briefed alongside the departure.
