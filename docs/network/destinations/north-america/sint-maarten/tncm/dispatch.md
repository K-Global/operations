## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single international passenger terminal (rebuilt/reopened in stages following Hurricane Irma 2017 damage; full reconstruction completed by Ballast Nedam, arrival hall reopened October 2024).
- **Our operation uses:** The single international terminal — no split-terminal consideration at this field.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Single arrivals hall with immigration/customs; hours 07:00–21:00 LT.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Confirm current terminal capacity/gate-count against the AIP parking/dock charts before treating stand numbers as fixed.

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Not individually itemised in the reachable AIP extract; single apron (TNCM A, PCN 50/F/B/X/U) — confirm widebody-capable positions with handling before planning a Code E/F rotation. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Taxiways F, G1, G2, H (5.0–11.0 m width, PCN 9) are narrow/low-strength — treat any stand fed only by these taxiways as unsuitable for K Global aircraft. 🟥
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not confirmed — verify with handling. 🟧
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** See [Briefing §13](index.md) — usable taxiway set is A–E only for our types.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None identified in reachable sources.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No formal night curfew found; AD/ATS hours are 07:00–21:00 LT with extension by request only — treat this as a *de facto* operating window rather than a legal curfew until confirmed otherwise. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** No ATFM/CTOT regime identified for this field. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** Build schedule buffer to land/depart within the confirmed AD/ATS window; late-day rotations should not assume after-hours ATS availability without prior coordination.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** PJIAE Operations provides AD-level handling coordination; specific third-party ground handler for our operation not confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 12 min / taxi-out 15 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]`.
- **Services:** Fuelling and handling both published 07:00–21:00 LT (or last scheduled flight); standard turnaround services expected within that window.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Single-runway/single-apron field — any runway or apron disruption has an outsized knock-on effect versus a multi-runway hub; short LDA (Briefing §7) also constrains max landing weight and therefore fuel/payload planning at the turn.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** Winter high season (Dec–Apr) is the standard Caribbean leisure-demand peak; hurricane season (Jun–Nov) suppresses demand and carries real closure risk (Irma 2017 precedent).
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for this field — treat as standard Caribbean leisure-skew pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** Not a primary cargo gateway in reachable sources; treat as passenger-driven belly cargo only. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — destination field, not a network connection point for K Global.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** Hurricane-season weather (Jun–Nov) and the field's dependence on visual conditions for RWY 28 arrivals (Briefing §3.1) are the principal reliability risks.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** No ATFM/CTOT regime identified; delay exposure is weather-driven rather than flow-managed. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Hurricane-season months carry the highest schedule risk, including outright multi-week closure risk in a severe-storm scenario (2017 precedent). 🟧

---

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** No noise-abatement programme or noise-charge regime identified (Briefing §12 — "no procedures in place").
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** No formal curfew identified; the practical constraint is the AD/ATS operating window (07:00–21:00 LT). 🟧
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Build buffer so a normal operational delay does not push an arrival/departure outside the 07:00–21:00 LT window without prior arrangement; build hurricane-season contingency (reroute/divert) into seasonal scheduling.
