## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 2** handles domestic flights only. **Terminal 3** — opened 2018 (reported ~$274M project, phased capacity 5–6.5M pax/year) — serves regional, international and long-haul flights, with boarding-bridge gates and reported capability for very-large-aircraft (A380-class) international service. **Terminal 1**, the original passenger terminal, is no longer used for scheduled service and is reported to be under redevelopment as an FBO facility.
- **Our operation uses:** **Terminal 3** — the field's sole international/regional gateway terminal.
- **International arrivals / immigration hall:** Located in Terminal 3; Ghana Immigration Service processes arriving international passengers. Exact hours not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal 1's conversion timeline and any impact on ramp/apron layout near Terminal 3 not confirmed — verify locally if planning ground-movement routes near the former terminal.
