## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single international/domestic passenger terminal plus a separate GA apron; hotels are in Saint-Denis (off-airport), with restaurants, an ATM and a tourist-information office on the airport itself.
- **Our operation uses:** Numbered commercial stands per the published type/stand matrix (§3) — confirm the exact assignment with handling on the day.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Full international arrivals hall; customs/immigration confirmed H24.
- **Notes:** No terminal-programme changes identified in the reachable eAIP extract.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody stands:** A detailed compatibility matrix covers 17 numbered stands (plus sub-positions, e.g. 1A/1B, 9A–9D) and a GA apron. Types up to **B747, A340, B777, A330, B787 and A380** are matched to specific stands; **A339 is compatible with several numbered stands** — confirm the exact assignment with handling. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not separately itemised for A339 — cross-check the published matrix against the assigned type before planning a stand.
- **A380 designated stands:** **3** (nose-in), **12** and **17** (nose-out) — see §4 for the associated taxi restriction.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not separately identified.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Contact stand expected for a scheduled arrival; confirm with handling.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** **TWY B and TWY M are closed to A380 movements**; the **Antonov 124** regime closes specific adjacent stands while it occupies stand 12 or 17 (§4) — see [Briefing §13](index.md).

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **SW Indian Ocean tropical cyclone season (Nov–Apr)** — same regional exposure as FIMP/FSIA; RSMC La Réunion is the cyclone-warning authority for the whole basin.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Low outside cyclone season; a scheduled **A380/AN124 movement** can pause all other ground movement while it is under way — confirm the day's movement schedule if operating alongside a heavy-type rotation.
- **On-time reliability picture:** Cyclone-season weather holds and the RWY 14 PAPI/ILS non-harmonisation (a workload item on a marginal-weather approach) are the two watch items. 🟧
