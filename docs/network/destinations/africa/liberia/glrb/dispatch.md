## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A ~US$80m renovation delivered a new two-level, ~5,000 m² main passenger terminal (dedicated December 2017, fully operational including **the country's first passenger jet bridges** by September 2019), replacing facilities that had been damaged during the Second Liberian Civil War. A second terminal building ("Terminal B," opened March 2012) has historically handled specific carrier operations. Domestic/international split not individually confirmed. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not individually confirmed — assume the main (post-2019) international terminal pending local confirmation.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** Confirmed international arrivals facility (national Port of Entry); the ground floor handles check-in/security (departures) and baggage claim with international-standard carousels (arrivals) per public renovation-project descriptions. 🟧
- **Notes:** Other airside buildings are primarily used by United Nations operations, with a VIP facility adjacent to the original (pre-renovation) terminal building — a useful awareness item for apron/ramp coordination, not an operational constraint for scheduled commercial traffic.
