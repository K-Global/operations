## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
DNCA sits at 209–210 ft AMSL on the coastal Cross River estuary lowland near Calabar. No close-in high terrain is relevant to arrival, departure or missed-approach paths.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
Regional field under **Kano ACC (DNKK)** — see the [Kano (DNKK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/africa/kano-dnkk.md) and the [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md). Traffic density is low (domestic service to Abuja/Lagos, with a 2025 report of planned regional expansion by one operator to Enugu and Calabar); control type at the field not independently confirmed.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
No displaced thresholds identified in the reachable extract. Threshold elevations differ modestly by runway end (03: 198 ft, 21: 209 ft) — unremarkable slope for length but note in performance planning.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Coastal southeastern Nigeria, near the Cross River estuary and the Gulf of Guinea coast: **ITCZ convective/rainy season (roughly Apr–Oct)** is pronounced in this belt — heavy rain, embedded/towering CB, and reduced visibility in showers; **harmattan dust (Nov–Mar)** is lighter here than the northern interior. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
DNCA's tower publishes two frequencies (121.70 and 124.50) in reachable sources — confirm which is primary/active before use; treat both as valid pending AIP confirmation. No other field-specific operational constraint identified.
