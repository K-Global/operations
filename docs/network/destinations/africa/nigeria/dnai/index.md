## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
DNAI sits at ~162–170 ft AMSL in the flat, low-lying Niger Delta hinterland southeast of Uyo. There is no close-in high terrain — CFIT is not the operative threat at this field. Verify the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
Regional single-runway field under **Kano ACC (DNKK)** — see the [Kano (DNKK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/africa/kano-dnkk.md) and the [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md). Traffic density is low relative to Lagos/Abuja; control type (procedural vs radar) at the field is not independently confirmed this pass — brief for non-radar procedures as the conservative assumption.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
No displaced thresholds identified in the reachable extract; not independently confirmed. Threshold elevations differ by runway end (03: 151 ft, 21: 162 ft) — a modest longitudinal slope, unremarkable for length but note in performance planning.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Coastal southeastern Nigeria sits in the wetter, more equatorial part of the country: the **ITCZ convective/rainy season (roughly April–October)** brings heavy rain, embedded/towering CB and localised low ceiling/visibility; the **harmattan dust season (Nov–Mar)** affects this coastal belt less severely than the northern interior but can still reduce visibility in the dry-season months. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
The field only received full international-flight approval in **November 2025**, with full international operations expected to commence in **Q1 2026** — this is a **recently upgraded destination**; confirm current international-handling maturity (customs staffing, apron/stand capacity for international arrivals) before committing a schedule here. The field also hosts a substantial MRO (maintenance, repair & overhaul) facility built to Boeing 747 scale, indicating apron/hangar infrastructure beyond what the passenger terminal alone would suggest — a positive signal for ramp space, though not independently confirmed for our fleet's stand needs.
