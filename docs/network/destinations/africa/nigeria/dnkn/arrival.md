## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None sourced. Northern Nigeria savanna plain at 1,562 ft AMSL; no close-in high terrain found relevant to the approach paths.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Harmattan dust haze** is the field's defining approach hazard — dust can obscure runway markings and airfield lighting over a wide area in severe events, making visual reference extremely difficult even where instrument minima are nominally met.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Worked by Kano ACC/TRACON within the Kano FIR; mixed radar/procedural control — brief for possible vectoring, especially if radar serviceability is degraded (see [Kano (DNKK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/africa/kano-dnkk.md)).

## 10. Gotchas

- **Harmattan dust haze (Nov–Mar) is the single biggest arrival threat at this field** — severe events have been reported cutting visibility to roughly 300 m; brief a firm go-around/diversion trigger for any dry-season arrival.
- **RWY 05/23 is VMC-only and military-associated** — never expect it as a landing option for our operation.
- **The two VAMSYS-preferred alternates (DRRN Niamey, FTTJ N'Djamena) are cross-border and distant** — do not assume they are automatically clear of the same dust event affecting Kano; check their weather independently.
- **ILS runway assignment/ident not confirmed** — do not assume a specific instrument procedure without checking the current AIRAC.
- **K Global aerodrome category is not yet set** — confirm before treating this as a routine arrival field.
