## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** The main terminal building (with the control tower) serves international flights and domestic scheduled service; the terminal facilities lie between the two runways per public reporting. A separate domestic-terminal building on the south side, along RWY 06/24, has also been reported in use for domestic operations at various times.
- **Our operation uses:** The main (international) terminal for K Global widebody arrivals/departures.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear immigration/customs at the main terminal; no pre-clearance programme found.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Facilities are reported as comparatively modest for an international gateway (e.g. limited duty-free/lounge provision per older public reporting) — confirm current terminal condition and capacity before relying on legacy descriptions.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** 🟥 **Harmattan dust haze (Nov–Mar)** — public reporting describes severe events cutting visibility to roughly 300 m and NiMet warnings of widespread flight delays/cancellations across northern Nigeria in-season. This is the dominant reliability risk at DNKN, materially more severe than at DNMM or DNAA.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** No ATFM/CTOT regime confirmed; exposure not sourced beyond the harmattan-season item above. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Build firm schedule buffer and a low-visibility/diversion contingency for any Nov–Mar operation into DNKN. 🟧
