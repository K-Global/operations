## 10. Gotchas

- **Wet-season heavy rain is a documented factor in this field's accident history** — apply extra margin/caution on any wet-weather departure.
- **No crosswind runway alternative** — a strong crosswind on 03/21 has no other geometry on the field; brief the divert option early.
- **Documented wildlife (cattle) hazard** on/near the runway environment (2005 incident, on landing but relevant to runway-environment awareness generally) — maintain a visual scan of the runway/verge before and during the takeoff roll.
- **Do not assume CAT II/III instrument capability** from the field's "2008 CAT III lighting" claim — this is unconfirmed as an actual approach category (Briefing §1/§9).
