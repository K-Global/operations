## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None identified — low-lying Niger Delta terrain, no close-in high ground found in reachable sources.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Documented wildlife hazard** — an Airbus A330 struck a herd of cattle on the runway just after touchdown in 2005, damaging the nose and forcing a return-flight cancellation. Maintain a runway-environment visual scan through touchdown and rollout. A 1996 tree-strike/short-landing event and a 2001 bad-weather short-final crash are also on this field's public accident record.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Wet-season heavy rain is a documented, accident-correlated hazard at this field** — apply a firm stabilized-approach/go-around discipline; do not press an approach in marginal wet-weather conditions.
- **Documented wildlife (cattle) hazard on the runway** — maintain a visual scan through touchdown and rollout.
- **No crosswind runway alternative** — do not expect a config change to relieve a crosswind event; brief the divert option early.
- **Do not assume CAT II/III capability** from the unconfirmed "2008 CAT III lighting" claim.
