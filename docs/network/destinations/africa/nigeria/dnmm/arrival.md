## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat coastal Lagos Lagoon/Atlantic-fringe site at 135 ft AMSL.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 A sourced AIP caution warns of **bird activity on the approach and touchdown zone of RWY 18R** — brief accordingly. The RWY 18R PAPI is usable on the left side only (AIP-sourced).
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Busiest terminal in the Kano FIR; mixed radar/procedural control — brief for possible extended vectoring, especially if radar serviceability is degraded (see [Kano (DNKK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/africa/kano-dnkk.md)).

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat coastal site) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around drops you back into the busiest terminal in the Kano FIR — expect vectoring and possible holding before re-sequencing.
- **Go-around traps:** Bird activity in the RWY 18R touchdown zone remains a consideration through the missed-approach segment; maintain vigilance.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Bird activity on the approach and touchdown zone of RWY 18R** — a sourced, active hazard; brief accordingly.
- **Uncontrolled vehicles frequently cross the runways** — maintain vigilance throughout the approach, landing and taxi-in.
- **No approach confirmed for 36L/36R** — do not assume a north-configuration approach exists without checking the current AIRAC.
- **RWY 18R PAPI usable on the left side only** — brief the correct visual reference.
- **Two-terminal split** — confirm the correct (international MMIA) stand area on taxi-in; do not assume shared infrastructure with the domestic terminal (MMA2).
