## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** DNMM operates two physically and operationally **separate** terminal complexes — the international terminal (MMIA, FAAN-operated) handling all international widebody traffic, and the domestic terminal (**MMA2**), privately operated by Bi-Courtney Aviation Services, handling the bulk of domestic flights.
- **Our operation uses:** The international terminal (MMIA) for all K Global widebody arrivals/departures.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear immigration/customs at MMIA; no pre-clearance programme found.
- **Notes:** 🟧 The two-terminal split means shared connection/handling continuity between MMIA and MMA2 should not be assumed — confirm any domestic-connection routing separately.

## 7. Fuel

- **Supplier / into-plane:** Jet A-1/100LL per AIP; specific into-plane provider not confirmed. 🟧
- **Uplift availability & hours:** Assumed available given hub status; not independently confirmed. 🟧
- **Price / tankering angle:** Nigeria's aviation-fuel **scarcity** risk has eased since the **Dangote Petroleum Refinery** ramp-up (late 2025/2026), but **price remains extremely high and volatile** — reported in the roughly ₦2,200–3,300/litre range in 2026, up sharply from under ₦900 at end-2023. This is a live tankering-decision input for every leg into/out of DNMM. See [`OM E — Fuel Policy`](../../../../../flight-ops/fuel-policy.md).
- **Quality / freeze notes:** Standard Jet A-1; no cold-soak/fuel-freeze consideration specific to DNMM (tropical field).
