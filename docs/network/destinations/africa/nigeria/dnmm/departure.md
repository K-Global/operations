- **Uncontrolled vehicles frequently cross the runways** — a sourced, active ground-incursion hazard; maintain vigilance throughout ground movement and departure roll.

- **No confirmed slot/CTOT regime** — do not assume a flow-control clearance is required, but confirm current NOTAM status before departure.
