## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
Ilorin sits at 1,126 ft AMSL on the Kwara State plateau/savanna belt of west-central Nigeria — no close-in high terrain has been identified in reachable sources. This is not expected to be an operative CFIT threat, but the MSA ring should be verified on the current chart as routine practice, particularly since no AIRAC-sourced terrain/obstacle chart was reachable this pass.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟩
DNIL is a **secondary airport to Lagos** with modest scheduled traffic (regional carriers operating narrowbody equipment to Lagos and Abuja). The field is **procedural, non-radar** — it sits outside the Lagos/Kano/Abuja/Port Harcourt TRACON footprint described in the Kano (DNKK) FIR brief. Traffic density is low; the operative airspace consideration is procedural separation discipline rather than volume. Cross-ref [Kano (DNKK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/africa/kano-dnkk.md) and the [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md).

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
Single runway (05/23), 3,100 × 60 m — no displaced thresholds were found in reachable sources, but this is **not independently confirmed against the Nigeria AIP**. No runway-excursion accident history was found for DNIL specifically in this research pass (a belly-landing incident in 1992 and a light-aircraft belly landing in 2019 are recorded — see §4 — neither a runway-excursion/braking-action event). Treat wet-season contamination as a standing caution pending AIP confirmation of grooving/friction data.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟥
**Harmattan** — the north-easterly Saharan dust haze that affects this belt of Nigeria roughly **November–March** — is the field's principal recurring hazard, capable of reducing visibility well below normal VMC minima with little warning. The **wet season (roughly April–October)** brings organised convective squall lines and embedded CB typical of the West African ITCZ belt. See §14 and the [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md) §11.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟥
Single-runway field with **no crosswind runway option** — a runway-05/23 crosswind event has no alternative geometry on the field itself; plan around published crosswind limits. Procedural (non-radar) control environment. No special-airport/crew-qualification category found in reachable sources; **none categorised in VAMSYS**. Fuel and handling provision at a secondary regional field should be treated as limited pending confirmation (§16).

## 4. Cautions & Warnings

- **Single runway, no crosswind alternative** — a strong crosswind event on 05/23 has nowhere else on the field to go; brief the divert option early.
- **Harmattan haze (Nov–Mar)** can degrade visibility rapidly and without a sharp onset — treat as a standing dry-season watch-item.
- **Procedural (non-radar) control** — do not assume surveillance-based separation; comply with published reporting/position procedures.
- Historical belly-landing events at the field (a 1992 Boeing 707 test-flight belly landing and a 2019 light-aircraft belly landing, both without fatalities) — general awareness item, not a standing operational constraint for scheduled widebody/narrowbody service.
- Fuel/handling provision at a secondary field — do not assume major-hub-level availability; confirm before planning a fuel stop or turn here (§16).
