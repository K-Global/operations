## 10. Gotchas

- **Single runway (04/22)** — a go-around or runway-obstruction event drives straight into a full-field capacity question; hold a firm alternate plan.
- **Bird activity in the vicinity of the aerodrome** — sourced, active hazard; brief accordingly.
- **Harmattan dust haze (Nov–Mar)** — a real dry-season visibility hazard at this inland, moderate-elevation field.
- **Historical precedent:** the field was closed six weeks in 2017 for emergency runway rehabilitation — illustrates the single-runway exposure.
