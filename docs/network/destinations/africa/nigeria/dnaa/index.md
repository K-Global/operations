## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
DNAA sits at 1,123 ft AMSL on the rolling plateau of the Federal Capital Territory. No close-in high terrain relevant to arrival, departure or missed-approach paths was found in reachable sources; verify the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
Abuja is the second-busiest Nigerian terminal after Lagos, worked within the single **Kano (DNKK)** FIR. Surveillance is radar-assisted around the field but the wider FIR is mixed radar/procedural, and Nigerian radar-serviceability has been publicly reported as a watch item. Cross-ref [Kano (DNKK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/africa/kano-dnkk.md).

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
DNAA has a **single runway (04/22)** with no displaced threshold found in the reachable extract — both directions show equal TORA/LDA. Not independently re-confirmed against current AIRAC. Single-runway operation means any runway-closure event (e.g. the 2017 emergency rehabilitation that shut the field for six weeks) removes the field entirely rather than degrading capacity — a standing diversion-planning consideration.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Abuja's inland, moderate-elevation position makes it more exposed to **harmattan dust haze (Nov–Mar)** than the coastal fields — a real dry-season visibility hazard. The wet season (Apr–Oct) brings organised convective activity typical of the West African interior. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
Plan around: (1) a sourced AIP caution that **bird activity occurs in the vicinity of the aerodrome** — brief accordingly; (2) the **single-runway** configuration, meaning any runway obstruction/closure is a full-field event, not a capacity reduction — the field was closed for six weeks in 2017 for emergency runway rehabilitation, illustrating this exposure; and (3) **Jet A-1 price volatility** (§16) as at every Nigerian field.

## 4. Cautions & Warnings

- **Single runway (04/22)** — any runway obstruction or closure removes the field entirely from the network; hold a firm alternate plan.
- **Bird activity in the vicinity of the aerodrome** — sourced AIP caution; brief accordingly.
- **Historical precedent:** the field was closed for approximately six weeks in 2017 for emergency runway rehabilitation, with flights diverted to Kaduna — illustrates the single-runway exposure described above.
- Harmattan dust haze is a real dry-season hazard at this inland, moderate-elevation field — do not assume the coastal-field weather picture applies here.
- Confirm current RFF category (CAT 8, dated source) before basing the largest widebody types here.
