## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Density altitude / temperature:** The field's moderate elevation (1,123 ft) is noticeably higher than the coastal Nigerian fields — confirm performance margins on the hottest days; not classically hot-and-high but worth a margin check.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Single runway (04/22)** — any runway obstruction/closure removes the field entirely; hold a firm alternate plan (the field was closed six weeks in 2017 for emergency runway rehabilitation).
- **Bird activity in the vicinity of the aerodrome** — sourced AIP caution; brief accordingly.
- **Moderate field elevation (1,123 ft)** — confirm hottest-day performance margin, though not classically hot-and-high.
- **No confirmed slot/CTOT regime** — confirm current NOTAM status before departure regardless.
