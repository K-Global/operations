## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 **Morne Seychellois massif (~905 m/2,969 ft)** rises inland/west of the field — see [Briefing §3.1](index.md). The east/seaward approach direction is generally clear (open ocean), but any approach or missed-approach path toward the west must respect the current chart's terrain clearance — do not estimate.
- **Specific threats:** A **military base (SPDF)** co-located at the SE end of RWY 13 is a real ground/airspace-sharing consideration on approach to that runway end — confirm any restriction. 🟧
- **Airspace / traffic:** Frequent **domestic inter-island traffic** from the nearby domestic terminal adds local-circuit density beyond the international arrival flow — expect a busier local pattern than a comparably sized field without that traffic mix.
