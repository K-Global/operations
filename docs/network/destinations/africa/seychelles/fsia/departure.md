## 10. Gotchas

- **Displaced threshold on both runway ends (~305 m/1,001 ft)** — do not plan against the gross 2,987 m length without confirming the actual declared/usable distance.
- **SPDF military base at the SE end of RWY 13** — confirm any ground-movement or airspace-sharing restriction before assuming unrestricted use of that end.
- Terrain toward **Morne Seychellois (west/inland)** — no shortcut visual departures on that side without confirming the chart.
- Busy **domestic inter-island traffic** from the nearby domestic terminal — expect local-circuit density beyond the international schedule.
