## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A general-aviation/business-aviation-oriented field with main aprons A, B and C plus a general-aviation area west of the taxiway gate; not configured as a scheduled-airline terminal complex in the reachable AIP extract.
- **Our operation uses:** Main apron (A or B) — mandatory for all international flights per AIP.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** H24 customs and immigration confirmed; no dedicated international pier/hall detail found. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 As a company alternate rather than a scheduled destination, confirm current handling/stand capability with the airport authority before planning any K Global movement here beyond a genuine diversion.
