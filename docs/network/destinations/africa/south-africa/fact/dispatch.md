- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not detailed in reachable sources beyond the general apron-taxiway wingspan rule below.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not detailed in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate for a scheduled base turn; confirm with handling.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Aircraft with wingspan ≥52 m must use apron taxiways solely to access stands, not as a through-route, and reduce taxi speed to 10 kt or below — see [Briefing §13](index.md).

## 7. Fuel

- **Supplier / into-plane:** **Cape Town Intl Fuelling Services** (H24, primary); **Air BP/Signature Flight Support** (Mon–Fri 0300–1600, weekends/public holidays 0600–1600, on request outside these hours); **Shell/Execujet Aviation Group** (H24 on request).
- **Uplift availability & hours:** Jet A-1 hydrant rate 3,000 L/min on aprons A, B and C; two Jet A-1 tankers (600–900 L/min) for remote/general-aviation areas; AVGAS by tanker only.
- **Price / tankering angle:** Assess price differentials versus other network bases per leg. See [`OM E — Fuel Policy`](../../../../../flight-ops/fuel-policy.md).
- **Quality / freeze notes:** Standard Jet A-1; no cold-soak/fuel-freeze consideration specific to FACT itself.
