## 10. Gotchas

- 🟥 **The field is closed** — this page describes reference geometry, not a flyable arrival. Do not brief or plan an arrival into HLLT under current conditions.

- 🟧 Reported GPS interference in the wider conflict-affected region — do not rely solely on GNSS for any hypothetical future procedure here.
