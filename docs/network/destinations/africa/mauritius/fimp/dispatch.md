## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Commercial passenger terminal plus a separate GA/private apron; international arrivals/departures handled through the main terminal, with our business-jet traffic typically routed via the GA apron.
- **Our operation uses:** GA/private apron (Citation-class reference stand in the charted data) — 🟧 confirm a G650ER or ACJ321 assignment against the current stand plan (see [Briefing §13](index.md)).
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** Full international arrivals hall in the main terminal; customs/immigration operate during hours of operation (HO) rather than confirmed H24 — build buffer for an off-peak or late arrival. 🟧
- **Notes:** No terminal-programme changes identified in the reachable eAIP extract.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Stands for our types:** GA-apron reference stand (Citation-class charted); 🟧 **confirm a G650ER fits** — it is larger than the charted reference and may be assigned a commercial stand instead. ACJ321 (code C) fits commercial stands normally.
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not separately identified — verify with handling on the day.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not identified in reachable sources.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Tower assigns the bay on arrival; expect GA-apron contact stand for business-jet ops.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** **TWY B closed** (per FIR data, unconfirmed against current AIRAC) 🟧; narrow taxiways K (18.7 m) and L (15 m) — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None found in AD 2 — no IATA-level coordination published. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None** — ATS, fuelling, handling and security all H24; only the AD administration office keeps limited hours (MON–FRI 0445–1200; SAT/SUN/HOL closed). 🟩
- **CTOT / flow control:** No CTOT/ATFM regime identified — procedural, non-radar FIR; clearance is via Tower/Approach rather than a flow programme. 🟧

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Tropical cyclone season (Nov–Apr)** — the eAIP itself notes the airport "may be closed due to tropical cyclones"; this is the dominant reliability risk at FIMP.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Low outside cyclone season — procedural (non-radar) FIR with H24 ATS and no published slot/CTOT regime.
- **On-time reliability picture:** Cyclone-season weather holds and any RWY 32 (no-ILS) instrument arrival in marginal weather are the two watch items. 🟧
