## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A **$270 million** expansion project (approved Dec 2020) delivered a new passenger terminal, VIP terminal and taxiway upgrades; the new terminal opened **4 March 2023** under management of **Summa Airport (SL) Ltd**. Domestic/international split not confirmed. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not individually confirmed — assume the new (2023) terminal pending local confirmation.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** Confirmed international arrivals facility (national Port of Entry); exact hall layout not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟥 **The airport is physically separated from Freetown city by the Sierra Leone River estuary.** Passenger/crew transfer options are: government ferry (~1 hour), private water-taxi speedboats (30–40 min to Aberdeen/Murray Town/Government wharf), or road transport around the estuary (commonly 3+ hours with Freetown traffic). A "Lungi Bridge" project has been announced (2019) to resolve this permanently; current construction status is **not confirmed**. This is the field's single most important commercial/logistics fact and should be factored into every crew-positioning and passenger-connection plan.
