## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A main two-storey international terminal (built to replace the original single-storey building, which now serves as the VIP terminal) plus a dedicated cargo terminal. RwandAir's head office occupies the top floor of the main terminal building.
- **Our operation uses:** Main international terminal for scheduled arrivals/departures; the VIP terminal may be relevant for business-jet-scale operations — confirm with handling. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International PoE via the main terminal; hours not confirmed.
- **Notes:** A 2019-launched expansion phase (new apron, arrivals terminal, runway-strip grading) has progressed since; confirm current facility state with handling before finalising a stand plan. 🟧 A future replacement airport at **Bugesera** (south of Kigali) is under long-term development but is not this field — do not confuse the two in planning.
