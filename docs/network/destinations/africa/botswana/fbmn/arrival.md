## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 No close-in high terrain — the field sits on the flat Okavango Delta/Kalahari fringe plain (Briefing §3.1). Non-issue for terrain in the classic CFIT sense.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Very high volume of light GA/bush-charter traffic** in the circuit and on approach, feeding Okavango Delta lodges — this is the field's defining approach hazard, not a terrain or navaid issue (Briefing §3.2). Maintain heightened visual lookout on any visual approach.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Control-service level (Tower vs AFIS) and radar status not reconciled between sources (Briefing §1) — treat as procedural/non-radar by continental default; do not assume radar-vectored separation from light traffic.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Very high volume of light GA/bush-charter traffic** in the circuit and on any visual approach — the standing arrival threat at this field, especially during the Jun–Aug safari-season peak (Briefing §3.2/§14).
- **Runway-length discrepancy across sources** (~3,670–3,700 m vs an outlier 2,000 m figure) — verify against the current CAAB AIP before treating length as non-limiting.
- Morning/dusk mist reported locally — check current weather at the margins of the operating day (Briefing §14).
- Control-service level (Tower vs AFIS) and radar status not reconciled between sources — do not assume radar-vectored separation.
- No confirmed STAR, approach name, or LVP trigger — this page cannot substitute for the current-AIRAC chart at this field; treat every arrival planning figure here as a pointer only.
