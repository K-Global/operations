## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** The physical runway (~3,670–3,700 m per the converging sources in Briefing §1/§7) is presumptively non-limiting for any K Global narrowbody/regional type — but **OurAirports carries a conflicting 2,000 m figure**, and declared distances remain unpublished. Do not treat the long physical-runway figure as a substitute for a verified performance number pending AIP confirmation.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Moderate field elevation (3,094 ft) — not classically hot-and-high, but check take-off performance margins on hot dry-season (Sep–Oct) afternoons.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Wet/green-season (Nov–Mar) rainfall may reduce braking action for a rejected take-off case — factor into planning in season; no published contamination-specific data.
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) for type-specific reference; no field-specific consideration beyond the above is confirmed for any K Global type at this station.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Very high volume of light GA/bush-charter traffic** sharing the runway/ramp/circuit — this is the standing situational-awareness item at this field, not a chart-based restriction (Briefing §3.2).
- **Runway-length discrepancy across sources** (~3,670–3,700 m vs an outlier 2,000 m figure) — verify against the current CAAB AIP before treating length as non-limiting for a specific case.
- Reported AD/ATS hours (0730–1630 local) may constrain the available departure window — confirm current status before planning an early/late departure.
- Morning/dusk mist reported locally — check current weather even without a confirmed LVTO minima.
- No confirmed SID, take-off minima, or EOSID detail — this page cannot substitute for the current-AIRAC chart at this field; treat every departure planning figure here as a pointer only.
