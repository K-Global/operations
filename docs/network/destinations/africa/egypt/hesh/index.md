## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain  🟧
Sharm El Sheikh sits on the southern tip of the Sinai Peninsula's coastal plain. While the immediate airport environment is coastal/low-relief, the **Sinai interior is genuinely mountainous** — **Jebel Katherina/St. Catherine (~2,285 m)** and the surrounding high massif lie roughly 75 km to the NW, and the peninsula's terrain rises quickly away from the coast. This is a real regional CFIT consideration for any inland-routed arrival, departure or diversion track, even though the field's immediate approach/departure paths are presumed coastal. Quantify against the MSA ring and area chart on the current AIRAC — not independently confirmed this pass.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic  🟧
Control type (radar vs procedural) and airspace class are not confirmed in reachable sources — treat as **radar-served pending verification**, consistent with Egypt's other major international fields (cf. [HECA](../heca/index.md)). Sharm sits close to the **Gulf of Aqaba, the Egypt–Israel border, and Red Sea shipping lanes** — a regionally sensitive airspace picture that is owned by the **Middle East** brief for the wider conflict-zone/overflight-risk context; cross-check before any routing that approaches the Aqaba/Israeli boundary. **Self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** (CVFR/IFR only).

### 3.3 Runway excursion  🟧
Two parallel runways: **04L/22R** and **04R/22L**, each 3,081 × 45 m, asphalt, lighted, with **no displaced threshold reported** in the reachable extract. Both are ample length for K Global types, not normally length-limited. Declared distances and PCN are not published in reachable sources — verify per direction against the current AIRAC.

### 3.4 Weather threat  🟧
Hot Red Sea/Sinai coastal desert climate — summer heat drives high density altitude. The proximity of high Sinai terrain inland raises the plausibility of local terrain-influenced winds/turbulence (katabatic-style effects off the Sinai massif) though this is **not independently confirmed** for Sharm specifically. The regional spring khamsin dust season (documented for Cairo/inland Egypt) may have a lesser coastal expression here — not sourced.

### 3.5 Operational considerations  🟧
Plan around the confirmed geometry (two ~3,080 m parallel runways) and the strong likelihood of a busy seasonal leisure/dive-tourism traffic pattern. The standing regional watch-item at this field is **Sinai terrain and border-proximity airspace sensitivity** rather than a runway-performance limitation. RFF category, approach minima, comms, navaids, hours and fuel grade are all primary-pending — re-verify every figure against the Egypt AIP and current AIRAC before a performance- or minima-critical operation.
