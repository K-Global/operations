## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain  🟧
Marsa Alam sits on the Red Sea coastal plain, roughly 60 km north of Marsa Alam town. The immediate airport environment is expected to be low-relief coastal desert; the Eastern Desert hill country lies inland (west) of the field. No significant close-in high terrain has been identified in reachable sources, but this is **not independently confirmed** against a primary MSA/obstacle chart — verify on the current AIRAC.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic  🟧
Control type (radar vs procedural) and airspace class are not confirmed in reachable sources — treat as **radar-served pending verification**, consistent with the pattern at Egypt's other major international fields (cf. [HECA](../heca/index.md)). **Self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** (CVFR/IFR only) — expect full vectoring/procedural handling throughout.

### 3.3 Runway excursion  🟧
Single runway, physically ~3,430 × 45 m asphalt with **90 m displaced thresholds reported at both ends** in the most recent public cross-check (OurAirports, updated Jan 2026) — reducing the usable landing distance from either direction. **The runway's magnetic designation is unresolved between this pack's source instruction (12/30) and the current public cross-check (15/33)** — a discrepancy large enough (30°) that it is unlikely to be pure secular magnetic drift, and is flagged as a genuine open item rather than resolved here (§18). Confirm the current runway identity and declared distances against the Egypt AIP/current AIRAC before relying on either designation operationally.

### 3.4 Weather threat  🟧
Hot Red Sea coastal desert climate — summer heat drives high density altitude, a likely performance consideration despite the field's low-moderate elevation. The regional spring khamsin dust season (documented for Cairo/inland Egypt) may have a lesser coastal expression here — not independently confirmed for Marsa Alam specifically.

### 3.5 Operational considerations  🟧
Plan around the confirmed single-runway geometry (~3,430 m, both thresholds displaced 90 m per the most recent public data) while treating the **runway magnetic designation as an unresolved open item** requiring current-AIRAC confirmation before flight planning. RFF category, approach minima, comms, navaids, hours and fuel grade are all primary-pending — this is a lower-confidence brief than the network's AIP-verified fields; re-verify every figure against the Egypt AIP and current AIRAC before a performance- or minima-critical operation.
