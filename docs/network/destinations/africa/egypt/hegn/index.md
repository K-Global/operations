## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain  🟧
Hurghada sits on the flat Red Sea coastal plain. No significant close-in high terrain is expected at the field itself, but this has **not been independently confirmed** against a primary MSA/obstacle chart. The broader Eastern Desert hill country lies inland (west) of the coastal strip — respect MSA and any published terrain/obstacle data on the current-AIRAC chart, particularly for any inland-routed departure or missed approach.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic  🟧
Hurghada is a major European leisure-charter gateway with substantial seasonal traffic density, particularly in winter/high season. Control type (radar vs procedural) and airspace class are not confirmed in reachable sources — treat as **radar-served pending verification**, consistent with the pattern at Egypt's other major international fields (cf. [HECA](../heca/index.md)). **Self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** (CVFR/IFR only) — expect full vectoring/procedural handling throughout.

### 3.3 Runway excursion  🟧
Two parallel runways: **16L/34R** (4,015 × 45 m, asphalt, **threshold 16L displaced 302 m**) and **16R/34L** (4,014 × 60 m, asphalt). Both are long and not normally length-limited for K Global types. The displaced threshold on 16L reduces the usable landing distance from that direction — confirm the exact LDA against the current AIRAC before treating either runway as non-limiting. Declared distances, PCN and any further displaced-threshold detail are not published in reachable sources.

### 3.4 Weather threat  🟧
Hot Red Sea coastal desert climate — intense summer heat drives high density altitude, a likely performance consideration despite the low field elevation. The regional spring **khamsin** dust/sandstorm season (Mar–May, as documented for Cairo/inland Egypt) may extend to the Red Sea coast in a lesser form — not independently confirmed for Hurghada specifically. Red Sea coastal winds and occasional blowing sand from the adjacent desert are a plausible local hazard, not yet sourced to a primary reference.

### 3.5 Operational considerations  🟧
Plan around the confirmed geometry (two ~4,000 m parallel runways, one with a displaced threshold) and the strong likelihood of a busy seasonal leisure-charter traffic pattern. RFF category, approach minima, comms, navaids, hours and fuel grade are all primary-pending — treat this as a lower-confidence brief than the network's AIP-verified fields and re-verify every figure against the Egypt AIP and current AIRAC before a performance- or minima-critical operation.
