## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** **None in normal use — arrivals are radar vectored** via **CVO VOR**; holds **ALTOX** and **CRS** (FL120 min) 🟧.
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** Cairo Approach (radar) assigns the runway/config per traffic and wind; **05C/23C and 05R/23L are the most-used pair**.
- **Transition to approach:** Expect vectors direct to final for the assigned ILS; no charted STAR-to-approach transition.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** 250 KIAS below FL100 (standard ICAO norm) — confirm current chart; no charted STAR speed gates given the vectored environment.

---

## 3. Descent & energy plan

- **Top-of-descent trigger:** Standard descent-profile planning to meet the radar-vectored arrival sequence; no charted STAR altitude gates.
- **Speed control:** 250 KIAS below FL100; comply with any speed control assigned by Cairo Approach during vectoring.
- **Altitude constraints:** None charted — fly per ATC-assigned altitudes during vectoring; honour MSA at all times (Briefing §3.1).
- **Energy traps:** Extended vectoring in a busy three-parallel-runway radar environment; late runway/config change on a wind shift; reduced visibility on short finals during khamsin dust or winter fog events.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 Flat Nile valley/desert plain — no significant high terrain close-in; the field itself is **sloping** (thresholds 190–467 ft) — confirm the assigned runway's threshold elevation and honour MSA/step-downs.
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 Three parallel runways handling simultaneous arrival/departure flow under continuous radar vectoring; **05L/23R** is intermittently under maintenance and its LOC-only mode on 23R should be checked via NOTAM.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Class B TMA (40/60 NM), Class D CTR; **self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** — expect full IFR/CVFR vectoring; prohibited/restricted areas ring the capital.

---

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat plain) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart; be aware of prohibited/restricted airspace near the capital during any go-around turn.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around returns you to a busy three-parallel-runway radar-vectored environment — expect re-vectoring and possible holding (ALTOX/CRS, FL120 min).
- **Go-around traps:** Traffic/config awareness across the parallel set; confirm assigned runway/config hasn't shifted during the approach.

## 10. Gotchas

- **No charted STARs — expect full radar vectoring** via CVO VOR; brief the ALTOX/CRS hold (FL120 min) as a contingency.
- **Self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** — full IFR/CVFR vectoring applies throughout.
- **RWY 05L/23R is intermittently under maintenance** and 23R is sometimes LOC-only — check NOTAMs before planning this runway.
- **Sloping field** (thresholds 190–467 ft) — confirm the assigned runway's threshold elevation for the visual/PAPI picture.
- **Spring khamsin dust (Mar–May)** and **winter morning fog** can each sharply reduce visibility with limited warning.
- Prohibited/restricted airspace rings the capital — do not deviate from the assigned vector without clearance, including during a go-around.
