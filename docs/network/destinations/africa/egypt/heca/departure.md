## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** 4,000 m main pair (05C/23C, 05R/23L) non-limiting for any K Global type; 05L/23R (3,301 m) also ample when serviceable.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** **Summer high density altitude** is the primary performance driver despite the low (467 ft) field elevation — check take-off performance on hot days.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Not a significant seasonal factor at this desert field; occasional khamsin dust may affect visibility more than surface contamination.
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — field length/strength non-limiting for any home-network type; hot-day performance is the item to check for the business-jet fleet (G650ER) in summer.

## 10. Gotchas

- **No charted SIDs — all radar vectored** to named exit fixes (MENKU, DEGDI, MENLI, SOLAM) — brief the expected pattern, don't expect a charted departure procedure.
- **RWY 05L/23R is intermittently under maintenance** and often departures-only when open — check NOTAMs before assuming its availability.
- **Self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** — full IFR/CVFR vectoring applies throughout.
- **Summer density altitude** can materially affect take-off performance despite the field's low elevation — this is the standing performance watch-item at Cairo.
- **Spring khamsin dust (Mar–May)** can sharply reduce visibility with limited warning — brief a contingency.
- Prohibited/restricted airspace around the capital — do not deviate from the assigned vector without clearance.
