## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
DXXX sits at 72 ft AMSL on the low-lying Bight-of-Benin coastal plain. There is **no close-in high terrain** relevant to arrival, departure or missed-approach paths — a non-issue field for CFIT. Verify the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
Lomé functions as a **reported regional hub airport** for a network carrier operating extensively across West and Central Africa, concentrating substantial connecting traffic through a single runway. Per the task assignment for this field, the controlling FIR is **Accra (DGAC)** — name this FIR in all planning and cross-ref the [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md). 🟧 *(Note: Togo's national AIP is published within the ASECNA framework even though the terminal-control assignment used here is Accra rather than the Niamey ASECNA FIR bloc — this split has not been independently reconciled against a primary AIP source this pass; see §18.)* Exact control type (radar vs procedural) is not independently confirmed — treat as procedural pending confirmation, consistent with the regional pattern.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
No displaced threshold or excursion history was found in reachable sources for this field. RWY 04/22 (3,001 m) has no reported distance-usability caveat.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
**Harmattan dust haze (approx. Nov–Mar)** is the defining regional hazard along this stretch of West African coast, degrading visibility in the dry season. The wet season brings standard Gulf-of-Guinea convective/thunderstorm risk. No DXXX-specific frequency or severity statistic was found this pass. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
Plan around: (1) the **hub-traffic concentration** through a single runway (§3.2) — expect sustained connecting-bank traffic density at certain times of day; (2) **RFF category and exact approach minima are not confirmed** in reachable public sources — both are open items (§18); (3) the reported **FIR-assignment split (Accra vs the ASECNA Niamey bloc)** for Togo's terminal airspace is unresolved in public sources and should be reconciled against a primary AIP before treating this brief as verified.
