> **Read-me:** Tactical departure aid for the FlightFactor 777v2 in X-Plane 12, not a chart. Companion to the [GVAC Briefing](index.md), [Dispatch](dispatch.md) and [Arrival](arrival.md) pages. **Departure at GVAC is secondary to the field's reason for being in the pack** — it exists here as the **NORTH ETOPS-120 ETP diversion alternate** on the EDDF→São Paulo leg (paired SBFZ Fortaleza); this page captures the **post-diversion recovery-leg departure**, not a routine scheduled push. ## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From stands 01–07, taxi via the TWY system (23 m wide, CL/edge-lit) toward the assigned runway end; SMC allocates and a marshaller guides push-back.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 **TWY A2 is restricted at night** and requires **prior TWR coordination** before use (AD 2.20). **Stop bars are published on TWY A1 and A4** (AD 2.9) — expect a hold-short/read-back point there.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** Not separately itemised beyond the TWY A1/A4 stop bars.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** Not published; harmattan dust (§7 below) is a visibility factor but no SMGCS/low-vis taxi procedure is documented for GVAC. 🟧

---

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** No preferential-runway rule is published (Briefing §10) — selection is wind-driven. The runway is aligned **~360°/180°**, roughly across the steady **NE trade wind**, so expect a standing crosswind component on either end; **RWY 01** is the default plan because it carries the only ILS, useful for arrivals, and a marginally more favourable wind component in typical trade-wind conditions.
- **By departure direction:** Not applicable — GVAC is a single N/S runway with no directional SID structure documented.
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** **NIL** — AD 2.21 publishes no noise abatement procedure.
- **Interaction with arrivals:** Low-density, single-runway field; no dependent-runway interaction to manage.

## 8. Clearance / CTOT & departure frequency sequence

- **Clearance delivery:** Not separately published (AD 2.18) — expect clearance via **Amílcabral Tower 119.700** (H24) at this low-density field.
- **Frequency sequence:** **Tower 119.700 (H24) → Sal Approach 126.400 (H24) → Sal Control 126.400 (TMA) / 128.300 (North) / 127.100 (South)** — the **Sal Oceanic (GVSC)** FIR the departure feeds into. (Frequencies from Briefing §8.)
- **CTOT / slot handling:** **IATA Level 3** applies to scheduled movements; **waived for an unscheduled diversion-recovery departure** (Briefing §5/§16).

## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** **3,000 m TORA/ASDA** (both ends) is ample for a 777-300ER at any realistic departure weight off a diversion — non-limiting.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Non-issue at 179 ft, reference temperature 27.4 °C — no hot-and-high penalty.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** No seasonal contamination data published; the standing threat is the **NE trade-wind crosswind**, not surface condition.
- **Link to OM B:** See [`B77W — Dispatch`](../../../../../fleet/b77w/index.md) — **RFF CAT 9 exactly meets** the 777-300ER requirement with **no margin**; confirm no NOTAM'd downgrade before a heavy recovery departure (Briefing §3.5/§4).

---

## 10. Gotchas

- **This is a recovery-leg departure page, not a scheduled-operation one** — the field's entire reason for being briefed is the ETOPS-120 diversion inbound; treat the outbound leg as secondary planning.
- **RWY 19 has no ILS** — if the diversion arrival used 19 non-precision, do not assume the recovery departure has any different navaid picture; it doesn't change the runway's approach capability.
- **TWY A2 at night needs prior TWR coordination** — do not taxi it unco-ordinated after dark.
- **RFF CAT 9 is an exact match, zero margin, for the 777-300ER** — verify no NOTAM'd downgrade before committing to a full-weight departure.
- **PCN 58/F/A/W/U may have been overloaded by the arrival** — expect a pavement inspection may be required before the next movement (Briefing §3.3).
- **No published SID inventory** — pull the current-AIRAC SID list before the recovery leg; do not assume a specific departure procedure from this page.
