## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** One passenger terminal serving both domestic and international traffic; AD 2 GVAC does not describe a multi-concourse split.
- **Our operation uses:** The single terminal — no gate/concourse selection question at this field, unlike a multi-terminal hub.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear passport control within the single terminal; **H24** customs/immigration (AD 2.3).
- **Notes:** 🟧 No terminal-programme changes or construction noted in reachable sources.

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Nose-in guidance published on **stands 01–07** (Briefing §13); **no stand-suitability matrix by code letter is published** — a genuine gap for planning a full-weight Code E (777-300ER) parking assignment. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not itemised — confirm with handling that the assigned stand clears a 64.8 m wingspan.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not itemised in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** For an emergency diversion, expect priority handling to whichever stand clears the aircraft — do not assume a contact gate.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** **TWY A2 restricted at night** (prior TWR coordination); see [Departure §2](departure.md) and [Arrival §8](arrival.md).

---

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **IATA Level 3, schedule-coordinated** since winter 2017 (AD 2.23, Decree-Law 10/2016); requests via slot.coordination@asa.cv in IATA SSIM format.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None — H24.** Night **stop-parking** is limited to stands 01, 07 and TWY A2 (A2 needs prior TWR coordination) — a parking constraint, not a movement curfew.
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not published for this low-density field; not expected to be a live constraint. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The Level 3 slot regime governs a **scheduled** movement; it is explicitly **waived for an unscheduled safety diversion** (Briefing §5/§16) — do not let slot status deter a genuine ETOPS/EDTO divert.

---

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** **SafePort Cabo Verde S.A.** reported on field (tier-4) 🟧; H24 handling per AD 2.
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 5 min / taxi-out 7 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — for an ETOPS diversion, turnaround is secondary to the safe-arrival/pavement-inspection sequence (Briefing §3.3).
- **Services:** Fuel (Jet A-1 H24), customs/immigration H24, marshaller-guided stand entry; no de-icing.
- **Turnaround risk items:** A heavy 777-300ER diversion may trigger a **pavement inspection** before the next movement given the PCN 58/ACN-overload risk (Briefing §3.3/§7) — build that into any recovery-leg schedule.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Harmattan dust (Dec–Feb)** pushing visibility below CAT I minima on the only ILS runway (RWY 01) — the field's principal reliability threat (Briefing §3.4/§14).
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Low-density field; no ATFM/flow-program exposure sourced. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** For a scheduled movement, harmattan season (Dec–Feb) and the standing NE trade-wind crosswind (Briefing §3.3) are the two seasonal reliability watch-items; for the diversion role, reliability is not the relevant metric — capability (RFF margin, single ILS) is.

---

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** **NIL** — AD 2.21 publishes no noise abatement procedure and no noise-charge regime; isolated island field with no residential noise-sensitivity driver.
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** Not applicable — no curfew (H24).
- **Scheduling guardrails:** The only real scheduling guardrail at GVAC is **night stop-parking** (stands 01/07/TWY A2 only, A2 needing prior TWR coordination) — plan any overnight parking accordingly.
