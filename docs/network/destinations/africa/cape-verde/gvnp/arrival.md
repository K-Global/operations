## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 **The field's principal contrast with GVAC.** GVNP itself sits on Santiago Island's flatter southeastern coastal plain, but the island's **interior rises to ~1,394 m (Pico da Antónia)** with the **Serra Malagueta** range also prominent. No sourced MSA sectors or obstacle table were found this session (Briefing §3.1/§18) — treat any arrival routing that swings toward the interior with real terrain caution, not the benign GVAC assumption.
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 **No confirmed ILS** — expect a non-precision approach with its typically tighter/less-forgiving profile; 🟧 harmattan dust (Dec–Feb, by regional analogy) is a plausible visibility threat, not independently confirmed for Praia.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Shared APP/TWR frequency (118.200) suggests modest density, possibly non-radar — see [Briefing §3.2](index.md). Also under the **Sal Oceanic (GVSC)** FIR — see the [EUR/SAM Corridor brief](../../../../airspace/eur-sam-corridor.md).

## 10. Gotchas

- **No confirmed ILS** — brief for a non-precision approach as the default expectation, not an exception.
- **Santiago Island's interior terrain is real** — this is the single biggest tactical difference from the GVAC arrival picture; do not carry over GVAC's benign CFIT assumptions.
- **RWY 03's displaced threshold (~200 m)** reduces the usable landing distance on that end — confirm which runway is in use before assuming full-length LDA.
- **Shared APP/TWR frequency** — expect a lower-density, possibly non-radar handoff; brief accordingly.
- **This field's data is materially less complete than GVAC's** — the primary AIP was not reachable this session; treat every unconfirmed figure as a real gap, not a formality, before relying on this brief operationally.
