## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** None — Sahel plateau, no close-in high terrain (Briefing §3.1). 🟩
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Harmattan dust** (roughly Nov–Mar) is a materially significant visibility hazard at this inland field. 🟧 **Convective/thunderstorm activity** in the wet season is a documented real hazard (2013 precedent: severe thunderstorm, 44 kt surface gust, aircraft written off).
