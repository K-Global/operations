## 10. Gotchas

- **Single active runway** — the former parallel runway is permanently closed; no reciprocal-config fallback.
- **Harmattan dust (Nov–Mar)** — a materially more severe visibility factor here than at coastal fields; verify TAF/visibility before committing to a departure in season.
- **Documented severe-thunderstorm precedent** (2013, aircraft written off in a 44 kt gust event) — treat convective wet-season weather as a real hazard, not a theoretical one.
- 🟥 **Sahel security/overflight caution** — confirm the live advisory picture before departure (Briefing §9).
