## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟩
DFFD sits at 1,037 ft AMSL on the Sahelian plateau in Kadiogo Province. There is no close-in high terrain relevant to arrival, departure or missed-approach paths — CFIT is not the operative threat at this field. Verify the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
Ouagadougou handles the large majority (reported ~98%) of Burkina Faso's scheduled commercial traffic, with a mixed civil/military operation on the field (the airport co-locates a military sector). Control-type continuity is not independently confirmed this pass; per the regional pattern (see [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md) §3/§9), **Sahel ASECNA fields carry a documented history of ATC-service disruption** tied to regional insecurity — confirm current ATS provision at planning rather than assuming baseline availability. Burkina Faso's Ouagadougou UTA (Upper Terminal Area) is separately identified within the ASECNA/WACAF Free Route Airspace structure (§9 of this brief; corroborated in the Africa airspace brief §6).

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
Single active asphalt runway, 3,000 × 45 m; the former parallel runway (04R/22L) is permanently closed. No displaced-threshold or declared-distance detail confirmed this pass. No runway-excursion accident precedent was found in reachable sources for this specific runway, but treat wet/contaminated-runway braking action as a standing seasonal watch-item during the rains (§3.4).

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟥
Two hazard signatures dominate at this inland Sahel field: **harmattan dust** (roughly Nov–Mar), which is materially more severe here than at coastal West African fields and can reduce visibility significantly; and **convective/thunderstorm activity** in the wet season — a documented real-world precedent exists: a standing ATR 72 was substantially damaged (written off) at this airport on 17 September 2013 when a severe thunderstorm passed over the field, with surface winds recorded up to 44 kt. Treat both as durable seasonal drivers to re-check at §14/§15.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟥
This is a **Sahel-region field** — the standing operational consideration, ahead of any individual procedure detail, is the region's elevated security/overflight-risk profile (§9). The airport also co-locates a military sector alongside civil operations, and the government has long-standing plans (construction reported underway since 2021) to relocate international air traffic to a new field at **Donsin**, roughly 35 km northeast — monitor for any eventual transition NOTAM. No RFF, navaid or declared-distance figure was independently confirmed this pass; treat the durable operational picture as provisional pending a primary AIP pull.
