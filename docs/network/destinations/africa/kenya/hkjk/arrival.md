## 3. Descent & energy plan

- **Top-of-descent trigger:** Standard descent-profile planning to meet the first STAR altitude gate; plan the descent to cross FL100/9,000 ft appropriately given the field elevation of 5,330 ft (a comparatively short vertical descent window versus a sea-level field).
- **Speed control:** 250 KIAS below FL100/9,000 ft; charted STAR speed gates are pointers — verify chart.
- **Altitude constraints:** Key STAR crossing constraints are pointers — fly the charted altitudes; area MEA is FL100 given the surrounding high terrain.
- **Energy traps:** Late runway-end change (06↔24) ahead of a wind shift; the reduced margin between cruise/STAR altitudes and the 5,330 ft field elevation compresses the normal descent profile — brief an early-descent contingency.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟥 High-terrain region — **Ngong Hills** SW, **Aberdare Range**/**Mt Kenya** region N/NE, "MUA HILL" noted on the radar-minima chart. Field 5,330 ft; area MEA FL100. Honour MSA and the charted 10-1R radar-minima sectors — see [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 Single-runway environment concentrates arrival flow; **visual approach right base RWY 06 must remain ≥1.5 NM from THR 06** to avoid overflying the fuel tanks (Jeppesen 10-9, Briefing §4).
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟩 Radar-controlled (Nairobi Approach/Radar); busy hub — expect sequencing, especially with Wilson (HKNW) GA traffic co-located nearby.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** 🟥 Fly the published MAP and climb gradient exactly — do **not** turn toward the Ngong Hills (SW) or Aberdares/Mt Kenya (N/NE) below MSA. This is the field's primary missed-approach threat.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** Single-runway radar environment — expect vectoring/holding on a go-around given the concentrated arrival/departure flow.
- **Go-around traps:** High density altitude affects go-around climb performance — factor actual OAT/density altitude into the missed-approach climb-gradient assessment, not just the charted gradient.

## 10. Gotchas

- **High terrain close-in** (Ngong Hills SW; Aberdares/Mt Kenya N/NE) is the standing CFIT/missed-approach threat — never fly below MSA/step-downs early.
- **Visual right-base RWY 06 fuel-tank clearance** (≥1.5 NM from THR 06) — a sharp, specific caution from the source trip kit.
- **Single runway (06/24), no parallel** — expect sequencing/holding at busy banks.
- **Hot-and-high field (5,330 ft)** compresses the descent profile versus a sea-level field and affects go-around climb performance — brief accordingly.
- **24 has no ILS** — only RNP/RNAV/VOR available; 06 is the only precision-approach end.
- **Wilson (HKNW)** GA traffic operates a few miles away — maintain traffic awareness during vectoring.
