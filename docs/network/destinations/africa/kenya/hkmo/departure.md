- **By wind / config:** Wind-dependent selection between 03 and 21; **03 is reported as the ILS end** (Wikipedia) and the presumed preferred IMC/departure runway, consistent with a coastal field where the monsoon wind pattern (Kaskazi NE / Kusi SE) typically favours one orientation seasonally.
- **By departure direction:** Not confirmed — verify current-AIRAC SID assignment per runway end. 🟧

## 10. Gotchas

- **Sea-level field — no hot-and-high performance consideration**, a direct contrast to HKJK and other East-African highland stations on this network.
- **Runway 15/33 length figures disagree between sources** (1,260 m vs 1,363 m) — irrelevant to scheduled operations, but avoid citing either figure as authoritative.
- **Historical 2002 MANPADS attack** on a departing airliner near the field perimeter — no current standing restriction, but a recognised threat-awareness item; maintain standard SA per company security policy (Briefing §3.2/§4).
- **Kusi-season (Apr–Nov) coastal convective showers** are the standing seasonal departure-delay risk, not any regulatory/slot constraint.
- **Control type (procedural vs radar) is unconfirmed** — do not assume radar service without current-chart confirmation.
