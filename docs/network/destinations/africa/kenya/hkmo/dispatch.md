## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Three facilities — **Terminal 1** (international: immigration, customs, duty-free, scheduled international/regional carriers), **Terminal 2** (domestic), and a separate **General Aviation Terminal** (private/charter/business-jet, also used for medevac and high-end tourism charters).
- **Our operation uses:** The **General Aviation Terminal** is the presumed fit for K Global's business-jet-scale operation at this coastal station — offers dedicated handling and quicker clearance; confirm with handling before finalising a stand/terminal plan. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Terminal 1 houses immigration/customs for scheduled international traffic; the General Aviation Terminal provides its own dedicated clearance path for non-scheduled operations.
