## 10. Gotchas

- **Single runway (05/23), 3,300 m** — the shortest of the three regional fields in this build; check weight-limited performance before dispatching a widebody at high OAT.
- **FCBB and FZAA (Kinshasa) sit ~13.7 NM apart** under a shared cross-border TMA — expect coordination workload distinct from a normal single-ANSP departure.
- **The pre-2010 original runway alignment survives as a parallel taxiway** — do not mistake it for a second active runway.
- **Historical weather-related accidents at this field** (2009 approach crash, 2012 landing-short crash in bad weather) — brief conservative go-around/weather discipline even though these are landing- rather than departure-phase events; they reflect the field's marginal-weather risk profile generally.
