## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟧
DAAG sits on the coastal plain east of Algiers at 82 ft AMSL. The Tell Atlas foothills rise inland to the south of the Mitidja plain, with higher ground further south and east across northern Algeria — a general regional feature, not independently quantified (peaks/bearings/MSA sectors) from a primary source this pass. No close-in high terrain is expected to affect the immediate arrival/departure paths at the field itself, but confirm the sector MSA on the current chart before relying on this.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
As Algeria's principal international gateway, DAAG carries the country's heaviest traffic load; a radar approach service is assumed for a field of this size but not independently confirmed. Cross-ref the [Africa airspace brief](../../../../airspace/africa.md) for the **Algiers (DAAA)** FIR — a national FIR under **ENNA** — and general AFI/Maghreb airspace structure.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
The field has **two intersecting, non-parallel runways** (09/27 and 05/23, crossing at an oblique angle) rather than a parallel pair — a genuine runway-selection/crossing consideration distinct from most single- or parallel-runway fields in this batch. No displaced thresholds or declared-distance detail confirmed from a primary source; treat TORA/TODA/ASDA/LDA as **not published / verify** rather than assuming the full 3,500 m physical length is usable in every configuration.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
General Mediterranean-coastal climatology applies: a hot, dry, dust-laden **sirocco (chergui)** southerly wind can affect visibility and gusts in season, and winter frontal passages bring gusty westerly/north-westerly winds and rain. No field-specific frequency/severity statistic obtained this pass — treat as general regional climatology pending a primary MET source. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
The defining planning items are: (1) the **intersecting-runway configuration** (§3.3) — confirm the active runway-pairing logic before taxi; (2) **CAT III capability** claimed at the network level but not independently verified against a primary minima table; and (3) the general absence of independently-sourced AIP data this pass (declared distances, RFF, curfew, fuel) — most of §2's flags are currency/verification items rather than confirmed safety-critical gaps, but they should be closed before this brief is treated as Verified.
