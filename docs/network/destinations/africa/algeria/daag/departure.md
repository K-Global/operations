## 10. Gotchas

- **Two intersecting, non-parallel runways** — do not assume a simple reciprocal-pair configuration; confirm the active runway and crossing-traffic picture before taxi.
- Sirocco (chergui) dust events can reduce visibility with gusty wind — check current TAF/SIGMET.
- Multiple SID/minima/frequency fields are unverified this pass (§Open items) — treat quantitative departure planning figures as provisional pending an AIP cross-check.
