## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A historic (2014) chart-derived airport diagram shows **Terminal 1**, **Terminal 2** and **Terminal 3**, plus dedicated **Cargo** and **VIP** areas. Real-world reporting (Wikipedia) describes an active expansion programme — a new ~US$1.6bn "H-shaped," hub-style terminal — approved for tender in 2025, targeting completion by 2029 ahead of the 2030 FIFA World Cup, intended to raise total capacity from roughly 15M to 35M passengers/year. The **current-day (2026)** terminal picture, mid-programme, is not independently confirmed. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — terminal/pier assignment for K Global's operation is unconfirmed pending handling-agent/AIP data. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Port of Entry confirmed Yes; specific hall/terminal for international arrivals not independently confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 The airport is in an active, multi-year construction/expansion programme (new terminal, new ~3,700 m third runway, new ATC tower) — confirm current gate/terminal assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan, and expect this picture to keep changing through the programme's 2025–2029 build-out.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** One tier-4 source (ac-u-kwik) explicitly lists **"SCR LEVEL 3 - COORDINATED AIRPORT."** A second tier-4 source (Universal Weather) shows "Slots Required: N/A" for the same field. These are not reconciled against a primary AIP/IATA coordination-list source this pass — treat the Level 3 figure as the working assumption but confirm before relying on it operationally. 🟥
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard movement ban identified in reachable sources. A 2014 chart-derived source shows a **reversed day/night tailwind-takeoff runway preference** (RWY 35 config favoured 0600–2300L; RWY 17 config used 2300–0600L) but this is a runway-selection rule, not a curfew. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not independently confirmed for GMMN specifically; a Maghreb vACC operational cross-check describes **TSAT (Target Start-Up Approval Time)**-style sequencing during high-traffic periods, consistent with some form of departure-flow management in practice. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** If the Level 3 figure is confirmed, on-time pushback/schedule integrity has real network consequences; pending confirmation, treat schedule buffers conservatively at this hub.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** GMMN is the hub for Royal Air Maroc, Royal Air Maroc Express and Air Arabia Maroc, and was the 4th-busiest airport in Africa in 2022 (~10.4M pax, 2024) — a high year-round demand base is expected, but no K Global-specific seasonality data exists (GMMN is not a K Global base). 🟧
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for GMMN specifically. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** A dedicated Cargo apron area appears in cross-check chart material, consistent with GMMN's role as a regional cargo gateway; K Global belly-cargo uplift plans not confirmed. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — GMMN is not a K Global base; connection-bank timing is set by the network schedule at the relevant base hub.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** Inferred to be **seasonal coastal fog/low cloud**, consistent with the field's CAT III ILS infrastructure on 35L/35R and its published three-stage LVP regime — not independently confirmed with a frequency statistic. 🟧 A secondary driver is likely the **segregated-parallel-runway logic**, which constrains throughput relative to a true independent-parallel hub during high-demand banks.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Not independently sourced for GMMN; the field's hub status and reported Level 3 slot coordination suggest some exposure to flow management during peak periods. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Winter/transitional-season fog risk and the ongoing multi-year construction programme are the two picture-level reliability risks identified this pass. 🟧
