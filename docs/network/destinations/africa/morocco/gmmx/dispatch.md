## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Passenger terminals 1 and 2 are housed in one building; a third terminal (T3) has been constructed per the reachable Wikipedia/ONDA-sourced description. Reported capacity figures for T1/T2 are internally inconsistent in the source (42,000 m²/9,000,000 pax-per-year design capacity is cited alongside a separate 22,000 m²/2,500,000 pax-per-year figure for the same terminals) — treat both capacity figures as unverified. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not published / verify 🟧
