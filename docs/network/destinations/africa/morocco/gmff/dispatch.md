## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single passenger terminal building serves the field. Public reporting on its floor area is inconsistent between sources (~26,000 m² vs ~28,000 m²), and reported handling-capacity figures vary widely across sources (from 500,000 pax/year pre-expansion up to figures as high as 2.5–3 million pax/year post-expansion, with one source citing a 2018 modernisation to 1.5 million pax/year) — treat all these capacity figures as tier-4 and mutually inconsistent. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** The single terminal building — no domestic/international pier split confirmed in reachable sources.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** Present (Port of Entry confirmed, §1) — hall location/layout within the terminal not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** A further **expansion (~MAD 500 million budget, ~46,000 m² new terminal)** was under pre-selection tender as of 2024 reporting — construction status/completion date not confirmed this pass; expect the terminal footprint and stand layout to be in flux. 🟧
