## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single combined terminal building (~26,550 m² per Wikipedia, tier-4) with one large waiting room divided into a national section (no customs) and an international section. No multi-terminal/multi-pier structure identified in reachable sources.
- **Our operation uses:** Standard combined international arrivals/departures processing — no separate concourse or pier structure confirmed; confirm gate/stand assignment with handling at planning. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Standard passport control within the single terminal. Wikipedia (tier-4) notes passengers connecting onward through Casablanca on an international flight can clear passport control at Agadir itself to save transfer time at GMMN — a corroborating, not AIP-sourced, detail. 🟧
- **Notes:** Passenger-facility scale is modest relative to a hub field — confirm any construction/expansion NOTAMs at planning. 🟧

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Not confirmed. Apron stand groups C1–C6/C10/C14/C15 (concrete, PCR 726/R/A/W/T), C7/C11 (PCR 700), C8/C9 (PCR 670), C12 (PCR 710), C13 (PCR 720), plus E1–E3 and L1–L4 (bitumen, PCR 721/F/A/X/T) are published `[AIP AD 2.8]`, but code-letter/wingspan suitability per individual stand is not stated in the reachable table. 🟧

- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not confirmed. 🟧
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** A **WIG WAG warning-light system** is installed at the TWY D1/RWY and TWY D5/RWY intersections — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** **Swissport Maroc** and **RAM Handling** both provide passenger, baggage, ramp-operations, cabin-cleaning/servicing, fuel-and-oil (uplift-supervision portion only), flight-operations/crew-administration, and ground-transport services; a separate **RAM ground handling company** handles cargo and mail `[AIP AD 2.23]`. Which handler is contracted for our specific operation is not confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 6 min / taxi-out 8 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]`. 🟧
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin service expected as standard scope for an international regional destination.
- **Turnaround risk items:** A dual-handler environment (Swissport/RAM both certified for the same service categories) — confirm which is contracted for our operation before relying on a specific turnaround profile. On-field fuel-uplift throughput (4 servicers + 1 tanker on a single hydrant system) is modest and could be a constraint during a peak arrival bank.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** Agadir is a major European leisure/beach destination — Wikipedia (tier-4) reports ~2.3 million passengers in 2023 with a strong UK/France/Germany/Benelux charter and LCC presence (Ryanair, EasyJet, TUI, Jet2, Transavia, Vueling, Wizz Air, Condor and others). Treat demand as strongly seasonal (European winter-sun and summer-beach travel). 🟧
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Leisure/charter-skewed; expect banks aligned to European school-holiday windows — not independently quantified for K Global's specific schedule. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** No dedicated freighter facility identified; cargo handling is limited to a 2-tonne-capacity forklift `[AIP AD 2.4]` — belly cargo capacity at this Category S field is likely modest. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — GMAD is a destination, not a connecting hub, for K Global.

## 7. Fuel

- **Supplier / into-plane:** Into-plane provider company not individually named in reachable sources; on-field infrastructure is a Jet A-1 hydrant system (3 pumps @ 2.5 m³/min each, 4 servicers) plus a dedicated 18,000 L Jet A-1 tanker and a 1,000 L AVGAS 100LL cart `[AIP AD 2.4]`. 🟧
- **Uplift availability & hours:** **H24**, confirmed `[AIP AD 2.3]`. Payment accepted in MAD or foreign currency. 🟩
- **Price / tankering angle:** Not sourced this pass — pull live at planning. See [`OM E — Fuel Policy`](../../../../../flight-ops/fuel-policy.md).
- **Quality / freeze notes:** Standard Jet A-1; no cold-soak/fuel-freeze consideration at this coastal/warm field.
