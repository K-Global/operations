## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single passenger terminal, rebuilt 2016–2021 (old terminal demolished, new terminal inaugurated 9 Jul 2021); check-in on one side of the main hall, baggage reclaim on the other. No confirmed multi-concourse/pier split. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable — GMMW is not a K Global base; any operation here uses the single available terminal. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** Single arrivals hall handling international immigration/customs for scheduled EU services (Spain, Belgium, Netherlands, Germany per public route data); exact hall/desk configuration not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** Facility described as a modern (2021) rebuild; current passenger-flow capacity for a full-widebody arrival not documented — historical note (Wikipedia) flags that CIQ processing was overwhelmed when a Boeing 747 arrived under the pre-2021 facility; current-terminal capacity for a widebody surge is unconfirmed. 🟧

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** Strongly seasonal — public passenger statistics show nearly a third of GMMW's annual traffic moves in the summer months, driven by the Moroccan-diaspora market travelling from Western Europe (Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Spain). Traffic is thin outside this window relative to leisure-destination fields.
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not confirmed in detail; overall market is VFR/diaspora-driven rather than business travel, consistent with a summer/holiday-period skew.
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** Historical (pre-2010) cargo tonnage figures exist but are outdated and not representative of current volumes; no current cargo data confirmed. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — GMMW is a point-to-point regional destination, not a K Global connection hub.
