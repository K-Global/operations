## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** 🟩 **None found** — "Slots Required: No" per AC-U-KWIK; no coordinator identified (none expected). 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No formal curfew found; AD operates **0400–2000Z** with **out-of-hours (O/T) service available by 48 h prior notice plus a fee** `[AC-U-KWIK]` — this is the practical scheduling constraint, not a hard curfew. Control Tower is H24. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** No ATFM/CTOT programme found in reachable sources — traffic volumes at Namibia's primary gateway are modest relative to major African hubs. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The O/T regime (48 h PNR + fee outside 0400–2000Z) is the main schedule-integrity item — build any late-night or early-morning rotation around it and confirm the fee/PNR mechanics with handling before committing a schedule slot.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Ground handling is **mandatory** `[AC-U-KWIK]`. On-field FBO/handling agents identified in public sources: **Jetmate Aviation** and **General Aviation Services (G.A.S.) Namibia** — which agent is contracted for our operation is not confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 6 min / taxi-out 8 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard service times.
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU, water/lav and catering expected at a scheduled international stand; not individually confirmed for this field.
- **Turnaround risk items:** No published single-runway peak-bank congestion issue (traffic volumes are modest); the standing risk item is instead the **hot-and-high take-off performance margin** (5,640 ft field, §9 Departure) that can affect load planning on a hot afternoon — confirm the day's performance figure before committing to a tight turn-to-departure weight.
