## 10. Gotchas

- Published AD operating hours are **0600–1800 daily** — do not plan a departure outside this window without prior extended-hours coordination (see [FVFA Dispatch](dispatch.md) §4).
- **No SID names, minima, or take-off obstacle procedures are confirmed** for this field — do not depart against an assumed or inferred procedure.
- Field sits within the KAZA-TFCA wildlife corridor — qualitative wildlife-strike caution on departure climb-out (see [FVFA Briefing](index.md) §3.5); no confirmed strike statistic.
- Single combined Approach/Tower frequency — confirm the position's actual workload/traffic picture before assuming a quiet field, especially in the leisure/charter peak season.
- Hot, high-density-altitude afternoons (Sep–Oct) are the main performance watch-item, not runway length.
