## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 No close-in high terrain identified on the standard approach path — the field sits on the relatively flat plateau; the Zambezi gorge lies well clear of it (see [FVFA Briefing](index.md) §3.1). Numeric MSA/obstacle data not found in reachable sources — verify against the current AIRAC area chart.
- **Specific threats:** No closely-spaced parallels, offset/converging approaches, or wake-specific issue identified (single runway field). 🟧 **Qualitative wildlife-strike caution** given the field's location within the KAZA-TFCA wildlife corridor and adjacency to game reserves — no confirmed strike statistic (see [FVFA Briefing](index.md) §3.5). The **Victoria Falls spray column** can locally reduce visibility near the gorge for scenic-flight traffic operating in the vicinity, not for the main approach corridor to the terminal.
- **Airspace / traffic:** Combined single Approach/Tower frequency (121.1 MHz) suggests a lean, likely procedural traffic-management model; a mixed leisure/charter/scenic-flight traffic picture is expected in season (see [FVFA Briefing](index.md) §3.2). Cross-border proximity to Zambia's Livingstone gateway (FLHN, ~16–17 NM N) is a coordination consideration, not independently confirmed.
