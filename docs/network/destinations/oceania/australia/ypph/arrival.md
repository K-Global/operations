## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 The AIP explicitly notes the **Obstacle Limitation Surface is "extensively infringed by terrain east of the aerodrome"** for both runway pairs — corroborated by the public Darling Scarp/Perth Hills feature ~20–25 km east. Not an extreme terrain profile, but a genuine consideration for any arrival, missed-approach or go-around toward the east — verify the current terrain/obstacle chart. See [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 Severe turbulence below 3,000 ft during summer months (easterly wind-gradient related); low-level windshear on RWY 06 arrivals when wind is N–NW ≥37 kt; seasonal bird hazard (galahs/corellas in spring, ibis in winter/spring, nankeen kestrels year-round peaking spring/summer, ducks in winter/spring, black cockatoos Jan–May).
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟩 Moderate-density radar-controlled terminal airspace in the **Melbourne FIR (YMMM)**; estimated airborne holding up to 20 min during Mon–Fri peak windows (0100–0500, 0900–1300 UTC), 10 min during 0500–0900 UTC.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** The AIP-noted easterly terrain (Darling Scarp/Perth Hills) is the field's primary missed-approach watch-item — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart, particularly for an eastbound miss.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around returns the aircraft to a moderate-density radar terminal environment; expect vectoring per Perth Approach (123.6/132.95).
- **Go-around traps:** Terrain awareness on an eastbound miss is the standout trap at this field, more so than traffic density.

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** 03 **3,444 m**; 21 **3,444 m**; 06 **2,163 m**; 24 **2,163 m** — **no displaced thresholds published** for any runway end (a genuinely confirmed positive). RESA (90 m × 90 m) is confirmed at every runway end. The main pair (03/21) is ample for any K Global widebody; the secondary pair (06/24) is non-limiting for typical arrival weights.

- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟩 No displaced thresholds; RESA confirmed at every end. All runway ends are ungrooved for the first 100 m from the start of takeoff — a minor braking-surface note relevant to a rejected-landing/overrun scenario rather than a normal landing roll.

## 10. Gotchas

- **The Obstacle Limitation Surface is AIP-flagged as "extensively infringed by terrain east of the aerodrome"** — the field's one genuine terrain consideration; brief accordingly for any eastbound arrival, missed approach or go-around.
- **RWY 06 has no ILS and no centreline lights** — treat any RWY 06 arrival as a non-precision approach with heightened alignment awareness.
- **Only one runway is nominated during LVP, normally RWY 21** — do not expect a low-visibility arrival option on RWY 03, 06 or 24.
- **Low-level windshear on RWY 06 arrivals** when wind is N–NW ≥37 kt.
- **Severe turbulence below 3,000 ft** in the terminal area during summer months.
- **Seasonal bird hazard is real and AIP-documented** — multiple species with distinct seasonal peaks (§5); factor into the approach/landing brief accordingly.
- **No curfew** — unlike YSSY, a late or delayed arrival is not subject to a hard movement restriction here, though customs/immigration desk staffing off-peak is unconfirmed.
