## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** Not confirmed in reachable research pass — pull the current-AIRAC STAR list at planning. 🟧
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** Aircraft up to and including A330/B787/B772 may be processed to land on either parallel (16L/R or 34L/R) at clearance-delivery stage; larger K Global types (A388/B748/A35K/B77W) are governed by the fleet-specific taxi/operating-restriction document.
- **Transition to approach:** Expect a STAR-to-ILS/GLS transition with radar vectors from Sydney Director (E 125.3 / W 126.1) onto final, particularly during **PRM (Precision Runway Monitor) operations** for simultaneous independent approaches to the closely-spaced parallels.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** 250 KIAS below 10,000 ft AMSL when not on a SID/STAR/vector (AIP-confirmed); by day, ATC may apply a reduced 2,400 m runway-occupancy separation standard on the main parallel, allowing two aircraft to occupy the runway simultaneously — expect a tighter arrival sequence than the physical spacing might suggest.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat coastal site at 21 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path. Published obstacles are all man-made (telecommunications tower, transient container-train infringement, tree line clear of the obstacle-clearance surface) — see Briefing §3.1.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Closely-spaced parallel runways (16L/34R, 16R/34L)** require **PRM operations** for simultaneous independent instrument approaches — confirm PRM capability/priority status before the approach phase; a documented radio-failure contingency exists on the Director frequency (squawk 7600, do not track through final without complying with the published procedure). Wake-turbulence caution is published for parallel RWY 34 operations when wind is W–NW ≥5 kt.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 High-density radar-controlled terminal airspace in the Melbourne FIR (YMMM); a go-around from a visual approach in VMC must fly the GLS/ILS missed-approach procedure for the runway in use, or as ATC directs — do not assume a free-form visual go-around.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat coastal site) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around drops the aircraft back into a high-density, parallel-runway/PRM environment — expect vectoring and possible resequencing, particularly during a PRM-nominated period.
- **Go-around traps:** The AIP-mandated go-around procedure (fly the GLS/ILS missed approach, or as ATC directs) applies even from a visual approach in VMC — do not improvise a visual go-around track.

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** 16R **3,877 m**; 34L **3,962 m**; 16L **2,207 m** (DTHR 231 m); 34R **2,400 m** (DTHR 38 m); 07 **2,530 m**; 25 **2,429 m** (DTHR 101 m). The main parallel is ample for any K Global widebody; the shorter parallel and crosswind runway are non-limiting for typical arrival weights but always fly the LDA, not the physical runway length, given the displaced thresholds on 25/16L/34R.
- **Braking / vacate:** RWY 16R landings require approval to vacate left via TWY F, B3 or B4; RWY 16L/34R arrivals remain on Tower (124.7) until west of TWY S before contacting Ground (121.7); RWY 34R arrivals vacating TWY T2 taxi via TWY U/U1 unless otherwise advised; RWY 07/25 arrivals require approval to vacate on TWY C.
- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟧 Displaced thresholds on RWY 25 (101 m), RWY 16L (231 m) and RWY 34R (38 m) reduce usable LDA below the physical runway length — always confirm the correct figure. RESA (90 m × 90 m) is confirmed at every runway end.

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 07/25 is CAT I only** — do not plan a low-visibility arrival there; the parallel pair (16L/34R, 16R/34L) is the CAT II low-vis option.
- **PRM operations govern simultaneous independent parallel approaches** — confirm PRM capability/priority status; a non-PRM-capable aircraft may see reduced priority and should expect the documented radio-failure contingency to be briefed.
- **A go-around from a visual approach in VMC must fly the published GLS/ILS missed approach, or as ATC directs** — this is an explicit local AIP requirement, not a general assumption.
- **Circling approach to RWY 16L/34R is not permitted at night.**
- **Displaced thresholds on RWY 25 (101 m), RWY 16L (231 m) and RWY 34R (38 m)** — always confirm usable LDA before committing to a short-runway arrival.
- **Wake-turbulence caution during parallel RWY 34 operations** when wind is W–NW ≥5 kt.
- **Hard curfew 2300–0600** — a delayed arrival that cannot meet the shoulder-period exemption criteria is prohibited outright; do not plan a schedule reliant on a post-shoulder-period landing.
- **Wildlife activity is seasonal and material:** grey-headed flying foxes after last light (peak Apr–Jun) and Australian white ibis transiting RWY 16R/34L between TWY B8 and TWY L (Jun–Nov).
