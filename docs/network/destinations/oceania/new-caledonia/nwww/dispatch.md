## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A major terminal expansion completed in 2012 added a new arrivals area, larger check-in hall and two jetbridges; the terminal has **five stands capable of handling commercial jet aircraft**, two served by airbridge and three by stairs, plus several further stands for smaller aircraft. **Aircalin's own lounge ("Hibiscus Lounge")** sits one floor above the main transit hall.
- **Our operation uses:** Confirm gate assignment with handling; the field's widebody-capable stands are concentrated at **P11/P21** (§Briefing §13).
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International PoE by role; hours not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 The field closed to international traffic for a period during the **2024 New Caledonia civil unrest** — a reminder of non-weather access-disruption risk at this Pacific-island gateway; no current operational impact.

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** 🟧 **P11** (B787, B773, B744, A332, A359) and **P21** (B787, B772, B744, A359, **A380 via the "ZEC A380" extension**) form the main widebody cluster; **P1/P2** and **P52/D2** are secondary widebody-capable positions, each with paired-neutralisation logic (using one neutralises its pair) — see [Briefing §13](index.md).
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** **P51** (B737-700/A319 only), **P61–P65** (ATR/turboprop and VFR), **P7** (Aircalin ATR maintenance apron) are narrowbody/regional-only.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not individually confirmed beyond the named stand list; the apron is divided into a civil area (north), military area (south) and a freight-parking area (west of the main civil apron).
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a jetbridge-served stand (P11/P21 cluster) for scheduled widebody service; confirm with handling.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** **B777 push-back from P11/P21 is west-facing only via TWY W**; an **A380 at P21 activates the "ZEC A380" extension** with outer engines shut down during push-back via bretelle B — see [Briefing §11](index.md).

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** None identified in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No general curfew; a **night visual-approach restriction** applies from the north sector between LTO VOR radials 290° and 128° (see [Briefing §4](index.md)).
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not confirmed; Tontouta's ADS-B-based, non-radar-outside-coverage control model (Briefing §3.2) is the operative local procedure rather than a formal ATFM/CTOT programme.
- **Commercial impact:** The field's schedule-integrity risk is dominated by **South Pacific tropical-cyclone season (Nov–Apr)** rather than slot/curfew administration — see [Briefing §14](index.md).
