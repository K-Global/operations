## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟧
The immediate airport environment is flat coastal ground (33 ft elevation), and no current chart obstacle was identified in reachable sources — but the field's history includes a genuine close-in terrain event: on **1 November 1961**, a Panair do Brasil Douglas DC-7C on a night approach from Sal (Cabo Verde) **struck an 84 m hill approximately 2.7 km from the runway**, flying low and outside the normal pattern; 45 of 88 aboard were killed. Modern IFR obstacle-clearance criteria and approach design account for terrain of this scale, and no current restriction was found — but the accident is a standing reminder to **fly the charted vertical profile precisely on the final segment**, especially on any non-precision or visual approach. Verify MSA/close-in obstacle data on the current chart. 🟧

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟩
Recife FIR (SBRE) — see the [South America — Airspace Briefing](../../../../airspace/south-america.md) for FIR structure, RVSM band, and the Portuguese/English frequency environment. **CINDACTA III** (the Brazilian Air Force's NE integrated air-defense/ATC centre) is based in the Recife area. No unusual traffic-density concern identified for the airport itself. 🟩

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
Single runway (18/36); tier-4 sources disagree on exact length (3,007 m vs. 3,300 m — §18). No displaced threshold identified in reachable sources. NE Brazil's Feb–Jul rainy season implies occasional wet-runway braking-action degradation, not independently quantified. 🟧

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
Recife (Pernambuco) sits on the NE Brazilian coast with a tropical climate; the rainy season runs roughly **April–August** (Recife's rainy season is later in the year than Fortaleza's, reflecting its more southerly/coastal position) with the **ITCZ** and coastal convection contributing to heavier showers in that window. Trade winds E/SE prevail. No airport-specific fog/low-vis statistic found. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
No confirmed curfew, slot regime, or special crew-qualification gate identified in reachable sources. The field shares facilities with **Recife Air Force Base** — no operational restriction identified from this, but worth noting for local context. Confirm the K Global VAMSYS aerodrome category before scheduling, since it is unset. See §18.
