## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single integrated passenger terminal (built 2004, replacing the earlier facility), including a shopping mall; capacity increased from 1.5M to 9M pax/yr at that rebuild. Operated by **AENA** since the 2019 concession. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Main terminal — no dedicated concourse split identified.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** International arrivals hall confirmed by scheduled European service (Iberia Madrid, TAP Lisbon) and regional international service (Cabo Verde, Argentina); exact hall location not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** Airport connects directly to the Recife Metro (Airport Station) via covered walkway — relevant to crew/pax logistics context, not an operational constraint. 🟧
