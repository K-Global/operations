## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single passenger terminal; phase-1 of a new terminal building opened **May 2015** under the Aeroportos Brasil concession, replacing the older Infraero-era facility.
- **Our operation uses:** The single passenger terminal — exact gate/stand assignment for the K Global operation not confirmed. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals process through the single terminal; hall/desk detail not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** SBKP's commercial identity is dominated by its **cargo** role (Brazil's second-busiest cargo airport, Azul Airlines' main pax hub) — the passenger terminal footprint is comparatively modest next to the ~77,000 m² cargo complex. 🟧

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Winter (Jun–Aug) plateau fog/stratus** risk, mirroring SBGR ~40 km away — but SBKP carries a notably better historical weather-reliability record (commonly cited at ~5 closure days/year), which is exactly why it is the network's designated primary SBGR alternate.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Lower than SBGR/Congonhas given the absence of a confirmed slot regime, but the **single-runway** configuration means any runway-blocking event (e.g. the 2012 gear-collapse closure) produces a full-field stoppage rather than a partial-capacity reduction. 🟥
- **On-time reliability picture:** Generally strong given the field's weather-reliability reputation; the standing risk is a runway-blocking incident rather than routine weather delay. 🟧
