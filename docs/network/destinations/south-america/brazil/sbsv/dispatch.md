## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single integrated passenger terminal (opened 1998–2000, replacing the earlier facility), including a shopping mall; capacity ~6M pax/yr design, trading as "Salvador Bahia Airport" under the Vinci concession (since 2017). 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Main terminal — no dedicated concourse split identified.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** International arrivals hall confirmed by scheduled European service (Air Europa Madrid, TAP Lisbon) and South American service (Aerolíneas Argentinas, Sky Airline); exact hall location not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** A metro (subway) station ("Aeroporto") on Salvador's Line 2 opened April 2018, connecting to downtown Lapa — relevant to crew/pax logistics context, not an operational constraint. 🟧
