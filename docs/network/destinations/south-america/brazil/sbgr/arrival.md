## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 **Serra da Cantareira** range close north of the field (peaks ~1,000 m/3,300 ft) — a real historical precedent (1996 business-jet accident, wrong-direction go-around turn into this terrain) underscores the need to fly the published missed-approach turn direction. The Serra do Mar escarpment further south-east is regional context, not a close-in arrival factor.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 Dense **São Paulo TMA** traffic — GRU shares terminal airspace with Congonhas (SBSP) and Viracopos (SBKP); expect extended vectoring/holding, especially in marginal weather.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 High density under **São Paulo Approach** — brief for re-sequencing and extended vectoring, especially during a bank or a winter fog event.
