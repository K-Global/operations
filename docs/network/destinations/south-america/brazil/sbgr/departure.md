## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** 10L/28R (3,700 m) is non-limiting for any K Global type; **10R/28L (3,000 m)** is likewise generally non-limiting but warrants a performance check for the heaviest long-haul departures on a hot afternoon given the field's 2,461 ft elevation.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Not classic hot-and-high, but the combination of 2,461 ft elevation and summer afternoon temperatures trims takeoff margin on the shorter runway — confirm on the day's performance data.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Wet-season (Nov–Mar) heavy rain is the relevant seasonal consideration; no specific contamination/braking-action data confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — no field-length penalty on the main runway for any home-based type.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Runway designators changed 09/27 → 10/28** — confirm current-chart naming; do not brief off an older 09/27 reference.
- **Serra da Cantareira high ground close north** — never turn toward it on a go-around/EOSID without a published procedure to do so; historical precedent exists.
- **~45 movements/hour slot cap** — on-time pushback here has direct network-wide consequences as the home-region base.
- **São Paulo TMA congestion** — expect ground-delay/flow-management exposure during peak banks, compounded by Congonhas and Viracopos sharing the same terminal airspace.
- **Winter (Jun–Aug) fog/stratus** can affect low-vis taxi and takeoff planning with limited warning.
