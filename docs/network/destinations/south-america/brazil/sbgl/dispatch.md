## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** (opened 1977) and **Terminal 2** (opened 1999), both elliptical designs with jetway piers. **Since November 2016, Terminal 1's check-in and baggage-claim areas have been closed** — all passengers, regardless of which terminal's gate they use, process through **Terminal 2**.
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal 2 for check-in/baggage processing; the specific boarding gate/pier for our operation is not confirmed. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals process through Terminal 2 regardless of gate; connection-time implications for any onward network leg not independently confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** A new pier with **26 additional jet bridges** and a new apron for **~97 aircraft** were added by the RIOgaleão concessionaire (2016–17), materially expanding capacity beyond the original two-terminal complex — confirm current gate/pier assignment with handling. 🟧

- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None confirmed.** 🟩
- **CTOT / flow control:** Brazilian domestic ATFM/flow-management practice may still apply generally, especially given Santos Dumont cross-traffic in the shared Rio TMA; not independently sourced for SBGL specifically. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The absence of a confirmed slot/curfew regime gives SBGL more scheduling flexibility than the São Paulo-area or Santos Dumont fields — the operative commercial constraint here is more likely to be gate/terminal availability during peak international banks than a hard regulatory restriction.
