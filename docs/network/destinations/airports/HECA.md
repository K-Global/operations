## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain

Cairo lies on the flat Nile valley/desert plain — **no significant high terrain** close-in, so CFIT exposure is comparatively low. The real close-in threats are **obstacles** (urban masts, the Cairo metropolitan area to the SW) and **airspace** (prohibited/restricted areas around the capital). The field itself **slopes**, with published thresholds ranging 190–467 ft and an AD elevation of 467 ft. Read the MSA ring and obstacle data on the current-AIRAC AD/area chart. Honour MSA/step-downs on any non-precision or radar-vectored arrival.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic

Major hub with **radar control** (Cairo Approach/Radar). Notably, **HECA publishes no SIDs/STARs in normal use — all departures and arrivals are radar vectored** (departure exit fixes MENKU, DEGDI, MENLI, SOLAM; arrivals via CVO VOR; holds ALTOX and CRS, FL120 min). Three parallel runways concentrate flow. Class B TMA (40/60 NM), Class D CTR. **Self-positioned VFR is not permitted in Egypt** (CVFR/IFR only) — expect full vectoring throughout. Cross-ref OM C Airspace (Cairo FIR) when built.

### 3.3 Runway excursion

Three parallel runways: 05R/23L and 05C/23C (4,000 × 60 m) and 05L/23R (3,301 × 60 m), all 045°/225° magnetic, asphalt. Long and not normally length-limited. **05L/23R has had ongoing maintenance** — often departures only; check NOTAMs for closures. Declared distances, PCN and displaced thresholds are not published in reachable sources — verify per direction. Summer heat lengthens the roll (high density altitude despite low elevation).

### 3.4 Weather threat

Hot desert climate. **Intense summer heat** drives high density altitude (performance impact). **Spring khamsin (Mar–May)** brings dust/sandstorms that sharply reduce visibility. **Winter (Dec–Feb)** can bring radiation/morning fog. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations

Radar-vectored environment with no charted SIDs/STARs — brief the expected exit fixes and vectoring pattern, and keep the FMS flexible. Summer density altitude is the main performance item. RFF category, declared distances, PCN, hours and fuel grade are primary-pending — re-verify against the Egypt AIP and current AIRAC before a performance- or minima-critical op.
