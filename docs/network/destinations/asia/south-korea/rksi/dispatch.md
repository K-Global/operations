## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** (opened 2001) — 44 gates, plus the satellite **Concourse A** (opened 2008, 30 gates, six lounges) connected to T1 by twin 870 m underground passageways with an automated intra-airport transit (IAT) shuttle. **Terminal 2** (opened 18 Jan 2018, 37 gates) — built through a phased expansion, with a Phase 4 programme (additional apron/car-parking capacity, fourth runway) completed 3 Dec 2024.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — carrier/terminal assignment at RKSI is airline-specific (e.g. certain alliance-aligned carriers concentrate in T2, others in T1/Concourse A); confirm current gate assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear passport control in whichever terminal the flight is assigned; T1↔T2 connection is via a **free shuttle bus**, reported ~20 minutes' travel time at 5–8 minute intervals — build connection time accordingly for any onward network leg.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal/gate assignment for a new K Global service is not yet confirmed — coordinate with handling ahead of the first rotation.
