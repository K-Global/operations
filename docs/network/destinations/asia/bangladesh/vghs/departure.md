## 10. Gotchas

- **VGHS has only one runway, no parallel** — a runway-blocking event (weather, disabled aircraft, ground incident) removes all departure capacity at once; there is no alternate-runway fallback at this field.
- **18 October 2025 cargo-terminal fire** forced a multi-hour full airfield closure — a concrete illustration of this field's single-point-of-failure profile; not a live restriction, but relevant risk context.
- **Runway-length figures conflict between public sources** (3,505 m vs 3,200 m) — do not plan a performance-limited departure off either uncorroborated figure without AIP confirmation.
- **Shared civil/military aerodrome**, plus a second active Dhaka-area airfield (VGTJ) ~4 NM south — expect mixed-traffic sequencing.
- **Monsoon season (~May–Sep)** and pre-monsoon convective-squall risk (~Mar–May) — build weather contingency into any departure planned in these windows.
- **Terminal 3 construction** ongoing near the airfield — confirm current taxi/ground-movement layout locally pending its 2026/2027 opening.
