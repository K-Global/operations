## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat Bengal delta plain at 30 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 VGHS's **single-runway, no-parallel geometry** removes any alternate-runway contingency for a low-visibility or traffic-driven diversion decision within the field itself. The aerodrome is **shared with an active Bangladesh Air Force base**, and **Dhaka-Tejgaon (VGTJ)**, a second active airfield, lies ~4 NM south — brief for mixed civil/military traffic and possible re-sequencing.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Dhaka Approach's control capability (radar vs procedural) is not independently re-confirmed this pass — brief for a possible procedural fallback pending current-AIRAC confirmation (see [Briefing §3.2](index.md)).

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat delta plain) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart once obtained.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around re-enters a shared civil/military traffic environment with a second active airfield (VGTJ) nearby — expect vectoring/holding pending re-sequencing.

## 10. Gotchas

- **VGHS has only one runway, no parallel** — do not assume an alternate-runway option is available for a low-visibility contingency, a go-around re-attempt, or a runway-blocking event.
- **18 October 2025 cargo-terminal fire** forced a multi-hour full airfield closure and diversions to Kolkata (VECC), Chittagong (VGEG) and Sylhet (VGSY) — a factual illustration of this field's single-point-of-failure profile; not a live restriction, but relevant risk context for arrival contingency planning.
- **Runway-length figures conflict between public sources** (3,505 m vs 3,200 m) — verify current AIRAC before planning a near-max-weight landing.
- **Shared civil/military aerodrome**, plus Dhaka-Tejgaon (VGTJ) ~4 NM south — expect mixed-traffic sequencing on arrival.
- **Monsoon season (~May–Sep)** and pre-monsoon convective-squall risk (~Mar–May) — build weather contingency and an off-field alternate into any arrival planned in these windows; company preferred alternates are **VECC** (Kolkata) and **VIDP** (Delhi), both cross-country.
- **Terminal 3 construction** ongoing near the airfield — confirm current taxi-in routing and gate assignment locally.
