## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1 (T1)** and **Terminal 2 (T2)** are both international and share one building — T1 (ground floor) serves as the international **arrivals** concourse, T2 (first floor) serves as the international **departures** concourse. A separate, single-storey **Domestic Terminal** sits to one side, with arrivals and departures on the same floor. **Terminal 3** — a large new terminal under construction since December 2019 (partially inaugurated October 2023, ~90% physical completion reported at that point) — is **not yet operational**; per public reporting as recent as April 2026, opening is targeted for **16 December 2026 or early 2027**. On completion, Terminal 3 is intended to raise the airport's passenger-handling capacity substantially (design figures reported around 24 million/year vs an earlier ~8 million/year figure) and to add a large new cargo building.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — presumed T1 (arrival)/T2 (departure) international complex pending Terminal 3 entering service; terminal/gate assignment for a company outside the field's established carrier groupings would be set case-by-case. 🟧
- **International arrivals / immigration hall:** International arrivals process through Terminal 1; exact immigration desk hours not confirmed (§8). 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal 3 construction has been repeatedly delayed (originally targeted for 2024, then not-before-2026 per September 2024 reporting, now targeted December 2026/early 2027 per April 2026 reporting) — re-check status at the next update cycle; construction-area activity may affect ground movement near the site. A VIP terminal previously located near the main gate was demolished in 2023 to make way for Terminal 3, with a temporary VIP facility in use pending relocation into the new terminal.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** VGHS is Bangladesh's largest airport and the primary hub for the country's carriers (Biman Bangladesh Airlines, US-Bangla Airlines, Novoair, Air Astra); Wikipedia's most recently cited infobox figures show **~12.72 million annual passenger movements** and **~517,940 tonnes of annual cargo** — figures that, per the same source, sit **above an earlier-cited ~8 million/year passenger design capacity**, a genuine congestion signal pending Terminal 3 entering service. 🟧
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced — treat as standard international business/leisure/diaspora-travel mix (notably South Asia–Gulf and South Asia–UK/expatriate corridors, per the field's route network) pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** The airport's dedicated cargo terminal has a reported **200,000-tonnes/year capacity**, planned to rise to **500,000 tonnes/year** on completion of the ongoing renovation/expansion project; the cargo facility was the site of the **18 October 2025 fire** (§10) — confirm current cargo-handling capacity/restrictions before planning significant belly-cargo uplift. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — VGHS is not a K Global base; connection-bank timing is set by whichever base the flight connects through.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** The field's **single-runway, no-parallel geometry** is the structural reliability driver — any runway-blocking event removes all capacity. This was demonstrated on **18 October 2025**, when a fire in the cargo-terminal area (near Gate 8) forced a full suspension of airfield operations for several hours (reported ~14:30–21:18 local), with flights diverted to Kolkata (VECC), Chittagong (VGEG) and Sylhet (VGSY); publicly estimated damage ran from the hundreds of millions of US dollars to over US$1 billion, with the cause under investigation. Monsoon-season (~May–Sep) convection and pre-monsoon squall activity (~Mar–May) are the recurring seasonal weather-delay drivers (§ Briefing §14); no VGHS-specific delay statistic was found for either. 🟧
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Not independently confirmed; no ATFM/flow program identified for VGHS in reachable sources. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Passenger volume reported above the airport's earlier-cited design capacity (§6) is a plausible congestion/reliability pressure pending Terminal 3 entering service; not independently quantified this pass. 🟧
