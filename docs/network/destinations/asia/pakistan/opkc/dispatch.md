## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** The main complex — the "Jinnah Terminal," completed 1992 — connects via corridors to the **Jinnah East Satellite** (international operations, 8 gates/jetbridges) and the **Jinnah West Satellite** (domestic operations, 8 gates/jetbridges, with some international flexibility through gate reassignment). Separately, the historic **Terminal 1** (1938) now houses the Civil Aviation Authority HQ, **Terminal 2** (1978) is dedicated to Hajj operations, and **Terminal 3** (late 1980s) is used for commercial offices — none of the latter three handle scheduled passenger flights today. `[Wikipedia]`
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — terminal/gate assignment for a company outside the field's established carrier groupings would be set by the handling agent/Pakistan Airports Authority (PAA) on a case-by-case basis. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** International arrivals clear customs/immigration at the Jinnah Terminal international side (East Satellite); published hours not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** RWY 07L/25R underwent an ~18-month reconstruction completed **19 Feb 2026**; no terminal-level operational impact identified for current-day planning, but construction-adjacent ground movement/NOTAMs should be checked at the next update cycle. 🟧

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E) stands:** Widebody international carriers operate scheduled service to OPKC (Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad, Saudia, Turkish Airlines, SriLankan and others), confirming Code E parking capability exists at the field; exact stand/pier allocation not confirmed. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not confirmed — no narrow-body-only or size-restricted stand data obtained this pass. 🟧
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Reported at roughly 30 remote parking bays across the field's ~266,000 m² apron (of a total ~42-aircraft parking capacity, 12 via airbridge) `[Wikipedia]` — bussing implications not confirmed.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not confirmed — expect a contact gate as the default assumption for a scheduled international widebody arrival, pending local confirmation. 🟧
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** A 2025 NOTAM directive required **prior coordination before operating in, due to limited aircraft parking space** — cross-ref [Briefing §6](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections; treat parking availability as a genuine, not theoretical, constraint. 🟧

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** No IATA slot-coordination level confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No standing curfew confirmed. However, the field has a **documented recent history of ad hoc, security-driven flight-operations suspension** — a suspension "until midnight" was reported in May 2025 amid India–Pakistan regional tension — and separately, periodic airspace restrictions across the wider India–Pakistan border region have recurred during bilateral tension (documented episodes in 2019 and 2025). Treat this as a genuine, recurring **live-risk pattern rather than a routine schedule constraint**. 🟥
- **Delayed-flight nuances:** Not applicable in the way a routine curfew would apply — the operative constraint here is the possibility of a short-notice, security-driven suspension rather than a fixed nightly window.
- **CTOT / flow control:** No EUROCONTROL-style CTOT/ATFM programme identified for OPKC in reachable sources; the more concrete, documented constraint is the **2025 parking-capacity coordination directive** (§3). 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** Build schedule resilience around the possibility of short-notice security-driven suspension or airspace restriction, and confirm parking/stand availability ahead of any new or infrequent rotation into OPKC given the documented capacity-coordination requirement.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** OPKC is Pakistan's second-busiest airport by total passengers (surpassed by Islamabad in 2024–2025), handling roughly 6.7 million passengers and ~45,300 aircraft movements in FY2024–25 `[Wikipedia, citing Pakistan CAA statistics]`. Busiest routes by weekly frequency include Islamabad, Dubai, Lahore, Jeddah, Sharjah, Muscat, Doha, Abu Dhabi, Istanbul and Bahrain — indicating a strong domestic-trunk and Gulf/Middle-East international skew.
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for a K Global operation — treat as standard international business/leisure/labour-migrant mix pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** OPKC handled roughly 104,000 metric tons of cargo in FY2024–25 `[Wikipedia, citing Pakistan CAA statistics]`; a mix of scheduled belly cargo and dedicated freighter operators (DHL, Turkish Cargo, Qatar Airways Cargo and others) serve the field. Confirm K Global belly-cargo uplift plans against payload at planning. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — OPKC is not a K Global base; connection-bank timing is set by whichever base the flight connects through.
