## 10. Gotchas

- **ILS availability is unconfirmed** — the only reachable reference traces to a 2015-archived extract; do not plan on CAT I ILS minima without a current-AIRAC chart check.
- **RWY 07L/25R's current declared distances are an open question** — publicly reported at ~11,500 ft post-upgrade (completed Feb 2026), but no current-AIRAC table was obtained; do not rely on the older, widely mirrored 10,500 ft figure.
- **Hot-season arrivals** are not a performance concern in the way departures are, but confirm current OAT/wind for approach-speed and go-around performance planning during the Apr–Jun extreme-heat window.
- **Documented history of ad hoc, security-driven flight suspensions and airspace restrictions** during India–Pakistan regional tension — check live security bulletins/NOTAMs before planning an arrival during any period of elevated regional tension, and have a genuine diversion plan (company alternates OMDB, OERK) ready.
- **Parking-capacity coordination requirement** (2025 NOTAM directive) — confirm current stand/apron availability, especially for an unplanned or infrequent arrival.
- **India–Pakistan border GNSS-interference hotspot** — relevant to any RNP-based approach; cross-check DME/DME/IRU per [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
