## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single main passenger terminal building (Concourses/Gates **A–G**, domestic and international co-located but assigned to different parts of the concourse) plus the **SAT-1 (Satellite 1) terminal**, connected to the main terminal by an **Automated People Mover (APM)**. An Airport Rail Link and APM both serve the field; Motorway 7 provides road access.
- **Our operation uses:** International widebody/narrowbody scheduled service would route through the **C/D/E/F/G gate concourses or SAT-1**; domestic-style operations use the **A/B gates**. 🟧 K Global's specific gate/terminal assignment is not confirmed — confirm with handling at planning.
- **International arrivals / immigration hall:** International arrivals clear immigration in the main terminal (or via SAT-1 with APM transfer to the main terminal's immigration hall) — build APM transfer time into any tight connection.
- **Notes:** 🟧 SAT-1 is a comparatively recent addition to the terminal complex; confirm current gate/pier assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan.

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Confirmed Code F (A380-capable) positions are **C3, C7, E4, E8** (main terminal), **S111–S118** (SAT-1), and remote stands **201–203/401–403**. 🟧 K Global's specific contracted stands not confirmed.
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** **Taxiways T8, T9, T10, T11, T12 are prohibited for A380-class (Code F) aircraft** — a wingspan-limited constraint; any Code F movement must route around these. 🟥
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Cargo-designated remote stands cluster around **506–525** in front of the cargo terminal; additional remote positions at 201–203/401–403 are shared with A380 use.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact gate for a scheduled international widebody/narrowbody arrival; confirm with handling if a remote position is assigned.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Ground Control is split by apron sector (East 121.65 / Main 121.75 / West 121.95 MHz) — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections for routing between stands and the runway system.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧 Given the field's stated capacity constraints (movement cap raised from ~68 to ~94/hour only with the 2024 third-runway opening), at least IATA Level 2-style schedule facilitation is plausible but not independently sourced.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None found** — the aerodrome operator, ATS, customs, fuelling, handling and security are all published H24; no night-movement ban identified in reachable sources. 🟩
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not confirmed in reachable sources; treat as a capacity-constrained Asian mega-hub where ATFM-style flow management is plausible during peak/adverse weather, particularly monsoon convective events. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** With no curfew constraint, the primary schedule-integrity risk here is **monsoon-season convective weather** (ground-delay/holding exposure) rather than a slot or curfew wall — build weather buffer into the SW-monsoon-season schedule rather than a late-rotation buffer.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Multiple ground-handling providers are registered at the field (cargo and passenger handling); the specific agent(s) contracted for K Global operations are not confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 15 min / taxi-out 17 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody/narrowbody service times.
- **Services:** Push-back (Ground-Control-cleared, apron-sector-specific frequency), GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services expected as a major-hub-standard provision. Large widebody types have historically required a **towbarless tow tractor** at certain stands — confirm current equipment for the assigned stand.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Monsoon-season ramp delays (rain/lightning ramp holds are common tropical-airport practice), APM/gate-assignment uncertainty if positioned at SAT-1, and apron-sector frequency confusion (three separate Ground frequencies by sector) can each add unplanned turn time.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **SW-monsoon (May–Oct) convective thunderstorms and heavy rainfall** — the field's defining seasonal reliability risk, compounded by the CAT II (not CAT III) approach ceiling (see Briefing §3.4).
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Plausible during monsoon convective events and peak banks at a capacity-constrained mega-hub still bedding in its 2024 third-runway configuration; not independently quantified this pass. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Monsoon-season afternoons/evenings and any period of reduced visibility (CAT II ceiling) carry the highest schedule-risk exposure at this field. 🟧
