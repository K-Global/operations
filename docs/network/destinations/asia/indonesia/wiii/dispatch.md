## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Three passenger terminals — **Terminal 1** (oldest, three sub-terminals, primarily domestic), **Terminal 2** (opposite T1, international and domestic, three sub-terminals), and **Terminal 3** (newest, single large building, international section and domestic section, ~10 international / ~18 domestic gates per tier-4 sources) — plus a separate cargo terminal complex. A free automated **Skytrain** connects T1, T2, T3 and the airport rail-link station, roughly a 10-minute ride end-to-end. 🟧 (terminal-use breakdown is tier-4 sourced, not independently confirmed against a primary operator table).
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — the AVDGS-equipped, wide-body-capable stands (Apron G/J) most plausibly align with Terminal 3's international pier, but this mapping is inferred, not sourced directly. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear immigration in whichever terminal (historically T2, increasingly T3) hosts the arriving international flight; confirm current-day international-hall assignment with handling before finalising a stand/connection plan.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal/gate assignment for our operation is not independently confirmed — confirm with handling ahead of each rotation.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Apron G (stands **G15–G57**) and **Apron J** carry an Advanced Visual Docking Guidance System (Safedock Type 3, laser-scanning) rated for both narrow-body and wide-body aircraft `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.9]` — the likely widebody cluster, but the exact Code E-capable subset within this range is not itemised. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrower Apron A–F and G11–G14 positions (lower PCN, no AVDGS) are not confirmed as Code E-suitable — do not plan a widebody onto them without confirmation.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Remote Apron B, C, D and F are identified in the AIP apron/taxiway table `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.8]`; remote/hardstand use for scheduled widebody international service is not typical — confirm with handling if a contact gate is unavailable.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate for a scheduled international turn at this major hub.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Apron-specific pushback facing-direction/break-away sequences are separately published per stand; the **06/24 crossing-runway** interacts with ground routing between certain stands and the runway system — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** A dated tier-4 industry-coordination directory reports **IATA Level 2** for Jakarta CGK, with slot coordination historically performed by **Garuda Indonesia Schedule Planning** ("JKTSPGA") — **not independently reconfirmed for the current season**; treat as a caution rather than a confirmed current regime. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No curfew identified — ATS, AD operating hours, customs, fuelling, handling and security are all published as **H24** `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.3]`. 🟩
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not independently confirmed; given the field's high traffic density and a crossing third runway, some ATFM-style flow management at peak times is plausible but not sourced this pass. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** With no hard curfew, the main commercial guardrail here is the **reported Level 2 slot coordination** (if still current) rather than a night-ban risk — build schedule margin around confirmed slot times pending reconfirmation.

- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services expected at a major international hub stand (standard provision, not individually itemised in reachable sources).
