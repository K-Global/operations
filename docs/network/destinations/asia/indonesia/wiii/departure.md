## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** An extensive N/S/E/W-designated parallel and connector taxiway system feeds each runway from the apron system; confirm the exact taxi routing with Ground/Apron on the day. Departing aircraft change to the monitoring Tower frequency at a published sign-box point on TWY SP2/NP2 `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.20]`.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟧 No dedicated named hot-spot chart section was found in the reachable AIP extract — verify current AIRAC. The practical caution points are the **apron-specific pushback facing-direction/break-away sequences** (published per stand/apron) and the **06/24 crossing-runway** interaction with the parallel-runway taxi system.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** A departure routed to or interacting with **06/24** should expect explicit crossing/hold-short instructions where it intersects the parallel-runway taxi system.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** Low-visibility taxi procedure exists — aircraft are required to report a runway vacated only once the entire aircraft is beyond the relevant runway-holding position, and aircraft at taxiway intersections must not hold closer than the position limit defined by a clearance bar, stop bar or intersection marking `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.20.2.4]`. Follow-me availability not confirmed. 🟧

## 5. Initial climb, speed & noise-abatement departure

- **Initial climb / speed:** Departing aircraft are assigned an **interim FL280/FL290** climb clearance under a semi-circular level-assignment methodology, pending the final cruise level from Jakarta ACC `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.20.2.2]` — a workload item distinct from a direct-to-cruise-level clearance. 250 KIAS below FL100 (ICAO norm) assumed absent a local override.
- **Noise-abatement departure procedure (NADP):** None published — Noise Abatement Procedures section is "Reserved" `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.21]`. 🟩
- **Early turn / altitude constraints:** Published per SID/RNAV-1 transition — verify constraints on the current chart. 🟧

## 8. Clearance / CTOT & departure frequency sequence

- **Clearance delivery:** Pilots are requested to call **Soekarno-Hatta Clearance Delivery** for ATC clearance **25 minutes before pushback** relative to EOBT, to allow departure data processing `[AIP AD 2 WIII 2.20.2.2]`. Clearance may be cancelled if the aircraft is unable to push back within 15 minutes past EOBT, returns to the apron, or develops a technical problem.
- **Frequency sequence:** **Delivery (125.15 / 121.95) → Ground (121.75 / 121.6) → Tower (120.25 / 118.2, runway-dependent) → Jakarta Radar/Director/Arrival (124.35, 130.1, 127.90, 119.75, 123.75, 125.45 — sector- and time-of-day-dependent).** Take the assigned frequency and confirm current chart. (Frequencies from Briefing §8.)
- **CTOT / slot handling:** Dated tier-4 source reports **IATA Level 2** slot coordination; ATFM/CTOT specifics for WIII not independently sourced. 🟧 Comply with any assigned CTOT/EDCT-equivalent.

## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** The 3,600 m / 3,660 m parallel-runway pair is non-limiting for any K Global type. **RWY 06/24** (~3,000 m, tier-4 sourced) has not been independently confirmed as suitable for widebody departures — treat as narrowbody-only pending confirmation. 🟧
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Non-issue at 34 ft, equatorial climate — no hot-and-high penalty; note the AIP's reference temperature of 32°C is itself a signal of consistently high ambient temperature, relevant to engine/APU bleed-air planning even without a field-elevation performance penalty.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** Monsoon-season (roughly Nov–Mar) heavy convective rainfall is the relevant seasonal consideration — no specific contamination/braking-action data confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — no field-length or field-elevation performance penalty confirmed for any K Global type on the main parallel-runway pair at WIII.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Expect an interim FL280/FL290 climb clearance**, not a direct clearance to cruise level, pending Jakarta ACC's final-level assignment.
- **RWY 06/24 crosses the parallel runway pair** — brief for a possible runway-crossing clearance during cross-runway operations, and treat it as unconfirmed for widebody use.
- **Apron-specific pushback choreography** (facing-direction and break-away point) varies by stand — confirm the correct sequence rather than assuming a standard straight-back push.
- **No noise-abatement departure procedure is published** — do not assume a mandated NADP/noise routing; fly the charted SID as cleared.
- **Jakarta Radar/Director/Arrival frequencies split by time-of-day (UTC)**, not just by geography — confirm the active sector/frequency on the day.
- **Monsoon-season convective activity (Nov–Mar) with AIP-listed windshear-alerting equipment (LLWAS)** — treat convective windshear as a real, recognised departure hazard in season.
