## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat Pearl River Delta coastal/reclaimed terrain at 13 ft AMSL — no close-in high ground relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Pearl River Delta five-airport cluster congestion** (ZGSZ, [ZGGG](../zggg/index.md), VHHH, VMMC, ZGSD) — four of these within roughly 50 km — drives sustained vectoring and cross-border coordination constraints, particularly given ZGSZ's proximity (≈20 NM) to Hong Kong (VHHH) airspace under a different aeronautical authority (Hong Kong CAD, not mainland CAAC). The **newly-commissioned third runway (16R/34L)** and the field's eight-mode flexible-configuration logic mean crews should expect the traffic pattern here to still be settling into steady-state operation.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 High density under radar control; approach service is labelled **"Zhuhai Approach"** in the only reachable public frequency data — an unusual naming not independently reconciled against a primary source (see [Briefing §8/§18](index.md)). En-route inbound handoff is from **Guangzhou (ZGZU) ACC/FIR** — see the [Asia airspace brief](../../../../airspace/asia.md) and the [Hong Kong (VHHK) FIR brief](../../../../airspace/fir/asia/hong-kong-vhhk.md) for the cross-border boundary detail.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat delta site) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around drops you back into a very high-density, multi-airport Pearl River Delta radar environment with active cross-border (Hong Kong) coordination constraints — expect extended vectoring and possible holding before re-sequencing, especially during a typhoon-adjacent weather event or a winter fog event.
- **Go-around traps:** Traffic/config awareness given the field's flexible eight-mode runway logic and the still-settling third runway; confirm current runway assignment before assuming the miss returns you to the same approach.

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** Not published for any runway — physical lengths are 15/33 3,400 m, 16L/34R 3,800 m, 16R/34L 3,600 m, but no declared-distance table was found in reachable sources (see [Briefing §7](index.md)). 🟧 Treat as non-limiting for any K Global type based on physical length alone, but do not treat this as a confirmed declared distance.
- **Braking / vacate:** Rapid-exit taxiway detail not confirmed in reachable sources — confirm with Ground/Apron on the day. 🟧
- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟥 This field's one significant historical accident — **China Southern Airlines Flight 3456** (8 May 1997) — was a weather-driven runway-excursion event: a bounced first touchdown in severe convective weather (torrential rain, strong crosswind, windshear, very poor visibility), a go-around, and a high-speed second-approach contact that ran the aircraft off the runway. It carries no current operational restriction but is a concrete, field-specific reminder to treat convective/typhoon-adjacent landing weather with real caution rather than routine — see [`OM E — Adverse Weather and Windshear`](../../../../../flight-ops/adverse-weather-and-windshear.md).

## 10. Gotchas

- **Runway/approach data for the newly-commissioned third runway (16R/34L, opened 2025-11-29) should be treated as still-settling** — verify current NOTAM/AIRAC status before planning an arrival to it.
- **Runway designators are not fully reconciled across public sources** — do not rely on this page's runway references as chart-grade; cross-check the current AIRAC.
- **Active typhoon-season exposure (~May–Nov, peak Jul–Sep)** — this field has taken direct, large-scale disruption from a tracked South China Sea typhoon within the past week of this brief's build; brief a holding/diversion contingency for any arrival planned in season.
- **Winter early-morning fog risk (roughly 02:00–08:00 local)** is the field's other seasonal signature hazard — brief accordingly for any overnight/first-wave arrival in the Nov–Mar window.
- **Historical weather-driven runway-excursion accident (1997)** at this field underlines that convective/typhoon-adjacent landing weather deserves real caution, not routine handling (§7).
- **Pearl River Delta mega-TMA and proximity to Hong Kong airspace** — expect sustained vectoring and cross-border coordination constraints on arrival even outside weather disruption.
- **"Zhuhai Approach" naming for the field's approach service is unusual and unconfirmed** — do not assume this reflects the current AIRAC-published facility name without verification.
