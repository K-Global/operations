## 10. Gotchas

- **Runway/taxiway data for the newly-commissioned third runway (16R/34L, opened 2025-11-29) should be treated as still-settling** — verify current NOTAM/AIRAC status before assuming full parity with the two established runways.
- **Runway designators are not fully reconciled across public sources** (15/33 vs 16L/34R vs 16R/34L) — do not rely on this page's runway references as chart-grade; cross-check the current AIRAC.
- **Active typhoon-season exposure (~May–Nov, peak Jul–Sep)** — this field has taken direct large-scale disruption from a tracked South China Sea typhoon within the past week of this brief's build; expect CAAC-directed ground stops/flow restriction with little notice in season.
- **Winter early-morning fog risk (roughly 02:00–08:00 local)** — build contingency into any first-wave departure in the Nov–Mar window.
- **Pearl River Delta mega-TMA departure environment** — expect sustained vectoring and cross-border (Hong Kong) coordination constraints even outside weather disruption; do not expect a quiet, low-workload departure sequence.
- **"Zhuhai Approach" naming for the field's approach service is unusual and unconfirmed** — do not assume this reflects the current AIRAC-published facility name without verification.
