## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** Not confirmed in reachable research pass — pull the current-AIRAC STAR list at planning. 🟧
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** No fixed rule — CAAC's Middle-South Regional Air Traffic Management Bureau (MS-ATMB) dynamically allocates the active runway set using Stand-Based and Transfer-Point-Based strategies (Briefing §3.2); the "01x" runway set is used for northbound operations and the "19x/20x" set for southbound operations, broadly consistent with the region's monsoon-driven wind reversal — a **regional-climatology inference, not an ZGGG-specific sourced wind rule**. 🟧
- **Transition to approach:** Expect vectoring onto whichever runway ATC assigns given the field's continuously-reallocated five-runway configuration; confirm the charted transition.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** Published speed/altitude gates are pointers only — verify chart; expect additional flow-speed control given the Pearl River Delta's high-density, multi-airport traffic environment (Briefing §3.2).

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat Pearl River Delta alluvial plain at 50 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 The field's **five-runway, dynamically-reallocated configuration** (four near-parallel N–S runways plus the separately-aligned 03/21) sustains a continuously changing traffic and runway-use picture; the **RWY 02R/20L and 01L/19R ILS-protected-area interactions with the Terminal 3 taxiway system** (Briefing §3.2) are a recognised complexity when those runways run simultaneous operations. Wake-turbulence spacing between closely-spaced parallel/near-parallel runways is a standing consideration during dependent operations. 🟧
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 Very high density given ZGGG's position inside the **Pearl River Delta's five-airport, congested multi-airport TMA** (Guangzhou/Shenzhen/Hong Kong/Macau/Zhuhai — Briefing §3.2) under CAAC's strict, centralised flow-control regime — brief for re-sequencing, extended vectoring and possible holding, especially during peak banks or adverse weather.

## 8. Taxi-in routing to terminal & hot spots

- **Typical taxi-in by runway → terminal:** After landing on the ATC-assigned runway, taxi in to Terminal 2 or Terminal 3 per Ground/Apron assignment; VAMSYS mirror gives a planning taxi-in time of **14 min** `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]`.

Taxi with vigilance, especially during peak-traffic periods.

## 10. Gotchas

- **No single fixed landing runway** — the five-runway field runs a dynamically-allocated mixed mode; confirm the assigned runway/config with ATC rather than assuming a default.
- **Typhoon season (~May–Nov, peak Jul–Sep) can force sudden diversion/holding or a full operational suspension** — brief the diversion contingency (VHHH Hong Kong Intl or ZSPD Shanghai Pudong per the company alternate set) and monitor tropical-cyclone advisories closely in season.
- **Pearl River Delta multi-airport TMA congestion** (Guangzhou/Shenzhen/Hong Kong/Macau/Zhuhai) sustains continuous high traffic density — expect vectoring/holding, especially in marginal weather or during a bank.
- **RWY 02R/20L and RWY 01L/19R's ILS-protected-area interactions with the Terminal 3 taxiway system** remain a ground-workload factor on taxi-in during simultaneous-operations periods.
- **The fifth runway (03/21) is separately aligned from the four near-parallel N–S runways** — do not assume it shares the same approach/hot-spot picture as the older runway set; approach infrastructure for it is unconfirmed in this build.
- **No curfew exists at this field** — a late arrival is not a diversion-forcing movement restriction here (unlike several other network destinations), though CAAC ATFM/flow measures can still apply.
