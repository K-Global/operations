## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 The field itself sits in the flat Chengdu Basin — no close-in high terrain on a normal arrival. The region's defining terrain (Hengduan Shan/Tibetan Plateau edge) lies well to the west/northwest and is a factor for westbound routing/diversion, shared with [ZUUU](../zuuu/index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Three-runway crossing geometry** (11/29 crosses the 01/19–02/20 N–S pair) — a genuinely unusual layout; confirm dependent/independent approach procedures on the current chart before assuming simultaneous operations are unrestricted.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 A fast-growing new major gateway intended to absorb most of Chengdu's international traffic — expect increasing traffic density; field-specific sequencing data not confirmed.
