## 10. Gotchas

- **Two parallel runways differ in surface and width** (02L/20R asphalt 45 m vs 02R/20L concrete 60 m) — confirm the assigned runway/surface before planning.
- **Sichuan Basin haze/fog** can reduce visibility with little warning in season — check current LVP status before planning a departure.
- **Post-2021 ZUUU↔ZUTF traffic rebalancing** means route/terminal/gate assumptions should be reconfirmed each planning cycle.
- Most tactical departure data (SIDs, minima, frequencies) is **not confirmed from a primary AIP source this pass** — treat this page as a planning skeleton, not chart-equivalent.
