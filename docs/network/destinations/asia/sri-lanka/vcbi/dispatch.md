## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** (opened 1967) is the current sole international terminal, 12 gates, arrival/departure areas separated horizontally. **Terminal 2** (international, in delayed construction — see §Notes) is planned with 8 gates/14 boarding bridges and a dedicated Airbus A380 gate, arrivals/departures separated vertically. **Terminal 3** (opened November 2012) handles domestic flights only, horizontally separated. A dedicated Cargo Terminal (opened October 2009) handles cargo, separately from passenger operations.
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal 1 by default, as it is currently the field's only operating international terminal — exact gate/pier assignment for a K Global operation not confirmed. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** International arrivals clear immigration in Terminal 1; exact desk hours not independently confirmed (§8).
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal 2 (Phase II expansion, JICA-funded) has experienced repeated delay since 2007; construction mobilisation was most recently reported expected around mid-2026, with a currently-projected 2028 completion. Public reporting also indicates BIA has hit a terminal/space capacity limit, with additional weekly international frequency requests reportedly denied for the 2025 winter season — a genuine near-term commercial constraint distinct from Terminal 2's eventual capacity relief.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Five aprons are documented: **Apron Alpha** (9 bays: 5 remote + 4 bridge-equipped; up to 4× Boeing 747-class + 5× A330-200-class simultaneously), **Apron Bravo** (8 bays: 4 remote + 4 bridge-equipped; 8 wide-body simultaneously), **Apron Charlie** (8 remote bays; 8 wide-body simultaneously; **the only apron currently A380-capable**, used by Emirates A380 operations at least three times), **Apron Delta** (4 narrow-body bays), **Apron Echo** (newest, 17 bays, opened 25 November 2021). Exact stand/pier assignment for our operation not confirmed. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Apron Delta's 4 bays are narrow-body-configured — do not plan a wide-body K Global type onto them.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Confirmed to exist across Alpha (5 of 9), Bravo (4 of 8) and the whole of Charlie (8 remote); bussing implications for pax not confirmed. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not confirmed — expect either, pending local gate assignment.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** No taxiway wingspan/hot-spot data confirmed this pass — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** No IATA slot-coordination level confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No formal aircraft-movement curfew identified. A publicly reported **terminal visitor-access restriction (22:00–24:00, non-passengers)** exists for congestion management inside the terminal, separate from any aircraft-movement rule. 🟧
- **Capacity constraint:** BIA has publicly reported denying additional weekly international frequency requests for the 2025 winter season, citing space scarcity — a genuine scheduling/commercial constraint distinct from a curfew or a formal slot-coordination level. 🟥
- **CTOT / flow control:** No EUROCONTROL-style CTOT/ATFM program identified for VCBI in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The **single-runway, no-redundancy constraint** (Briefing §3.3/§3.4) combined with the **reported terminal-capacity limit** means any schedule change or new-frequency request at VCBI should assume genuine constraint risk, not open availability.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** VCBI handled approximately **8.8 million passengers and 195,379 metric tonnes of air freight in 2024**, with **55,848 aircraft movements** `[Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka, via Wikipedia, retrieved 2026-07-26]`. The busiest international routes as of early 2026 were reportedly Dubai (DXB), Malé (MLE) and Chennai (MAA).
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for a K Global operation specifically; treat as a standard international leisure/business/diaspora mix pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** A dedicated Cargo Terminal (opened 2009) operates separately from passenger flows; confirm K Global belly-cargo uplift plans against payload at planning. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — VCBI is not a K Global base; connection-bank timing is set by whichever base the flight connects through.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Monsoon convective weather** (thunderstorm cells, May–September and October–November) interacting with the **single-runway, no-redundancy field structure** — a weather cell over the field removes all capacity, unlike a multi-runway field. A secondary, non-weather driver is the **publicly reported terminal/space capacity constraint** (§4/§6), which has reportedly constrained new-frequency growth rather than day-of on-time performance directly.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Not independently confirmed; plausible during peak banks given the reported capacity constraint. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Monsoon-season afternoons/evenings and any single-runway disruption event carry the highest schedule-risk exposure for VCBI operations. 🟧
