## 10. Gotchas

- **Single runway, no parallel/second runway** — do not assume a runway-swap option is available if the assigned end becomes unsuitable; the only recourse is holding or diversion.
- **Confirm the Approach frequency (120.9 vs 132.4 MHz)** before relying on either published figure.
- **Monsoon-season convective weather** — expect the possibility of thunderstorm cells over the field May–September and October–November; brief a genuine diversion contingency (VOMM Chennai or VABB Mumbai, both cross-country from Sri Lanka).
- **No confirmed ILS** — treat any low-visibility arrival planning as unconfirmed pending current-AIRAC verification; do not assume CAT I/II/III capability exists at this field.
- **Reported terminal/capacity constraint** — a late arrival into a congested gate environment is a plausible ground-flow risk given VCBI's publicly reported space limitations.
