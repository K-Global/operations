## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Four passenger terminals (T1–T4) plus the Jewel Changi Airport retail/entertainment complex interconnecting T1–T3. Reported gate-code allocation: **Terminal 1** — C/D gates; **Terminal 2** — E/F gates (gate F31 reported as the first A380-ready gate); **Terminal 3** — A/B gates, 28 aerobridge gates of which 8 are A380-capable; **Terminal 4** — G gates, 4 widebody stands. A fifth terminal (**Terminal 5**) is under construction as part of the wider Changi East programme, targeted for the mid-2030s. 🟧 Gate-code data is aggregator-sourced, not an official CAG map.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed in reachable sources — confirm current gate/terminal assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan. 🟧
- **International arrivals hall:** As an all-international gateway, every terminal is equipped with immigration-processing facilities; no domestic hall exists at Changi.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal programme is mid-expansion (Terminal 5 under construction, groundbreaking 14 May 2025, targeted mid-2030s) alongside the wider Changi East third-runway conversion — confirm current gate/concourse assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan.

- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrowbody-oriented gates in any terminal are not sized for Code E/F — do not plan a widebody onto them; specific gate-by-gate data not confirmed.

- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate for a scheduled home-hub turn, pending confirmation.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Ongoing Changi East construction activity (RWY 02C/20C and the RWY 02R/20L conversion) has driven multiple historical taxiway/stand-adjacent NOTAM closures — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Not confirmed — no evidence found of formal IATA Level 2/3 coordination at Changi in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None.** Changi has historically operated as a 24-hour, no-curfew gateway with no night movement ban identified in reachable sources. 🟩
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not confirmed; airspace-density-driven flow management (rather than curfew/slot administration) is the more likely constraint given the dense multi-airport TMA — see [Briefing §3.2](index.md). 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** With no curfew and no confirmed slot regime, the dominant scheduling guardrail at this field is **airspace/traffic density and monsoon-season weather** (Sumatra squalls, haze) rather than administrative movement limits.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Ground handling at Changi is split between **SATS** (majority market share, historically ~70%) and **dnata Singapore** (formerly CIAS); the specific agent contracted for our operation is not confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 12 min / taxi-out 15 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody service times.
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services all expected at a home-hub Code E/F stand (standard major-hub provision); no de-icing service required given the equatorial climate.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Gate availability during the terminal-expansion/construction period (T5 build, Changi East works), and any haze-season or Sumatra-squall-driven ground stops, are the principal sources of unplanned turn-time risk.

## 7. Fuel

- **Supplier / into-plane:** Jet A-1 via the **Changi Airport Fuel Hydrant Installation (CAFHI)** joint venture — members reported as Air BP, TotalEnergies (formerly Air Total), Chevron, ExxonMobil, PetroChina and Shell.
- **Uplift availability & hours:** H24 assumed as a major hub; not independently confirmed. 🟧
- **Price / tankering angle:** As the home base, WSSS is typically the **tankering reference point** rather than a tanker-out destination — assess price differentials to outstations per leg. See [`OM E — Fuel Policy`](../../../../../flight-ops/fuel-policy.md).
- **Quality / freeze notes:** Standard Jet A-1; equatorial ambient temperatures mean no cold-soak/fuel-freeze consideration specific to the WSSS uplift itself — relevant only to the outbound long-haul/high-latitude leg profile.

## 8. Customs / immigration & PoE for pax

- **Port of Entry:** Yes — every terminal (T1–T4) carries international immigration facilities; Changi has no domestic traffic.
- **CBP-equivalent / immigration hours:** H24, consistent with the field's no-curfew operating model. 🟩
- **Pre-clearance / visa-transit notes:** Standard international entry per nationality; Singapore operates a widely used visa-free/visa-on-arrival regime for many nationalities — specific transit-visa exceptions not detailed here.
- **Late/overnight/diversion caveat:** No curfew-driven diversion risk exists at WSSS (unlike curfew-bound hubs elsewhere in the network); the more relevant late/overnight caveat is haze-season or squall-driven ground stops rather than an administrative movement ban. 🟩

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Sumatra squalls** (sudden predawn/early-morning wind shear, Apr–Nov) and **transboundary haze** (visibility reduction, Aug–Oct) are the field's principal weather-driven reliability risks; the **dense multi-airport TMA** (Changi/Seletar/Paya Lebar/Tengah) is the standing traffic-driven risk. 🟧
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Not independently confirmed; expected to be airspace-density-driven rather than administrative (no confirmed slot/CTOT regime). 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Haze-season days (Aug–Oct) and any predawn Sumatra-squall passage carry the highest weather-driven schedule-risk exposure at the home hub. 🟧

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Not confirmed in reachable sources — Changi's arrival/departure corridors run predominantly over water, which structurally limits residential noise exposure relative to land-locked hubs elsewhere in the network; no per-movement noise-charge structure identified. 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** **Not applicable** — no curfew exists at WSSS.
- **Scheduling guardrails:** With no curfew, the operative scheduling guardrails at this field are **haze-season and Sumatra-squall weather buffers** and the general workload of operating inside a dense multi-airport TMA, rather than a movement-restriction cutoff.
