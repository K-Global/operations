## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** (opened 1998) is the original passenger terminal, extended by the **Midfield Concourse** (opened 2015, Code E/F-capable, linked by automated people mover). **Terminal 2** has been closed for passenger departures since 2015 pending redevelopment; its departure facilities are scheduled to **reopen in May 2026** as part of the airport's move toward its longer-term capacity targets alongside the Three-Runway System.
- **Our operation uses:** Long-haul widebody service is expected to route through the **Midfield Concourse** or **Terminal 1** contact stands; confirm current gate/concourse assignment with handling given the Terminal 2 reopening is a live 2026 transition. 🟧
- **International arrivals / immigration hall:** Terminal 1 houses the main international arrivals immigration and customs hall; a transit passenger lounge and bonded transhipment storage are both confirmed available `[CAD AIP AD 2 VHHH 2.5]`.
- **Notes:** 🟧 Terminal programme is **mid-transition in 2026** (Terminal 2 departure-facility reopening) — confirm current gate/concourse assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan.

---

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** **Midfield Concourse** (opened 2015, purpose-built Code E/F gates) is the primary widebody cluster; Terminal 1 contact stands also handle widebody long-haul traffic. Exact stand count/allocation for our operation not confirmed. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrowbody-oriented Terminal 1 gates are not sized for Code E/F — do not plan a widebody onto them.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** The field publishes distinct North/South, West, Cargo, Maintenance and Business Aviation Centre (BAC) aprons `[CAD AIP AD 2 VHHH 2.8]`; remote/hardstand use for scheduled widebody international service is atypical — confirm with handling if a contact gate is unavailable.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate for a scheduled home-hub turn.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** The **Sky Bridge** underpass over Taxilane B7 near Stand N12/R16 (28 m height clearance) affects ground routing in that area — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** No formal IATA Level 1/2/3 coordination confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧 AAHK/CAD instead operate an **Airport Collaborative Decision Making (ACDM)** system with **Target Start-up Approval Times (TSAT)** for ground-flow management, particularly at high-demand periods and scheduled events.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None published — H24 operation** 🟩. A daily **noise-mitigation period (1500–2300 UTC / 2300–0700 HKT)** places the South Runway on standby where conditions permit — a runway-assignment practice, not a movement restriction (see Briefing §12).
- **CTOT / flow control:** ACDM/TSAT flow management applies during peak-demand periods; individual event-specific CTOTs are used for high-traffic periods 🟧 (verify current practice).
- **Commercial impact:** The **H24, no-curfew status is a genuine scheduling advantage** — red-eye and long-haul rotations are not curfew-constrained at the home hub. The operative commercial guardrail here is **typhoon-season risk** (a T8+ signal grounds all flights outright) rather than a curfew or slot ceiling.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Multiple third-party ground handlers operate at HKIA; the specific handler(s) contracted for our operation are not confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 15 min / taxi-out 18 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody service times.
- **Services:** Fixed 960V/400Hz ground power confirmed at all main apron and cargo stands `[CAD AIP AD 2 VHHH 2.4]`; push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services all expected at a home-hub Code E/F stand.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Gate availability during the Terminal 2 reopening transition (2026), Lantau-windshear-driven go-around/holding knock-on delay, and typhoon-season ground stops can each add unplanned turn time.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** As the home hub, VHHH anchors year-round Asia-Pacific and long-haul intercontinental demand across the K Global network; Lunar New Year and the northern-hemisphere summer are recognised regional leisure-travel peaks in the wider Hong Kong market — general climatology, not independently sourced K Global data. 🟧
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for VHHH specifically — treat as standard East Asian hub business/leisure mix pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** Hong Kong is one of the world's busiest air-cargo gateways; the field publishes dedicated cargo-apron and cargo-handling facilities (up to 4,500 kg handling per position) `[CAD AIP AD 2 VHHH 2.4]`. Confirm K Global belly-cargo uplift plans against payload at planning. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** As the base, VHHH sets the master connection-bank timing for the network — cross-ref the route register for inbound/outbound wave structure.

- **Port of Entry:** Yes — international arrivals hall in **Terminal 1**.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **Typhoon-season ground stops** (Typhoon Signal No. 8 and above — a complete flight suspension, not a partial capacity cut) and **Lantau-terrain windshear/turbulence-driven go-arounds**.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** High during named tropical cyclone passages (~May–Nov, peak Jul–Sep); ACDM/TSAT flow management applies during peak-demand periods generally. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Typhoon season (summer/early autumn) carries the highest schedule-risk exposure at the home hub; the 2026 Terminal 2 reopening transition adds a secondary gate-assignment-uncertainty risk. 🟧

---

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Not confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** Not applicable — there is no hard curfew at VHHH; the South Runway noise-standby period (Briefing §12) is an ATC/runway-assignment practice, not a chargeable infringement regime.
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Build schedule buffer for **typhoon-season complete ground stops** (T8+ suspends all operations with no partial-capacity fallback) rather than for a curfew; monitor the Hong Kong Observatory Tropical Cyclone Warning Signal well ahead of an approaching system.
