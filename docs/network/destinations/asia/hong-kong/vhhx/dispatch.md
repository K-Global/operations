## 2. Terminals & concourses

Not applicable — closed aerodrome. The historical linear passenger terminal (with attached car park and eight boarding gates) was demolished in the mid-2000s after operating from 1962. The site now hosts government offices, retail, recreational facilities and, more recently, cruise-terminal, sports-park and residential redevelopment — see [VHHX — Briefing §13](index.md) for orientation context only (Wikipedia — Kai Tak Airport, retrieved 2026-07-26).

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Not applicable — closed aerodrome.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** Not applicable today. Historically, a night curfew of approximately **2330–0630 local** constrained operations — a heritage fact only, retained for context, not a current planning input.

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

Not applicable — closed aerodrome. Historically a night curfew (§4) and building-height/noise concerns around the field drove the decision to relocate Hong Kong's international airport to Chek Lap Kok; those pressures have no current commercial bearing on VHHX itself.
