## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From Terminal 3, Ground/Apron routes toward the assigned departure runway per the active four-runway configuration; confirm the exact taxi with Apron/Ground on the day. 🟧
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 **Eastern Link taxiways** — the AIP specifically cautions extra pilot vigilance entering these, with correct taxiway identification and accurate read-back of taxi instructions called out explicitly. The **closely-spaced parallel runway pair (11L/29R & 11R/29L)** is the other AIP-flagged complex ground zone — expect runway-crossing instructions and sequencing discipline here, especially when a departure is routed to cross an active parallel.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** Any departure routed to or crossing the closely-spaced parallel pair should expect explicit crossing/hold-short instructions; the AIP specifically flags crossing-clearance caution on RWY 11L/29R after a landing on 11R/29L.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** Winter dense-fog conditions will require SMGCS-consistent low-visibility taxi procedures; follow-me availability and specific low-vis taxi routing not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧

The AIP's obstacle table (AD 2.10) catalogues numerous close-in urban obstacles around all four runway sectors — buildings, water tanks, trees, light/electric poles and (near the RWY 09/27 sector specifically) unlit metro electric-traction overhead structures reaching up to ~855–960 ft AMSL (roughly 180 ft AGL). ## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** Non-limiting for any K Global type on the main runways (up to 4,430 m on 11R/29L, 4,400 m on 11L/29R); RWY 09/27 (2,813 m) and RWY 10/28 (3,810 m) are also ample for typical departure weights. Mind that RWY 09/27 is restricted to VVIP/state/defence/scheduled-airline traffic only (Briefing §5).
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Field elevation (778 ft) is not hot-and-high, but the AIP's reference temperature of **41.0°C** is a genuine warm-day performance consideration for pre-monsoon-season (Apr–Jun) departures — factor into takeoff performance planning on hot afternoons.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** No snow/ice season; monsoon-season (Jul–Sep) rainfall is the relevant wet-runway consideration — no VIDP-specific contamination/braking-action data confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — no field-length or field-elevation performance penalty identified for any K Global type at VIDP.

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 09/27 is restricted-use** — VVIP/state/defence aircraft and scheduled civil flights only; do not plan a non-scheduled/positioning departure from this runway.
- **Closely-spaced parallel runways 11L/29R and 11R/29L** — expect explicit crossing-clearance instructions; the AIP specifically flags caution here, plus the "Land, Vacate, Hold and Proceed" principle after a landing on 11R/29L that can affect departure sequencing on the parallel.
- **Eastern Link taxiways** — AIP-flagged for extra caution on taxiway identification and read-back accuracy.
- **Winter dense-fog season (Dec–Jan, sometimes Nov–Feb)** can trigger LVTO conditions with limited warning — check current LVP/fog status before a winter-season departure.
- **86-movements/hour capacity ceiling** is a real, AIP-published flow constraint — expect sequencing delay at busy banks even in good weather, and materially more during a fog event.
- **Large threshold displacements** exist on the arrival side (RWY 27, 11R, 29L) — not a departure-runway-length issue on their reciprocal ends, but confirm which threshold is in use if a late runway-direction change occurs.
