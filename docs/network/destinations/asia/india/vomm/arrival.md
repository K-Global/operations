## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** Not confirmed in reachable research pass — pull the current-AIRAC STAR list at planning. 🟧
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** Wind determines the 07-vs-25 choice on the primary pair; Chennai Approach/Radar assigns the runway and any 12/30 supplemental use in day/VMC per current cross-runway operating practice (see Briefing §3.2).
- **Transition to approach:** Expect a VOR(MMV)/radar-vectored transition onto the ILS for 07/25, or a VOR(DME)-based approach for 12/30 in day/VMC; verify the charted transition.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** Published speed/altitude gates are pointers only — verify chart.

## 3. Descent & energy plan

- **Top-of-descent trigger:** Standard descent-profile planning; plan TOD to meet the first STAR/approach altitude gate. Minimum radar vectoring altitude is **1,800 ft inside 10 NM, 2,300 ft 10–25 NM** for all sectors — a useful planning reference for the approach environment.
- **Speed control:** Standard ICAO 250 KIAS below FL100 assumed — confirm current chart. 🟧
- **Altitude constraints:** The published surveillance-radar-approach profile shows a nominal **2,300 ft at the IF (~5.5 NM), 1,800 ft at the FAF (~2 NM)** on all four runway ends, descending at roughly a 3° (5.27%) gradient — a pointer only, verify the current chart.
- **Energy traps:** Late runway/config changes (07↔25, or a late call to use 12/30) ahead of a wind shift; holding at the MMV VOR (1-minute right-hand pattern, inbound track 260°M, minimum holding altitude 3,000 ft) if resequencing is required, particularly during an NE-monsoon convective/heavy-rain event.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 Flat coastal site at 52 ft AMSL — no high terrain. The real hazard is the AIP-documented **close-in obstacle field** near the RWY 07/25 approach paths (trees, poles, hoardings, walls, an overhead water tank to ~150 ft AMSL) and the AIP's own note that the **new ATC tower penetrates the Obstacle Limitation Surface**. Fly the charted vertical profile precisely, especially on a go-around. Cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 No CAT II/III capability means marginal-visibility events (heavy monsoon rain, haze) can force a hold/diversion decision rather than a lower-minima recovery. Cross-runway (07/25 × 12/30) traffic during daytime peaks adds workload and possible wake/sequencing considerations near the runway intersection.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Chennai is the 5th-busiest airport in India by movements; expect vectoring/sequencing under Chennai Radar/Approach, particularly during peak banks or when weather compresses the arrival stream (Oct–Dec).

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Terrain is not the driver (flat coastal site). Published missed-approach guidance (pointer only, verify current chart): **RWY 07** climb straight ahead to 2,300 ft then climbing turn left to the MMV VOR; **RWY 25** climb straight ahead to 2,300 ft then climbing turn right to the MMV VOR; **RWY 12** climb heading 110° to 2,300 ft then climbing turn left to the MMV VOR; **RWY 30** climb straight ahead to 2,300 ft then climbing turn right to the MMV VOR — in each case joining the hold at 3,000 ft over MMV (1-minute right-hand pattern, inbound track 260°M).
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around returns you to a busy radar environment at a top-5 Indian hub running cross-runway operations — expect vectoring/holding before re-sequencing, especially during an NE-monsoon weather event.
- **Go-around traps:** The AIP-documented close-in obstacle field near the RWY 07/25 extended centrelines (§5) is the relevant low-level watch-item on the miss, not terrain; fly the published climb gradient/turn precisely.

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** RWY 07 and RWY 25 — **3,658 m** each, ample for any K Global type. RWY 12 carries a **780 m displaced threshold, cutting LDA to 2,110 m** (day/VFR only); RWY 30 LDA is 2,680 m (day/VFR only). Cross-ref [Briefing §7](index.md).
- **Braking / vacate:** A rapid-exit taxiway was added on RWY 07 in May 2023 (approx. 1,831 m from threshold), reducing runway-occupancy time as part of the field's capacity-increase program; specific rapid-exit provision on RWY 25/12/30 not confirmed. 🟧
- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟥 **NE-monsoon downpours (Oct–Dec) and Bay-of-Bengal cyclone activity** are this field's demonstrated contamination/waterlogging risk — brief contaminated-runway braking-action performance specifically for this window, distinct from a generic wet-runway caution.

## 8. Taxi-in routing to terminal & hot spots

- **Typical taxi-in by runway → terminal:** After landing on the assigned 07/25 (or day-VMC 12/30) runway, taxi in to **Terminal 2** via the parallel taxiway system (B/R for the primary runway, N for the secondary, P/Q linking the two); VAMSYS mirror gives a planning taxi-in time of **12 min** `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]`.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 Taxiway **R1**'s junction with taxiway R (code-C and below only, no shoulder), taxiway **E**/adjoining apron taxilane (code-C up to 35.9 m wingspan on certain stand ranges), taxiway **T** (29.2 m wingspan or less) and taxiway **L1** (A320-type only) — confirm these restrictions do not fall on the assigned taxi-in route for a widebody type. Cross-ref [Briefing §13](index.md).
- **Stand/gate notes:** See [Dispatch §3](dispatch.md) — widebody clusters in Apron III (12 bays, 1 A380-rated) and Apron A (11 of 33 bays); **every stand is power-in/push-back only, tow-bar mandatory** — factor this into the taxi-in/turnaround plan.

## 10. Gotchas

- **No CAT II/III capability** — a below-CAT-I weather event (heavy monsoon rain, haze) forces holding/diversion; do not expect an autoland recovery option at this field.
- **RWY 12/30 is never an option outside day/VMC** — do not expect it as an arrival runway at night or in IMC.
- **AIP-documented close-in obstacle field near RWY 07/25** and a **new ATC tower that penetrates the Obstacle Limitation Surface** per the AIP's own note — fly the charted vertical profile precisely on approach and go-around.
- **Every stand is power-in/push-back only** — no self-manoeuvre capability; a tow-bar is mandatory at every stand.
- **Cross-runway operations (07/25 × 12/30) are routine daytime practice** — expect crossing traffic and read-back-heavy ground handling near the runway intersection.
- **Oct–Dec (NE monsoon + Bay-of-Bengal cyclone season) is the field's highest-risk arrival window** — the airport has closed outright twice in the past decade (Dec 2015 week-long closure; Cyclone Michaung, Dec 2023); brief a firm diversion/holding-fuel plan for any sector into this window.
