## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Chennai runs **three operational passenger terminals**: **Terminal 1** and **Terminal 4** handle domestic traffic; the new integrated **Terminal 2** (partial operations from 7 July 2023, replacing the demolished old Terminal 3) handles **international** traffic. A satellite terminal, connected by a 1.5 km tunnel, is under construction as part of the ongoing Phase-2 expansion (targeted for completion in the 2026–2027 window per current public reporting, subject to repeated delays). 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** **Terminal 2 (international)** for K Global's Chennai service, on current public information; confirm the specific pier/gate assignment with handling given the terminal programme is still expanding. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Terminal 2 houses the international arrivals/immigration hall (Bureau of Immigration, Ministry of Home Affairs); confirm connection/transfer time to any domestic (T1/T4) onward leg. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 The terminal programme is **mid-expansion** — Phase-2 works (new satellite terminal, additional gates/check-in) have slipped from an original 2025 target toward late 2026/early 2027 per recent public reporting; confirm current gate/pier assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** **Apron III** (between the two runways) holds 12 wide-body bays including **one A380-rated position**; the main **Apron A** (south of the primary runway, alongside taxiway B) holds 33 bays of which 11 are wide-body-capable; a legacy AIP-listed stand (No. 104) is separately rated A380/B747-8. 🟧 Confirm the current widebody cluster and gate numbering with handling.
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrow-body-oriented positions in **Apron II** (36 narrow-body bays, between the runways) and the remote **Apron I** (8 narrow-body bays, east of the secondary runway) are not sized for our Code E/F types.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Apron I (remote, narrow-body only) and Apron T (3 bays, east end of the secondary runway) are the field's remote positions — not expected to be used for a scheduled K Global widebody turn.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate at Terminal 2 for a scheduled arrival, subject to confirmation given the ongoing terminal expansion.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** 🟥 **Every stand at VOMM is power-in/push-back only — there is no power-out capability, and a tow-bar is mandatory for every aircraft type.** This is a genuine ground-ops planning item, not a generic caution — factor tow-bar/tug availability into turnaround timing. See [Briefing §13](index.md).

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Not confirmed in reachable sources — treat as unconfirmed rather than assume a specific IATA level. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None** — the AIP confirms the aerodrome is available for all-weather operations, day and night, for all flights. The only time-of-day restriction at the field is that the **secondary runway (12/30) is day/VFR-only**; this does not restrict the primary runway pair (07/25) at any hour.
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not independently confirmed for VOMM specifically; as a busy Indian gateway running cross-runway operations at high utilisation, expect the possibility of local ATFM-style flow measures during peak banks or adverse weather (esp. Oct–Dec). 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** With no curfew, the main commercial scheduling constraint is **weather-driven** — build buffer for the Oct–Dec NE-monsoon/cyclone window (§10/§11) rather than for a night-ban-style hard cutoff.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Reported (2025) as **AIASL (Air India Airport Services Ltd)** and **Bird Worldwide Flight Services (BWFS)**, following the Government of India's May 2025 revocation of Çelebi Airport Services India's security clearance (Çelebi had served nine Indian airports including Chennai). Not independently re-confirmed against a primary AAI notice this pass. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 12 min / taxi-out 15 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody service times and the tow-bar/push-back-only constraint (§3).
- **Services:** Push-back (mandatory, tow-bar required — no power-out), GPU, water/lav, catering (TajSATS / Taj Madras Flight Kitchen joint ventures operate catering at the field) and cabin services expected at a Code E/F stand.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Mandatory tow-bar/push-back at every stand adds a dependency not present at power-out-capable fields; the terminal-expansion programme may affect gate availability/routing during the build; NE-monsoon downpours (Oct–Dec) can add ground-handling delay via apron waterlogging.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** Chennai is the **5th-busiest airport in India by passenger traffic and movements** and the **4th-busiest by international/cargo traffic**, handling over 22 million passengers and 0.37 million tonnes of cargo in FY2024–25 — a substantial regional gateway; K Global's own demand pattern at this destination is set by the network schedule rather than third-party data. 🟧
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for K Global's specific Chennai service — treat as standard South-Indian business/leisure/diaspora mix pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** Chennai runs a dedicated air cargo complex (AAICLAS-operated, ~0.3 Mt/year capacity target) and is a hub for Blue Dart Aviation — a genuine cargo gateway; confirm K Global belly-cargo uplift plans against payload at planning. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not independently sourced — cross-ref the route register for K Global's inbound/outbound wave structure at this destination.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** **NE-monsoon rainfall and Bay-of-Bengal cyclone activity, October–December** — the field's demonstrated failure mode is runway/apron waterlogging and, in severe cases, full closure (Dec 2015 week-long closure; Cyclone Michaung, Dec 2023, ~1-day closure with 70 cancellations/30+ diversions). Outside this window the field is not otherwise flagged for a distinct chronic delay driver in reachable sources.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Cross-runway (07/25 × 12/30) operations at a top-5 Indian hub carry routine daytime crossing-clearance workload; no formal ATFM/flow-program data sourced for VOMM specifically. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Oct–Dec carries by far the highest schedule-risk exposure at this field; the rest of the year is not separately characterised in reachable sources. 🟧

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Not confirmed in reachable sources; the only published noise instruction is to minimise reverse-thrust use after landing (Briefing §12). 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** Not applicable — VOMM has no curfew.
