## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From Terminal 2 (Apron III / Apron A), Ground routes toward the assigned runway via the parallel taxiway system — **taxiway B (long) and R** parallel the primary runway (07/25); **taxiway N** parallels the secondary runway (12/30); **taxiways P and Q** link the two runway systems. Confirm the exact taxi with Ground/Apron on the day.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 Taxiway **R1**'s junction with taxiway R is available only to **code-C aircraft and below** (no shoulder provision) — a genuine routing constraint for any of our Code E/F types; taxiway **E** and the adjoining apron taxilane are restricted to code-C aircraft up to 35.9 m wingspan between certain stand ranges; taxiway **T** is restricted to 29.2 m wingspan or less; taxiway **L1** is restricted to A320-type aircraft. Confirm the current wingspan/code-letter restriction map against our widebody types before accepting a route through these taxiways.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** A departure interacting with cross-runway (07/25 × 12/30) operations should expect explicit crossing/hold-short instructions at the runway intersection area — this is routine daytime practice at VOMM (see Briefing §3.2), not an edge case.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** SMGCS/low-vis taxi routing not independently confirmed; follow-me availability not confirmed. 🟧

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** Standard wind-driven selection between RWY 07 and RWY 25 on the primary pair; both ends carry equivalent declared distances (3,658 m) and ILS CAT I.
- **By departure direction:** No specific directional-preference restriction confirmed beyond the wind-driven 07/25 choice.
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** No formal preferential-runway noise program identified; the AIP's sole noise-abatement instruction is to minimise reverse-thrust use **after landing** (arrival-side, not departure-side) — see [Briefing §12](index.md).
- **Interaction with arrivals:** Cross-runway operations (07/25 primary traffic alongside day-VMC use of 12/30) mean a departure may interact with crossing traffic at the runway-intersection area during daytime peak periods — brief for crossing-clearance workload.

## 6. Engine-out / emergency-turn & obstacle considerations

- **Engine-out / emergency-turn procedure:** No published EO/emergency-turn specifics confirmed in reachable sources — apply the operator/type EOSID for the assigned runway. 🟧
- **Close-in / distant obstacles:** 🟧 The AIP's own obstacle chart lists a dense field of trees, poles, hoardings, walls and an overhead water tank ranging roughly 45–150 ft AMSL close to the RWY 07/25 extended centrelines (within roughly 1.5 km of the thresholds), plus a lit GP-antenna mast near the RWY 25 approach and an antenna array near the RWY 07 threshold. Terrain itself is flat/non-factor, but the obstacle field is a real close-in consideration on any early-turn or engine-out departure profile off 07/25 — cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md).

## 9. Performance watch-items for our types

- **Field-length / weight:** RWY 07/25 (3,658 m) is non-limiting for any K Global type. RWY 12/30 is day/VMC-only and should never be planned as a departure runway for a scheduled widebody operation.
- **Density altitude / temperature:** Non-issue at 52 ft, tropical coastal climate — no hot-and-high penalty, though high reference temperature (38.0°C) should still feed standard performance planning.
- **Contamination / wet-runway:** 🟥 **NE-monsoon downpours (Oct–Dec) and Bay-of-Bengal cyclone activity** are the field's demonstrated contamination/waterlogging risk — factor braking-action/contaminated-runway performance planning specifically into this seasonal window. See [`OM E — Adverse Weather and Windshear`](../../../../../flight-ops/adverse-weather-and-windshear.md).
- **Link to OM B:** See [`OM B — Fleet Capability Matrix`](../../../../../fleet/capability-matrix.md) — no field-length or field-elevation performance penalty for any K Global type at VOMM.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Every stand is power-in/push-back only** — a tow-bar is mandatory for all aircraft types; do not plan a self-manoeuvre departure from stand.
- **RWY 12/30 is day/VFR operation only** — never expect or plan an IFR/night departure from the secondary runway.
- **No CAT II/III capability at this field** — a below-CAT-I event forces a ground delay/hold rather than a lower-minima departure option.
- **Cross-runway operations (07/25 × 12/30) are routine daytime practice** — expect crossing-clearance workload and read-back-heavy ground handling, not just an occasional occurrence.
- **Taxiway R1's junction with R, taxiway E, taxiway T and taxiway L1 all carry code-letter/wingspan restrictions** — confirm before accepting a route through them with a widebody type.
- **Oct–Dec is the highest-risk departure window** — NE-monsoon rain and Bay-of-Bengal cyclone activity have twice closed the field outright in the past decade; build schedule/fuel buffer accordingly.
