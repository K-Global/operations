## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single passenger terminal (renovation begun late 2018) serves the field's scheduled passenger carriers (Wizz Air; Transavia and TUI fly Netherlands adding capacity from Feb 2027 during Eindhoven Airport's scheduled closure). A separate dedicated **cargo terminal**, built on the site of the former second runway (07/25, closed and removed, construction begun 2008), supports the field's substantial scheduled all-cargo operation.
- **Our operation uses:** Passenger terminal for any scheduled K Global passenger movement; the cargo terminal is not applicable to a passenger operation. 🟧 Confirm gate/stand assignment with handling.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Customs reported present (hours not independently confirmed).
- **Notes:** Expect a materially busier field environment **February–July 2027**, when Transavia and TUI fly Netherlands temporarily relocate a significant share of their Eindhoven Airport schedule here during EHEH's runway-renovation closure. 🟧

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** **Jet Center** provides general/business-aviation handling from a hangar on the east side of the airport; the specific handler for a scheduled K Global passenger or cargo movement is not confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 6 min / taxi-out 8 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — notably short, consistent with a compact single-runway regional field.
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, water/lav, catering and cabin services expected at a scheduled-carrier stand; cargo-specific ground-support equipment (forklifts, cargo loaders) present given the field's freighter traffic.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Single-runway field — any runway closure is a full-field closure with no configuration fallback; confirm current NOTAM status before planning a tight turn.

## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** EHBK is the **Netherlands' second-largest cargo hub by tonnage**, with time-sensitive perishables (cut flowers) and general/express freight cited as a specialisation; a substantial roster of scheduled all-cargo carriers (Atlas Air, Avianca Cargo, Cargolux, Emirates SkyCargo, Ethiopian Airlines, My Freighter Airlines, Qatar Airways Cargo, Royal Jordanian Cargo, Sky Vision Airlines, Turkish Cargo, Zimex Aviation) operates from the field. 🟧 Reported annual cargo tonnage varies materially by source and year (CBS/Statistics Netherlands: 29,448 t in 2024, 41,637 t in 2025; some third-party aggregators cite a much larger stated capacity figure — treat the CBS figures as the more reliable reference and the larger figure as an unconfirmed capacity claim).
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for the K Global operation specifically. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** This is fundamentally a **cargo-hub field** — if K Global ever routes cargo through EHBK, confirm current freighter-slot availability and ramp capacity with handling; belly-cargo interaction with the passenger operation is not a primary consideration here relative to dedicated freighter traffic.
- **Connection banks:** Not applicable — EHBK is not a K Global base; point-to-point/alternate role only.
- **Temporary capacity note (Feb–Jul 2027):** Expect materially higher passenger-side demand and stand competition during Eindhoven Airport's scheduled runway-renovation closure, when Transavia and TUI fly Netherlands relocate flights here (and to Weeze, Germany).
