## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain  🟩
EHBK sits at 375 ft AMSL in the rolling South Limburg countryside near the Dutch/Belgian/German tri-border area. No significant high terrain was identified in reachable sources near the field or its approach paths; CFIT is not the operative threat here. Verify the MSA ring on the current chart as routine practice.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic  🟩
A **single-runway regional field** with modest movement counts relative to the national hubs — traffic density is low by comparison with EHAM. The **Maastricht Upper Area Control Centre (MUAC)**, a EUROCONTROL facility physically sited at the airport, manages upper airspace across the Benelux and NW Germany, but this is an en-route/upper-airspace function and does not itself add local terminal-area complexity at EHBK.

### 3.3 Runway excursion  🟧
The single runway (03/21, 2,750×45 m) reportedly carries **displaced thresholds at both ends (~250 m each)**, per a secondary source — reducing usable LDA below the physical length in both directions. This has **not been independently confirmed against the primary AIP** this pass; treat the exact declared LDA as unverified and confirm before any low-margin performance calculation. The runway was resurfaced and renumbered from 04/22 to **03/21 in 2005** to reflect magnetic drift — a reminder that this field's charted heading references have changed within living memory.

### 3.4 Weather threat  🟧
Inland South Limburg climate — temperate NW-European weather patterns (frontal wind/rain, potential winter fog/low cloud) are plausible but **not confirmed as an EHBK-specific hazard** in reachable sources. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations  🟧
EHBK's defining operational character is as a **major Dutch all-cargo hub** (2nd-largest by tonnage nationally) hosting numerous scheduled all-cargo carriers, overlaid on a modest scheduled-passenger operation (Wizz Air, with Transavia/TUI fly Netherlands adding capacity from Feb 2027 during Eindhoven Airport's scheduled five-and-a-half-month runway-renovation closure). A reported night curfew with a limited annual cargo-movement allowance (§12) is the standing scheduling constraint for any night-sensitive K Global rotation — confirm current terms against the primary AIP before planning a night movement.

## 4. Cautions & Warnings

- **Single runway (03/21)** — no configuration alternative; a runway closure means a full field closure.
- **Displaced thresholds reported at both ends (~250 m)** — not independently AIP-confirmed this pass; do not assume full 2,750 m LDA without verification.
- **Runway was renumbered 04/22 → 03/21 in 2005** for magnetic drift — a reminder that headings/idents at this field have shifted within recent history; confirm current chart designators.
- **Reported night curfew (23:00–06:00) with a limited annual cargo-movement allowance** — not independently AIP-confirmed; verify current terms before planning any night rotation. 🟧
- **Significant temporary traffic-pattern shift expected Feb–Jul 2027** — Eindhoven Airport (EHEH) is scheduled to close for ~5.5 months for runway renovation, with Transavia and TUI fly Netherlands relocating flights to Maastricht (and Weeze, Germany) during that period — expect a materially busier field in that window if any K Global routing touches this period.
- **CAT III ILS exists only on RWY 21** — confirm runway/approach pairing before planning a low-visibility arrival; RWY 03 approach category not confirmed.
