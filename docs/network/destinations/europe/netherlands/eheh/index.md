## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain  🟩
EHEH sits at 74 ft AMSL in flat Noord-Brabant countryside, roughly 7.6 km west of Eindhoven city. No significant high terrain was identified in reachable sources near the field or its approach paths; CFIT is not the operative threat here.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic  🟥
EHEH is a genuinely **joint civil/military airfield** — the western part of the site is **Eindhoven Air Base**, home to the Royal Netherlands Air Force's **Air Mobility Command** (334 Squadron, Airbus A330 MRTT tanker/transport and Gulfstream G650ER; 336 Squadron, Lockheed C-130H Hercules; plus maintenance/support squadrons), while the eastern part is the civil airport, now the **second-busiest passenger airport in the Netherlands** (6.96 million passengers in 2025) with a dense Ryanair/Wizz Air/Transavia low-cost network. Approach control is provided by **RAPCON South**, a **military-operated radar approach facility** — a materially different ATC-provision model from the fully civil-ANSP fields elsewhere in this network. The airport additionally hosts the **Movement Coordination Centre Europe (MCCE)**, coordinating military airlift/sealift/air-to-air-refuelling across participating nations, and (since September 2010) the **European Air Transport Command (EATC)**, a seven-nation shared military airlift command — both administrative/coordination bodies rather than direct traffic-flow factors, but confirming this field's deep and durable military integration. Expect a genuinely mixed civil/military traffic picture and confirm current joint-use procedures before planning ops here.

### 3.3 Runway excursion  🟧
The single runway (03/21, 3,000×45 m) reportedly carries **displaced thresholds at both ends (~250 m each)**, per a secondary source — reducing usable LDA below the physical length in both directions. This has **not been independently confirmed against the primary AIP** this pass; treat the exact declared LDA as unverified.

### 3.4 Weather threat  🟧
Inland Noord-Brabant climate — temperate NW-European weather patterns (frontal wind/rain, potential winter fog/low cloud) are plausible but **not confirmed as an EHEH-specific hazard** in reachable sources. Notably, the field's scheduled 2027 runway-renovation programme includes an expansion of the **approach lighting system specifically to enable landings in dense fog** — an indirect signal that fog is a recognised operational constraint at this field today. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations  🟥
Three durable constraints stand out: (1) the **joint civil/military character** itself, including military-operated approach control (RAPCON South) and a materially different traffic mix from a purely civil field; (2) a **noise-driven visual-approach constraint** — aircraft flying a visual approach are required to intercept the final approach leg at or above 1,500 ft AMSL specifically to minimise noise nuisance, a concrete and unusual TEM item worth briefing; and (3) the **scheduled full closure 1 February – 19 July 2027** for runway widening, a new east-side taxiway, replacement of the RNLAF arresting system, and an ILS/approach-lighting expansion — this is a hard planning constraint for any K Global routing that might touch this field in that window (see §18).

## 4. Cautions & Warnings

- **Single runway (03/21)** — no configuration alternative; a runway closure means a full field closure.
- **Joint civil/military field** with military-operated RAPCON South approach control — confirm current joint-use procedures; do not assume a purely civil ATC-provision model.
- **Visual-approach noise constraint:** aircraft flying a visual approach must intercept the final approach leg at or above **1,500 ft AMSL** to minimise noise nuisance — brief this before planning a visual arrival.
- **SCHEDULED FULL CLOSURE 1 Feb – 19 Jul 2027 (~5.5 months)** for runway renovation (widening, new east-side taxiway, RNLAF arresting-system replacement, ILS/approach-lighting expansion for fog operations) — the field is **completely unavailable** in this window; confirm before any routing that could touch this period. 🟥
- **Displaced thresholds reported both ends (~250 m)** — not independently AIP-confirmed this pass; do not assume full 3,000 m LDA without verification.
- **De facto absence of scheduled night flights**, alongside a reported (not fully reconciled) military opening-hours window of 07:00–23:00 LT — confirm current operating-hours reality before planning any early/late-day rotation.
