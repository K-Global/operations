## 2. Stand → runway taxi routing & hot spots

- **Typical routings by runway:** From Terminal 1 (expected assignment, §Dispatch), Ground/Apron routes toward the assigned runway end (02 or 20); confirm the exact taxi with Ground/Tower on the day.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟧 Areas not visible from the Tower include Apron 10/11 stands 104–108/114–117 and various positions across Aprons 20/30/40/41/42/50 (TWY M3, M4, L1, L2, V, N1, Z2, taxilanes Z1, Z3, A1) — see [Briefing §13](index.md). Stop bars protect all CAT II/III RWY 02/20 holding positions.
- **Runway crossings / read-back-required points:** Runway-incursion microwave alarm sensors are fitted at CAT II/III holding positions on TWY A6, A7, H1, H3, H4, M5, N2, P, S3, T5, T6, U5, U6 — expect these to be active, read-back-heavy crossing points.
- **Low-vis taxi caveats:** SMGCS/low-vis taxi routing applies consistent with the field's CAT III capability; follow-me availability not confirmed. 🟧

## 3. Runway / SID selection logic

- **By wind / config:** Runway selection (02 vs. 20) is purely wind-driven — with no crosswind runway remaining, expect the field to hold a configuration through gusting/shifting wind rather than switch for crosswind relief (Briefing §3.4).
- **By departure direction:** No separate directional-preference runway exists (single physical runway, both directions used per wind).
- **Noise / preferential-runway program:** Visual/noise routing differs by direction of flight (Briefing §12) — RWY 02 flies a left-hand traffic pattern element on some routings; RWY 20 has fewer directional restrictions from the north.
- **Interaction with arrivals:** Single-runway field — departures and arrivals share the one runway; expect sequencing gaps built around the arrival stream (PMS-managed) rather than simultaneous independent operations.

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 02's 6% obstacle-driven climb gradient (to 2,000 ft) is a real performance planning input** — do not treat it as boilerplate; verify aircraft performance meets it at weight.
- **No crosswind runway remains at LPPT** — 17/35 is permanently closed/repurposed; expect the assigned 02/20 configuration to hold through gusting/shifting autumn–winter wind.
- **Field operates at/near capacity** — expect sequencing/flow delay as routine, not exceptional, on this single-runway hub.
- **Hard night curfew 00:00–06:00** — a late-day departure delay risks being prohibited outright; build buffer into the last rotation of the day.
- **Instrument-approach training is generally not permitted at LPPT** due to traffic demand — irrelevant to a revenue departure but a useful field-character marker.
