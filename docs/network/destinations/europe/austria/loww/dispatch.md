## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Four terminal buildings — **Terminal 1**, **Terminal 1A** (standalone, opposite T1, hosts check-in for a range of leisure/long-haul carriers), **Terminal 2** (refurbished 2016–2022, now feeds Concourses C/D via the original T2 building), and **Terminal 3** (the "Austrian/Star Alliance Terminal," opened 2012, feeding Concourses F and G). The central arrivals hall for all terminal areas is in Terminal 3. `[Wikipedia, retrieved 2026-07-26]`
- **Our operation uses:** Not published / verify — no confirmed terminal/concourse assignment for the K Global operation at this field. 🟧
- **International arrivals / non-Schengen hall:** Non-Schengen destinations are handled through **Concourse D** (pier east) and **Concourse G** (pier north, Level 3/basement), each with shared passport control at the pier entrance. Schengen traffic uses Concourses B, C and F.
- **Notes:** A new building (**"T3 Southern Enlargement"**), connecting the existing pier east and pier north with 18 additional bus gates (9 Schengen / 9 non-Schengen) and ~70,000 m² of terminal space, has been under construction since February 2024 with a completion date expected in early 2027 `[Wikipedia, retrieved 2026-07-26]` — treat the terminal programme as **mid-build** through the network's current planning horizon. 🟧
