## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** **Terminal 1** is the main commercial terminal, historically organised into multiple piers including a legacy **French-sector pier (Pier F)** that allowed passenger movement to/from France without transiting Swiss customs/territory (a 1960s France/Canton-of-Geneva arrangement, largely superseded in passport-control terms since Switzerland's 2008 Schengen accession but physically retained). A dedicated long-haul/widebody wing opened **December 2021**, replacing an older pier in the same location, with fixed contact-stand electrical/PCA power eliminating apron-bus transfers for that wing. A separate historical terminal is used seasonally for winter charter traffic only. **Terminal 3** is a dedicated business-aviation terminal ~2 km from T1 with its own FBOs, VIP lounges, and private customs/immigration.
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal 1 (commercial) — exact pier/gate assignment not confirmed in reachable public sources. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International passport control is at Terminal 1; the historical French-sector pier (Pier F) retains a residual cross-border function but no longer requires passport control post-Schengen.
- **Notes:** Confirm current gate/pier assignment with handling before finalising a stand plan. 🟧

- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA (fixed power at the long-haul wing), potable water/lav, catering and cabin services expected at a major-hub Code E/F stand (standard provision, not independently itemised for LSGG's commercial side).
- **Turnaround risk items:** Narrow-taxiway oversteer requirements for larger aircraft (reported in a tier-4 source), and the single-runway field's lack of an on-field fallback if the runway is obstructed, are the most likely sources of unplanned turn time or knock-on delay.
