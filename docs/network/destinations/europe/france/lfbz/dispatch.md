## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Traffic areas split into **Aire Alpha/Bravo** (general aviation, PPR-gated) and **Aire Charlie** (commercial aviation); a separate Hotel helicopter line exists (H1/H3, max length 13.73 m). No terminal-transition programme identified.
- **Our operation uses:** Aire Charlie (commercial aviation) is the expected area for a K Global Category S operation — not individually confirmed. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Customs/immigration hours not confirmed from a primary table this pass. 🟧

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** N/A — this is a Category S field/fleet fit; no widebody consideration applies.
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Aire Alpha stand groups carry specific wingspan caps (4–11, 14, 16, 18, 20: ≤ 15 m; 15, 17, 19, 21–23: ≤ 20 m) — relevant only if a K Global type were ever parked on the GA apron rather than Aire Charlie.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Aire Alpha/Bravo are restricted-access GA aprons requiring 48 h PPR via the airport's General Aviation service (aviationgenerale@biarritz.aeroport.fr) — not the expected area for scheduled K Global commercial operations.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not confirmed — expect standard Aire Charlie allocation for a Category S type.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** TWY N1/S1 (connecting the runway to parking area K) are closed at night and below RVR 800 m by day — see [Briefing §13](index.md).

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Not confirmed — treat as unregulated pending verification. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard movement curfew identified. **ATS hours are 0430–2100 local**, extendable beyond 2100 for scheduled/medevac/pre-authorised traffic subject to prior clearance from the Delegation Manager requested before 1400 — treat this as an **operating-hours gate requiring advance coordination**, not an ad hoc late-evening option. Separately, **TWY N1 and S1 are closed at night** (a ground-movement restriction affecting access to parking area K, not the runway itself). 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** No slot-coordination level confirmed — general EUROCONTROL ATFM/CTOT practice assumed, not independently LFBZ-sourced. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** Build any late-evening rotation around the ATS-hours extension process (pre-1400 request) rather than assuming ad hoc coverage; treat coastal fog (§Briefing §3.4) as the more time-critical operational risk day-to-day.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** General-aviation handling confirmed for Aire Alpha/Bravo (PPR 48 h, aviationgenerale@biarritz.aeroport.fr); commercial handler for Aire Charlie not individually confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 6 min / taxi-out 8 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — the shortest taxi figures in this regional cluster, consistent with the field's compact single-runway layout.
- **Services:** Standard regional-field ground services assumed for Aire Charlie; APU use at any stand is time-limited to 30 min after arrival / 30 min before departure.
- **Turnaround risk items:** TWY N1/S1 access restrictions (night closure, RVR 800 m day threshold) could affect ground-movement timing if the assigned stand routes via those taxiways; coastal fog onset can compress an otherwise-quick turn into an unplanned hold.
