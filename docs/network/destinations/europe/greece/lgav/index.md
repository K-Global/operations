## 3. Threat & Error Management (TEM)

### 3.1 CFIT / Terrain 🟧
The airfield itself occupies the relatively flat **Mesogeia plain** in eastern Attica, roughly 20 km east of central Athens. The wider Attica basin is not without relief, however — higher ground lies generally to the west and northwest of the airport (the Hymettus/Ymittos and Penteli ridge lines that frame the Athens basin) and forms part of the region's general MSA sectoring. This is a moderate, not extreme, terrain environment — nothing comparable to a true mountain/hot-and-high field — but it is not the flat, featureless basin EDDF sits in either. No primary obstacle chart or MSA sector table is available from a reachable public source; brief the current MSA ring and terrain depiction before an arrival, departure or missed approach.

### 3.2 Airborne conflict / traffic 🟧
LGAV is a large, growing international gateway — the **15th-busiest airport in Europe** as of 2025, having handled roughly 32 million passengers and continuing double-digit year-on-year growth through 2024 (13.1% pax growth, 32.9% cargo growth that year). Traffic is controlled by **Athinai Approach** under full radar/TMA surveillance. The two runways (03L/21R and 03R/21L) are widely spaced compared with a closely-spaced-parallel field such as Frankfurt, and are reported to support **independent/segregated arrival and departure operations** (one runway typically dedicated to one direction of traffic) rather than continuous dependent-parallel approaches — this reduces, though does not eliminate, sequencing workload during the summer leisure peak. No dedicated **Athinai (LGGG) FIR brief** exists yet in this system; cross-ref the general [Europe airspace brief](../../../../airspace/europe.md) for continental context, noting that document is scoped to a different (Germany–France–Iberia) transit corridor and does not itself describe Greek/Athinai airspace — a gap flagged in §18.

### 3.3 Runway excursion 🟧
Both runway pairs carry a **300 m (984 ft) displaced threshold at each end** (03L, 21R, 03R and 21L all displaced) per the reachable runway data — this materially reduces the usable landing distance below the full physical runway length and must be checked against the current AIP declared-distance table rather than assumed from the raw runway length. No further excursion-specific factors (contamination frequency, tailwind-component history) are confirmed.

### 3.4 Weather threat 🟧
LGAV sits in a **Mediterranean climate** (Köppen **Csa**) — hot, dry summers and a mild, wet winter. The defining seasonal wind hazard for the wider Aegean and Attica region is the **Meltemi**, a strong, dry, northerly-quadrant wind most prominent **June–September**, which can sustain significant strength and gusts for days at a time. Depending on the runway/configuration in use on a given day, the Meltemi can present a meaningful crosswind or tailwind-component consideration — check the current METAR/TAF/TREND and runway-in-use before planning in season. Winter is mild and wet by comparison; snow/ice at the airport itself is rare (not a defining hazard) but not unheard of in the Attica region. See §14.

### 3.5 Operational considerations 🟧
The open administrative/verification items at this field are concentrated in exactly the areas a primary AIP table would normally settle: RFF category, exact ILS category/runway assignment, slot-coordination level, curfew/night-restriction status, and PCN/declared distances. None of these are confirmed from a reachable primary source (§18). The field itself — long runways, full radar control, confirmed Schengen/non-Schengen customs split, and a modern (2001-built) two-terminal layout — is not, in itself, an operationally restrictive or "special" airport in any reachable source.

## 4. Cautions & Warnings

- **Meltemi-season (Jun–Sep) northerly winds** can produce sustained crosswind or tailwind-component conditions depending on runway in use — check the current METAR/TAF/TREND and runway assignment before planning.
- **Both runways carry a 300 m displaced threshold at each end** — do not assume the full physical runway length is usable for landing; verify the current AIP declared-distance table.
- **Wider Attica-region high ground (Hymettus/Penteli)** lies generally west/northwest of the field — respect the current chart's MSA rings on arrival, departure and any missed approach.
- **Peak summer season (Jun–Sep)** carries the airport's heaviest scheduled and leisure-charter load, coinciding with Meltemi wind season — expect busier radar/tower workload and possible sequencing delay.
- 🟧 **RFF category, exact ILS CAT sub-category/runway assignment, slot-coordination level and any curfew/night-restriction are not confirmed from a primary source** — treat as open items before relying on them operationally (§18).
