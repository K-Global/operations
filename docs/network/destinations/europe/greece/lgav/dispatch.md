## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** LGAV has operated two terminals since opening in 2001. The **Main Terminal** handles all intra-Schengen flights plus several non-Schengen flights, over three levels (arrivals, departures, and a food-court level with a view of the eastern runway); it has 153 check-in desks, 14 jet bridges and 11 baggage belts. **Hall A** serves non-Schengen and non-European flights; **Hall B** serves intra-Schengen and domestic services. The **Satellite Terminal** (two levels — arrivals and departures) is reached from the Main Terminal via an underground link with moving walkways; it has 10 jet bridges and a capacity of roughly 6 million passengers/year, restarted full operations 24 May 2018 and is used predominantly by low-cost/leisure carriers (Ryanair, easyJet, Vueling, Eurowings, Norwegian, Transavia and Transavia France, TUIfly Belgium, Wizz Air, Delta Air Lines, Air Transat, among others).
- **Our operation uses:** Terminal/hall assignment for a K Global category-L service is not confirmed — expect the Main Terminal's Hall A (international, non-Schengen origin/destination) as the default assumption pending confirmation. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International (non-Schengen) arrivals clear passport control via **Hall A** in the Main Terminal.
- **Notes:** The airport is mid-way through a long-run, six-phase expansion programme — the current phase already handles roughly 26 million passengers/year against an original 16 million design capacity via IT/process efficiency gains, and a Main Terminal south-wing physical expansion (tendered 2018, ~14,950 m² over five levels, additional check-in/security capacity) has been progressing. Confirm current-year terminal/gate state with handling before finalising a stand plan. 🟧

- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** Not independently sourced for the K Global operation specifically — treat as a leisure/tourism-driven seasonal peak pending network-schedule confirmation. 🟧
