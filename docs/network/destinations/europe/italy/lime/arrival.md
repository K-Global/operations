## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟧 Terrain rises to the north, north-east and east of the field; tier-4 local-flying material reports the highest MSA sector to the NE at approximately 12,400 ft. Not a close-in threat on a normal straight-in approach, but relevant to any circling manoeuvre or northerly transition — cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md).
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 Single runway carrying very high combined LCC/cargo/adjacent-GA traffic under Milano ACC — expect sequencing pressure and possible extended vectoring during peak banks; both runway ends carry a displaced threshold reducing usable LDA (see [Briefing §7](index.md)).
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟥 High-density single-runway operation; no dedicated Milano ACC (LIMM) FIR brief exists yet in this tree — see the interim [Europe — General airspace brief](../../../../airspace/europe.md) 🟧.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Do not turn toward high ground to the north, north-east or east on a go-around without a verified missed-approach track — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around at this single-runway field returns you into the same traffic stream that includes LCC, cargo and adjacent GA/training circuit activity — expect vectoring and possible holding before re-sequencing, especially during a winter fog event or a peak bank.
- **Go-around traps:** No parallel/crossing runway to relieve congestion during a miss — expect ATC to manage the single-runway sequencing carefully; maintain a heightened traffic scan.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Single runway, no redundancy** — expect ATC to manage all traffic (LCC, cargo, adjacent GA) through the one strip; a runway-blocking event stops all arrivals.
- **Terrain rises N/NE/E of the field** — respect MSA on any circling or non-standard arrival routing.
- **Winter Po Valley radiation fog** can drop the field into LVP conditions with limited warning — brief a holding/diversion contingency in season.
- **Config swap (10↔28) driven by a wind shift** can bring a late runway change — watch the ATIS/wind trend.
- **Displaced thresholds on both ends** reduce usable LDA below the full published runway length — do not assume full-length landing distance.
- **Curfew/operating-hours status is unconfirmed** — do not plan an arrival near the edges of the operating day assuming any particular window (see [Dispatch §4](dispatch.md)).
