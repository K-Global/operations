## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Three terminals — the original terminal (opened 2007, 22 gates, 6 loading bridges) plus **Terminal B** and **Terminal C** (both added 2018); Terminal C has historically operated exclusively for one low-cost carrier's traffic.
- **Our operation uses:** Not confirmed — verify current terminal/stand assignment for K Global service. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International sections present across the terminal complex; exact hall/desk arrangement not confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** 🟧 A **16 July 2023 fire** partially damaged the airport, suspending all flight activity for several days (redirected to Comiso, Palermo, Trapani) with a roughly three-week disrupted-recovery period before full normal operations resumed on 5 August 2023 — a real precedent for non-ash infrastructure disruption at this field; factor general resilience/diversion planning accordingly, not only for ash events.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** 🟥 **Mount Etna volcanic ash** — recurring VONA red-alert closures (full or sector-based) have interrupted operations multiple times per year in recent operating history, including within the current cycle. This is the field's defining reliability driver, well above conventional weather/ATFM causes.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Ash-driven flow restriction/closure is possible independent of conventional ATFM; general EU flow-management practice may also apply at peak. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** 🟧 Expect the ash-exposure risk to periodically drive multi-day disruption (per the July 2026 and prior events) rather than the more typical single-event delay pattern seen at non-volcanic fields — build this into schedule-resilience planning, not just tactical OTP tracking.
