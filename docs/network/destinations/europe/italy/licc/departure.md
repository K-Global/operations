## 10. Gotchas

- **Check current Etna VONA/ASHTAM status before every departure request** — this is the field's single most consequential go/no-go item, more so than weather or slot status in the conventional sense.
- **RWY 26 carries no ILS** — while primarily an arrival consideration, be aware of the asymmetry when assessing runway-in-use during marginal weather.
- **A 2023 terminal fire suspended all operations for days** — a reminder that non-ash disruption is also a real possibility at this field; do not plan around ash risk alone to the exclusion of general resilience.
- **NAS Sigonella traffic (~10 NM)** shares regional airspace — maintain normal traffic vigilance.
- **Curfew window (~00:00–04:00) is tier-4-only** — do not treat as confirmed; verify before planning a late-night departure.
