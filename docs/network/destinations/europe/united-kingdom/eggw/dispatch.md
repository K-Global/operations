## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single two-storey terminal — ground floor with 62 check-in desks and a separate security hall; upper floor departures lounge with **~30 gates in three piers** (1–19, 20–28, 30–43) `[Wikipedia]`.
- **Our operation uses:** The single terminal — no alliance/concourse split as at a multi-terminal hub. Confirm current gate/pier assignment with handling.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear UK Border Force within the single terminal.
- **Notes:** A **Luton DART** automated people-mover (Parkway station ↔ terminal) opened for passenger service March 2023, replacing the prior shuttle-bus link — relevant to ground transport/crew logistics, not gate operations. A proposed **second terminal (Terminal 2)**, targeting 32M pax/yr by 2039, was under consultation per Wikipedia — current status not confirmed this pass. 🟧

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Not a routine widebody field — the AIP's local regulations explicitly restrict widebody movements: **no widebody transit via Taxiway Echo 1** (maximum size B757/A321 under power), and **widebody aircraft require a Follow-Me vehicle via Taxiway Bravo 8** `[UK AIP AD 2.20]`. Confirm current stand/taxi plan before scheduling any widebody type here.
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Not itemised for our Category S fleet specifically — the field is overwhelmingly narrowbody-oriented; confirm with handling for any type-specific stand restriction.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** ~60 total stands, mix of Standard Apron/Stand and MARS (Multiple Aircraft Ramp System) `[UK AIP AD 2.9]`; hardstand use is not atypical at this LCC/charter-focused field.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Confirm with handling — both contact and remote positions are in routine use here, unlike a strictly-contact-gate major hub.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Nose-in parking, marshaller-guided; Stands 54/56/58/62/71/80/81 excepted from nose-in; Stands 8 & 9 carry a >1% slope caution `[UK AIP AD 2.9]`. See [Briefing §13](index.md) for the Taxiway E (19 m, 1.7% slope), Echo 1 (no-widebody) and Bravo 8 (widebody Follow-Me) restrictions.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **IATA Level 3** — formally escalated from Level 2 in **Summer 2013** after winter departure-peak breaches could not be resolved under voluntary Level 2 moves; coordinator **Airport Coordination Limited (ACL)** `[ACL Luton case study, UK AIP AD 2.20]`. 🟥
- **Curfew / night restriction:** Luton's **own locally-run scheme** (distinct from the DfT-designated Heathrow/Gatwick/Stansted scheme), governed by the **London Luton Airport Consultative Committee (LLACC)** and its **Noise and Track Sub-Committee (NTSC)**: noise-violation limits **80 dBA day (0700–2300) / 79 dBA night (2300–0700)** at three named Noise Monitoring Terminals; **QC>2 banned at night**; **Chapter 2 aircraft banned outright at night**; **scheduled arrivals only accepted 0545–0700**, all other arrivals must land after 0700 `[UK AIP AD 2.21]`. 🟥
- **CTOT / flow control:** All flights require an ACL slot allocation, including ad-hoc requests via the online-coordination portal `[UK AIP AD 2.20]`; EUROCONTROL Network Manager ATFM/CTOT regulation applies as standard practice.
- **Commercial impact:** The **0545–0700 early-arrival restriction** and the night noise-violation charge regime are the two biggest scheduling guardrails at this field — plan the last inbound rotation to avoid both an early unscheduled arrival and a late night-period violation.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** A **nominated handling agent is mandatory** for all visiting aircraft `[UK AIP AD 2.20]`; confirmed operators at the field generally include **Harrods Business Aviation**, **Signature Flight Support**, **Swissport** and **Menzies** — the specific agent for our operation is not confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 12 min / taxi-out 14 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard narrowbody/Category S service times.
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services all standard at an LCC/charter-focused single-terminal field.
- **Turnaround risk items:** Gate/stand availability during peak leisure-season banks, and the taxi restrictions around Echo 1/Bravo 8 if a larger-than-Category-S type is ever scheduled.

## 10. Typical delays / reliability

- **Signature delay driver:** The field's **own night noise/QC scheme** (80/79 dBA limits, 0545–0700 arrival window) shapes schedule-timing risk more distinctively here than at EGLL/EGKK; the elevated, rising-terrain siting is a secondary seasonal-weather consideration.
- **Ground-delay / flow-program exposure:** Moderate — Level 3 slot-coordinated but a smaller single-runway field than EGLL/EGKK; expect EUROCONTROL flow management in peak/adverse conditions. 🟧
- **On-time reliability picture:** Winter weather days and any schedule slipping toward the 0545 early-arrival boundary or the 2300 night-scheme boundary carry the highest schedule-risk exposure. 🟧

---

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Night departures exceeding **79 dB at 6.5 km from start of roll** incur a nominal fine under LLA's Charges and Conditions of Use; track deviations outside the published NPR "Lateral Swathe" also incur a nominal fine `[UK AIP AD 2.21]`.
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** The **0545–0700 early-arrival restriction** (early arrivals ahead of schedule are refused) and the **QC>2/Chapter 2 night bans** are hard restrictions, not merely charges.
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Build buffer so a normal operational advance does not produce an unscheduled arrival before 0545, and so a normal delay does not push a late-night departure into a noise-violation charge exposure.
