## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Single passenger terminal; ground operations split across a **Main Apron** (stands 1–14, 32–41; access via Taxiways Uniform/Victor/Whisky) and an **Eastern Apron** (stands 51–56; access via Taxiway Tango). No domestic/international terminal split identified. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not specified for a K Global rotation this pass — confirm apron/stand assignment with handling ahead of the first scheduled service. 🟧
- **International arrivals / Border Force hall:** Customs and immigration confirmed H24; no separate satellite facility identified.
- **Notes:** Eastern Apron stand markings (**51/54 up to B757, 52/55 up to B767, 53/56 up to B737**) indicate the field's stand infrastructure is sized for narrowbody/regional operations — worth carrying into any K Global widebody scheduling decision. 🟧

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Coordinated via **Airport Co-ordination Ltd (ACL)** — every flight except GA/military requires prior Airport Operations Director approval **and** prior ACL notification; ad-hoc requests Mon–Fri 0830–1700 (0730–1600) by SITA/e-mail/phone, or via the online coordination portal at any time. Externally reported as an IATA Level 3 coordinated field, though that specific phrase was not found verbatim in the reachable AIP text. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard 24 h movement ban corroborated in reachable sources. 🟥 A **Night Noise Quota System** applies: quota period 2300–0700 (2200–0600), quota-count period 2330–0600 (2230–0500); **QC/8 and QC/16 aircraft banned from scheduled movements 2330–0600 (2230–0500)** and from the 2300–2330 (2200–2230) edge window; **QC/16 banned 0600–0700 (0500–0600)**. **RWY 09 is night-restricted for take-off use** (2300–0700/2200–0600) except when overriding operational considerations necessitate it. VFR movements 0000–0700 (2300–0600) subject to ATC PPR.
- **CTOT / flow control:** **DPI (Departure Planning Information) messages are fully operational** to the Network Manager Operations Centre in Brussels. Broader UK NATS flow-management exposure not independently EGGP-sourced this pass. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The **combination of mandatory PPR/handling for every visiting flight and the Night Noise Quota System's QC-band movement bans** are the two biggest schedule-integrity risks at this field — confirm PPR/quota-count status well ahead of any night movement, and check the assigned aircraft's noise chapter/QC classification against the quota bands before scheduling a night slot.

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Specific dB-based noise-charge tariff not found in reachable sources; the field instead applies a **quota-count (QC) system** modelled on the CAA's Heathrow/Gatwick/Stansted framework, with hard QC/8 and QC/16 movement bans in defined night windows (§4). 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** No hard movement curfew corroborated, but the **QC-band bans are a hard ATC/scheduling rule**, not merely a charge; **RWY 09 night take-off use is similarly restricted to operational necessity only**.
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Confirm the assigned aircraft's QC/noise classification before scheduling any movement in the 2300–0700 (2200–0600) window, and build buffer so a normal operational delay does not push a movement into a QC-banned period unprepared.
