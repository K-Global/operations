## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **IATA Level 2, schedules-facilitated**, with an additional **Level 3 coordination window 2200–0600 (Summer only)** specifically for night movements — PPR required in that window. Ad-hoc requests go to ACL (Mon–Fri 0730–1600, Summer only) or the Airport Duty Manager at other times.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard 24 h movement ban corroborated in reachable sources. 🟥 **PPR applies 2300–0700 (2200–0600)**; a **night landing surcharge applies 2200–0700 (2100–0600)**; training flights are **not permitted 2200–0700 (2100–0600)** under any circumstances; the **Night Noise Quota System is in force 2330–0600 (2230–0500)**.
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not independently sourced for EGGD-specific ATFM exposure this pass — treat as standard UK NATS flow-management exposure pending confirmation. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The **Level 3 night-coordination/PPR regime**, combined with the AIP's explicit statement that **diversions may be refused except in an emergency**, are the two biggest schedule-integrity risks at this field — build in confirmed slot/PPR lead time for any night movement, and do not rely on EGGD as a casual tactical diversion.

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Noise limit of **90 dB(A) (103 PNdB) by day** (0600–2329/0500–2229) and **85 dB(A) (96 PNdB) by night** (2330–0559/2230–0459) at monitoring points 6.5 km from start of roll on both runways; exceedances are subject to a penalty per the airport's fees and charges. Specific per-movement tariff not confirmed. 🟧
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** No hard movement curfew corroborated beyond the PPR/surcharge regime; a **night landing surcharge applies 2200–0700 (2100–0600)**, and **training is banned outright 2200–0700 (2100–0600)**.
- **Scheduling guardrails:** Build buffer so a normal operational delay does not push a movement into the **Level 3 night-coordination window (2200–0600 Summer)** unprepared for PPR, and do not rely on EGGD as a casual diversion given the stated stand-scarcity/diversion-refusal policy.
