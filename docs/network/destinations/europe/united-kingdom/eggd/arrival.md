## 3. Descent & energy plan

- **Top-of-descent trigger:** Standard rule-of-thumb pending current-chart constraint confirmation. 🟧
- **Speed control:** 250 KIAS below FL100 (UK/ICAO norm); IFR visual approaches must not join final below 2,200 ft QNH.
- **Altitude constraints:** ILS descent restriction — aircraft must not descend below the specified altitude before intercepting the glidepath, nor fly below it thereafter (AIP §2.20). Visual-approach final-track intercept for aircraft >5,700 kg on RWY 27: not less than 3 DME from the north, not less than 4 DME from the south.
- **Energy traps:** The field's **hilltop/escarpment siting drives a real windshear/turbulence caution on strong SE (RWY 09) / strong W (RWY 27) winds** — brief energy management accordingly, especially on a long-body type. See [Briefing §3.4](index.md).

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** No close-in high terrain at the field itself (622 ft AMSL plateau); charted obstacles (888 ft hill/masts ~2 NM NE, 1,158 ft radio mast S, 1,963 ft TV mast further S) have not been independently cross-checked against the current MSA/obstacle chart this pass — cross-ref [Briefing §3.1](index.md). 🟧
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 AIP-explicit **windshear/turbulence caution** for strong SE (RWY 09) / strong W (RWY 27) winds — the field's standout approach hazard. Two locally reduced **forward sight distance** patches exist on the runway surface itself (near RWY 09 THR/TDZ and mid-runway) — a surface-visibility caution during rollout, not an approach hazard per se.
- **Airspace / traffic:** Radar-controlled Class D CTR/ATZ; local hot-air balloon, glider/hang-glider and UAV/model-aircraft activity in the vicinity — cross-ref [Briefing §3.2](index.md).

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** RWY 09 — **2,011 m** (no displacement); RWY 27 — **1,881 m** (threshold displaced ~130 m from the physical runway end/TORA). Never assume full-length landing distance on RWY 27. See [Briefing §7](index.md).
- **Braking / vacate:** Named B787/A330 arrival routes: RWY 09 vacates via BX or AX to Taxiway Z; RWY 27 vacates via GX, Taxiways G and Z. **B787 and A330 aircraft cannot backtrack.** All landing aircraft should proceed to the runway end to vacate unless instructed otherwise by ATC.
- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟧 RWY 27's displaced threshold (LDA 1,881 m vs 2,011 m TORA/ASDA) is the key excursion-relevant figure; contamination/braking-action data for winter operations not confirmed this pass.

## 8. Taxi-in routing to terminal & hot spots

- **Typical taxi-in by runway → terminal:** RWY 09 arrivals vacate BX/AX to Taxiway Z to the allocated stand; RWY 27 arrivals vacate GX, Taxiways G and Z to the allocated stand.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 Minimum main-gear pavement-edge clearances are tight for A330-class aircraft on several transitions (as low as 3.0–4.1 m); taxiways D, F, G, Z carry gradient notes up to ~2.65% — cross-ref [Briefing §13](index.md).
- **Stand/gate notes:** RWY 27 arriving aircraft should delay the "runway vacated" call until clear of the ILS localiser sensitive-area taxiway centreline lights when LVP are in force — cross-ref [Dispatch §3](dispatch.md).

## 10. Gotchas

- **AIP-explicit windshear/turbulence caution** on strong SE (RWY 09) / strong W (RWY 27) winds — the field's signature arrival hazard.
- **RWY 27's displaced threshold** cuts LDA to 1,881 m versus 2,011 m TORA/ASDA — do not assume full-length landing distance.
- **B787/A330 cannot backtrack** — plan the vacate routing accordingly.
- **Two reduced forward-sight-distance patches** on the runway surface (near RWY 09 THR/TDZ and mid-runway) — a rollout visual-reference caution.
- **Racing pigeon activity year-round, especially April–October**, plus active bird-dispersal operations — brief an elevated bird-strike watch on approach.
- **Diversions may be refused except in an emergency** due to limited stand availability — do not treat EGGD as a casual tactical alternate.
