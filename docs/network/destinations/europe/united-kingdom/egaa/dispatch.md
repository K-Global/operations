## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **None** — no slot coordination required, per tier-4 public corroboration; not independently confirmed against a primary regulatory source this pass. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **No hard curfew found in the reachable primary AIP text.** Tier-4 sources report H24/365-day operation and a **continuous descent approach (CDA) noise-mitigation procedure 2200–0700** (approx. 220 kt by 20 NM, 180 kt on base leg, 160 kt on the ILS) — this item is **tier-4-sourced only** and should be confirmed against the airport's own noise/operations pages before being treated as an operational constraint. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** No slot-coordination or CTOT regime confirmed; standard UK/EU ATFM flow-management practice may still apply in constrained weather/traffic circumstances. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** Absent a confirmed slot or curfew regime, the field's primary commercial-scheduling constraints are instead the **RFF Category 8/9 24-hour advance-notice requirement** (§Open items; see Briefing §3.5) for any widebody movement, and the **Aldergrove Director HX 0700–2200 (0600–2100)** service window, which should be factored into off-hours schedule planning.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** HAE / Swissport / Global Trek Aviation (H24); EAC (0900–1700); Woodgate Executive Air Charter (0900–2000 plus by arrangement); Jet Assist (H24 by prior arrangement). The specific agent contracted for the K Global operation is not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 6 min / taxi-out 8 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-26]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard service times for the assigned type.
- **Services:** Not itemised per agent in reachable sources — confirm push-back, GPU/PCA, water/lav, catering and cabin-clean availability with the assigned handler ahead of any turn. 🟧
- **Turnaround risk items:** The **RWY 07/25 Taxiway Bravo PPR requirement** (apron pushback procedures) and the **RWY 25-arrival-via-RWY 35 crossing routing** (see Briefing §13) can each add unplanned ground-movement time to a turn; winter de-icing "as required" is a further seasonal risk item.
