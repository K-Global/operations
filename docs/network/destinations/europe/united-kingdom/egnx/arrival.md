## 2. STAR / transition selection

- **STARs (names only — verify current AIRAC):** Not confirmed in reachable research pass — pull the current-AIRAC STAR list at planning. 🟧
- **Selection by arrival direction / runway:** Wind-driven choice between RWY 09 and RWY 27; **East Midlands Radar/Director** assigns the transition for the active runway.
- **Transition to approach:** Expect radar vectors from East Midlands Radar/Director onto the ILS final for the assigned runway — verify the charted transition.
- **Speed / flow constraints on the STAR:** 250 KIAS below FL100, reducing to 250–210 KT during the intermediate approach phase; **continuous descent approach (CDA) technique is required of all turbo-jet/turbo-prop aircraft at all times** — a monitored requirement (airport noise and track-keeping system logs any level segment >2.5 NM below 6,000 ft QNH), not merely advisory.

---

## 3. Descent & energy plan

- **Top-of-descent trigger:** Plan for a continuous descent profile — CDA is a stated operational requirement here, not optional best practice; ATC will provide range checks, and pilots requiring additional track mileage should advise early.
- **Speed control:** 250 KIAS below FL100; 250–210 KT during the intermediate approach phase (§2).
- **Altitude constraints:** Key STAR crossing constraints are pointers — fly the charted altitudes; verify current chart.
- **Energy traps:** A **late wind-driven runway swap** on this single-runway field is the principal energy trap. Critically, a swap **from RWY 27 (CAT II/IIIB) to RWY 09 (CAT I/LTS CAT I only)** in marginal visibility is also a **minima trap**, not just an energy one — brief this explicitly before descent.

## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None of significance. Flat Trent-valley field at 306 ft AMSL. The circling area contains a 758 ft AMSL chimney at Ratcliffe-on-Soar (643 ft AGL, ~2.9 NM NE) plus a cluster of temporary cranes/transporters (up to ~361 ft AMSL) — verify current obstacle NOTAM before any circling manoeuvre.
- **Specific threats:** 🟧 **Building-induced turbulence and wind-shear** are specifically warned for **RWY 09** approaches in strong southerly winds. A slight turbulence increase is also possible near two wind turbines (230°/1,076 m from ARP, blade-tip 148 ft AGL) in southerly wind. Single-runway field — no parallel/converging-approach conflict, but total operational dependency on the one runway.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 Class D CTR under East Midlands Radar; traffic density is modest by major-hub standards but the field's DHL/UPS overnight cargo sort-wave role concentrates a real nighttime traffic bank — see [Dispatch §6](dispatch.md).

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Not terrain-driven (flat field) — fly the published missed approach and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around re-enters the Class D CTR under East Midlands Radar for re-sequencing; not a high-density major-hub environment, but expect vectoring.
- **Go-around traps:** A **RWY 27 go-around must not commence any turn until past the runway end**, and must avoid overflying **Castle Donington** (sector 360°–065° magnetic, 0.65 NM radius from the localizer).

## 7. Landing runway / LDA & braking / vacate

- **LDA by runway:** RWY 09 **2,714 m** (180 m displaced threshold); RWY 27 **2,764 m** (131 m displaced threshold) — both reduced from the full 2,894 m paved length; cross-ref [Briefing §7](index.md).
- **Braking / vacate:** Landing traffic on **RWY 27 must not vacate via Taxiway S without first requesting ATC permission** — it is the normal departure route on that runway. Rapid-exit taxiway detail otherwise not confirmed in reachable sources. 🟧
- **Runway-excursion watch:** 🟧 Displaced thresholds reduce usable LDA versus the full paved length — confirm required landing distance for type/weight against the LDA figures above, not the TORA. Grass verges beside taxiways/aprons are weak and liable to waterlogging (not runway-surface itself, but a nearby ground hazard).

## 8. Taxi-in routing to terminal & hot spots

- **Typical taxi-in by runway → terminal:** Taxi per ATC/Ground instruction to the assigned stand; VAMSYS mirror gives taxi-in time as **n/a** 🟧 (not set) for this field.
- **Hot spots / tight taxiways:** 🟥 Expect **other aircraft holding at intersections H, M, S and W** on Taxiway A — visual acquisition can be difficult in poor visibility or at night; maintain wingtip-separation vigilance and hold/request marshaller assistance if in doubt. Runway intersections **M and S are closed to aircraft larger than Code C.** The **Maintenance Area and Taxiway M south of M3 are not controlled by ATC** — expect uncontrolled vehicle/aircraft movement if routed near this area.
- **Stand/gate notes:** See [Dispatch §3](dispatch.md) — widebody/freighter-specific stand assignment not detailed in the public AIP; expect Airport Authority/handling-agent-directed allocation.

## 9. Arrival frequency sequence

- **Sequence:** **East Midlands Radar 126.180 (H24) / Director 120.130 (as directed by ATC) → East Midlands Tower 124.005 (H24) → East Midlands Ground 121.905 (H24).** Take the assigned frequency and confirm current chart. (Frequencies from Briefing §8.)
- **Approach/Departure control:** **East Midlands Radar** is the single facility for both approach and the Lower Airspace Radar Service (134.180) at this field — no separate area-control handoff is described for the terminal environment; London FIR (EGTT) context beyond the CTR is not covered by a dedicated brief in this library (see [Airspace — Europe](../../../../airspace/europe.md) for general context only). 🟧

## 10. Gotchas

- **RWY 09 = CAT I/LTS CAT I ceiling — never plan or expect a CAT II/III arrival there.**
- **RWY 27 = the field's only CAT II/IIIB runway** — the low-visibility contingency plan lives here, not on RWY 09.
- A **late wind-driven runway swap from 27 to 09** in marginal visibility is a real minima trap, not just an energy trap.
- **Building-induced turbulence/wind-shear** landing RWY 09 in strong southerly winds.
- **Do not vacate RWY 27 via Taxiway S** without ATC permission — it is the runway's normal departure route.
- Circling-area obstacles (Ratcliffe-on-Soar chimney, temporary cranes) — verify current obstacle NOTAM before any circling approach.
- Avoid the final turn onto RWY 27 approach over the village of **Kegworth**.
- **No general curfew** here — but a delayed Chapter-3-noncompliant arrival after 2300 (2200 summer) is restricted; modern equipment is unaffected.
