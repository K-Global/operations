## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** 🟥 **PPR (prior permission required) for all flights, at all times** — a standing requirement, not an occasional restriction. Filing a flight plan does **not** constitute permission. Office-hours slots (0900–1700 / 0800–1600) via **Airport Coordination Ltd (ACL)**; outside office hours, or for military/GA/business aviation, permission is via **Samson Aviation** in advance of each individual movement.
- **Curfew / night restriction:** 🟧 The AIP text reached this pass confirms **training-flight-hour restrictions** (Mon–Sat 0730–2300, Sun 1000–2300) but does **not** explicitly confirm a scheduled-service night curfew. A commonly cited ~2300–0600 curfew with emergency/medical/state exemptions is **tier-4 sourced only** — flagged pending primary-source confirmation (e.g. the Newcastle Airport Noise Action Plan 2024–2028). Do not rely on this as a confirmed hard restriction for scheduling purposes without further verification.
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not independently confirmed for EGNT specifically. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The **PPR requirement is the dominant scheduling guardrail** at this field, more so than any curfew — every movement, scheduled or otherwise, needs confirmed PPR/slot status before dispatch; build this into standard turnaround planning rather than treating it as an exception-handling process.
