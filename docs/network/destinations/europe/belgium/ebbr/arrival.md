- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None. Flat Flemish/Brabant plateau at 184 ft AMSL — no close-in high terrain relevant to any arrival path.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Runway configuration is federally mandated and can be one of six combinations** — never assume a fixed landing runway; confirm via ATIS/batc.be, and watch for a late configuration change ahead of a wind shift.
- **Displaced thresholds exist on every runway end** — confirm usable LDA for the assigned runway.
- **Reverse thrust is banned except when safety-required** — confirmed operator policy; brief crews before landing and plan the rollout accordingly.
- **Autumn/winter fog** can reduce visibility with limited warning — check current LVP status at planning.
- **Field's runways are physically shared with the adjacent Melsbroek military airfield (EBMB)** — be aware of possible military traffic on approach/taxi-in.
- **Night-slot cap** — do not plan a schedule that relies on an unassigned post-23:00 landing slot.
