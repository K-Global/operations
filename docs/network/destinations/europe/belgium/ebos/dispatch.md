## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** Not detailed in the reachable AIP extract; the field's infrastructure is organised around Apron 1/2/3 (passenger/GA/cargo mix) rather than a multi-terminal complex. 🟧
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable — EBOS carries no assigned K Global role.
- **International arrivals / CBP hall:** Customs/immigration confirmed **H24** `[AIP Belgium AD 2 EBOS]`; hall-specific detail not in the reachable extract. 🟧
- **Notes:** General aviation handling on Apron 3 available upon request; for all non-home-based aircraft 2–45.5 t MTOW without public ticketing, Apron 3 handling is **mandatory**. Between 07:00 (06:00) and 19:00 (18:00), North Sea Aviation Center facilities are accessible for all GA, private crew and passengers; outside these hours only for crew/passengers handled by them `[AIP Belgium AD 2 EBOS]`.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Apron 1 (PCN 101/R/D/W/T, highest strength on the field) and Apron 2 (PCN 86/F/C/W/T) are the higher-strength aprons. **B777-300-class aircraft taxiing to/from Apron 2 via TWY K must route via TWY C2** `[AIP Belgium AD 2 EBOS]` — brief this routing for any K Global widebody visit.
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Apron 3 (PCN 52/F/C/X/T) is the lowest-strength apron, oriented to GA/cargo handling — confirm suitability before planning a widebody onto it.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Not detailed beyond the three-apron structure. 🟧
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Not applicable — no assigned K Global operation.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** Apron 2 stands **230, 231, 232, 233 have no push-back service — self-manoeuvring only, max span width 43 m** `[AIP Belgium AD 2 EBOS]`. 🟥

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **None identified.** 🟩
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard movement ban, but a **noise/QC restriction 22:00–05:00 (21:00–04:00)**: aircraft >8,618 kg MTOW certified to ICAO Annex 16 Vol. I Ch. 2/3/5 are permitted only with **QC ≤12**; **QC >12 is forbidden outright** `[AIP Belgium AD 2 EBOS]`. 🟥
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not identified — no ATFM/slot regime found in the reachable extract.
- **Commercial impact:** Any K Global type considered for this field would need its certified QC value confirmed against the ≤12 threshold before a night movement (22:00–05:00/21:00–04:00) could be planned.

## 11. Curfew & noise-charge commercial impact

- **Noise-charge regime:** Training aircraft <2,000 kg MTOW without a noise certificate ≤76 dB(A) (Annex 16 Vol. I Part II) incur an **"environmental surcharge"** on the airport-charges invoice `[AIP Belgium AD 2 EBOS]`; exact scheduled-service noise-charge structure not detailed in the reachable extract.
- **Curfew infringement consequence:** A movement with QC >12 in the 22:00–05:00 (21:00–04:00) window is **forbidden outright**, not merely surcharged — a hard compliance restriction. 🟥
- **Scheduling guardrails:** If EBOS is ever added to the K Global network, confirm the certified QC value for the assigned type before planning any movement inside the 22:00–05:00 (21:00–04:00) window.
