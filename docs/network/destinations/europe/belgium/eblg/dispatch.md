## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** **None identified** — no evidence of IATA slot coordination found in reachable sources. 🟩
- **Curfew / night restriction:** **None — Liège operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with no noise-based movement restriction**, a deliberate and well-documented operating model that has made it one of Europe's top cargo hubs `[Air Cargo Week; TIACA, retrieved 2026-07-26]`. 🟩
- **CTOT / flow control:** Not confirmed; the field's comparatively lower traffic density (cargo-dominant, no scheduled passenger service as of the sources reviewed) suggests low ATFM exposure relative to the Brussels/Amsterdam core, but this is not independently confirmed. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** The **absence of a curfew/slot regime is a genuine commercial advantage** at this field — schedule flexibility around the clock, unlike EBBR. The standing caution is simply that "no curfew" does not mean "quiet" — expect active night-freight ramp/taxi traffic at any hour.
