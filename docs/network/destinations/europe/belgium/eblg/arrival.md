## 10. Gotchas

- **No curfew, but do not assume a quiet overnight field** — Liège runs genuine 24/7 freighter traffic; brief for active night-ramp/taxi activity on any late/early arrival.
- **Secondary runway (05L/23R) is notably shorter (2,340 m)** than the main pair — confirm suitability if assigned.
- **Displaced threshold on the 05R end (236 m)** — confirm usable LDA before committing to a short-runway plan.
- **Field elevation (659 ft) is higher and more inland than the Belgian coastal/Brabant fields** — do not assume identical fog/weather climatology to EBBR/EBOS.
- **Passenger-handling readiness is uncertain** given the field's current no-scheduled-passenger-service status — confirm ahead of any K Global passenger operation.
