## 6. Load factors & seasonality

- **Demand peaks:** Strong leisure/tourism demand year-round; GCLP is a base for **Binter Canarias, Canaryfly, Ryanair, Norwegian Air Shuttle and Vueling** `[Wikipedia]`.
- **Day-of-week / seasonal pattern:** A notable **winter-season charter pattern** exists to Cape Verde and Gambia (TUI fly Germany/Nordic winter operations) `[Wikipedia]` — general seasonal-leisure indicator, not independently confirmed for the K Global operation specifically. 🟧
- **Cargo / belly capacity:** Not confirmed. 🟧
- **Connection banks:** Not confirmed — GCLP is a spoke, not a K Global connection point; note its role as an inter-island connecting point for Binter Canarias/Canaryfly traffic, relevant to overall airport congestion rather than our own network.
