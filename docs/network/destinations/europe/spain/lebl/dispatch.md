## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** LEBL operates **two terminals roughly 4 km apart**: **T1** (opened 2009, the larger and newer terminal, primary base for the field's dominant carrier and most other airline traffic) and **T2** (the older complex, comprising areas 2A/2B/2C, primarily used by low-cost carriers).
- **Our operation uses:** 🟧 Not yet confirmed — depends on handling-agent and gate assignment at contract.
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear passport control in the assigned terminal; hours confirmed H24 at AIP level.
- **Notes:** 🟧 **T1 and T2 are not connected airside** — a free inter-terminal bus runs every 7–10 minutes (roughly 14-minute journey), but any connection crossing the two terminals is a landside transfer, not an airside walk. Relevant to any onward network connection routed through LEBL.

## 3. Stands / gates (by code letter & our fleet)

- **Widebody (Code E/F) stands:** Present across Ramps 1–3, 9–17 and 30/32; exact current widebody gate assignment for our operation not confirmed. 🟧
- **Stands NOT usable by our types:** Narrowbody-oriented stands in the older T2 apron areas are not sized for Code E/F — do not plan a widebody there.
- **Remote / hardstand positions:** Ramp 0 is reserved exclusively for general/business aviation, subject to declared capacity; not applicable to scheduled widebody service.
- **Contact vs remote for our arrivals:** Expect a contact widebody gate for a scheduled destination turn; confirm with handling on the day.
- **Ground-movement stand caveats:** The AIP-flagged runway-holding-position siting anomalies near RWY 02 and RWY 06L (§13 of the Briefing) affect ground routing for Code D+ aircraft through specific taxiways — see [Briefing §13](index.md) and the Departure/Arrival taxi sections.

## 4. Slots, curfew & CTOT regime

- **Slot coordination level:** Not independently confirmed this pass; a **Local Schedule Coordination Office** and real-time (day-of-operation) slot process exist via the Airport Operations Center. 🟧
- **Curfew / night restriction:** No hard night curfew found in reachable sources. A **summer (Jun–Sep) daytime restriction bars arrivals for aircraft MTOW ≤ 15,000 kg between 0700–1130 LT** — this does not affect K Global widebody operations. 🟧
- **CTOT / flow control:** Full **A-CDM** with EUROCONTROL **FAM** (Flight Activation Monitoring) — flight plans must stay updated (TOBT/EOBT) from three hours before EOBT or risk automatic suspension. The LEBL operations office may also **refuse a flight plan whose EOBT/ETA does not match the assigned airport slot**. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** Slot/flight-plan-timing alignment is a hard gate here — a mismatched EOBT/ETA risks outright non-acceptance of the flight plan, not just a delay. A-CDM/TOBT accuracy is the operative lever for on-time pushback.

## 5. Handling agents & turnaround

- **Handling agent(s):** Aviapartner, Groundforce BCN and Menzies Aviation operate ramp handling at LEBL; the agent contracted for our operation is not yet confirmed. 🟧
- **Typical turnaround time for our type:** Not independently confirmed; VAMSYS mirror gives **taxi-in 14 min / taxi-out 18 min** for planning purposes `[VAMSYS mirror 2026-07-25]` — build the gate turn around these plus standard widebody service times.
- **Services:** Push-back, GPU/PCA, potable water/lav, catering and cabin services expected at a major-hub Code E/F stand; **powerback (reverse-thrust exit) is prohibited** at all stands.
- **Turnaround risk items:** A-CDM/TOBT accuracy directly affects sequencing; the AIP-flagged ground-taxi complexity near RWY 02/06L (§13 of the Briefing) can add unplanned taxi time; any inter-terminal (T1 ↔ T2) connection adds a landside transfer.

## 9. De-icing provisioning & season

- **Availability:** **Available** — provided **by the handling agent**, not a dedicated airport-run remote de-icing pad; no fixed pad location confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Season:** Published snow plan applies **15 Nov–15 Mar**; GRF (Global Reporting Format) runway-condition reporting year-round despite the mild coastal climate.
- **Provisioning:** Handling-agent-provided; specific fluid type/holdover throughput not confirmed this pass. 🟧
- **Commercial impact:** De-icing is a low-frequency event at this coastal field, but throughput could be a constraint precisely because it is handling-agent-provided rather than a dedicated high-capacity facility — worth a planning check ahead of any winter operation. See [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md).
