## 5. Approach hazards (terrain / CFIT / specific threats)

- **CFIT / terrain:** 🟩 None of operational significance. Flat coastal plain at 242 ft AMSL; the nearest high ground (Wicklow Mountains, order of 3,000 ft) lies well south of any normal EIDW arrival path.
- **Specific threats:** 🟥 **Atlantic-facing wind exposure** — gusty and crosswind conditions are the field's defining approach hazard, not an exceptional case; brief the crosswind limit every time, particularly for a 16/34 assignment. Wake-turbulence spacing on the parallel-runway pair is a standard consideration during simultaneous operations.
- **Airspace / traffic:** 🟧 High traffic density as Ireland's principal gateway under radar control (Shannon FIR / Dublin Approach); moderate, not extreme, compared with the largest continental hubs — brief for possible vectoring/sequencing at peak banks.

## 6. Missed approach watch-items

- **Terrain-driven MAP notes:** Not a driver (flat coastal plain) — fly the published MAP and verify climb-gradient/turn on the current chart.
- **Re-sequencing environment:** A go-around returns you to Dublin's dense, radar-controlled traffic flow — expect vectoring and possible re-sequencing, especially at peak banks or during a wind-driven configuration change.
- **Go-around traps:** Wind/gust handling through the go-around and possible late configuration changes (28↔10, or into 16/34) are the standing traps at this field, more than any terrain or airspace-structure factor.

## 10. Gotchas

- **Wind is the standing arrival consideration at this field** — Atlantic exposure means gusty/crosswind conditions should be expected as routine, not exceptional; brief the crosswind limit every time.
- **Late configuration changes (28↔10, or into 16/34) driven by a wind shift** are a real possibility — watch the ATIS wind trend on the descent.
- **RWY 16/34 doubles as a daytime taxiway** — do not assume it is available as a landing runway without confirming the active configuration with ATC.
- **The North Runway (10L/28R) carries a night-time operating restriction** tied to its planning conditions — a very late arrival that might depend on it should be planned with a confirmed-status check; the older runway (10R/28L) is not subject to the same restriction.
- **Ireland is outside the Schengen Area** — all non-Common-Travel-Area passengers clear Irish immigration on arrival; not a flight-deck item but relevant to connection-time expectations.
