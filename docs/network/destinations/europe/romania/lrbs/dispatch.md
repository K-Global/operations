## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single historic 1952-era terminal building (central dome, three-wing "propeller" design) — protected as a historic monument and **cannot be structurally expanded**, capping practical throughput at roughly 600,000 passengers/year and one departure per 25 minutes.
- **Our operation uses:** Not applicable — LRBS is not currently in the K Global network. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** Reported available with customs/immigration H24 by the field's ground handler; not primary-confirmed. 🟧
- **Notes:** A new terminal has been publicly discussed for construction by 2035 given the structural limits of the historic building; status/timeline not confirmed this pass. 🟧
