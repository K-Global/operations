## 2. Terminals & concourses

- **Terminal layout:** A single passenger terminal — arrivals hall (10,812 m², opened 22 May 2008), departures hall (16,150 m², opened 15 May 2009) and a connecting building (opened November 2009). A further extension (7,200 m², adding three gates for a total of 12) opened 31 May 2024.
- **Our operation uses:** Not independently confirmed — the field is single-terminal, so no concourse-selection decision is expected. 🟧
- **International arrivals / CBP-equivalent hall:** International arrivals clear passport control in the single arrivals hall; Romania's Schengen air-border status should be reconfirmed for current-day passenger-flow planning. 🟧
- **Notes:** A second terminal (~40,000 m²) has been publicly discussed as a future development — status/timeline not confirmed this pass. 🟧
