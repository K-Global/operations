## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Ezeiza FIR covers **east-central Argentina and the Buenos Aires metropolitan area**, including the national capital and its dense terminal airspace. Argentina is divided into **five FIRs — Ezeiza (SAEF), Córdoba (SACF), Mendoza (SAMF), Comodoro Rivadavia (SAVF) and Resistencia (SARR)** — each with its own EANA Search-and-Rescue Coordination Sub-Centre. Ezeiza is the busiest of the five by traffic volume, containing the country's principal international gateway.
- **Confirmed containment:** 🟩 **SAEZ (Ezeiza Intl–Ministro Pistarini)** and **Aeroparque Jorge Newbery (SABE, domestic)**, Buenos Aires' two principal commercial aerodromes, both sit inside Ezeiza FIR and share the Buenos Aires TMA.
- **Vertical structure:** 🟧 Controlled/Class A airspace above **FL195**, where EANA operates radar-surveillance control across the whole FIR (a modernisation programme distinct from older mixed procedural/radar coverage). Exact class-per-band and any upper/lower ATSU split not verified — confirm AIP ARGENTINA ENR 2.1.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Córdoba (SACF).** Central Argentina; the interior domestic FIR.
  - **N — Resistencia (SARR).** Northern Argentina, bordering Paraguay/Bolivia further north.
  - **S/SW — Comodoro Rivadavia (SAVF).** Patagonia; the long southern FIR.
  - **E — Montevideo (Uruguay), across the Río de la Plata.** 🟧 exact ANSP/ident not verified this pass.
- **Sectorisation:** EANA has implemented dedicated radar sectors within Ezeiza FIR as part of its modernisation programme; the transit-relevant fact is continuous radar coverage above FL195, with a parallel **TMA-Baires redesign** (Indra-led) reshaping the Buenos Aires terminal sectors — treat any specific sector/frequency detail as provisional pending that project's completion. 🟧

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Ezeiza FIR sits over the **Pampas** — flat, low-lying agricultural plain. No significant high terrain within the FIR itself.
- **Boundary terrain:** the Andes rise to the **west**, well outside Ezeiza FIR (in the Mendoza/Comodoro Rivadavia FIR system) — not a factor for a Buenos Aires-area transit or arrival.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟩 not a constraint — low terrain throughout the FIR removes any driftdown/MORA concern.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟧 relevant on occasional southern-hemisphere winter cold snaps at SAEZ; see the airport brief and OM E Cold Weather Operations if applicable.
