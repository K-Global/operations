# Colombo (VCCF) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — Sri Lanka and a very large **procedural/oceanic extent over the central Indian Ocean** south of the subcontinent, containing **Bandaranaike Intl Colombo (VCBI)** · **Parent area brief:** [Asia](../../asia.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Chennai (VOMF, India/AAI) N · Malé (VRMM, Maldives) W · 🟧 further southern/south-eastern oceanic boundary neighbour(s) not verified
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — verified vs AASL/Sri Lanka CAA AIP structure + ICAO APAC/BOBASIO and BOBTFRG material; southern oceanic boundary and exact class-per-band 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Colombo is a **mixed FIR**: radar/ADS-B-controlled over Sri Lanka and its near-coastal waters, opening into a **genuine procedural-oceanic block** over the central Indian Ocean that is one of the larger such extents in the region — a Bay-of-Bengal/Indian-Ocean gateway FIR, not a compact continental one. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for VCBI live in the airport briefing (§9). A **Sri Lanka Air Defence Clearance (ADC)** is required to enter the FIR's ADIZ (§10) — this is a live entry requirement, not a formality. Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Colombo FIR (VCCF) covers **Sri Lanka and a very large block of the central Indian Ocean** to the south — one of the larger FIRs in the South Asian region by area, reflecting its role as the oceanic gateway between the Bay of Bengal, the Arabian Sea and the wider Indian Ocean. 🟧 The precise southern/south-eastern lateral limit and the identity of the next FIR(s) beyond it (e.g. toward the Southern Indian Ocean / Australian oceanic sectors) are **not verified from public sources** — confirm at the AASL AIP before relying on this for anything beyond planning context.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Lower FIR** GND–FL245; **Upper Colombo FIR (UPR)** FL245–FL460 — both worked by the single **Colombo ACC** (no separately-branded upper unit). Confirm the exact split at **AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Chennai (VOMF, India, AAI).** The subcontinental boundary across the Palk Strait/Gulf of Mannar; Chennai's own Bay-of-Bengal oceanic sector sits further north-east (see the Asia area brief §3).
  - **W — Malé (VRMM, Maldives).** The Indian-Ocean boundary toward the Maldivian archipelago.
  - **S/SE — not verified 🟧.** The FIR's oceanic extent to the south is substantial; the adjacent oceanic FIR(s) beyond it are not confirmed from public sources — open item (§12).
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 not published in the sources reached; a hub transit is worked as a single **Colombo ACC** block near the coast with procedural handling further out to sea. Fly the current-AIRAC frequencies.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower- & upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Colombo ACC** ("Colombo Control", AASL) works the whole FIR GND–FL460; no separate UAC. Approach/terminal control for VCBI feeds off the ACC.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Colombo ACC; not a distinguishing factor for a hub transit remaining in controlled airspace.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 **radar (MSSR) to ~240 NM and ADS-B to ~330 NM** near Sri Lanka support 5 NM separation; 🟧 **procedural (position-report / ADS-C) control** applies once outside that coverage over the open ocean.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Colombo FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — part of the ICAO APAC **Bay-of-Bengal/Arabian-Sea/Indian-Ocean (BOBASIO)** regional RVSM implementation. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNP 4 / RNP 10** on the oceanic BOBASIO tracks; **RNAV/RNP** enroute over the landmass; **RNP 1** terminal; **RNP APCH** arrivals. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **Datalink (CPDLC/ADS-C):** 🟧 **ADS-C + CPDLC (FANS 1/A)** equip the oceanic/procedural sector, enabling integration with the surveillance-based direct-routing environment nearer Sri Lanka. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** required; **ADS-B Out** underpins the near-coast surveillance service.
- **8.33 kHz:** not an APAC requirement; standard 25 kHz VHF spacing applies.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Colombo ACC ("Colombo Control")** near the coast and handed to procedural/oceanic control further out; exact sectors and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Colombo ACC provides flight information service in the FIR.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO standard).
- **HF / SATCOM:** 🟧 required once outside VHF/radar range over the oceanic block — brief the applicable HF family and/or SATCOM/CPDLC login before entering the procedural sector.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 a **phased FRA implementation is underway in the Upper Colombo FIR (FL245–460)** — a notable exception to the generally fixed ATS-route structure of South/South-East Asia. Per the current ICAO APAC (BOBTFRG) material, direct-routing requests within the free-route concept require **prior permission at least seven (7) days in advance**; the programme is being rolled out in phases (ground-system/monitoring-function assessment first). Confirm current status and any operator eligibility at planning — this is a live, evolving procedure, not a settled structure.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available):** the fixed ATS-route network still governs the lower FIR and any FRA-excluded traffic; the BOBASIO oceanic tracks carry the Indian-Ocean/Bay-of-Bengal trunk flow.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary entry/exit fixes toward Chennai (N) and Malé (W), and the oceanic entry points onto the BOBASIO tracks; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Bandaranaike Intl Colombo (VCBI)** — the FIR's principal aerodrome and the network destination. Other Sri Lankan fields (e.g. Mattala Rajapaksa Intl) exist but have no dedicated brief in this network yet 🟧.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Sri Lanka's **central highlands** (Pidurutalagala, ~2,524 m / ~8,281 ft, and the surrounding hill country) are the only terrain of note; well below cruise levels and not a driftdown driver. The **overwhelming majority of the FIR is open ocean** — flat, low-MORA.
- **Boundary terrain:** none of operational significance across the Chennai or Malé boundaries.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 not a terrain constraint; the operative constraint over the oceanic majority of the FIR is **diversion range** (ETP/EDTO planning), not terrain — see §9.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not applicable — tropical FIR, no cold-soak/altimetry correction driver.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Air Defence Identification Zone (ADIZ) / Air Defence Clearance (ADC):** 🟥 **Sri Lanka operates an ADIZ over the FIR, and no flight may operate within, into or out of it without a valid ADC number** (Sri Lanka AIP ENR 5.2). The Area Controller (enroute) or tower controller (departures) coordinates with the Air Defence Command and Control Centre to obtain the ADC number, which is passed to the crew before entering the ADIZ; **an aircraft without a valid ADC is liable to interception** under the published interception procedure. An issued ADC is valid for the whole flight to destination; departure is permitted up to 30 minutes after the ADC is obtained, and a **fresh ADC is required if the departure slips more than 1 hour** past the flight-planned off-block time. Treat this as a **mandatory entry procedure**, not an optional formality — confirm current process at planning.
- **Military / danger / restricted areas:** 🟧 named areas are AIP/NOTAM-published; not individually catalogued here.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟩 none beyond the standard ADC process for a normal VCBI hub transit.

> **SUA currency:** re-check the ADC procedure, active danger/restricted-area status and any NOTAM-driven ADIZ change at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO standard — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance/filed route and levels, attempt the previous/adjacent frequency and **121.5**. 🟧 Confirm any Sri Lanka refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8/GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical near the coast (**request from ATC**); over the oceanic sector, the **ICAO general weather-deviation / strategic-offset method** (SLOP where published) applies as in any procedural oceanic airspace.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **SW monsoon (~May–Sep) and NE monsoon (~Nov–Mar)** — 🟥 deep convection, heavy rain and reduced visibility across Sri Lanka and the surrounding sea areas; the dominant enroute/terminal weather driver.
  - **Bay-of-Bengal tropical cyclones** — 🟧 the pre-monsoon (Apr–May) and post-monsoon (Oct–Dec) cyclone windows can affect the northern part of the FIR and the approach/transit corridors from the subcontinent; monitor SIGMET/tropical-cyclone advisories in season.
  - **Volcanic ash** — 🟩 no local exposure; a distant, low-probability wildcard from Indonesian/other regional volcanic arcs. See [`OM E — Volcanic Ash Avoidance`](../../../../flight-ops/volcanic-ash-avoidance.md).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, current **ADC/ADIZ procedure status**, SIGMET/AIRMET and tropical-cyclone advisories, the **Upper Colombo FIR FRA** phase/eligibility status, CPDLC/ADS-C login requirements for the oceanic segment, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
