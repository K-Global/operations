# Taipei (RCAA) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — Taiwan and its surrounding waters (Taiwan Strait, part of the East China Sea and the northern Bashi Channel/Luzon Strait approaches), containing **Taiwan Taoyuan Intl (RCTP)** · **Parent area brief:** [Asia](../../asia.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Shanghai (ZSHA, China) NW · Fukuoka (RJJJ, Japan) NE/E — via the Naha sector · Manila (RPHI, Philippines) S · Hong Kong (VHHK) SW

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Taipei is a **radar/ADS-B-controlled FIR** covering Taiwan and a busy block of surrounding water — no oceanic procedural segment on a normal RCTP hub transit, but it sits at one of East Asia's busiest crossroads, wedged between the mainland-Chinese, Japanese and Philippine FIR systems. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for RCTP live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions (NOTAMs, typhoon advisories, any regional airspace notices) are pulled at planning (§11). This brief states regional-context matters (ADIZ overlap, general East-Asia traffic-awareness items) **factually and neutrally**; any live assessment is carried in the Asia area brief §9, not repeated or editorialised here. Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Taipei FIR (RCAA) covers **Taiwan** and a substantial block of surrounding water — the **Taiwan Strait** (W), part of the **East China Sea** (N/NE) and the northern **Bashi Channel/Luzon Strait** approaches (S) toward the Philippines. It sits at a major East-Asia crossroads bordered by **four** other FIRs.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Taipei ACC works the FIR surface-to-upper-limit**; no separately-branded upper-area control centre confirmed. The cruise band for hub transits sits in the RVSM upper airspace (FL290–410). Confirm the exact band split at **AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Shanghai (ZSHA, China).** The northern boundary across the East China Sea toward the Chinese mainland trunk.
  - **NE / E — Fukuoka (RJJJ, Japan).** Reached via the Naha (Okinawa) sector of the Fukuoka FIR system — see the **[Tokyo / Fukuoka (RJJJ) FIR brief](tokyo-rjjj.md)**.
  - **S — Manila (RPHI, Philippines).** The southern boundary over the Bashi Channel/Luzon Strait.
  - **SW — Hong Kong (VHHK).** Across the SCS/Taiwan-Strait approaches — see the **[Hong Kong (VHHK) FIR brief](hong-kong-vhhk.md)**, which lists Taipei as its own eastern neighbour.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Taipei ACC is divided into internal sectors; the transit-relevant fact is the **single "Taipei Control" block** feeding the RCTP terminal area. Exact sector geometry is AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC frequencies.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower- & upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Taipei ACC** ("Taipei Control") works the whole FIR surface-to-upper-limit; no separate UAC confirmed. Approach/terminal control for RCTP feeds off the ACC. Flight plans and related movement messages for traffic into/via the Taipei FIR are addressed to Taipei ACC.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Taipei ACC for the FIR; not a factor for an IFR hub transit that stays in controlled airspace throughout.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 radar + ADS-B across the FIR's main route network and the RCTP TMA; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Taipei FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410 inclusive** — ICAO APAC RVSM; published semicircular flight-level assignments apply by direction (e.g. southbound FL300/FL340/FL380 among the published levels). See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 aircraft carrying RNAV equipment certified to **ICAO Doc 9613 (PBN Manual)** criteria may plan on the RNAV ATS-route network; terminal RNP SID/STAR and **RNP APCH** apply at RCTP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 availability/mandate band not individually verified for the Taipei FIR — confirm at ENR/NOTAM. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B** surveillance across the main route network.
- **8.33 kHz:** not an APAC requirement; standard 25 kHz VHF spacing applies.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Taipei ACC** across its sectors and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC (Shanghai, Fukuoka/Naha, Manila, Hong Kong) at the boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Taipei ACC provides flight information service in the FIR.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO standard); Mandarin also used domestically.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 the Taipei FIR is **fixed ATS-route / airway-structured**, not a continental FRA; confirm any user-preferred-route or flex-track programme at ENR.
- **Airway spine:** Taipei FIR is a **major East-Asia crossroads** — the north–south spine between the mainland-China/East-China-Sea network (via Shanghai) and the Philippines/SCS network (via Manila), and the east–west spine between Hong Kong/SCS and Japan/the Pacific (via Fukuoka/Naha). RCTP is a principal terminal for this flow.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the boundary entry/exit fixes feeding the RCTP terminal area and the trunk routes across the four adjacent FIRs; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Taiwan Taoyuan Intl (RCTP)** — the served K Global aerodrome. Hong Kong Intl (VHHH) sits across the boundary in **Hong Kong FIR (VHHK)** — not this FIR.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 **Taiwan's Central Mountain Range** runs the length of the island north–south, with peaks **exceeding 3,900 m (Yu Shan/Jade Mountain 3,952 m)** — a significant grid-MORA feature for any routing transiting the island's interior; the west-coast RCTP corridor and the surrounding water are comparatively low.
- **Boundary terrain:** no unusual high terrain immediately across the adjacent-FIR boundaries relevant to an over-water RCTP cruise transit; Taiwan's own mountain spine is the FIR's most significant terrain feature.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 relevant only to routings that transit the island's mountainous interior; on the west-coast/over-water hub corridor, diversion-field density (RCTP in-FIR, VHHH cross-boundary) mitigates the concern.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not applicable at RCTP — subtropical, no cold-temperature terminal correction driver.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO comms-failure procedure — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance / filed route and levels, and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Taiwan refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Typhoon season (~May–Nov, peak Jul–Sep)** — 🟥 the defining seasonal hazard; tropical cyclones in the western Pacific/SCS regularly disrupt RCTP and the wider Taiwan-Strait/East-China-Sea traffic system, forcing large re-routes and terminal closures. Plan weather-independent alternates and extra fuel/holding in season.
  - **Winter monsoon / reduced visibility** — north-east monsoon flow in the cooler months can bring low cloud/reduced visibility to the north and east coasts.
  - **Spring convective season** — pre-summer instability affecting the island and Strait.
  - **Volcanic ash:** 🟩 low direct exposure; the wider Japanese/Philippine volcanic arcs (across the Fukuoka and Manila boundaries) are distant wildcards, not a defining feature of this FIR. **VAAC Tokyo** covers the northern approach; pull VA advisories at planning if relevant.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, active danger/restricted-area status, tropical-cyclone warnings and SIGMET/AIRMET (typhoon, convection), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here. Re-check the Asia area brief §9 for any live regional-airspace advisory relevant to the East-China-Sea/Strait area.
