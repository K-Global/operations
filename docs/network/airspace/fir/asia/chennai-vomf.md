# Chennai (VOMF) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — southern India plus a large **Bay of Bengal oceanic sector**, containing **Chennai Intl (VOMM)** and **Kempegowda Intl/Bengaluru (VOBL)** · **Parent area brief:** [Asia](../../asia.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Kolkata (VECF) N · Mumbai (VABF) NW · Colombo (VCCF) S · Male (VRMF) SW (oceanic) · Yangon (VYYF) E (oceanic) · Kuala Lumpur (WMFC) SE (oceanic) · Jakarta (WIIF) SE (oceanic, distant)
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Chennai FIR has a **radar/ADS-B-controlled continental core** around VOMM/VOBL, but — like Mumbai on the Arabian Sea side — it also owns a **very large oceanic upper-airspace block over the Bay of Bengal**, extending toward Myanmar, Malaysia and Indonesia. It directly interfaces **Kolkata FIR** to the north (the other Bay-of-Bengal Indian FIR) and Colombo (Sri Lanka) to the south. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances (radar continental / procedural-oceanic as applicable). Terminal procedures for VOBL/VOMM live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions (NOTAMs, NE-monsoon/cyclone bulletins) are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Chennai FIR (VOMF) covers **southern India** (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and the southern Deccan) plus a **very large Bay of Bengal oceanic block**, extending east/south-east toward Myanmar (Yangon FIR), Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur FIR) and — at its furthest reach — the Indonesian (Jakarta FIR) boundary. This oceanic extent mirrors Mumbai's Arabian Sea responsibility on the opposite coast, making Chennai and Mumbai the two "oceanic" Indian FIRs against Delhi's purely continental one.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Chennai ACC works the FIR surface-to-upper-limit** continental side; the oceanic sector's vertical/procedural structure is a distinct operating environment. Confirm the exact band split at **AIP ENR 2.1**. Cruise levels for hub transits sit in the RVSM band (FL290–410).
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Kolkata FIR (VECF, India/AAI).** The other Bay-of-Bengal Indian FIR; AAI's Chennai contingency-plan material lists Kolkata as a directly-affected adjacent authority.
  - **NW — Mumbai FIR (VABF, India/AAI).** Land boundary across the southern Deccan; also listed as a directly-affected adjacent authority.
  - **S — Colombo FIR (VCCF, Sri Lanka CAA).** Confirmed adjacent authority in AAI's Chennai contingency-plan material.
  - **SW — Male FIR (VRMF, Maldives CAA).** Confirmed adjacent authority (oceanic), per the same source.
  - **E — Yangon FIR (VYYF, Myanmar DCA).** Confirmed adjacent authority (oceanic Bay of Bengal), per the same source.
  - **SE — Kuala Lumpur FIR (WMFC, Malaysia CAAM)** and **Jakarta FIR (WIIF, Indonesia AirNav)** — both confirmed adjacent authorities in AAI's Chennai contingency-plan material, reflecting the FIR's very large oceanic south-eastern reach.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Chennai ACC divides into continental sectors around VOMM/VOBL plus a distinct oceanic sector structure; exact geometry is AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC frequencies.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower- & upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Chennai ACC** ("Chennai Control", AAI) works the whole FIR surface-to-upper-limit continental side; no separate UAC. Approach/terminal control for VOMM and VOBL feeds off the ACC (VOBL sits under Bengaluru-area TMA procedures within the Chennai FIR). The oceanic sector is worked as a distinct operating environment within the same ACC organisation.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Chennai ACC/OCC (Oceanic Control Centre) for the respective continental/oceanic portions.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 radar + ADS-B over the continental core (part of AAI's nationwide multi-radar/ADS-B mosaic feeding Auto Track-III automation); 🟧 the oceanic sector's radar/ADS-B extension offshore is not confirmed — plan on a procedural, HF/ADS-C basis over open Bay-of-Bengal water unless current AIP data confirms otherwise.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Chennai FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410 inclusive** — India-wide RVSM (implemented 2003). See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** continental enroute; **RNP 1** terminal; **RNP APCH** arrivals (GAGAN SBAS-supported at VOMM and VOBL). 🟧 Oceanic-sector navigation basis (RNP 10/RNAV 10-equivalent, consistent with adjoining South-East-Asian oceanic airspace) is not confirmed in AAI public material — verify before planning an oceanic Chennai-FIR routing. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **ADS-C/CPDLC confirmed in oceanic use** — AAI operational/contingency material cites the **VOMF** AFTN/ADS-C logon address for the Chennai oceanic sector (e.g. on the Muscat–Chennai–Colombo contingency route), with **HF as the secondary/backup** means. Continental-sector CPDLC mandate scope is unconfirmed. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** required; **ADS-B Out mandated at/above FL290** (FL285 on named routes from Jun 2023) on designated continental PBN routes (DGCA/AAI AIP Supplement) — confirm applicability to any specific continental routing; oceanic-sector ADS-B coverage not confirmed.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟧 not confirmed as an India-wide mandate; standard 25 kHz VHF spacing believed to apply — confirm at AIP GEN 3.4.
- **Oceanic procedures:** 🟥 HF, SLOP and oceanic contingency procedures apply on the Bay-of-Bengal sector — see [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md) and [`OM E — ETOPS-EDTO Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/etops-edto.md) if the routing carries an extended-diversion-time segment.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Chennai ACC ("Chennai Control")** across its continental sectors and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC (Kolkata, Mumbai, Colombo) at the land/near boundary, or to the **Chennai Oceanic Control Centre** for the Bay-of-Bengal sector (with onward handoff toward Yangon/Kuala Lumpur/Jakarta); exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Chennai ACC/OCC provides flight information service in the FIR.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO standard).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air; HF as required over the oceanic sector.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 continental Chennai FIR is **fixed ATS-route / airway-structured** (part of India's ~210-route ATS network); the oceanic sector runs on established oceanic tracks/ATS routes rather than a European-style FRA. Confirm any flow-management programme at ENR/NOTAM.
- **Airway spine:** Chennai sits on the **south-India trunk** linking Mumbai/Bengaluru with the Bay-of-Bengal oceanic network, and is a **gateway toward Myanmar, Malaysia and Indonesia** on long-haul east-bound routings — a defining feature for K Global's South-East-Asian reach from the southern Indian hubs.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary entry/exit fixes feeding the VOMM and VOBL terminal areas, and the oceanic entry points toward Kolkata/Colombo/Male/Yangon/Kuala Lumpur/Jakarta; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Chennai Intl (VOMM)** and **Kempegowda Intl/Bengaluru (VOBL)** — the two K Global-served aerodromes in this FIR.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 **generally low near VOMM** (coastal plain); moderate terrain near **VOBL** on the Deccan plateau/Western Ghats fringe, well below cruise levels. Not a cruise-level factor at FL290+.
- **Boundary terrain:** not a significant factor toward Kolkata/Mumbai (plains/plateau) or the oceanic sector (open water).
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 the oceanic Bay-of-Bengal sector carries **sparser diversion coverage** than the continental core, particularly toward the Myanmar/Malaysia boundary — plan ETP/critical-point and driftdown analysis per OM E ETOPS-EDTO procedures for any qualifying oceanic segment.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not applicable — tropical/coastal, no cold-temperature terminal-correction driver.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military / danger areas:** 🟧 danger/restricted areas exist over the **Bay of Bengal**, including a **volcanic-exclusion sector around Barren Island** (Andaman Sea, India's only historically active volcano — periodic VAAC Darwin ash advisories to FL040–FL100+ have been issued in recent eruptive episodes); activation/status is **AIP/NOTAM-published** — confirm at planning.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟥 **prior DGCA/AAI overflight approval is required** for foreign operators transiting Indian FIRs (durable procedural requirement, per AAI contingency-plan material); no additional diplomatic sensitivity is expected on a normal VOMM/VOBL hub transit, but the wider Bay-of-Bengal/South-East-Asian oceanic reach carries standard regional-sensitivity considerations — re-check live bulletins (§11).
- **Restricted/prohibited pockets:** named areas and status are AIP/NOTAM-published — pull live at planning.

> **SUA currency:** re-check active danger/restricted-area status, the Barren Island volcanic-exclusion sector, and any Bay-of-Bengal bulletins at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO comms-failure procedure — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance/filed route and levels. 🟧 Confirm any Chennai-specific refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**; oceanic comms-failure follows the applicable oceanic/TIBA procedure.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical continental (**request the deviation from ATC**); the **ICAO general weather-deviation procedure** (offset and level change with position broadcast) applies over the oceanic sector where radar/CPDLC contact is degraded. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **North-East monsoon (~Oct–Dec)** — 🟥 the defining seasonal hazard; this is the **Bay-of-Bengal cyclone season**, bringing severe convective rainfall and flood risk to Chennai/VOMM — most notably the **December 2015 Chennai floods**, which shut the airport for roughly a week after the Adyar river overflowed onto the airfield. Monitor IMD/JTWC cyclone tracking and plan weather-independent alternates in season.
  - **SW monsoon (~Jun–Sep)** — affects the western/VOBL side more than the Tamil Nadu coast; convective rain and reduced visibility.
  - **Barren Island volcanic ash** — 🟧 intermittent eruptive activity (observed as recently as 2025–2026 per VAAC Darwin advisories, ash to FL040–FL100+) over the Andaman Sea/Bay-of-Bengal sector; **VAAC Darwin** is the responsible advisory centre — pull current VA advisories at planning for any oceanic routing that transits the Andaman area.
  - **Pre-monsoon heat/convection (Apr–Jun)** — afternoon thunderstorm build-up over the southern peninsula.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, active danger/restricted-area status (Bay of Bengal, Barren Island exclusion sector), NE-monsoon/cyclone SIGMET/AIRMET, **VAAC Darwin** volcanic-ash advisories, oceanic-sector NOTAMs, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
