# Dhaka (VGFR) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — Bangladesh, containing **Hazrat Shahjalal Intl Dhaka (VGHS)**; a continental FIR with a complex maritime/oceanic sliver over the northern Bay of Bengal · **Parent area brief:** [Asia](../../asia.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Kolkata (VECF, India/AAI) W · Yangon (VYYF, Myanmar) SE · 🟧 Indian FIR sector(s) N/NE not individually confirmed
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — verified vs CAAB AIP structure + ICAO APAC material; the FIR-delegation/reclaim picture and current ADIZ scope are live and flagged 🟧/🟥

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Dhaka is a **radar/ADS-B-controlled continental FIR** over Bangladesh's low-lying delta territory, with two defining structural features not seen in most of the region: **historically delegated airspace** over the territorial sea (parts of the FIR worked by Kolkata ACC, India, and Yangon ACC, Myanmar — §3), which Bangladesh is actively working to reclaim; and a **country-wide Air Defence Identification Zone (ADIZ)** requiring an **Air Defence Clearance (ADC)** on most routings (§10). Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for VGHS live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Dhaka FIR (VGFR) covers **Bangladesh** — a compact, low-lying deltaic territory surrounded by India on three sides and Myanmar (a short border) to the south-east, opening onto the **northern Bay of Bengal** to the south.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Dhaka ACC/FIC** is the notional controlling unit surface-to-upper-limit; confirm the exact band split at **AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **A structurally unusual FIR — historic delegation, now under reclaim:** 🟥 for decades, parts of the Dhaka FIR **over the territorial sea** have been worked by **Kolkata ACC (India)** and **Yangon ACC (Myanmar)** rather than by Dhaka itself, reflecting historic maritime-boundary disputes and radar-capacity constraints. **Bangladesh has since settled its maritime boundary disputes with Myanmar (2012) and India (2014)**, and CAAB is actively pursuing the **FIR-amendment process to reclaim full control** of the delegated airspace (per ICAO APAC ATM/SG working-paper material) — this is a **live, evolving structural picture**, not a settled one. Flight-plan, departure and delay messages for the delegated portion are still, per current published procedure, addressed to Dhaka ACC/FIC, which coordinates directly with Kolkata for descent into that sector.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **W — Kolkata (VECF, India, AAI).** The principal boundary; also the delegated-airspace interface (above).
  - **SE — Yangon (VYYF, Myanmar).** The short land/sea boundary toward Myanmar's Rakhine coast; also a historic delegation interface (above).
  - **N/NE — India** 🟧. Bangladesh is nearly enclosed by Indian territory/FIRs to the north and east (Assam/Tripura/Meghalaya sector, potentially a Kolkata sub-sector or the Guwahati sub-FIR); the exact designation is **not confirmed** from the sources reached — open item (§12).
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 not published in the sources reached; fly the current-AIRAC frequencies for the day's sector plan.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Controlling ATSU:** 🟩 **Dhaka ACC/FIC** ("Dhaka Control", CAAB) is the notional single unit surface-to-upper-limit; **parts of the FIR over the territorial sea are worked by Kolkata (India) and Yangon (Myanmar) ACC** under historic delegation (§3) — a structural fact unique among the FIRs in this network.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Dhaka ACC/FIC within its retained sectors.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/ADS-B coverage over the landmass; automation/surveillance modernisation has been reported as an ongoing CAAB programme — confirm current coverage at planning.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 not implemented; the Dhaka FIR is **fixed ATS-route structured**, consistent with the rest of South Asia.
- **Airway spine:** Dhaka sits on the trunk between the **Kolkata/eastern-India gateway** and the **Yangon/SE-Asia gateway**; published ATS routes including **P646, N895, M770, L524 and W112** cross the FIR (these carry a specific ADIZ exemption — §10).
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary fixes toward Kolkata (W) and Yangon (SE); the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Hazrat Shahjalal Intl Dhaka (VGHS)** — the network destination. Chittagong (Shah Amanat Intl) and Sylhet (Osmani Intl) are Bangladesh's other international gateways; no dedicated brief exists for either in this network yet 🟧.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Bangladesh is an almost entirely **low-lying river delta**; the **Chittagong Hill Tracts** in the south-east are the only notable relief (modest hills, well below cruise levels). Not a driftdown/cruise-level constraint.
- **Boundary terrain:** none of operational significance across the Kolkata or Yangon boundaries on a normal VGHS routing.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟩 not a constraint in this FIR.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not applicable — tropical/subtropical FIR.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Air Defence Identification Zone (ADIZ) / Air Defence Clearance (ADC):** 🟥 Bangladesh has established an ADIZ over the **entire country plus a substantial block of airspace off the south coast**, extending into the adjoining Indian (Kolkata/VECF) FIR. **Any aircraft intending to fly into, through or within the Bangladesh ADIZ must obtain an ADC (Air Defence Clearance) number** — normally at least **10 minutes before entry**; where that lead time is not feasible for a departure from an adjacent FIR, the ADC must be obtained **before departure**. Flights remaining on **ATS routes P646, N895, M770, L524 or W112** are **exempt** from the ADC requirement **unless they deviate toward Bangladeshi landmass**. The ADC number is issued via **AFTN** or may be requested directly from the **Bangladesh Air Force**. Treat this as a **mandatory entry procedure** on any routing that leaves the exempt trunk routes — confirm current process and route-exemption list at planning.
- **Delegated-airspace interface (§3):** 🟧 the historic Kolkata/Yangon delegation over the territorial sea is a live, changing structural item — confirm current delegation status alongside the ADC requirement.
- **Military / danger / restricted areas:** 🟧 named areas are AIP/NOTAM-published; not individually catalogued here.

> **SUA currency:** re-check the ADC/ADIZ requirement (including the route-exemption list), the delegated-airspace status, and any active danger/restricted-area NOTAM at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO standard — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance/filed route and levels, attempt the previous/adjacent frequency and **121.5**. 🟧 Confirm any Bangladesh refinement at AIP ENR 1.8/GEN.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Pre-monsoon "Nor'westers" (~Mar–May)** — 🟥 severe, fast-developing convective squall lines with hail and severe turbulence, a well-known Bangladesh/eastern-subcontinent seasonal hazard; the dominant pre-monsoon enroute/terminal weather driver.
  - **SW monsoon (~Jun–Oct)** — 🟥 sustained deep convection, heavy rain and low visibility; the primary wet-season hazard.
  - **Bay-of-Bengal tropical cyclones** — 🟧 the coastal/southern part of the FIR is exposed to pre- and post-monsoon Bay-of-Bengal cyclone tracks; monitor SIGMET/tropical-cyclone advisories in season.
  - **GNSS interference** — 🟧 the Asia area brief notes reported **GPS jamming/spoofing on the northern China–Bangladesh overflight corridor (airway N895)**; cross-check DME/DME/IRU and follow OM E GNSS-interference guidance if anomalies are observed.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, current **ADC/ADIZ status and route-exemption list**, the **Kolkata/Yangon delegated-airspace status**, SIGMET/AIRMET (Nor'wester/monsoon convection, tropical cyclone), GNSS-interference advisories, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
