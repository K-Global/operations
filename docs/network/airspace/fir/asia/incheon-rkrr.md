Incheon is a **radar/ADS-B-controlled continental-plus-littoral FIR** covering South Korea and its near seas — no oceanic procedural segment on a normal RKSI hub transit. The FIR sits at a busy NE-Asia crossroads with **four** adjacent FIRs, including a published cross-boundary ATS delegation to Shanghai ACC over part of the Yellow Sea and a northern boundary against the Pyongyang FIR — noted here strictly as a factual boundary fact; any live overflight-risk assessment is carried in the Asia area brief §9, not repeated here. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for RKSI live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions (NOTAMs, SIGMETs, winter weather) are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Incheon FIR (RKRR) covers the **Republic of Korea** and extends over the surrounding **Yellow Sea (west)** and **East Sea/Sea of Japan (east)** waters to the FIR boundary. It sits at a compact but very busy NE-Asia crossroads, bordered by **four** other FIRs.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Incheon ACC works the FIR surface-to-upper-limit**; no separately-branded upper-area control centre confirmed. The cruise band for hub transits sits in the RVSM upper airspace (FL290–410). Confirm the exact band split at **AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Pyongyang (ZKKP, North Korea).** The northern land boundary of the Korean Peninsula. 🟩 Stated here as a **factual geographic boundary only**; any overflight-avoidance / live-security assessment for the Pyongyang FIR is carried in the **Asia area brief §9** and is not re-derived in this file.
  - **NW — Shenyang (ZYSH, China).** A boundary near the quad-point shared with Incheon, Shanghai and Pyongyang, over the northern Yellow Sea/Bohai approaches.
  - **SW — Shanghai (ZSHA, China).** 🟧 A **published delegated-ATS sector** exists here: air traffic services within a defined horizontal area and a vertical scope of **FL240 through FL410 inclusive** are delegated to **Shanghai ACC** — a durable regional quirk relevant to frequency/handoff expectations near this boundary, not merely a straight geographic handoff. Confirm the exact delegated-area coordinates at the current AIP.
  - **E / SE — Fukuoka (RJJJ, Japan).** The eastern boundary over the East Sea/Sea of Japan and toward the Tsushima Strait — see the **[Tokyo / Fukuoka (RJJJ) FIR brief](tokyo-rjjj.md)**.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Incheon ACC is divided into internal sectors; the transit-relevant fact is the **single Incheon-ACC block** feeding the RKSI terminal area, including the delegated Shanghai-ACC sector at the SW boundary. Exact sector geometry is AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC frequencies.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower- & upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Incheon ACC** (KOCA) works the whole FIR surface-to-upper-limit, with the noted delegated exception toward Shanghai ACC (§3). No separate UAC confirmed. Approach/terminal control for RKSI feeds off the ACC.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Incheon ACC for the FIR; not a factor for an IFR hub transit that stays in controlled airspace throughout.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 radar + ADS-B across the FIR's main route network and the RKSI TMA; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.
- **Transition altitude / level:** 🟧 South Korea publishes a nationwide **transition altitude of 14,000 ft and transition level FL140** — notably higher than many neighbouring states; brief this on descent into RKSI. Confirm current value at ENR.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Incheon FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** on all ATS routes within the Incheon FIR; **2,000 ft** vertical separation applies between non-RVSM-compliant aircraft and all others in the stratum. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV / RNP** enroute; terminal RNP SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** arrivals at RKSI. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 availability/mandate band not individually verified for the Incheon FIR — confirm at ENR/NOTAM. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B** surveillance across the main route network.
- **8.33 kHz:** not an APAC requirement; standard 25 kHz VHF spacing applies.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Incheon ACC** across its sectors — including the delegated Shanghai-ACC sector at the SW boundary (§3) — and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC (Pyongyang, Shenyang, Shanghai, Fukuoka) at the boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Incheon ACC provides flight information service in the FIR.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO standard); Korean also used domestically.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 the Incheon FIR is **fixed ATS-route / airway-structured**; a **Free Routing Concept** covering Incheon and Fukuoka FIRs has been studied at the ICAO-APAC level — confirm current implementation status at ENR/NOTAM before treating direct-routing capability as standing policy.
- **Airway spine:** the FIR sits on the **NE-Asia trunk** linking the Yellow-Sea/China network (via Shenyang/Shanghai) with Japan and the Pacific (via Fukuoka). RKSI is a principal terminal for this flow.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the boundary entry/exit fixes feeding the RKSI terminal area and the trunk routes across the four adjacent FIRs; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Incheon Intl (RKSI)** — the served K Global aerodrome. Seoul's other field, **Gimpo (RKSS)**, sits within the same FIR but has no OM C brief yet 🟧.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 low around RKSI and the west-coast Incheon/Seoul corridor. Korea's higher interior terrain (the **Taebaek mountain range**, running the eastern side of the peninsula) sits well below cruise levels. Not a cruise-level factor at FL290+.
- **Boundary terrain:** no unusual high terrain immediately across the adjacent-FIR boundaries relevant to an RKSI cruise transit.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint on the RKSI corridor; diversion-field density around Seoul/Incheon mitigates any escape-route concern on that routing.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟥 **applicable** — Korean winters bring sustained sub-zero temperatures; apply cold-temperature altimetry corrections at RKSI per [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md) and the airport briefing.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO comms-failure procedure — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance / filed route and levels, and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Korean refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Winter operations (~Nov–Mar)** — 🟥 the defining seasonal hazard: sustained cold, snow and icing affect RKSI ground operations and approach; plan de/anti-icing and cold-temperature altimetry corrections. See [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md).
  - **Spring dust (yellow dust / Asian dust)** — seasonal dust transport from the continental interior can reduce visibility at RKSI in spring.
  - **Summer monsoon (~Jun–Sep)** — heavy rain, convective activity and occasional typhoon-remnant systems affecting the peninsula.
  - **Winter jet-stream CAT** — 🟧 possible clear-air turbulence on the winter NE-Asia trunk; see the Asia area brief §11.
  - **Volcanic ash:** 🟩 low direct exposure; distant Japanese-arc activity is a wildcard, not a defining feature of this FIR.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, active danger/restricted-area status, SIGMET/AIRMET (winter weather, dust, convection), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here. Re-check the Asia area brief §9/§11 for any live overflight or seasonal-security picture relevant to the northern boundary.
