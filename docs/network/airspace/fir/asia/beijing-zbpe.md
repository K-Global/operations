# Beijing (ZBPE) — FIR Briefing

**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — structure verified vs public ICAO-APAC/CAAC-derived material; exact boundary decimals and ACC sector split 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Beijing is a **radar/ADS-B-controlled, metric-level FIR** at the northern hub of China's continental network — dense, flow-controlled, and the site of the country's largest-ever airspace restructuring (the 2019 Daxing opening). Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances and any active flow-control restriction. Terminal procedures for ZBAA live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Beijing FIR (ZBPE) covers **north-central China** — the Beijing municipality, Tianjin and Hebei — extending toward the **Mongolian border** in the north/northwest. It is the political and one of the busiest traffic capitals of the national network, feeding the Beijing Capital (ZBAA) and Daxing (ZBAD, out of this brief's scope) metroplex.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 Beijing ACC is understood to work the FIR surface-to-upper-limit as a single ATSU (the regional norm across China's continental FIRs); no separately-branded upper-area unit identified in public material — confirm at **AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NE — Shenyang (ZYSH, CAAC).** Toward Northeast China/Manchuria; the corridor feeding the Bohai/Yellow Sea and, further east, the Incheon/Pyongyang quad-boundary area referenced in regional planning material.
  - **SE — Shanghai (ZSHA, CAAC).** The trunk toward the East China coast and the country's busiest single FIR.
  - **S — Wuhan (ZHWH, CAAC).** The central-China corridor south.
  - **W/SW — Lanzhou (ZLHW, CAAC).** Toward Northwest China and the interior.
  - **N — Ulaanbaatar (ZMUB, Mongolia).** 🟧 The international boundary north of the Chinese FIR network — unconfirmed exact contact point; treated as the nearest non-Chinese neighbour on this heading.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Beijing ACC is divided into internal sectors around the Capital/Daxing metroplex; the **2019 Daxing-driven restructuring** adjusted over 200 airways and ~4,700 km of new route mileage across an area running roughly China–Mongolia border to Guilin (N–S) and western Inner Mongolia to Dalian (E–W) — the largest single airspace adjustment in Chinese civil-aviation history. Exact current sector geometry is AIRAC-dependent.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower- & upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟧 **Beijing ACC**, under the **North China Regional ATMB** (one of CAAC's seven regional air traffic management bureaus), works the FIR surface-to-upper-limit; terminal/approach control for ZBAA feeds off the ACC.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Beijing ACC; not a distinct factor for an IFR hub transit remaining in controlled airspace.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 continuous **radar + ADS-B** across the developed metroplex; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Beijing FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410 inclusive**, assigned in **METRIC levels** (China implemented metric RVSM 22 Nov 2007); ATC clears a metric flight level and the crew reads the **feet-equivalent off the approved China RVSM FLAS conversion table** and flies in feet. The metric↔feet transition normally occurs at the FIR boundary in the adjacent non-Chinese FIR. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5 / RNP** enroute; **RNP 1** terminal (SID/STAR); **RNP APCH** arrivals — the standard continental-China provision (not individually re-verified for ZBPE). See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC (FANS/ATN) available** on the regional trunk network; mandate band and login sector not confirmed for this FIR specifically. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out** mandated (China-wide provision underpinning the continental surveillance service).
- **8.33 kHz:** not a Chinese/APAC requirement; standard **25 kHz** VHF spacing applies.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Beijing ACC** across its internal sectors and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC (Shenyang, Shanghai, Wuhan, Lanzhou) at the boundary; exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Beijing ACC provides flight information service in the FIR.
- **Language:** 🟧 **Mandarin Chinese** primary; **English** available per ICAO requirement for international IFR traffic — confirm consistency outside the core metroplex sectors.
- **8.33 kHz:** not applicable; standard 25 kHz spacing.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 **not implemented** — China runs a fixed ATS-route/airway network with tactical vectoring, not continental FRA.
- **Airway spine:** Beijing sits at the **northern anchor** of China's internal trunk system, feeding the Beijing/Tianjin/Hebei metroplex from the Northeast (Shenyang), East (Shanghai), Central (Wuhan) and Northwest (Lanzhou) corridors; the 2019 restructuring reshaped much of this network around one-way/parallel-route design.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the boundary entry/exit fixes feeding the ZBAA terminal area; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Beijing Capital Intl (ZBAA)** — this brief's served airport. 🟧 Beijing Daxing Intl (ZBAD) also lies within this FIR/metroplex but is out of scope for this document set.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 the North China Plain around Beijing/Tianjin/Hebei is generally **low terrain**; not a cruise-level constraint. Higher ground rises toward the **Mongolian Plateau** on the FIR's northern/western fringe.
- **Boundary terrain:** no significant driftdown-relevant high terrain identified immediately across the adjacent FIR boundaries on the primary hub-transit headings.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟩 not a binding constraint in this FIR — contrast with the Kunming/Tibetan-plateau case (see the Kunming (ZPKM) FIR brief).
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟧 relevant at ZBAA in winter (northern-China terminal) — see [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md) and the ZBAA airport brief.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military / danger areas:** 🟥 nationally, **China's airspace is predominantly military-managed** — public estimates put roughly **70%+ historically under military control** with only a minority (~30% or less) available for civil use, a defining structural constraint behind the country's flow-control regime; Beijing's dense metroplex is a heavily coordinated case of this. Exact TSA/danger-area footprint is **AIP/NOTAM-published** — not individually catalogued here.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟧 China requires prior **overflight-permit/approval** for foreign operators (state-level requirement, not FIR-specific) — confirm at planning.
- **Restricted/prohibited pockets:** named areas and status are AIP/NOTAM-published — pull live at planning.

> **SUA currency:** re-check active restricted/danger-area status and any regional ATFM measures at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO standard — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance/filed route and levels, attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Beijing-specific refinement at AIP ENR 1.8/GEN.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued.
- **China metric-level contingency:** 🟧 any contingency level change inside this FIR must be coordinated in **metric** with ATC and cross-checked against the FLAS feet-equivalent.
- **China ATFM / flow control:** 🟧 expect **CTOT/MINIT slots, ground holding and short-notice flow restrictions** coordinated by the regional ATMB — plan for slot compliance; pull the day's ATFM measures at planning.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):** summer convective activity (thunderstorms) over the North China Plain; winter cold-temperature terminal corrections; occasional dust/haze episodes reducing visibility.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, active restricted/danger-area status, China regional ATFM measures (CTOT/MINIT), SIGMET/AIRMET, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned (metric-in-China) levels. Not stored here.
