# Kuala Lumpur (WMFC) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — Peninsular Malaysia, containing **Kuala Lumpur Intl–Sepang (WMKK)**, and the northern Malacca-Strait / Malay-Peninsula traffic corridor · **Parent area brief:** [Asia](../../asia.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Bangkok (VTBB, Thailand) N · Singapore (WSJC) S — incl. delegated cross-boundary sectors · Jakarta (WIIF, Indonesia) S/SW across the Malacca Strait · Kota Kinabalu (WBFC, Malaysia — East Malaysia/Sabah/Sarawak) E across the South China Sea
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — verified vs CAAM AIP ENR structure + ICAO APAC framework; exact ACC sector split and the Singapore-delegation boundary detail flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Kuala Lumpur is a **radar/ADS-B-controlled continental-plus-littoral FIR** — no true oceanic procedural segment on a normal hub transit, covering Peninsular Malaysia and the northern Malacca Strait, tightly interfaced with the Singapore FIR to the south via a published cross-delegation arrangement. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for WMKK live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions (NOTAMs, danger-area activation, SIGMETs) are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Kuala Lumpur FIR (WMFC) covers **Peninsular (West) Malaysia** and the **northern Malacca Strait**, extending toward the Thai and Indonesian boundaries. It is distinct from the **Kota Kinabalu FIR (WBFC)**, which covers East Malaysia (Sabah/Sarawak, Borneo) across the South China Sea — the two Malaysian FIRs are separately published and separately worked.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Lumpur ACC works the FIR surface-to-upper-limit**; no separately-branded upper-area control centre. The cruise band for our hub transits sits in the RVSM upper airspace (FL290–410). Confirm the exact band split at **AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Bangkok (VTBB, Thailand).** The northern boundary down the Thai/Malay peninsula — see the **[Bangkok (VTBB) FIR brief](bangkok-vtbb.md)**.
  - **S — Singapore (WSJC).** 🟧 A **published cross-delegation arrangement** exists at this boundary: Lumpur ACC provides ATS within certain designated areas of the Singapore FIR, and Singapore ACC reciprocally provides ATS within certain designated areas of the Kuala Lumpur FIR (with published vertical limits) — a durable regional quirk relevant to frequency/sector expectations near the boundary, not merely a straight geographic handoff.
  - **S / SW — Jakarta (WIIF, Indonesia).** Across the southern Malacca Strait; southbound flow toward WIII.
  - **E — Kota Kinabalu (WBFC, Malaysia).** Across the South China Sea toward East Malaysia/Borneo.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Lumpur ACC is divided into multiple internal sectors; the transit-relevant fact is the **single "Lumpur Control" block** feeding the WMKK terminal area. Exact sector geometry is AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC frequencies.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower- & upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Lumpur ACC** ("Lumpur Control", CAAM) works the whole FIR surface-to-upper-limit; no separate UAC. Approach/terminal control for WMKK feeds off the ACC.
- **FIS / FSS:** flight information service is provided by Lumpur ACC for the FIR; not a factor for an IFR hub transit that stays in controlled airspace throughout.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 radar + ADS-B across the FIR's main route network, including the Strait and South China Sea approaches; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 / RNP** enroute; **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** arrivals at WMKK. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).

- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B** surveillance across the main route network.
- **8.33 kHz:** not an APAC requirement; standard 25 kHz VHF spacing applies.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Lumpur ACC ("Lumpur Control")** across its sectors and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC (Bangkok, Singapore — incl. the delegated sectors, Jakarta, Kota Kinabalu) at the boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Lumpur ACC provides flight information service in the FIR.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO standard).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 the Kuala Lumpur FIR is **fixed ATS-route / airway-structured**, not a continental FRA; confirm any user-preferred-route programme at ENR.
- **Airway spine:** the FIR sits on the **Malacca-Strait trunk** — the north–south spine linking Bangkok/Indochina (N) with Singapore/Jakarta and the SE-Asian SCS network (S), and the east–west link toward Kota Kinabalu/Borneo. WMKK is a principal terminal for this flow.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the boundary entry/exit fixes feeding the WMKK terminal area and the Strait trunk routes; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Kuala Lumpur Intl–Sepang (WMKK)** — the served K Global aerodrome. Singapore–Changi (WSSS) sits across the boundary in **Singapore FIR (WSJC)** — not this FIR, but see the **[Singapore (WSJC) FIR brief](singapore-wsjc.md)** for the interface detail.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 **low around WMKK** and the west-coast plain; the peninsula's **central mountain spine (Titiwangsa Range)** runs the length of the peninsula but stays well below cruise levels. Not a cruise-level factor at FL290+.
- **Boundary terrain:** no significant high terrain immediately across the adjacent-FIR boundaries relevant to a WMKK cruise transit; Sumatra's Barisan mountains (across the Jakarta-FIR boundary, SW) are the nearest notable range, more relevant to a low-level driftdown south of the Strait.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint within this FIR; the **dense diversion-field set** (WMKK in-FIR, WSSS/VTBS within short range across boundaries) removes any terrain-limited escape problem.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not applicable — equatorial, no cold-temperature terminal correction driver.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** ICAO comms-failure procedure — squawk **7600**, continue per the last acknowledged clearance / filed route and levels, and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Malaysian refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Equatorial convection year-round** — deep CB and embedded thunderstorms, particularly in the pre-dawn/late-afternoon build-up over the peninsula and Strait; the dominant enroute/terminal hazard, handled tactically.
  - **Monsoon seasons** — **south-west monsoon (~May–Sep)** and **north-east monsoon (~Nov–Mar)**, with an inter-monsoon convective peak; heavy rain and reduced visibility at WMKK, particularly under the NE monsoon on the peninsula's east coast.
  - **Haze** — trans-boundary smoke haze (regional biomass burning, chiefly the drier mid-year period) can reduce visibility across the FIR and at WMKK.
  - **Typhoons:** 🟩 the peninsula sits near the equator, south of the main tropical-cyclone track — direct typhoon strikes are not a feature; monsoon convection and haze are the seasonal drivers here.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, active danger/restricted-area status, SIGMET/AIRMET (convection, haze), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
