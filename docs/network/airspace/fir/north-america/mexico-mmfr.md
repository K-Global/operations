# Mexico (MMFR) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — central Mexico, including the **Mexico City high-elevation hub (MMMX)** · **Parent area brief:** [North America](../../north-america.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Monterrey (MMTY, SENEAM) N · Mérida (MMID, SENEAM) E · Mazatlán (MMZT, SENEAM) W/NW · Mazatlán Oceanic (MMFO, SENEAM) S/SW 🟧
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference, not a chart or clearance. Mexico FIR is a **radar/ADS-B-controlled** continental FIR administered by **SENEAM** (Servicios a la Navegación en el Espacio Aéreo Mexicano), Mexico's ANSP under the civil aviation authority (AFAC). The defining features are SENEAM's **multi-FIR/multi-ACC national structure** (four ACCs sharing the national airspace) and the **high-elevation Mexico City hub (MMMX, ~7,316 ft)**, which is a terminal performance driver cross-linked to OM E. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for MMMX live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Mexico FIR covers **central Mexico**, including the Valley of Mexico / Greater Mexico City area and the surrounding central plateau. SENEAM's public materials describe the Mexico ACC as bordering **Monterrey ACC to the north, Mérida ACC to the east, Mazatlán ACC to the west, and Mazatlán Oceanic (MMFO) to the south** 🟧 (exact boundary coordinates/decimals — VERIFY AIP México ENR 2).
- **Vertical split:** no separate lower/upper ATSU split identified for this FIR; SENEAM's Mexico City ACC is understood to work the full column. 🟧
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):** **Monterrey (MMTY)** N, **Mérida (MMID)** E, **Mazatlán (MMZT)** W/NW, **Mazatlán Oceanic (MMFO)** S/SW — all SENEAM. 🟧 (confirm against current AIP México ENR 2 boundary chart.)
- **Sectorisation:** SENEAM's national structure comprises **four Area Control Centres** — Mexico City, Monterrey, Mazatlán and Mérida — each responsible for its own FIR/sector set; internal Mexico ACC sector count not independently identified. 🟧

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟥 the Valley of Mexico is a high basin (MMMX field elevation ~7,316 ft / 2,230 m) ringed by significant volcanic terrain — **Popocatépetl (~17,802 ft)** and **Iztaccíhuatl (~17,159 ft)** to the SE, and the broader Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt. This drives both enroute MORA and MMMX terminal terrain-clearance procedures.
- **Boundary terrain:** high terrain continues into the adjacent Mérida and Mazatlán FIR sectors as the volcanic belt and Sierra Madre ranges extend laterally. 🟧 (per-quadrant grid-MORA — verify AIP México ENR charts.)
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟥 the high field elevation at MMMX (~7,316 ft) combined with surrounding terrain to ~17,800 ft materially affects driftdown/escape planning and emergency-descent minimum safe altitudes — see [OM E — High-Elevation Aerodrome Operations](../../../../flight-ops/high-elevation-aerodrome-operations.md) for the terminal-performance treatment; enroute driftdown clearance over the surrounding volcanoes is a separate consideration not yet independently derived here. 🟧
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not identified as a driver at MMMX (tropical-latitude, though high-elevation) — see the airport briefing and OM E if applicable to specific alternates.
