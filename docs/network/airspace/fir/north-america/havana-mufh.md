# Havana (MUFH) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the island of Cuba plus a large Gulf/Caribbean oceanic sector between Florida, the Bahamas and the Yucatán Channel · **Parent area brief:** [North America](../../north-america.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Miami (KZMA, FAA, oceanic) N · Nassau (MYNA, Bahamas) NE · Port-au-Prince (MTPP, Haiti) SE · Kingston (MKJK, Jamaica) S · Mérida (MMID, Mexico) W 🟧
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference, not a chart or clearance. Havana FIR is administered by **IACC** (Instituto de Aeronáutica Civil de Cuba), Cuba's civil aviation authority, and covers **both Cuban sovereign territory and a large adjacent oceanic sector of the Straits of Florida / NW Caribbean**, sitting directly against the **Miami Oceanic FIR** boundary. The defining feature for planning purposes is a **factual, neutral overflight/permit sensitivity** — non-scheduled/charter civil flights routing through this FIR require a Cuban overflight permit (diplomatic clearance) arranged in advance, and operators of certain other-state registrations may face additional national licensing requirements. This is stated as a planning fact, not a political position. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for MUHA live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Havana FIR covers **the island of Cuba** and a substantial surrounding oceanic area of the **Straits of Florida and north-western Caribbean Sea**, extending north toward Florida/the Bahamas, east toward Haiti, and south toward Jamaica. Public reference sources describe the FIR as bordering **Miami (oceanic, north), Nassau FIR (north-east), Port-au-Prince and Kingston FIRs (south/south-east), Cenamer FIR corridor, and Mérida FIR (west)**. 🟧 (exact boundary coordinates — VERIFY AIP Cuba ENR 2 / ICAO regional air navigation plan.)
- **Vertical split:** no separate lower/upper ATSU split independently identified; treat as a single-column FIR pending confirmation. 🟧
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):** **Miami (KZMA, FAA — oceanic)** N, **Nassau (MYNA, Bahamas)** NE, **Port-au-Prince (MTPP, Haiti)** SE, **Kingston (MKJK, Jamaica)** S, **Mérida (MMID, Mexico)** W. 🟧 (confirm against AIP Cuba / adjacent-state AIPs.)

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Cuba is predominantly **low-relief**, with the highest terrain in the **Sierra Maestra** in the south-east (**Pico Turquino ~1,974 m / 6,476 ft**, the island's highest point) and lesser ranges (Sierra del Escambray, Sierra de los Órganos) elsewhere. None of this is a binding constraint for a transiting jet at cruise/RVSM levels.
- **Boundary terrain:** no high terrain of operational significance at the Miami/Nassau/Port-au-Prince/Kingston/Mérida boundaries — the FIR's hazard profile is maritime/weather-driven (§11), not terrain-driven.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟩 not a terrain-driven concern; standard overwater/oceanic engine-out and diversion planning applies given the large oceanic component of this FIR — nearest-suitable-airport planning should account for the FIR's overflight-permit context (§10) for any unplanned diversion into Cuban territory. 🟧 (diversion-into-Cuba contingency planning not independently derived here.)
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not applicable — tropical/subtropical FIR.

- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations — 🟧 factual, neutral note:** public operator guidance indicates that **civil aircraft transiting Havana FIR on a non-scheduled/charter basis generally require an overflight permit (diplomatic clearance)** issued by IACC, typically requested **at least 48 hours before departure**, coordinated through the appropriate channel; two airways (**B646/UB646**) are cited in that guidance as exempt from the permit requirement on some routings.
