# Sana'a (OYSC) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **Republic of Yemen** landmass plus a large **maritime/oceanic sector** over the southern Red Sea, Gulf of Aden and part of the Arabian Sea, containing **Aden Intl (OYAA)**, **Sanaa Intl (OYSN)** and **Sayun Intl (OYSY)** · **Parent area brief:** [Middle East](../../middle-east.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Jeddah (OEJD, Saudi Arabia) N · Muscat (OOMM, Oman) E (ATS/FIS/alerting on parts of route L425/N315 within Sanaa FIR temporarily **delegated to Muscat ACC**) · maritime boundary across the Gulf of Aden/Bab-el-Mandeb toward the Horn of Africa (Djibouti/Somalia FIRs) 🟧 exact ident/boundary not confirmed
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; structure from public/AIP-adjacent sources, **conflict-zone status is live/perishable and flagged 🟥 (re-check at planning)**

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit/standby reference only, **not a chart, not a clearance, and not an endorsement of routine use.** The Sana'a FIR is Yemen's national FIR/UIR, administered by **CAMA (Civil Aviation and Meteorology Authority)**. **Yemen is an active-conflict zone.** The overwhelming majority of this FIR — all land territory and most of the airspace at all altitudes — is assessed **HIGH risk** and is, in practice, **not normally usable** for K Global operations: multiple states prohibit their operators from any land-portion overflight, control of the ground and airspace has shifted repeatedly through the conflict, and the airport infrastructure itself has been struck (OYSN runway, Jul 2026). **This file exists as reference/standby only** — to record the FIR's structure and served airports against the day the security picture changes, not as a basis for planning a flight through it. **Before any use of this FIR or its airports for any purpose, verify the current airspace status, security assessment and NOTAM picture from live sources (§11) — do not rely on this document for currency.** Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** the Sana'a FIR covers the **whole Republic of Yemen** — the western highlands, the central plateau (Sana'a), the southern coast (Aden, Gulf of Aden) and the eastern Hadhramaut region (Sayun) — plus a **substantial offshore/maritime extension** over the southern **Red Sea**, the **Gulf of Aden** and part of the **Arabian Sea**.
- **Confirmed containment:** 🟩 **Aden Intl (OYAA)**, **Sana'a Intl (OYSN)** and **Sayun Intl (OYSY)** all sit within the Sana'a FIR (OYSC).
- **Route-level delegation (not a lateral boundary change):** 🟧 ATS, FIS and alerting service on **portions of route L425 and N315** within the Sana'a FIR are **temporarily delegated to Muscat ACC (Oman)** — a live operational arrangement, not a redrawn FIR boundary; confirm current scope at planning.
- **Maritime risk tiering (not a structural boundary, but operationally load-bearing):** 🟧 the risk assessment differentiates the **land portion** (HIGH at all altitudes) from the **maritime portion east of route B400** (assessed **MEDIUM**) — the FIR is not a uniform risk block; the routing-relevant detail is which slice of it, if any, a permitted operator might use (historically limited offshore routes such as UT702/M999 for some states' operators — confirm current applicability, not assumed here for K Global).
- **Adjacent FIRs:**
  - **N — Jeddah (OEJD, Saudi Arabia, GACA).** The Saudi FIR to the north; the southern-bypass Gulf corridor described in the Middle East area brief runs well clear to the north of Yemen.
  - **E — Muscat (OOMM, Oman).** Eastern neighbour; also the FIR that Sana'a delegates the L425/N315 route portions to.
  - **S/SW — maritime boundary across the Gulf of Aden and Bab-el-Mandeb** toward the Horn of Africa. 🟧 The specific adjacent FIR ident(s) (Djibouti / Somalia-area oceanic) are **not confirmed** from the sources used to build this brief — verify against current ICAO regional air navigation documentation before relying on this boundary.
- **Vertical structure:** 🟧 not independently verified to current AIP; treat any structural detail (Class A upper limits, sector boundaries) as **unconfirmed** while the FIR is in its current conflict state.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 not published/verify — moot for a FIR this brief treats as standby-only.

## 10. Special-use airspace

> 🟥🟥 **STANDING CAUTION — ACTIVE CONFLICT ZONE. This entire section is a live/perishable snapshot and must be re-verified before any consideration of this FIR. Do not plan a flight into, through, or to any airport in this FIR from this document.**

- **Conflict-zone status (the entire point of this brief):** 🟥 Yemen (Sana'a FIR, OYSC) is assessed **HIGH risk at all altitudes and flight levels** over its land territory, driven by ongoing high-intensity military operations, political fragmentation, and the presence of armed groups with access to sophisticated anti-aircraft weaponry, including **surface-to-air missile and UAS systems** that create a material risk of **misidentification of civil aircraft**. Multiple states **prohibit their operators from any overflight of the land portion** of the FIR. The **maritime portion east of route B400** carries a materially lower — but still not "normal" — **MEDIUM** assessment. As at build date, Yemen's own aviation authority has at times **closed all airports until further notice**, and in **July 2026** a runway strike occurred at **Sana'a (OYSN)** to prevent an aircraft landing — illustrating that even the ground infrastructure is an active target, not merely the overflight airspace.
- **Practical effect for K Global:** 🟥 this FIR and its three served airports (OYAA, OYSN, OYSY) are treated as **not normally usable**. This file is maintained as **reference/standby** so the network record is complete and so the FIR can be re-assessed quickly if conditions change — it is **not** a routing or destination option under current conditions.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟧 historically a **72-hour prior-permission requirement** has applied for any Sana'a-FIR operation; moot while the FIR is avoided outright, but noted for completeness.
- **Military / restricted / danger areas:** 🟧 not published/verify — a granular restricted-area picture is not meaningful context while the entire land FIR is assessed HIGH risk.

> **Conflict-zone currency:** re-check the **ICAO Conflict Zone Information Repository, EASA CZIBs, state NOTAMs (Yemen + neighbouring states), OPSGROUP/safeairspace, and operator routing policy** before any consideration of this FIR — §10 is a durable-awareness snapshot, not a live clearance, and it **will be out of date** by the time it is read.
