# Budapest (LHCC) — FIR Briefing

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Budapest is a **fully radar/surveillance-controlled continental FIR** — no procedural or oceanic segment — and a genuine **Free Route Airspace pioneer**: HungaroControl abolished Hungary's entire fixed enroute route network in 2015, years ahead of the EU-wide mandate, and its concept became the template for the wider SEE FRA block. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for the aerodrome inside the FIR (LHBP) live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in this FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute · **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR · **RNP APCH** arrivals — EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** — **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**.
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**) — HungaroControl participates. VHF voice remains primary. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt — **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**.

- **Language:** 🟩 **Hungarian and English**; English always available on the controlled sectors.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 all enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** — mandatory equipage (§5).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA) — HungaroControl leadership:** 🟩 HungaroControl became the **first ANSP in Europe to abolish the entire fixed enroute route network**, on **5 February 2015**, under the concept **HUFRA (Hungarian Free Route Airspace)** — allowing aircraft to fly the shortest possible path between defined entry and exit points across the whole of Hungary's airspace, with no time or space limitation, years ahead of the EU-wide 1 January 2022 mandate. HungaroControl estimated the change could save on the order of **1.5 million km of flying per year**, with associated fuel and CO₂ savings.
- **SEE FRA — regional expansion of the Hungarian concept:** 🟩 based on HungaroControl's HUFRA model, **Romania and Bulgaria joined in November 2019**, and **Slovakia adopted the concept at the end of January 2021**, forming one of the largest contiguous free-route airspace blocks in Europe — now known as **SEE FRA**. The **Czech Republic formally joined SEE FRA in February 2023** (see the Praha (LKAA) FIR brief). A further cross-border corridor linking **SEE FRA with SECSI FRA** (the Austria-led South-East Europe block, see the Wien (LOVV) FIR brief) went live **28 November 2024** across the Vienna–Bratislava–Budapest–Prague control-area boundaries.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** with the fixed route network abolished, direct routeing is the default above the FRA floor; below it and in terminal airspace, SID/STAR feeder structures apply into Budapest (LHBP).
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA horizontal entry/exit points at the FIR boundary and the terminal transition fixes feeding Budapest; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Budapest (Liszt Ferenc Intl, LHBP)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefing (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Hungary sits within the flat **Pannonian (Carpathian) Basin** — the highest points are modest hills (**Kékes, Mátra range, ~1,014 m**; **Bakony**, west of Budapest, lower still). Not a cruise-level factor at FL290+.
- **Boundary terrain:** the Carpathian arc rises higher in Slovakia, Romania and western Ukraine, well outside this FIR and outside this network's routing.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint; the FIR's flat terrain and central position give ample diversion options.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at the terminal aerodrome in winter — see the airport briefing (§9).

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Standardised European Rules of the Air, Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules (last acknowledged clearance / filed route and levels), and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Hungarian refinement at **AIP Hungary ENR 1.8/GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — **request the deviation from ATC** and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Summer convection** — embedded CB/hail over the Pannonian Basin; thunderstorm avoidance handled tactically.
  - **Winter terminal icing / low visibility / fog** at Budapest — a terminal, not enroute, driver; see the airport briefing and OM E LVO/Cold-Weather docs.
  - **Volcanic ash (strategic wildcard)** — Icelandic eruptions can propagate ash across European upper airspace; **VAAC London** is the responsible advisory centre for this sector.
  - **Conflict-zone bulletins** — given the NE border adjacency to Ukraine, re-check ICAO Conflict Zone Information Repository / NOTAMs at planning even though this network does not route near that boundary.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, AUP/UUP (active TRA/TSA status) and current RAD, SIGMET/AIRMET, VAAC London volcanic-ash advisories, conflict-zone bulletins, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
