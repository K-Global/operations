# Praha (LKAA) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the Czech Republic, including the **Prague (LKPR) hub** · **Parent area brief:** [Europe (Continental)](../../europe.md) — 🟧 that brief covers the DE/FR/ES/PT continental corridor only and does **not** cover the Czech Republic/Central Europe; this FIR brief closes that scope gap · **Adjacent FIRs:** Bremen (EDWW) NW 🟧 (Saxony sector) · München (EDMM) SW 🟧 (Bavaria sector) · Wien (LOVV) S · Bratislava (LZBB) SE · Warszawa (EPWW) N
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Praha is a **fully radar/surveillance-controlled continental FIR** — no procedural or oceanic segment. It is a low-terrain, benign Central European transit FIR that hosted the **CEATS** project directorate (see the Wien (LOVV) FIR brief §3 for the wider CEATS context) and has fully integrated into **SEE FRA**. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for the aerodrome inside the FIR (LKPR) live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Praha FIR covers the entire Czech Republic — Bohemia (west, incl. Prague) and Moravia (east, incl. Brno/Ostrava).
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 Czech free-route operations (§7) are published as spanning **FL95 up to FL660** within named sectors (**W, N, S** per AIP ENR 2.1), excluding the Prague TMA — this suggests a single FIR/UIR designator (LKAA) rather than a separate lower/upper split, similar to the Swiss/Italian model, but this is not independently confirmed against the full AIP structure in this pass.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Bremen (EDWW, DFS), Germany.** 🟧 the Czech Republic's north-western neighbour across Saxony is asserted as Bremen FIR based on that FIR's stated wide northern/eastern German coverage (see the Langen (EDGG) FIR brief, which lists Berlin/Hamburg/Hannover as Bremen-FIR territory) — not independently confirmed for the Saxony sector specifically.
  - **SW — München (EDMM, DFS), Germany.** Bavaria borders western/south-western Czech Republic; see the München FIR brief.
  - **S — Wien (LOVV, Austro Control), Austria.** Confirmed via published Letters of Agreement between the two FIRs (see the Wien (LOVV) FIR brief).
  - **SE — Bratislava (LZBB), Slovakia.** Common interface with Prague and Vienna near the Czech/Slovak/Austrian tripoint.
  - **N — Warszawa (EPWW), Poland.** Confirmed via public FIR-adjacency references (see the Warszawa (EPWW) FIR brief).
- **Sectorisation:** Praha ACC operates (at minimum) **WEST** and **EAST** working sectors per public corroboration, with the East sector interfacing toward Bratislava; free-route sectors **W, N, S** are separately defined for FRA purposes (§7).

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Controlling ATSU:** 🟩 **Praha ACC (LKAA, ANS CR)** — radar/surveillance control across the FIR, including the Prague (LKPR) TMA feed.
- **FIS / FSS:** Czech FIS for lower/uncontrolled airspace; not a factor for an IFR hub transit remaining in controlled airspace.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 continuous radar + **ADS-B**; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in this FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute · **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR · **RNP APCH** arrivals — EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** — **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**.
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**) — ANS CR participates. VHF voice remains primary. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt — **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Praha ACC** and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC at the boundary (Bremen, München, Wien, Bratislava or Warszawa); exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Czech FIS for information service in lower/uncontrolled airspace.
- **Language:** 🟩 **Czech and English**; English always available on the controlled sectors.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 all enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** — mandatory equipage (§5).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 Free Route Airspace in the Praha FIR operates from **FL95 to FL660** within sectors **W, N and S** (AIP ENR 2.1), excluding the Prague TMA — allowing direct point-to-point planning between defined entry/exit points, with intermediate significant points, without reference to the fixed ATS route network (subject to airspace availability). ANS CR originally implemented this as **FRACZECH**; on **23 February 2023**, FRACZECH formally **merged into SEE FRA**, the cross-border free-route system also encompassing **Romania, Hungary, Slovakia, Moldova and Bulgaria** (see the Budapest (LHCC) FIR brief, which describes SEE FRA's Hungarian origin). Cross-border planning is also permitted with **LZBB (Bratislava)**, **Baltic FRA** and **SECSI FRA**.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** below FL95 and in terminal airspace, the fixed ATS-route network applies, including the SID/STAR feeder structure into Prague (LKPR).
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA horizontal entry/exit points at the FIR boundary and the terminal transition fixes feeding Prague; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Prague-Ruzyně (LKPR)**. Secondary Czech international fields — **Brno-Tuřany (LKTB)**, **Ostrava-Mošnov (LKMT)** — sit inside the FIR but have no OM C brief yet (§9). Terminal detail for LKPR is in the airport briefing (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 low-to-moderate throughout. Border-hill ranges include the **Krkonoše (Giant Mountains, Sněžka ~1,603 m)** on the Polish border, the **Šumava** range on the Austrian/German (Bavarian) border, and the **Ore Mountains (Krušné hory)** on the German (Saxony) border. None approach cruise-level significance at FL290+.
- **Boundary terrain:** none of the bordering ranges rise to a level that materially affects driftdown/escape planning for this fleet — a marked contrast to the Alpine FIRs (Switzerland, München, Wien, Milano) in this library.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint; the FIR's low-terrain profile and central position give ample diversion options.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at the terminal aerodrome in winter — see the airport briefing (§9).

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Standardised European Rules of the Air, Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules (last acknowledged clearance / filed route and levels), and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Czech refinement at **AIP Czech Republic ENR 1.8/GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — **request the deviation from ATC** and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Summer convection** — embedded CB/hail over Bohemia and Moravia; thunderstorm avoidance handled tactically.
  - **Winter terminal icing / low visibility / snow** at Prague (and Brno/Ostrava) — a terminal, not enroute, driver; see the airport briefing and OM E LVO/Cold-Weather docs.
  - **Volcanic ash (strategic wildcard)** — Icelandic eruptions can propagate ash across European upper airspace; **VAAC London** is the responsible advisory centre for this sector.
  - **Space weather:** negligible at these mid-latitudes.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, AUP/UUP (active TRA/TSA status) and current RAD, SIGMET/AIRMET, VAAC London volcanic-ash advisories, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
