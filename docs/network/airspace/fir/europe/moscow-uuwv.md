## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Sanctions / overflight restriction:** 🟥 following the **February–March 2022** reciprocal measures, **Russia closed its airspace to carriers of the EU, US, UK, Canada and a number of other states**, in response to those states closing their airspace to Russian carriers. This remains in force in various forms; an active **EASA Conflict Zone Information Bulletin ("Airspace of the Russian Federation," CZIB 2025-01 R3)** recommends against EU-operator activity in this airspace, last revised **24 July 2026** and valid through **31 January 2027** absent earlier review. **US FAA** prohibitory NOTAMs have separately covered Moscow, Samara and Rostov FIRs for US operators. Verify current status before any use — this is an actively managed, changing restriction, not a fixed historical fact.
- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟧 not assessed — out of scope for this reference-only brief.
