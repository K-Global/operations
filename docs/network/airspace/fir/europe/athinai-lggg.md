# Athinai (LGGG) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — Greece plus a large Aegean / eastern-Mediterranean sector · **Parent area brief:** [Europe (Continental)](../../europe.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Sofia (LBSR) N · Istanbul (LTBB/LTAA) NE · Nicosia (LCCC) SE · Brindisi (LIBB) NW · Malta (LMMM) W · 🟧 confirm exact boundary list at AIP ENR 2.1
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — built from public/AIP/HCAA sources; exact sectorisation and FRA name/date flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Athinai FIR is a **radar/surveillance-controlled FIR over a mixed continental + large maritime (Aegean) sector** — no procedural/oceanic segment in the ICAO sense, but the Aegean portion behaves like a sparse-diversion maritime area. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for LGAV live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions (NOTAMs, AUP/UUP, SIGMETs) are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Athinai FIR covers **all of mainland Greece** and an **exceptionally large offshore sector spanning the Aegean Sea and part of the eastern Mediterranean**, including the hundreds of Greek islands (Cyclades, Dodecanese, Crete, north-eastern Aegean islands) out to the FIR boundary with Turkey and Cyprus. This maritime extent is unusually large relative to Greece's land area.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 **Athinai FIR (lower)** to **FL245**; **Hellas UIR (upper)**, FL245 to the upper control limit (typ. FL660). Confirm the exact split level and whether a distinct upper-airspace ATSU exists, or whether Athinai ACC works both bands — not separately verified here.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Sofia (LBSR, BULATSA).** Bulgarian FIR.
  - **NE — Istanbul (LTBB/LTAA, DHMİ).** Turkish FIR/UIR; the Aegean boundary with Turkey has historically been a subject of bilateral discussion — noted factually, not a political assessment.
  - **SE — Nicosia (LCCC, DCA Cyprus).** 🟧 the Athinai/Nicosia FIR interface sits over the eastern Mediterranean; see the [Nicosia (LCCC) FIR brief](nicosia-lccc.md) for the GNSS-interference context on the Cyprus side of that boundary.
  - **NW — Brindisi (LIBB, ENAV).** Italian FIR across the Ionian Sea/Otranto Strait.
  - **W — Malta (LMMM).** Interfaces over the central Mediterranean on southerly/westerly routings.
- **Sectorisation:** Athinai ACC is divided into multiple sectors covering the mainland, the northern Aegean, the central Aegean/Cyclades, and the south-eastern Aegean/Dodecanese approaches to Nicosia FIR; 🟧 exact sector map not verified here.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower/upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Athinai ACC** (Hellenikon) — radar/surveillance control across the FIR, including the LGAV Athens TMA feed.
- **Regulator vs ANSP:** 🟧 **HCAA** is the regulatory authority; **HASP (Hellenic Aviation Service Provider)** is understood to provide the operational air navigation services, following a structural separation of regulator and service-provider functions. Confirm current organisational status at planning if it materially affects a briefing.
- **FIS / FSS:** Greek Flight Information Service for uncontrolled/lower airspace and remote island approaches; relevant mainly to island terminal operations, not a hub-to-hub cruise transit.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 radar/ADS-B over mainland and the main island routes; 🟧 continuity over the remote south-eastern Aegean not independently confirmed.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Athinai FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM. See [OM E — RVSM Operations](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute; **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** arrivals — per the EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [OM E — PBN and RNP Operations](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** for IFR/GAT radios in the ICAO EUR region (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**). See [OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt — per **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 Greece has implemented free-route operations in its upper airspace as part of the wider EUROCONTROL Pan-European FRA rollout; the specific Hellenic FRA name, effective FL band and implementation date are **not independently confirmed here** — verify at the current AIP/HCAA-HASP publication before relying on it.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** below the FRA floor and where RAD restricts direct routeing, the fixed ATS-route network applies, including SID/STAR feeder routes into the Athens (LGAV) TMA and inter-island airway segments.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA/airway entry-exit points at the FIR boundary, notably at the interfaces with Istanbul (NE) and Nicosia (SE); the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Athens Eleftherios Venizelos (LGAV)** — the principal international gateway and the only curated brief in this pack; numerous island aerodromes exist across the Aegean sector but are not separately briefed here.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 mainland Greece is traversed by the **Pindus mountain range**; the largest island, **Crete**, carries the **White Mountains / Lefka Ori (~2,456 m / 8,058 ft)**. Not a cruise-level factor at FL290+; relevant to island terminal approach/departure design.
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟩 no materially higher terrain immediately across the adjacent FIR boundaries relative to the mainland/Cretan peaks already inside Athinai FIR.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 the Aegean sector is largely maritime with **island aerodromes of variable runway length/category** — an engine-out or depressurisation escape plan over the open Aegean should account for **sparser suitable-field density** than a continental core FIR, even though terrain itself is benign.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at terminal aerodromes in winter, chiefly on the mainland — see the airport briefings (§9).

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules. 🟧 confirm any Greek refinement at AIP ENR 1.8/GEN.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Summer meltemi winds** over the Aegean — a surface/low-level wind phenomenon, primarily a terminal/island-operations consideration rather than an enroute cruise factor.
  - **Summer convection** over the mainland interior.
  - **Winter terminal icing / low visibility** at LGAV and mainland fields — a terminal, not enroute, driver.
  - **Volcanic ash (strategic wildcard)** — Mediterranean/regional VAAC advisories (Toulouse) are the responsible source for this sector.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, AUP/UUP (active TRA/CDR) and current RAD, SIGMET/AIRMET, VAAC ash advisories, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
