# Milano (LIMM) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — northern Italy (Lombardy, Piedmont, Liguria, Aosta Valley, Veneto, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Emilia-Romagna), including the Alpine arc · **Parent area brief:** <../../General/Europe.md> — 🟧 that brief covers the DE/FR/ES/PT continental sector only and does **not** cover Italy; this FIR brief (with Roma (LIRR) and Brindisi (LIBB)) closes that scope gap · **Adjacent FIRs:** Switzerland (LSAS) N/NW · München (EDMM) NE · Wien (LOVV) E 🟧 · Zagreb (LDZO) E/NE over the northern Adriatic 🟧 · Marseille (LFMM) W 🟧 (French Alps/Riviera) · Roma (LIRR) S
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Milano FIR/UIR is a **fully controlled continental FIR** — Class G/D lower airspace and controlled TMAs, Class C above FL195 (the Italian FIR/UIR split level), no procedural or oceanic segment. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR (LIMC, LIME, LIMF, LIMZ, LIPE, LIPX, LIPZ) live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Milano FIR/UIR covers **northern Italy** — Piedmont, Liguria, Lombardy, the Aosta Valley, Veneto, Friuli-Venezia Giulia and Emilia-Romagna (Bologna). It is the northernmost of Italy's three FIR/UIRs, bounded by the **Alpine arc** on its northern and western edges and by the **Roma FIR (LIRR)** to the south.
- **Vertical split:** 🟩 Italy uses a **single ICAO designator for both the FIR and its overlying UIR** — **lower FIR GND–FL195** and **upper UIR FL195 and above** are both **LIMM**, worked by the same controlling ACC. This differs from the German model (e.g. Langen EDGG / Karlsruhe EDUU), where the lower and upper units carry different idents.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N/NW — Switzerland (LSAS, skyguide).** Alpine boundary.
  - **NE — München (EDMM, DFS)** and 🟧 **Wien (LOVV, Austro Control)** — the Alto Adige/South Tyrol and north-eastern approaches.
  - **E/NE — Zagreb (LDZO, Croatia Control)** 🟧 over the northern Adriatic/Friuli coast — confirm the exact boundary at the current AIP; not independently verified this pass.
  - **W — Marseille (LFMM, DSNA)** 🟧 across the Alps/French Riviera.
  - **S — Roma FIR (LIRR).** The Milano/Roma FIR boundary runs roughly along the northern Apennines/Emilia-Romagna line.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟩 **Milano ACC** is the primary unit; **Padova ACC** operates a delegated sector covering the **north-east (Veneto/Friuli)**, including the approach feed for **Verona (LIPX)** and **Venice (LIPZ)**, within the same Milano FIR/UIR structure.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Controlling ATSU:** 🟩 **Milano ACC (LIMM, ENAV)** — radar/surveillance control across the FIR/UIR, GND through the upper control limit, since the FIR and UIR share the same designator and unit (unlike the German lower-ACC/upper-UAC split).
- **Delegated sector:** 🟩 **Padova ACC** — the north-eastern sector (Veneto/Friuli), feeding Verona (LIPX) and Venice (LIPZ) approach.
- **FIS / FSS:** local **AFIS** units exist at minor fields within the FIR (e.g. Cuneo, Bolzano) for aerodrome-level flight information service; not a factor for an IFR hub transit remaining in controlled airspace.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 continuous radar + **ADS-B**; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in this FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM (in force across the EUR region). See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute across ECAC upper airspace; **RNP 1** for terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** for arrivals — per the EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** for IFR/GAT radios in the ICAO EUR region (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**; full implementation completed 31 Dec 2018).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**, amending 29/2009) — ENAV's ACCs participate. A continental **Link 2000+** mandate for equipped aircraft, **not** an oceanic FANS-1/A requirement; VHF voice remains primary. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt — per **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Milano ACC** (or the delegated **Padova ACC** sector in the north-east) and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC at the boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** local **AFIS** at minor/uncontrolled fields (not the major hubs in §9).
- **Language:** 🟩 **Italian and English**; English is always available on the controlled sectors.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 all enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** — mandatory equipage (§5).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 **FRAIT (Free Route Airspace Italy)** applies **H24 across the combined area of responsibility of ENAV's four ACCs (Milano, Padova, Roma, Brindisi)** — direct point-to-point routeing between defined entry/exit points, with no reference to the fixed route network above the FRA floor. ENAV was the first of the five major European ANSPs to implement FRA, commissioning it at FL335 in **December 2016**, and progressively lowered the floor — reaching **FL195 (6,500 m) on 21 March 2024**, ahead of the EU-wide mandatory deadline of December 2025.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** below the FRA floor and in terminal airspace, the **fixed ATS-route network** applies, including SID/STAR feeder structures into the Po Valley terminals.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA horizontal entry/exit points at the FIR boundary and the terminal transition fixes; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Milano-Malpensa (LIMC)**, **Bergamo/Orio al Serio (LIME)**, **Torino-Caselle (LIMF)**, **Cuneo-Levaldigi (LIMZ)**, **Bologna (LIPE)**, **Verona (LIPX)**, **Venice (LIPZ)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟥 the **Alpine arc** dominates the northern and western edges of the FIR — **Mont Blanc (4,809 m / 15,777 ft)**, **Monte Rosa (4,634 m)** and **Gran Paradiso (4,061 m)** sit on or near the France/Switzerland boundary; the **Dolomites (Marmolada, 3,343 m)** rise in the north-east near the Padova sector. The **Po Valley** itself, where all seven in-FIR aerodromes sit, is low (well under 500 ft) and not terrain-constrained.
- **Boundary terrain:** the highest Alpine peaks sit astride or just across the **Switzerland (LSAS)** and **France (LFMM)** boundaries — a driftdown/escape corridor over the high Alps is a real consideration for any track that approaches the northern/western edge of this FIR, distinct from the benign Po Valley core.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 relevant for tracks that transit near the Alpine boundary — one-engine driftdown / emergency-descent planning should account for Alpine MORA; the Po Valley hub cluster itself provides dense, low-terrain diversion coverage. Confirm grid MORA per-leg at planning.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at the terminal aerodromes in winter (notably the Alpine-adjacent fields), not in enroute cruise — see the airport briefings (§9).

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military TRA / TSA:** 🟧 Alpine and Po Valley military training areas activated via **AUP/UUP**; the **Aviano air base** (Friuli, a major NATO/US Air Force installation) sits within this FIR's north-east and generates recurring military traffic and associated restricted volumes. Conditional Routes (CDR1/2/3) and the **RAD (Route Availability Document)** govern flyable levels/segments — confirm active TRAs/CDRs at planning.
- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟧 numerous Alpine training areas and danger areas — named areas and status are AIP/NOTAM-published; pull live at planning.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟩 none — standard EU/ECAC operation, no permits.

> **SUA currency:** re-check active TRA/TSA/CDR status (AUP/UUP) and the current RAD at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Standardised European Rules of the Air, Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules (last acknowledged clearance / filed route and levels), and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Italian refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — **request the deviation from ATC** and comply with the vector/level issued. No oceanic weather-deviation offset table applies. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Alpine convection and mountain-wave turbulence** in summer; **winter terminal icing/snow** at the Po Valley and Alpine-adjacent fields (LIMF, LIMZ, LIPX especially) — a terminal, not enroute, driver; see the airport briefings and OM E LVO/Cold-Weather docs.
  - **Fog (nebbia)** is a recurring autumn/winter Po Valley phenomenon affecting LIMC/LIME/LIMF/LIPE — terminal LVO planning matter, see airport briefs.
  - **Volcanic ash:** 🟧 not a primary hazard in this northern FIR (the active Italian volcanoes — Etna, Vesuvius, Stromboli/Vulcano — lie in the Brindisi FIR sector, see that brief); a distant **Icelandic-eruption** ash cloud could still transit European upper airspace as a strategic wildcard. **VAAC Toulouse** is the responsible advisory centre for the Italian/Mediterranean volcanic region generally.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, **AUP/UUP** (active TRA/CDR status) and current **RAD**, SIGMET/AIRMET (Alpine convection, turbulence, fog), VAAC advisories, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
