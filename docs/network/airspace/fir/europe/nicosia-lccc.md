# Nicosia (LCCC) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — Cyprus plus a large eastern-Mediterranean sector · **Parent area brief:** [Europe (Continental)](../../europe.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Athinai (LGGG) NW · Ankara (LTAA) N (🟧 boundary subject to a published ATS coordination dispute, see §3/§10) · Tel Aviv (LLLL) E · Beirut (OLBB) NE · Damascus (OSTT) E · Amman (OJAC) SE · Cairo (HECC) S

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Nicosia FIR covers **the Republic of Cyprus** and an unusually **large surrounding eastern-Mediterranean maritime sector**, extending toward the Levantine coast and interfacing with several Middle-East FIRs — a larger offshore extent than the island's land area would suggest.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 controlled airspace **Class C above FL195** up to **FL660**; lower-band structure and exact class letters below FL195 not independently confirmed here — see AIP Cyprus ENR 1.4/2.1.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Athinai (LGGG, HCAA/HASP).** Greek FIR; see the [Athinai (LGGG) FIR brief](athinai-lggg.md) for the other side of this boundary.
  - **N — Ankara (LTAA, DHMİ).** 🟧 **The Ankara–Nicosia FIR boundary has been the subject of a formal joint IFALPA/IFATCA safety briefing** (published 30 March 2023) addressing air-traffic-service coordination and recognition issues along this boundary — a factual, on-record operational/procedural matter, not a political position. Crews should be aware the boundary has non-standard coordination history; confirm current guidance at planning.
  - **E — Tel Aviv (LLLL).** Israeli FIR.
  - **NE — Beirut (OLBB).** Lebanese FIR.
  - **E — Damascus (OSTT).** Syrian FIR; historically, traffic transiting toward Damascus ACC has needed to establish contact ~10 minutes before a defined boundary waypoint, or relay position reports via **Latakia Radio** if direct contact is not possible — confirm current procedure and FIR-status at planning, as Middle-East ATS arrangements in this area are subject to change.
  - **SE — Amman (OJAC).** Jordanian FIR.
  - **S — Cairo (HECC).** Egyptian FIR, interfacing on southerly transits.
- **Sectorisation:** Nicosia ACC typically operates **5 sectors, expandable to 6** during peak periods (e.g. summer weekends); 🟧 exact sector map not verified here.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Controlling ATSU:** 🟩 **Nicosia ACC**, operated by the **Department of Civil Aviation (DCA), Republic of Cyprus** — DCA functions as both regulator and ANSP for the FIR, and also controls the Larnaca and Paphos towers.
- **Civil–military coordination:** the **Cyprus Airspace Management Cell (AMC)** is the joint civil–military body coordinating Flexible Use of Airspace (FUA) within Nicosia FIR.
- **Regional cooperation:** Cyprus participates in the **Blue Med Functional Airspace Block (FAB)**, established by state-level agreement (signed in Cyprus, 12 October 2012), covering Mediterranean-area ANS cooperation with Greece.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 radar/ADS-B across the FIR; 🟧 surveillance/nav-integrity is materially affected by the GNSS-interference condition described in §10 — this is a navigation-signal issue, not a radar-coverage gap.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Nicosia FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM. See [OM E — RVSM Operations](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute; **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** arrivals — per the EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [OM E — PBN and RNP Operations](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md). 🟥 **Note:** given the active GNSS-interference condition (§10), RNP-based procedures may be locally unreliable — crews should be prepared to revert to conventional navigation.
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** for IFR/GAT radios in the ICAO EUR region (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**). See [OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt — per **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a transit is worked sector-to-sector by **Nicosia ACC** (typically 5, up to 6 sectors); exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** Cyprus FIS for information service in lower/uncontrolled airspace.
- **Language:** 🟩 **Greek and English**; English always available on ACC.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 all enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** (§5).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air. 🟧 In the Damascus-boundary sector, **Latakia Radio** may serve as a relay for position reports where direct Damascus ACC contact is not available — confirm current applicability.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 benign. Cyprus's own high terrain — the **Troodos massif (~1,952 m / 6,404 ft)** and the lower **Kyrenia (Pentadaktylos) range** — is well below any cruise-level factor. The wider FIR is predominantly open eastern-Mediterranean sea.
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟩 no materially significant terrain step-up across the adjacent FIR boundaries relevant to cruise operations.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 given the large maritime extent of the FIR, an engine-out or depressurisation escape plan on an eastern/south-eastern transit should account for **longer over-water segments** with fewer immediate diversion options than a continental core FIR; Larnaca is the natural anchor point.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a significant factor in this Mediterranean climate; applied per standard procedure at LCLK if relevant.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military TRA / TSA:** 🟧 details not independently verified here — confirm at planning via AIP Cyprus / current NOTAM.
- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟧 named areas AIP/NOTAM-published — pull live at planning.
- **GNSS interference (navigation-hazard overlay):** 🟧 **Nicosia FIR (including Larnaca and Paphos) has recorded exceptionally high volumes of GPS jamming and spoofing** — reported among the highest-incident airspaces globally, attributed to electronic-warfare spillover from the wider Middle-East conflict environment. Reported effects include false position/altitude indications (aircraft showing positions well offset from actual, and altitude errors of several thousand feet at cruise). Airlines have briefed crews on navigation anomalies before every flight through this FIR, with some operators rerouting around it. **Mitigation:** brief conventional/INS backup navigation, expect possible loss of GPS-based approach guidance, and report anomalies to ATC. This is a factual, ongoing operational condition — not a political statement.
- **Regional-tension / Middle-East FIR interfaces:** 🟧 Nicosia FIR's eastern and north-eastern boundaries interface directly with **Tel Aviv (LLLL)**, **Beirut (OLBB)** and **Damascus (OSTT)** FIRs, all situated within the wider eastern-Mediterranean/Middle-East regional-tension context; conflict-zone advisories affecting these neighbouring FIRs can indirectly affect routing and diversion planning near the Nicosia-FIR boundary. Check current conflict-zone bulletins before planning any routing that approaches these boundaries.
- **Ankara–Nicosia FIR boundary:** 🟧 a **joint IFALPA/IFATCA safety briefing (23ATSBL01, 30 March 2023)** documents an on-record ATS coordination matter along this boundary — confirm current guidance before planning routes near the northern FIR edge.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟩 standard EU/ECAC operation for the FIR itself; no permit required for transit within Nicosia FIR, though routings approaching the Middle-East-facing boundaries should be checked against current conflict-zone/overflight bulletins.

> **SUA currency:** re-check active TRA/TSA status, GNSS-interference NOTAMs, and conflict-zone bulletins for the Middle-East-facing boundaries at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules. 🟧 confirm any Cyprus-specific refinement, and the Damascus-boundary relay procedure via Latakia Radio, at AIP ENR 1.8/GEN.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — request the deviation from ATC and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Navigation-integrity contingency:** 🟧 given the severe GNSS-interference pattern (§10), crews should be prepared to revert to conventional (VOR/DME, INS) navigation if GPS-derived position becomes unreliable, and to report anomalies to ATC per current NOTAM guidance.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Summer convection** is limited in this Mediterranean climate; more relevant is heat/visibility haze at LCLK in summer.
  - **Winter terminal weather** at LCLK — a terminal, not enroute, driver.
  - **Volcanic ash (strategic wildcard)** — regional VAAC advisories (Toulouse) are the responsible source.
  - **Conflict-zone bulletins** — check current status for the Levant-facing boundaries (Beirut, Damascus, Tel Aviv) at every planning cycle; this is a live/changing condition.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs (including GNSS-interference bulletins), conflict-zone bulletins for the Middle-East-facing boundaries, SIGMET/AIRMET, VAAC ash advisories, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
