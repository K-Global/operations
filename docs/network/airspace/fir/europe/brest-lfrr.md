# Brest (LFRR) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — western France and the Bay of Biscay/eastern-Atlantic oceanic-transition sector · **Parent area brief:** [Europe (Continental)](../../europe.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Paris (LFFF) E · Bordeaux (LFBB) SE · London (EGTT, NATS) N across the Channel · Shanwick Oceanic (EGGX) W (oceanic transition via BOTA) · Santa Maria (LPPO) SW (further oceanic transition)
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Brest is a **fully radar/surveillance-controlled continental FIR** — no procedural segment domestically, but it is **the DSNA gateway to the North Atlantic**, delegated the **Brest Oceanic Transition Area (BOTA)** from Shanwick. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances domestically, and NAT/oceanic procedures once inside Shanwick OCA. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR (LFRS, LFBZ) live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Brest FIR covers **western France** — Brittany, Pays de la Loire's Atlantic coast, and the Bay of Biscay approaches — extending out over the eastern Atlantic to the domestic/oceanic boundary.
- **Vertical split:** 🟩 **Lower airspace SFC–FL195** is Brest ACC's own FIR. Above FL195, **France's single unified UIR** applies — the same **Brest ACC (LFRR, DSNA)** continues to control its own upper-airspace slice domestically.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **E — Paris (LFFF, DSNA).** 🟧 Brest is delegated a slice of the **Paris UTA** (FL295–UNL) when Brest ACC is active — see the Paris (LFFF) brief §3.
  - **SE — Bordeaux (LFBB, DSNA).**
  - **N — London (EGTT, NATS)** across the western Channel/Channel Islands approaches.
  - **W — Shanwick Oceanic Control Area (EGGX)**, jointly run by NATS (Prestwick) and IAA (Shannon). Domestic/oceanic boundary; Brest is delegated the **Brest Oceanic Transition Area (BOTA)** above FL55 by Shanwick.
  - **SW — Santa Maria Oceanic Control Area (LPPO, NAV Portugal — Azores)**, the next oceanic sector south of Shanwick, relevant to more southerly NAT tracks.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Brest Control is reported organised around a primary sector with an upper subsector split as traffic requires (per third-party sector documentation); confirm current sectorisation against the DSNA AIP.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Brest ACC (LFRR, DSNA)** — radar/surveillance control, SFC–FL195, including the Nantes-Atlantique (LFRS) and Biarritz (LFBZ) approaches.
- **Upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Brest ACC (LFRR, DSNA)** continues above FL195 domestically as part of the unified **France UIR**.
- **Oceanic delegation:** 🟧 the **Brest Oceanic Transition Area (BOTA)** — the airspace between the French coast and the Shanwick OCA boundary — is delegated by **Shanwick (EGGX)** to **Brest ACC** above FL55, with Brest providing radar-based ATS in this zone rather than Shanwick working it directly. Coordination with Shanwick is required approaching the oceanic boundary (typically referenced against the 003°W meridian in NAT operating practice); confirm current procedure against the Shanwick/Brest Letter of Agreement and NAT documents at planning.
- **FIS / FSS:** French **FIS** (DSNA) for lower/uncontrolled airspace outside CTR/TMA/CDR structure.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 continuous radar + **ADS-B** domestically and within the BOTA; genuine oceanic procedural separation (non-radar) applies once inside Shanwick OCA proper.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Brest FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM domestically; **NAT HLA (High Level Airspace) RVSM** applies once inside Shanwick OCA — see [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute; **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** arrivals domestically (EU 2018/1048) — see [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md). **NAT MNPS/RNP 4** navigation performance applies oceanic — see the oceanic doc below.
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** domestically (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**); not applicable oceanic (HF/VHF relay regime instead).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 domestic **CPDLC ATN B1** above FL285 (EU 2015/310 amending 29/2009). 🟥 **FANS-1/A CPDLC/ADS-C** is the separate, mandatory datalink stack for the **oceanic (NAT) segment** beyond the BOTA/Shanwick boundary — a different avionics/procedure set entirely. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md) for the full oceanic requirement (this FIR brief does not re-derive NAT procedures).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** domestically (EU 2017/386).

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a transit through Brest FIR is worked domestically by **Brest Control**, then — for oceanic-bound traffic — coordinated into the BOTA and handed to **Shanwick Oceanic** at the coast-out boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** French **FIS** (DSNA).
- **Language:** 🟩 **French and English**.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 domestic enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** (§5); not applicable to oceanic HF.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air domestically. Oceanic emergency procedures per the NAT doc.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 **French FRA applies in the Brest ACC upper airspace above FL195.** Brest was one of the first three DSNA centres (with Paris and Bordeaux) to go live with Free Route on **2 December 2021**. Per the March 2026 DSNA/FABEC announcement, France now has **FRA fully deployed above FL305 (100%)**, with **full FL195 nationwide FRA targeted by 2028**.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** below the FRA floor and where the RAD restricts direct routeing, the fixed **ATS-route network** applies, including the SID/STAR feeder structure into Nantes-Atlantique and Biarritz.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA horizontal entry/exit points at the domestic FIR boundary, and — for the westbound NAT feed — the **BOTA entry/exit and Shanwick coast-out fixes**; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route and the current NAT Track message provide the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **NAT feed note:** 🟩 Brest ACC is the **domestic gateway for North Atlantic transits** coasting out/in via the eastern NAT region — westbound flights typically transit Brest FIR before entering the BOTA and Shanwick OCA; eastbound NAT arrivals reverse the sequence, handed from Shanwick to Brest at the oceanic boundary before continuing into French/onward continental airspace.
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Nantes-Atlantique (LFRS)**, **Biarritz (LFBZ)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9). 🟧 Biarritz sits close to the Bordeaux FIR boundary in the far south of the Brest FIR — confirm exact membership at planning if routing near the southern edge.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 low. The **Armorican Massif** (Brittany's interior upland) tops out around **~1,300 ft** — the only relief of note, and not a cruise-level factor at any planned level.
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟩 none of operational significance at the Paris/Bordeaux/Channel boundaries.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a domestic terrain constraint; the relevant escape consideration in this FIR is instead **oceanic diversion planning** once past the coast (ETP/PET/CP planning per the fleet's ETOPS/EDTO and oceanic contingency procedures — see OM E) rather than terrain.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at the terminal aerodromes in winter — see the airport briefings (§9).

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military TRA / TSA:** 🟧 French airspace here carries **military TRA/TSA and naval exercise areas** (Atlantic approaches, and the vicinity of the **Île Longue** naval/submarine base near Brest) activated **daily via the AUP/UUP**; associated **Conditional Routes (CDR1/2/3)** open/close accordingly, and the **RAD** governs flyable levels/segments. Confirm active TRAs/CDRs at planning.
- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟧 danger areas exist over parts of the Atlantic approaches and naval training zones; pull current AIP/NOTAM detail at planning.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟩 none domestically — standard EU/ECAC operation. Oceanic (Shanwick) transit requires standard NAT flight-planning compliance (MNPS/RNP4, NAT Track message) rather than a diplomatic permit — see OM E oceanic doc.

> **SUA currency:** re-check active TRA/TSA/CDR status (AUP/UUP) and the current RAD at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** domestically per **SERA (Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules, attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟥 **NAT/oceanic comms-failure procedure is different** (specific NAT contingency and lost-communication procedures apply once past the coast) — see OM E Datalink and Oceanic Procedures; do not apply the domestic SERA rule beyond the BOTA/Shanwick boundary.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** domestically radar-tactical via ATC. 🟥 **Oceanic weather deviation** follows the **NAT in-flight contingency / strategic lateral offset (SLOP)** procedures once inside Shanwick OCA — a materially different construct; see OM E.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Atlantic frontal weather** — the Brest FIR sits directly in the path of Atlantic low-pressure systems; strong winds, turbulence and low cloud/fog are a recurring coastal and oceanic-approach factor, more prominent here than in the more continental French FIRs.
  - **Summer convection** — less dominant than inland France, but still present.
  - **Winter icing / low visibility** at the terminal aerodromes — see the airport briefings and OM E LVO/Cold-Weather docs.
  - **Volcanic ash (strategic wildcard)** — **Icelandic eruptions**; **VAAC London** is the ICAO-designated centre for Icelandic-origin ash, most directly relevant here given Brest's proximity to the North Atlantic approach to Iceland; **VAAC Toulouse** is France's own designated centre and reciprocal backup.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, **AUP/UUP** (active TRA/CDR status) and current **RAD**, SIGMET/AIRMET (Atlantic frontal systems, convection), **VAAC London** volcanic-ash advisories, the current **NAT Track message** and oceanic NOTAMs, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
