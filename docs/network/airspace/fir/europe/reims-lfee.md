# Reims (LFEE) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — north-eastern France, Champagne/Lorraine/Alsace and the trinational Basel interface · **Parent area brief:** [Europe (Continental)](../../europe.md) · **Adjacent FIRs:** Paris (LFFF) W · Brussels (EBBU) N · Langen (EDGG, Germany) E · Switzerland (LSAS/LSAG) SE · Marseille (LFMM) S
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Reims is a **fully radar/surveillance-controlled continental FIR** — no procedural or oceanic segment. Its distinguishing feature is the **trinational Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg interface** where France, Switzerland and Germany share a single airport (LFSB) inside French sovereign airspace. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical radar-ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR (LFSB, LFST, LFJL) live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions (NOTAMs, AUP/UUP conditional-route status, SIGMETs, ash bulletins) are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Reims FIR covers **north-eastern France** — Champagne-Ardenne, Lorraine and Alsace, running east to the Rhine and the German/Swiss borders. It is the DSNA FIR that interfaces directly with **Langen (EDGG)** to the east and, at its south-eastern tip, with **Swiss airspace (LSAS/LSAG)** around the trinational Basel corner.
- **Vertical split:** 🟩 **Lower airspace SFC–FL195** is Reims ACC's own FIR. Above FL195, **France's single unified UIR** applies — the same **Reims ACC (LFEE, DSNA)** continues to control its own upper-airspace slice, a structural contrast with the DFS Langen/Karlsruhe (EDGG/EDUU) FIR/UAC split next door.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **W — Paris (LFFF, DSNA).** The FIR at the hub of the network.
  - **N — Brussels (EBBU, skeyes).** Belgian FIR (with Maastricht UAC EDYY owning the upper airspace above FL245).
  - **E — Langen (EDGG, DFS), Germany.** 🟧 Part of Reims's lower airspace near the **Sarre (Saar)** region is delegated to **Langen Radar** for ATS provision — a cross-border delegation, not a hard lateral boundary; confirm current extent against the DFS/DSNA Letter of Agreement.
  - **SE — Switzerland (LSAS lower / LSAG Geneva ACC upper), skyguide.** The Basel corner is the tightest three-way interface in the FIR (§10).
  - **S — Marseille (LFMM, DSNA).**
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 Reims Control is reported organised around a primary sector (with an upper subsector split as traffic requires, per third-party sector documentation); confirm current sectorisation against the DSNA AIP — not individually re-verified here.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Reims ACC (LFEE, DSNA)** — radar/surveillance control, SFC–FL195, including the Strasbourg (LFST) and Metz-Nancy-Lorraine (LFJL) approaches; the Basel-Mulhouse (LFSB) approach is worked under the trinational arrangement described in §10.
- **Upper-airspace ATSU:** 🟩 **Reims ACC (LFEE, DSNA)** continues above FL195 as part of the unified **France UIR** — no internal FIR/UIR handoff.
- **FIS / FSS:** French **FIS** (DSNA) for lower/uncontrolled airspace outside CTR/TMA/CDR structure.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 continuous radar + **ADS-B**; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Reims FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute; **RNP 1** terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** arrivals — per the EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** for IFR/GAT radios (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**, amending 29/2009) — Reims ACC participates in the Link 2000+ area. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**).

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit through Reims FIR is worked by **Reims Control** enroute, feeding the Strasbourg/Metz-Nancy-Lorraine approaches or the trinational Basel arrangement, then handed to the adjacent ACC (Paris/Brussels/Langen/Geneva/Marseille) at the boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** French **FIS** (DSNA).
- **Language:** 🟩 **French and English**; **German** in practical day-to-day use around the Basel/Strasbourg interface given the cross-border traffic mix.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 all enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** — mandatory equipage (§5).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 **French FRA applies in the Reims ACC upper airspace above FL195.** Reims was among the second wave of DSNA centres (with Marseille) to go live, on **21 February 2024**, taking French FRA coverage above FL195 to ~70%. Per the March 2026 DSNA/FABEC announcement, France now has **FRA fully deployed above FL305 (100%)**, with **full FL195 nationwide FRA targeted by 2028**.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** below the FRA floor and where the RAD restricts direct routeing, the fixed **ATS-route network** applies, including the SID/STAR feeder structure into Strasbourg, Metz-Nancy-Lorraine and the trinational Basel-Mulhouse arrangement.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA horizontal entry/exit points at the FIR boundary (including the Langen/EDGG and Swiss/LSAS interfaces) and the terminal transition fixes; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg / EuroAirport (LFSB)**, **Strasbourg-Entzheim (LFST)**, **Metz-Nancy-Lorraine (LFJL)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 moderate. The **Vosges mountains** (Grand Ballon, ~4,672 ft / 1,424 m) run along the western side of the Alsace plain; the **Rhine valley** itself, where Strasbourg and Basel-Mulhouse sit, is low-lying (~800–900 ft). Not a cruise-level factor at FL290+, but relevant to terminal MORA/obstacle clearance at LFST and LFSB.
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟩 the **Black Forest (Schwarzwald)** lies just across the eastern boundary in **Langen FIR (EDGG)**, mirroring the Vosges on the German side of the Rhine graben; neither belt is severe.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint within Reims; the Vosges/Black Forest belts are modest and the **in-FIR diversion density** (LFSB/LFST/LFJL) supports normal escape planning.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at the terminal aerodromes in winter, not in enroute cruise — see the airport briefings (§9).

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military TRA / TSA:** 🟧 French and cross-border airspace here carries **military TRA/TSA** activated **daily via the AUP/UUP**; associated **Conditional Routes (CDR1/2/3)** open/close accordingly, and the **RAD** governs flyable levels/segments. The SimBrief route is validated against the current RAD — confirm active TRAs/CDRs at planning.
- **Trinational Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg interface:** 🟧 **EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse-Freiburg (LFSB)** is a unique binational/trinational construct — the airport sits on **French sovereign territory** but is jointly owned/operated by **France and Switzerland**, with a distinct **Swiss sector** (customs-separated, served from Switzerland) alongside the French/EU side, and close operational proximity to **German** airspace and **EuroAirport's** own role in the wider Basel/Freiburg air-traffic picture. Terminal-specific detail (sector access, customs, ATC handling) lives in the LFSB airport briefing (§9); this FIR brief flags the interface's existence and its effect on cross-border routing and controller-language mix.
- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟧 restricted/danger areas exist regionally (military ranges); pull current AIP/NOTAM detail at planning.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟩 none — standard EU/ECAC operation, no permits (Switzerland participates in the single European sky framework operationally though outside the EU).

> **SUA currency:** re-check active TRA/TSA/CDR status (AUP/UUP) and the current RAD at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules, and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any French refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — **request the deviation from ATC** and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Summer convection** — embedded CB/hail over Alsace-Lorraine and the Rhine valley; thunderstorm avoidance handled tactically.
  - **Winter terminal icing / low visibility / fog** in the Rhine valley (Strasbourg, Basel-Mulhouse are both prone to valley fog) — a terminal, not enroute, driver; see the airport briefings and OM E LVO/Cold-Weather docs.
  - **Volcanic ash (strategic wildcard)** — **Icelandic eruptions**; **VAAC London** is the ICAO-designated centre for Icelandic-origin ash, with **VAAC Toulouse** (Météo-France) as France's own designated centre and reciprocal backup covering the Mediterranean/Africa AoR.
  - **Space weather:** negligible at these mid-latitudes.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, **AUP/UUP** (active TRA/CDR status) and current **RAD**, SIGMET/AIRMET (convection, valley fog), **VAAC London / VAAC Toulouse** volcanic-ash advisories, conflict-zone bulletins, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
