# Brindisi (LIBB) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — southern Italy (Campania, Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria, Sicily) and the southern Adriatic/Ionian sector · **Parent area brief:** <../../General/Europe.md> — 🟧 that brief covers the DE/FR/ES/PT continental sector only and does **not** cover Italy; this FIR brief (with Milano (LIMM) and Roma (LIRR)) closes that scope gap · **Adjacent FIRs:** Roma (LIRR) NW · Zagreb (LDZO) N 🟧 (southern Adriatic) · Athinai (LGGG) E 🟧 (Ionian Sea) · Malta (LMMM) S 🟧 · Tunisi (DTTC) SW 🟧
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Brindisi FIR/UIR is a **fully controlled continental FIR** — Class G/D lower airspace, Class C above FL195 (the Italian FIR/UIR split level), no procedural or oceanic segment. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR (LIRN, LICC) live in the airport briefings (§9). This FIR's signature hazard is **active volcanism (Etna, and secondary Vesuvius/Stromboli/Vulcano)** — brief every crew on volcanic-ash awareness before operating here (§8/§11, and cross-link OM E — Volcanic Ash Avoidance). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Brindisi FIR/UIR covers **southern Italy** — Campania (Naples), Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria — **Sicily**, and the **southern Adriatic and Ionian Sea**. It is the southernmost of Italy's three FIR/UIRs, bounded by **Roma FIR (LIRR)** to the north-west.
- **Vertical split:** 🟩 **Lower FIR GND–FL195** and **upper UIR FL195+** share the single **LIBB** designator and are worked by the same **Brindisi ACC**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Roma (LIRR).** Boundary runs across the central-southern Apennines.
  - **N — Zagreb (LDZO, Croatia Control)** 🟧 across the southern Adriatic — exact lateral limit not independently verified this pass.
  - **E — Athinai (LGGG, Greece)** 🟧 across the Ionian Sea.
  - **S — Malta (LMMM)** 🟧 south of Sicily.
  - **SW — Tunisi (DTTC, Tunisia)** 🟧 across the Sicilian Channel.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟩 **Brindisi ACC** is the sole ATSU for this FIR/UIR, with a distinct **Catania/Sicily sub-sector** handling the Etna/Sigonella local-area traffic.

---

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Controlling ATSU:** 🟩 **Brindisi ACC (LIBB, ENAV)** — radar/surveillance control across the FIR/UIR, GND through the upper control limit. **Catania Approach/Director** provides local terminal control at LICC, feeding into Brindisi ACC en route.
- **FIS / FSS:** local **AFIS** units exist at minor fields within the FIR (e.g. Foggia, Crotone, Lampedusa, Pontecagnano/Salerno); not a factor for an IFR hub transit remaining in controlled airspace.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 continuous radar + **ADS-B**; no procedural pockets on a normal IFR routing.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in this FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — ICAO EUR RVSM. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟥 **RNAV 5 (B-RNAV)** enroute; **RNP 1** for terminal SID/STAR; **RNP APCH** for arrivals — per the EU **PBN Implementing Rule (EU) 2018/1048**. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟥 **mandatory** for IFR/GAT radios in the ICAO EUR region (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012**).
- **Datalink (CPDLC):** 🟧 **CPDLC ATN B1 over VDL Mode 2**, expected/mandated above **FL285** in the EU datalink service area (**Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310**, amending 29/2009). See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode S** transponder required; **ADS-B Out (1090 MHz ES) mandatory** for aircraft >5,700 kg MTOW or >250 kt — per **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/386 (SPI IR)**.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a hub transit is worked by **Brindisi ACC** (with Catania Approach/Director for the terminal segment) and handed to the adjacent-FIR ACC (Roma, Zagreb, Athinai, Malta, Tunisi) at the boundary; the exact sector split and frequencies are AIRAC-dependent — fly the current-AIRAC/AIP frequencies, not a stored table.
- **FIS / lower airspace:** local **AFIS** at minor/uncontrolled fields (not the major hubs in §9).
- **Language:** 🟩 **Italian and English**; English is always available on the controlled sectors.
- **8.33 kHz:** 🟥 all enroute frequencies are **8.33 kHz-spaced** — mandatory equipage (§5).
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 **FRAIT (Free Route Airspace Italy)** applies **H24 across the combined area of responsibility of ENAV's four ACCs (Milano, Padova, Roma, Brindisi)** — direct point-to-point routeing between defined entry/exit points. FRA reached **FL195 (6,500 m) on 21 March 2024**, having started at FL335 in December 2016, ahead of the EU-wide mandatory deadline of December 2025. 🟧 The FRA has also been **extended to bordering non-Italian airspace** (Austria, Slovenia, Bosnia-Herzegovina, Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro, Albania, N. Macedonia) under the CLEAN ATM co-funded project — relevant to the Adriatic/Balkan approach to this FIR, confirm current scope at planning.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / RAD-constrained):** below the FRA floor and in terminal airspace, the **fixed ATS-route network** applies, including SID/STAR feeder structures into Naples and Catania.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FRA horizontal entry/exit points at the FIR boundary and the terminal transition fixes; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Naples/Capodichino (LIRN)** and **Catania/Fontanarossa (LICC)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 moderate. Calabria's **Sila (~1,900 m)** and **Aspromonte (~1,955 m)** plateaus are the highest mainland terrain in this FIR; not a cruise-level factor at FL290+. 🟥 **Mount Etna (current summit ≈3,400 m / ~11,165 ft, height varies with eruptive activity)** stands roughly 17–20 NM NNW/N of Catania (LICC) and is both a terrain feature and — far more significantly — a **recurrently erupting stratovolcano**.
- **Boundary terrain:** the southern Apennines continue north across the boundary into **Roma FIR (LIRR)**; no comparably high terrain sits on the Zagreb/Athinai/Malta/Tunisi maritime boundaries.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟧 not a cruise-level constraint (cruise clears all terrain with wide margin); the Etna region is a **volcanic-ash**, not a terrain-escape, concern — see §11 and OM E — Volcanic Ash Avoidance.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** applied at the terminal aerodromes in winter, not in enroute cruise — see the airport briefings (§9).

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military TRA / TSA:** 🟧 **NAS Sigonella** (a joint Italian Air Force/US Navy air station, ICAO LICZ, ~10 NM from Catania) shares regional airspace and generates recurring military/maritime-patrol traffic in the Catania sector. Activation of associated restricted volumes via **AUP/UUP**; Conditional Routes (CDR1/2/3) and the **RAD** govern flyable levels/segments — confirm active TRAs/CDRs at planning.
- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟧 danger areas over the Ionian/southern Adriatic and around Sicily are AIP/NOTAM-published — pull live at planning. Note also the **Etna summit exclusion/no-fly considerations** during active eruptive phases (ASHTAM/NOTAM-driven, not a standing charted restricted area).
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟩 none — standard EU/ECAC operation, no permits.

> **SUA currency:** re-check active TRA/TSA/CDR status (AUP/UUP) and the current RAD at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** per **SERA (Standardised European Rules of the Air, Regulation (EU) No 923/2012)** — squawk **7600**, continue per the SERA IFR comms-failure rules, and attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm any Italian refinement at **AIP ENR 1.8 / GEN**.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical — **request the deviation from ATC** and comply with the vector/level issued. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - 🟥 **Volcanic ash (Etna) — the FIR's signature, standing hazard, not a rare event.** Etna erupts recurrently; VONA Red alerts and full/partial closures of Catania (LICC) happen multiple times per year. **VAAC Toulouse** is the responsible advisory centre for Etna and the wider Italian/Mediterranean volcanic region. Crews must be current on the operator's procedure before operating to LICC: [`OM E — Volcanic Ash Avoidance`](../../../../flight-ops/volcanic-ash-avoidance.md).
  - 🟧 **Secondary volcanic sources:** **Vesuvius** (near Naples/LIRN, dormant but continuously monitored) and the **Aeolian Islands (Stromboli, Vulcano)** off Sicily's north coast (small, persistently active) — lower current risk than Etna but part of the same regional watch.
  - **Summer convection** over the southern Apennines/Ionian; **winter terminal weather** at Naples and Catania — a terminal, not enroute, driver; see the airport briefings.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, **AUP/UUP** (active TRA/CDR status) and current **RAD**, SIGMET/AIRMET, **current VAA/ASHTAM/VONA status for Etna from VAAC Toulouse** (mandatory pull for any Catania rotation), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
