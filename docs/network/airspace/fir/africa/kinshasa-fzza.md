## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Kinshasa FIR (**FZZA**) is **coterminous with the entire Democratic Republic of the Congo** — confirmed as the country's single FIR (no separate eastern/Kisangani split, notwithstanding the country's vast size and the presence of major eastern fields such as Goma (FZNA) and Kisangani (FZIC) within the same FIR).
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 not confirmed — no evidence found this pass of a separate upper-airspace (UTA-type) unit distinct from the FIR itself; treat as a single vertical structure pending AIP confirmation.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N / W — Brazzaville (FCCC, ASECNA — Republic of the Congo/Gabon/Cameroon FIR).** The DRC/Congo border; includes the shared cross-ANSP **"Brazzaville–Kinshasa Pool" TMA** around the twinned FCBB/FZAA terminals, ~13.7 NM apart across the Congo/Malebo Pool, jointly coordinated by **ASECNA** (Congo side) and **RVA** (DRC side) — a genuine two-ANSP coordination point for any operation into or out of Kinshasa (see the FZAA and FCBB airport briefs §3.2).
  - **SW — Luanda (FNAN, Angola/ENANA).** Along the DRC/Angola border, including the area near Angola's Cabinda exclave.
  - **E / SE / NE — multiple East and Central African FIRs** (in the general direction of Uganda/Entebbe, Rwanda/Burundi, Zambia/Lusaka and South Sudan) border the DRC's vast eastern territory. 🟧 **Not individually detailed in this brief** — the exact adjacent-FIR identifiers and boundary geometry on the eastern side are out of scope for the western Kinshasa-gateway operating picture this pack supports; flag for a future build if DRC-domestic or transiting-east routing is required (§12).
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 not confirmed; a transit into/out of Kinshasa is worked by **RVA** — internal sector structure is AIP/AIRAC-dependent.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 **Not confirmed / likely not implemented.** The DRC (RVA) is not an ASECNA member state, and the documented AFI/ASECNA free-route rollout (see the Brazzaville FIR brief) has **not been confirmed for the Kinshasa FIR**. Plan on the fixed ATS-route network unless a current source confirms otherwise.
- **Airway spine:** the fixed ATS-route network feeds **Kinshasa (FZAA)** and connects toward the Brazzaville and Luanda boundaries; eastern routings connect toward the DRC's other major fields (Lubumbashi, Kisangani, Goma) and onward to the eastern neighbour FIRs (§3).
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary fixes toward Brazzaville (FCCC) and Luanda (FNAN) are the relevant ones for this pack's western-gateway operating picture; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Kinshasa — N'djili (FZAA)**, K Global's DRC gateway. The wider DRC network includes **Lubumbashi (FZQA)**, **Kisangani (FZIC)**, **Mbandaka (FZEA)** and **Goma (FZNA)** — the last of these sits inside the eastern conflict zone (§10) and is **not** a recommended diversion. Terminal detail for FZAA is in the airport briefing (§9); the others are outside this pack's built scope.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Kinshasa itself sits on the flat **Congo-Basin lowland**, south bank of the Congo/Malebo Pool — no close-in high terrain relevant to FZAA. 🟧 The country's significant high terrain — the **Albertine Rift** and the **Virunga volcanic range** (active volcanoes near Goma, up to ~11,400 ft / 3,470 m at Nyiragongo) — sits in the **far east**, coincident with the conflict zone (§10); not relevant to the western Kinshasa gateway.
- **Boundary terrain:** not a factor on the Brazzaville/Luanda-facing (western) boundaries relevant to this pack.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint for the Kinshasa-area/western routings; would become relevant only for DRC-domestic or transiting-east operations near the Albertine Rift — out of scope here.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a factor — equatorial/tropical climate at Kinshasa.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military / danger / restricted areas:** 🟧 not confirmed for the western (Kinshasa-side) FIR in reachable sources this pass — pull the active AIP/NOTAM list at planning.
- **Regional security note — DR Congo's conflict is in the EAST, not at Kinshasa (🟧):** 🟥 DR Congo's active conflict is concentrated in the **far east of the country — the Kivu provinces / Goma area** — involving M23 and allied armed groups, with publicly reported instances of **surface-to-air fire against civil aircraft** near the eastern border in the vicinity of Rwanda/Burundi. Public state guidance (e.g. UK/Canadian operator advisories reported via Safe Airspace) has recommended avoiding operation within roughly **200 NM of the eastern FZZA boundary between 1°N and 5°S**, and **below FL260 near the Rwanda/Burundi border**, due to anti-aircraft weapon risk. **Kinshasa (FZAA), in the far west, is geographically remote from this conflict zone and is this brief's operating assumption as K Global's DRC gateway** — the western approach/departure environment at FZAA is not itself affected by the eastern situation. **Do not conflate the two** when briefing this FIR; re-check the live Safe Airspace / OPSGROUP / state-advisory picture before any DRC-domestic routing that approaches the east.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟧 the DRC requires overflight/landing permits for scheduled/non-scheduled foreign operations — a real-world dispatch item; confirm current requirements.

> **SUA / security currency:** the eastern conflict-zone picture is **dynamic — re-check live every time** (Safe Airspace / OPSGROUP / state advisories); this list is durable context, not a live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; apply the ICAO **Doc 7030 (AFI Regional Supplementary Procedures)** comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels); attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 Confirm any DRC AIP refinement.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in this **procedural (non-radar) airspace**, apply the AFI/ICAO weather-deviation and contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique, and coordinate as able. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Equatorial convection** — organised thunderstorms and embedded CB, a near-year-round feature at Kinshasa's latitude, with the wetter season roughly **Sep–May**.
  - **Security wildcard** — the eastern insurgency (Kivu/Goma) is the strategic risk that would materially reshape any DRC-domestic routing east; not a factor for the Kinshasa gateway itself (§10).
