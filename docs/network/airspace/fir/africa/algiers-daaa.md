# Algiers (DAAA) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Algeria** (DAAA is the country's **only** FIR/UIR), spanning the Mediterranean coastal strip and the vast Saharan interior, and containing the **Algiers (DAAG) hub** · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Casablanca (GMMM) W · Tunis (DTTC) E · Tripoli (HLLL) SE (via the Algerian–Libyan border) · Niamey (DRRR) / ASECNA structure S (Sahara/Sahel border with Mali & Niger) 🟧 · Marseille (LFMM, France) N across the Mediterranean (per published LoA) 🟧 confirm exact hand-off with Palma/Madrid-area FIRs further NW
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR ident, ANSP and RVSM confirmed against public AIS-Algeria (ENNA) / ICAO sources, but exact airspace-class letters, internal ACC sector split and the southern (Saharan) boundary hand-off are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Algiers FIR is a **mixed radar/procedural continental FIR** — radar/surveillance around the northern coastal terminals (Algiers, Oran, Constantine) but **procedural over most of the Saharan interior**, in line with AFI regional practice. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions — NOTAMs and the southern-border security picture — are pulled at planning (§11). ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Algiers FIR (**DAAA**) is coterminous with **Algeria** — the country's **only** FIR/UIR, from the Mediterranean coast in the north to the deep Sahara in the south, bounded by the Algerian–Moroccan, Algerian–Malian, Algerian–Nigerien, Algerian–Libyan and Algerian–Tunisian borders.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (terminal CTAs/CTRs and the airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; the exact lower/upper handoff level is AIP-defined — confirm at **ENR 2.1**. RVSM applies **FL290–FL410** over the northern/coastal RVSM area, with a **Transition Area** south of it over the Sahara — confirm current geometry.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **W — Casablanca (GMMM, Morocco/ONDA).**
  - **E — Tunis (DTTC, Tunisia/OACA).**
  - **SE — Tripoli (HLLL, Libya/LYCAA)** — via the long Algerian–Libyan desert border; see the **[Tripoli (HLLL) FIR brief](tripoli-hlll.md)** for the standing conflict-zone caution on that side.
  - **S — the ASECNA structure (Niamey DRRR / adjacent Sahel FIRs, Mali–Niger border)** 🟧 — exact hand-off point not confirmed here; treat the deep south as a remote-procedural boundary.
  - **N — Marseille (LFMM, France)** across the Mediterranean, per a published Algiers–Marseille Letter of Agreement; further NW the boundary runs toward the **Palma/Madrid-area** Spanish FIRs 🟧 confirm exact geometry.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 **Algiers ACC** is the single ATSU for the FIR; internal sector boundaries (coastal vs Saharan sectors) are AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **Algiers ACC**, operated by **ENNA (Établissement National de la Navigation Aérienne)**, Algeria's air-navigation-services provider, working the single DAAA FIR 24 hours.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/surveillance around the **Algiers, Oran and Constantine** terminals and their airways; **procedural** control over most of the Saharan en-route structure. Confirm current radar serviceability at planning.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by ENNA outside radar cover, particularly over the southern desert.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Algiers FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** over the northern RVSM area (in force since **23 OCT 2007**, ahead of the AFI-wide rollout), with a Transition Area south of it. An **RVSM approval** is required to plan the band. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute presumed under the AFI PBN programme; terminal RNP at DAAG where published — confirm the current Algeria AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 not an AFI requirement (an ICAO EUR mandate) — standard 25 kHz VHF applies; the northern Mediterranean boundary interfaces with EUR 8.33 kHz airspace.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 not asserted as a mature FIR-wide capability. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B-Out requirement not asserted here — confirm the current Algeria AIP/NOTAM at planning.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 **no FRA confirmed** for Algiers FIR in public sources at this time (distinct from the ASECNA/WACAF FRA programme to its south) — confirm current status at planning; assume the fixed airway network applies.
- **Airway spine:** the fixed ATS-route network feeds the Algiers, Oran and Constantine terminals and connects toward the Casablanca, Tunis, Tripoli and southern (ASECNA) boundaries.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FIR-boundary reporting points toward the four adjacent continental FIRs and the Mediterranean handoff to Marseille; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Algiers (DAAG, K Global-served)**, plus **Oran (DAOO)** and other domestic majors. Terminal detail for DAAG is in the airport briefing (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 the **Tell Atlas** (coastal range) and **Saharan Atlas** (interior range) run roughly W–E across northern Algeria; the **Ahaggar (Hoggar) massif** in the far south rises to **Mount Tahat ≈ 3,003 m (~9,852 ft)**, the country's highest point. Between the ranges and south of the Saharan Atlas the terrain is mostly high desert plateau.
- **Boundary terrain:** not a major factor at the adjacent-FIR boundaries; the Ahaggar massif sits well within DAAA itself.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** relevant when routing through the Atlas ranges or the Ahaggar massif; over the open Sahara plateau the binding constraint is **diversion-field reach**, not terrain.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not generally a factor — arid/desert climate; apply per airport briefing where relevant at higher-elevation southern fields.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military TRA / danger / restricted areas:** 🟧 AIP/NOTAM-published across the FIR, particularly military ranges in the Sahara — pull the active list at planning.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟥 Algeria requires **overflight clearance**, typically applied for through the civil-aviation/air-transport authority with a **lead-time on the order of 48 working hours** — a real dispatch item; confirm current requirements before every operation.
- **Security exposure (far south):** 🟧 the **southern Sahara / Sahel border belt** (toward Mali/Niger/Libya) has a documented history of insurgent (AQIM-linked) activity and periodic instability — treat as an **elevated-risk watch item**, not a standing closure; re-check the current advisory picture before routing near the border.

> **SUA / security currency:** re-check active danger/restricted areas and the southern-border security picture at planning — this list is durable context, not live clearance.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Algeria AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 confirm the Algeria-specific refinement at AIP ENR 1.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in the **non-radar Saharan interior**, apply the standard ICAO Doc 4444 contingency/weather-deviation procedures (offset, level change, broadcast intentions); radar-tactical via ATC around the coastal terminals.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Sirocco / Chergui dust and sandstorms** — hot, dust-laden southerly winds affecting visibility, most disruptive in spring/summer.
  - **Winter Mediterranean frontal weather** — affects the northern coastal terminals (Algiers/Oran) in the cooler months.
  - **Security wildcard** — the far-south/Sahel-border picture is the strategic risk that would reshape a routing (§10).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, danger/restricted-area status, southern-border security advisories, SIGMET/AIRMET (dust, convection), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
