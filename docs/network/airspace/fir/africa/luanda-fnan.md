## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Luanda FIR (**FNAN**) is **coterminous with Angola**, including — geographically — the **Cabinda exclave**, which is separated from mainland Angola by DRC territory and borders the Republic of the Congo directly.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 not confirmed — no evidence found this pass of a separate upper-airspace (UTA-type) unit distinct from the FIR itself; treat as a single vertical structure pending AIP confirmation.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Kinshasa (FZZA, DRC/RVA).** Along the DRC/Angola mainland border.
  - **N (Cabinda area) — Brazzaville (FCCC, ASECNA).** 🟧 Angola's Cabinda exclave borders the Republic of the Congo directly, implying a Luanda/Brazzaville FIR boundary near Cabinda — **not independently confirmed**; flagged for verification (§12).
  - **E — Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia).** Along the Angola/Zambia border.
  - **S — Windhoek (FYWF, Namibia).** Along the Angola/Namibia border.
  - **W — Atlantic oceanic**, off the Angolan coast.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 not confirmed; internal sector structure is AIP/AIRAC-dependent.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Luanda FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**, confirmed applicable in the Luanda FIR; an RVSM approval is required. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute presumed per the AFI norm — not independently confirmed for FNAN; terminal RNP at FNBJ not confirmed. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not a mature FIR-wide capability**. The **AFI In-Flight Broadcast Procedure (IFBP)** — listening watch on **126.9 MHz** — is the published mitigation; Luanda is one of the FIRs on this network. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement not asserted here — confirm current Angola AIP/NOTAM at planning.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 **Not confirmed / likely not implemented.** Angola is not an ASECNA member state, and the documented AFI/ASECNA free-route rollout (see the Brazzaville FIR brief) has **not been confirmed for the Luanda FIR**. Plan on the fixed ATS-route network unless a current source confirms otherwise.
- **Airway spine:** the fixed ATS-route network feeds **Luanda** and connects toward the Kinshasa, Lusaka and Windhoek boundaries and the Atlantic oceanic exit.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary fixes toward the adjacent continental FIRs; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟥 **Luanda's civil gateway has changed.** **FNLU (4 de Fevereiro)** — the field this brief's task anchor originally referenced — has been **closed to scheduled commercial operations since 1 March 2026**; the operating civil gateway for Luanda is now **FNBJ (Dr. António Agostinho Neto Intl)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 the Luanda coastal area (both FNLU at 243 ft AMSL and FNBJ) sits on the flat Atlantic coastal strip — no close-in high terrain relevant to arrival/departure. Angola's interior **Bié Plateau** and the southern **Serra da Chela** escarpment are well inland and not a factor for the coastal Luanda gateway.
- **Boundary terrain:** not identified as a factor on the FIR's boundaries relevant to this pack.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint for the Luanda coastal routings.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a factor — the **Benguela current** moderates the coastal climate (cooler/drier than the classic equatorial belt further north) but this is a seasonal-visibility issue (§11), not a cold-temperature correction one.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; apply the ICAO **Doc 7030 (AFI Regional Supplementary Procedures)** comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels); attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 Confirm any Angola AIP refinement.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in this **procedural (non-radar) airspace**, apply the AFI/ICAO weather-deviation and contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique, and coordinate as able. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
