# Dar es Salaam (HTDC) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Tanzania**, including the **Kilimanjaro (HTKJ)** gateway and **Zanzibar (HTZA)** island · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Nairobi (HKNA) N · Entebbe (HUEC) NW · Kigali (HRYR) W · Kinshasa (FZZA) W/SW (across Lake Tanganyika) · Lusaka (FLFI) SW 🟧 · Beira (FQBE) S 🟧 · Seychelles (FSSS) / Antananarivo (FMMM) E/SE (oceanic) 🟧
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR ident/authority/RVSM verified against public sources; exact adjacent-FIR boundary set, ACC sector split and live radar serviceability are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Dar es Salaam FIR is a **mixed radar/procedural continental FIR** in line with ICAO AFI/ESAF practice, worked by the **Dar es Salaam ACC** (reported Eastern and Western sectors). It spans a coastal/hub segment (Zanzibar, the Indian Ocean littoral) and an inland segment reaching toward Kilimanjaro and the Great Lakes border belt. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Dar es Salaam FIR (**HTDC**) is coterminous with **Tanzania** — it is the country's single FIR, bordered by Kenya and Uganda to the north, Rwanda, Burundi and the Democratic Republic of the Congo to the west, and Zambia, Malawi and Mozambique to the south, with a long Indian Ocean coastline (including Zanzibar/Pemba) to the east. 🟧 Precise coordinate-defined boundary points are AIP-published — confirm at **Tanzania AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (terminal CTAs/CTRs and the airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; the lower/upper handoff level is AIP-defined — confirm at **ENR 2.1**. RVSM applies **FL290–FL410**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Nairobi (HKNA, Kenya)** — the principal East-Africa hand-off, including traffic to/from HTKJ near the Kenya border.
  - **NW — Entebbe (HUEC, Uganda)** 🟧.
  - **W — Kigali (HRYR, Rwanda)** and **Kinshasa (FZZA, DRC)** — the Great Lakes/Congo-basin border belt, across Lake Tanganyika/Lake Victoria catchments 🟧.
  - **SW — Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia)** 🟧 — precise boundary not verified against AIP; treat as durable-context placeholder.
  - **S — Beira (FQBE, Mozambique)** / **Malawi airspace** 🟧 — precise FIR ident and boundary for the Tanzania/Malawi/Mozambique tripoint not verified; confirm at planning.
  - **E/SE — Seychelles (FSSS)** and **Antananarivo (FMMM, Madagascar)** — oceanic hand-off across the SW Indian Ocean/Mozambique Channel approaches 🟧.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟩 TCAA has introduced a dedicated **Dar es Salaam ACC**, reported with **Eastern and Western sectors**; exact internal boundaries and controlling frequencies are AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **TCAA — Tanzania Civil Aviation Authority**, both the ANSP and safety regulator, operating the **Dar es Salaam ACC** for the single HTDC FIR.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/surveillance is expected around **Dar es Salaam (HTDA)**, **Kilimanjaro (HTKJ)** and **Zanzibar (HTZA)**; the interior/border-belt en-route structure is expected to be **procedural** per AFI/ESAF norms. Confirm live serviceability — do not assume continuous coverage.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by TCAA outside radar cover; the **AFI IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) supports separation awareness where VHF/radar is thin.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Dar es Salaam FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an **RVSM approval** from the State of Registry/Operator is required. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; terminal RNP where published at HTKJ/HTZA — confirm the current Tanzania AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not a mature FIR-wide capability** in HTDC. Where VHF/radar is limited the **AFI IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) is the mitigation, **not** a FANS CPDLC service. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current Tanzania AIP/NOTAM at planning.
- **Terrain-critical approach note (HTKJ):** 🟥 Kilimanjaro terrain adjoins the HTKJ terminal environment — apply RNAV/RNP approach guidance and minimum safe altitudes per the current chart; see the HTKJ airport brief.

- **Airway spine (where FRA not available):** the fixed ATS-route network feeds Dar es Salaam, Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar and connects to the Nairobi, Entebbe, Kigali, Kinshasa, Lusaka and Beira boundaries.

- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Kilimanjaro Intl (HTKJ)** — the northern/inland gateway near Mount Kilimanjaro and the Kenya border, serving Arusha/Moshi; **Abeid Amani Karume Intl (HTZA)** — Zanzibar's island hub. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟥 **Mount Kilimanjaro (~5,895 m / 19,341 ft)** — the highest peak in Africa — dominates the terrain picture, sitting close to **HTKJ**. Elsewhere Tanzania is largely plateau and coastal lowland; **Zanzibar (HTZA)** is a low-lying island.
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟧 further high ground continues north into the **Kenyan highlands/Mount Kenya** (Nairobi FIR) and along the Great Rift Valley shoulders to the west toward the Great Lakes; relevant to driftdown/escape on routings through those corridors, not for a Dar es Salaam–Zanzibar coastal transit.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** relevant on routings via HTKJ given the immediate Kilimanjaro terrain; the coastal/island corridor (Zanzibar) is low and comparatively unconstrained by terrain.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟧 relevant at HTKJ given its elevation and terrain proximity — see the airport brief and [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md); not a factor at the low-lying Zanzibar terminal.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Tanzania AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 Confirm the Tanzania refinement at AIP ENR 1.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in non-radar (procedural) airspace, apply the AFI/ICAO weather-deviation and contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique, and coordinate as able. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **SW Indian Ocean tropical cyclones (≈ Nov–Apr, peak Jan–Mar)** — affect the Zanzibar/coastal side and the wider Mozambique Channel approaches; a cyclone-season planning and diversion-coverage factor for HTZA.
  - **ITCZ / equatorial convection** — seasonal migration produces embedded/towering CB, particularly around the long/short rains.
  - **Kilimanjaro-adjacent terrain/weather interaction** at HTKJ — orographic cloud and turbulence in proximity to the mountain.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, western-border-belt advisories (Safe Airspace / state advisories), SIGMET/AIRMET (ITCZ convection, SW Indian Ocean cyclone tracking), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
