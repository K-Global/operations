# Brazzaville (FCCC) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **large Central-African FIR/UTA** administered by **ASECNA**, covering the **Republic of the Congo, Gabon and Cameroon** (+ Central African Republic and Equatorial Guinea; Chad's inclusion is 🟧 unconfirmed — see §3), containing the **FCBB Brazzaville**, **FKKD Douala**, **FKYS Yaoundé Nsimalen** and **FOOL Libreville** gateways · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Kano (DNKK) NW · N'Djamena (FTTT) NE · Kinshasa (FZZA) SE · Luanda (FNAN) S (Cabinda-area boundary) · Gulf of Guinea oceanic W
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR extent, RVSM and FRA verified against public ASECNA/ICAO/OPS sources, but exact lateral boundary detail, class letters and the local sub-FIR/ACC naming used in the K Global airport briefs are flagged 🟧 for reconciliation

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Brazzaville FIR is a **procedural continental FIR** administered by **ASECNA** (Agence pour la Sécurité de la Navigation Aérienne en Afrique et à Madagascar) on behalf of its member states, with **Free Route Airspace (FRA) available FL250 and above** in the Brazzaville UTA since the January 2024 ASECNA-wide rollout. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9) — **note the important FIR-naming reconciliation in §3/§12**: several K Global airport briefs record a local "Douala FIR (FKKK)" or "Libreville FIR (FOOO)"; this brief treats those as **local ACC/sub-designator shorthand inside the single Brazzaville FIR**, not independent controlling FIRs. Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Brazzaville FIR (**FCCC**) is the **ASECNA-administered FIR covering the Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Cameroon, Central African Republic and Equatorial Guinea** (plus São Tomé). Public boundary data places its perimeter along the **Cameroon/Nigeria border** (NW), the **Central African Republic/Sudan border** (N/NE), the **CAR/DRC and Congo/DRC borders** (E/SE), and the **Congo/Cabinda border down to the coast** (S). 🟧 **Chad** appears in one ASECNA administrative source as a state served by the Brazzaville-based directorate, but Chad's national airspace is more commonly associated with the **separate N'Djamena FIR (FTTT)**, itself ASECNA-administered — **this brief does not assert Chad's territory lies inside the FCCC lateral boundary**; treat as an open item (§12).
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower **Brazzaville FIR** beneath an overlying **Brazzaville UTA** (upper airspace unit) that hosts the FL250+ Free Route Airspace; the exact lower/upper handoff level is **not independently confirmed** — ASECNA FIRs commonly split in the FL245–FL250 range, treat as indicative only pending AIP confirmation.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Kano (DNKK, Nigeria/NAMA).** Along the Cameroon/Nigeria border.
  - **NE — N'Djamena (FTTT, Chad/CAR, ASECNA).** Northern boundary.
  - **SE — Kinshasa (FZZA, DRC/RVA).** Along the Congo/DRC border; includes the shared cross-ANSP **"Brazzaville–Kinshasa Pool" TMA** around the twinned FCBB/FZAA terminals (brief for coordination workload — see the FCBB and FZAA airport briefs §3.2).
  - **S — Luanda (FNAN, Angola/ENANA).** 🟧 Likely boundary near the **Cabinda exclave** (Angola's territory north of the Congo River, bordering the Republic of Congo directly) — not independently confirmed; flagged for verification (§12).
  - **W — Gulf of Guinea oceanic**, transitioning toward the Accra (DGAC, Ghana) sector further along the coast.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 **Reconciliation note — read before relying on any airport-brief FIR reference:** the **FKKD (Douala)** and **FKYS (Yaoundé Nsimalen)** airport briefs record their controlling airspace as **"FIR Douala (FKKK)"**, and the **FOOL (Libreville)** brief records **"FIR Libreville (FOOO)"**. Public-source research for this brief found that **FKKK is the location indicator for a "Douala Sub-FIR" / "Douala ACC"** used in ASECNA's own contingency-planning documentation (e.g. the ASECNA "ATM Contingency Plan for Douala ACC"), but that **the actual controlling Flight Information Region for Douala is the single, ASECNA-administered Brazzaville FIR (FCCC)** — FKKK is **not** an independent ICAO FIR with its own boundary in the way FCCC, FZZA or FNAN are. The same logic is assumed to apply to the Libreville-area "FOOO" reference (Gabon delegates air navigation service provision at Libreville to ASECNA), though this has **not been independently corroborated** to the same standard as the Douala case — flag 🟧. **This FIR brief (FCCC) is the source of truth for the actual controlling FIR**; the individual airport briefs are unchanged in this pass (out of scope for this task) — recommend a follow-up edit to align FKKD/FKYS/FOOL with Brazzaville (FCCC) as the controlling FIR, noting FKKK/FOOO as local ACC/sub-designator shorthand.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **Lower-airspace ATSU:** **ASECNA Brazzaville ACC** (Brazzaville FIR) — procedural/non-radar assumption per AFI norm, not independently confirmed.
- **Upper-airspace ATSU:** **Brazzaville UTA** — the ASECNA upper-airspace unit hosting the FL250+ Free Route Airspace; exact lower/upper handoff not confirmed (§3).
- **FIS / FSS:** ASECNA-provided flight information service outside positive control; local control positions (Douala, Yaoundé, Libreville, Brazzaville) handle terminal-area traffic within the single FCCC FIR (§3 reconciliation).
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 predominantly **procedural**; radar/MLAT/ADS-B coverage at the terminal majors (Brazzaville, Douala, Yaoundé, Libreville) **not independently confirmed** — do not assume radar service without local confirmation.

- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; **Free Route Airspace FL250 and above** in the Brazzaville UTA (part of the ASECNA-wide FRA rollout effective 25 Jan 2024) — confirm terminal RNP per the current national AIP. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement not asserted here — confirm current AIP/NOTAM at planning.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 **FL250 and above** in the **Brazzaville UTA**, part of the ASECNA-wide free-route rollout effective **25 January 2024** — direct planning between defined boundary/reporting points. Confirm the current FRA point set / conditions at planning.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / below floor):** the **fixed ATS-route network** feeds Brazzaville, Douala, Yaoundé Nsimalen and Libreville, connecting to the Kano, N'Djamena, Kinshasa and Gulf-of-Guinea-oceanic boundaries.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the FRA/airway boundary fixes toward the four adjacent FIRs; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Brazzaville — Maya-Maya (FCBB)**, **Douala (FKKD)**, **Yaoundé — Nsimalen (FKYS)**, **Libreville — Léon-Mba (FOOL)** — the four K Global gateways in this FIR. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9). Other national fields (e.g. further Congolese, Cameroonian and Gabonese domestic airports) exist within the FIR but are outside this pack's scope.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 The FIR is predominantly **Congo-Basin equatorial lowland** — generally low relief. Two notable exceptions: **Mount Cameroon** (~13,255 ft / 4,040 m, an **active volcano**, last erupted 2000) sits inland of the Gulf-of-Guinea coast west of Douala; and the **Yaoundé plateau/hill country** ("city of seven hills," field elevation ~2,278 ft) around FKYS. Libreville and Brazzaville themselves sit at low coastal/riverine elevations with no close-in high terrain.
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟧 the Adamawa Plateau (northern Cameroon) and the Mambilla/Adamawa highlands sit near the Cameroon/Nigeria boundary further north (see the Kano (DNKK) FIR brief for the Nigeria-side detail); not a factor for the FCBB/FKKD/FKYS/FOOL routings covered here except as regional context.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint over most of the FIR; Mount Cameroon's proximity to Douala is the one terrain feature warranting standing caution for arrival/departure and missed-approach planning at FKKD — confirm current chart terrain/obstacle data locally.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a factor — equatorial/tropical climate throughout.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; apply the ICAO **Doc 7030 (AFI Regional Supplementary Procedures)** comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels); attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 Confirm any national AIP refinement.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in this **procedural (non-radar) airspace**, apply the AFI/ICAO weather-deviation and contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique, and coordinate as able. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Equatorial convection** — organised thunderstorms and embedded CB are a near-year-round feature of this FIR's climate, with a wetter season roughly **Sep–May** and a shorter drier spell **Jun–Aug** (varies by exact latitude/coast vs. interior); tactical avoidance is the norm, not the exception.
  - **Coastal/riverine humidity and haze** at the Gulf-of-Guinea gateways (Douala, Libreville) — visibility-reduction potential, confirm current conditions at planning.
