# Seychelles (FSSS) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **Seychelles archipelago** (Mahé/inner islands, Praslin, Amirantes, Aldabra group) and a large surrounding tract of the **NW/SW Indian Ocean**, bridging East Africa to the open ocean · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Mogadishu (HCSM) NW 🟥 conflict-zone-adjacent, re-check live · Nairobi (HKNC) W · Dar es Salaam (HTDC) SW · Antananarivo (FMMM) S · Mauritius (FIMM) SE · Mumbai Oceanic (VABF) NE
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR/TMA structure confirmed against public sources, but exact FIR area/boundary coordinates and current PBN/FRA status are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Seychelles FIR is a **fully procedural oceanic FIR — no radar/SSR installation exists in it** — ATC relies on ADS and mandatory position reports. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). **The FIR's one live strategic-planning item is its NW boundary with Mogadishu (HCSM)**, which the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §9 flags as a 🟥 conflict/overflight-risk zone — a routing that approaches that boundary must re-check the current Somalia advisory picture, even though Seychelles airspace itself is benign. Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Seychelles FIR (**FSSS**) covers the **Seychelles archipelago** — the granitic inner islands around Mahé and Praslin, plus the coralline **Amirantes, Farquhar, Aldabra and Cosmoledo groups** further south-west — and a substantial surrounding tract of the **NW/SW Indian Ocean**, bridging the East African coast to the open ocean toward India.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 the FIR is declared **surface-to-unlimited**, with **Class A above FL145** and effectively uncontrolled airspace below FL145 outside the Mahé terminal structure (Seychelles CTA FL105–FL145, TMA-A/B GND–FL075, TMA-C FL075–FL105, all Class D; CTR GND–3,500 ft, Class D). **Transition altitude 4,500 ft within 25 NM of the Seychelles Intl ARP, 3,500 ft beyond**; first usable flight level **FL060**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary, confirmed against a public adjacency table):**
  - **NW — Mogadishu (HCSM, Somalia).** 🟥 **Conflict/overflight-risk zone** — the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §9 flags Mogadishu FIR as avoid-below-FL260 with an active advisory picture; re-check before any routing that nears this boundary.
  - **W — Nairobi (HKNC, Kenya).**
  - **SW — Dar es Salaam (HTDC, Tanzania).**
  - **S — Antananarivo (FMMM, ASECNA/Madagascar).** See the [Antananarivo FIR brief](antananarivo-fmmm.md).
  - **SE — Mauritius (FIMM).** See the [Mauritius FIR brief](mauritius-fimm.md).
  - **NE — Mumbai Oceanic (VABF, India).**
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 the FIR is worked as a **single Seychelles Control sector** in the sources used here; no internal split is evidenced.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **Seychelles Control**, under the **Seychelles Civil Aviation Authority (SCAA)** — the national regulator and ANSP.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 **no radar/SSR installation exists in the Seychelles FIR** — confirmed. ATC is procedural throughout, using **ADS** and mandatory position reporting.
- **FIS:** procedural service is provided by Seychelles Control; no separate upper-airspace unit is evidenced.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Seychelles FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an **RVSM approval** is required to plan the AFI RVSM band. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute (AFI baseline). At **Seychelles Intl (FSIA)**, public procedure naming references **"RNP 1 required" / GNSS-required** arrivals on at least one runway — indicating a **PBN-based terminal environment**, but the full RNP spec is not confirmed here. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 not a mature FIR-wide capability confirmed here. Where VHF is thin, the AFI **IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) is the mitigation. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current AIP/NOTAM.
- **ETOPS / long over-water:** the legs beyond the inner-island group (toward the Amirantes/Aldabra or the Mumbai oceanic boundary) are long-diversion segments — see [`OM E — ETOPS-EDTO Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/etops-edto.md).

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a transit is worked by **Seychelles Control** throughout; exact frequencies are AIRAC/AIP-dependent — fly the current AIP, not a stored table.
- **HF & remote:** 🟧 **HF** and the **AFI IFBP (126.9 MHz listening watch)** cover the oceanic reaches beyond VHF range of Mahé/Praslin.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO) throughout.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air; **126.9 MHz** AFI IFBP.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 not established in this FIR per the sources used here — confirm against the current SCAA AIP/ESAF FRA rollout status (per the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §6, ESAF trials are the 2026 focus).
- **Airway spine:** a fixed ATS-route network (B/G/L/M/N/R/W-prefixed airways) radiates from **Praslin (PRA VOR/DME)** toward Madagascar (the **BERIL** boundary fix family), East Africa and India-bound tracks.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary fixes toward Mogadishu, Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Antananarivo, Mauritius and Mumbai; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string.
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Seychelles Intl (FSIA), Mahé — the only served field in this brief's scope**; **Praslin (FSPP)**, a shorter regional strip, sits within the same FIR but is out of scope here.

---

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 the Seychelles inner islands are granitic with modest relief (Mahé's highest point, Morne Seychellois, is under 910 m); the outer coralline atolls are near sea level. **No enroute MORA/driftdown factor.**
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** the binding constraint on the longer oceanic legs is diversion-field reach, not terrain — see §9.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a factor — equatorial/tropical climate.

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Danger / restricted / prohibited areas:** 🟩 minor local restricted areas around Mahé (island/bird-sanctuary protection — Frégate, Cousin, Aride, Bird Islands — plus a prison-protection area near the airport) — routine and low-altitude.
- **🟥 Conflict-zone-adjacent boundary:** the FIR's **NW boundary with Mogadishu (HCSM)** sits next to a confirmed AFI conflict/overflight-risk zone (avoid below FL260, active advisory per the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §9). This is not a hazard inside Seychelles airspace itself, but any routing that approaches or threads that boundary must re-check the current Somalia advisory picture.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟧 Seychelles permit requirements are not detailed here — confirm current requirements for the operation.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO comms-failure rules (last clearance/filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 confirm the Seychelles-specific refinement at the current SCAA AIP.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** apply the standard **ICAO Doc 4444 general in-flight contingency and weather-deviation procedures** in this non-radar FIR — offset, vertical-offset convention, broadcast on IFBP/HF.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - Seychelles sits largely **north of the main SW Indian Ocean tropical-cyclone belt** (which is centred further south, around Madagascar/Mauritius/Réunion) — cyclone exposure here is comparatively **lower**, but the outer islands (Aldabra group) can still be grazed; monitor the season regardless. See [`OM E — Adverse Weather and Windshear`](../../../../flight-ops/adverse-weather-and-windshear.md).
  - **ITCZ convection** migrates across/near Seychelles seasonally, producing embedded/towering CB.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, the **Mogadishu-boundary security/overflight advisory** (Safe Airspace / OPSGROUP / state advisories), SIGMET/AIRMET, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
