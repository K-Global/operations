# Antananarivo (FMMM) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — **the whole of Madagascar** plus the surrounding SW Indian Ocean airspace, including the **French island of Réunion** (FMEE/FMEP) and Mayotte (FMCZ) within its lateral boundary · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Mauritius (FIMM) E · Seychelles (FSSS) N · Dar es Salaam (HTDC) NW · Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO) SW/S 🟧 confirm exact azimuths
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR structure/RVSM verified against public ASECNA/ICAO-AFI sources, but ACC sector detail, exact class-letter bands and the current Réunion-delegation citation are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Antananarivo FIR is an **ASECNA-run, mixed radar/procedural oceanic-island FIR** — SSR/primary radar around the Ivato (Antananarivo) terminal, procedural (ADS/position-report) over the rest of Madagascar and the surrounding ocean, in line with AFI practice. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). The **Réunion delegation** (§3/§12) is the standing open item: FMEE/FMEP sit inside this FIR's lateral boundary but ATS there is understood to be delegated to French authorities. Live restrictions and the SW Indian Ocean cyclone picture are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Antananarivo FIR (**FMMM**) is coterminous with **Madagascar** plus the surrounding SW Indian Ocean airspace, and — per its ASECNA/Malagasy administration — **its lateral boundary also encompasses the French islands of Réunion (FMEE Roland Garros, FMEP Pierrefonds) and Mayotte (FMCZ)**, both outside Madagascar's sovereign territory. This is a genuine ASECNA-FIR peculiarity: one Malagasy-administered FIR whose boundary reaches into French Indian Ocean airspace.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 upper **UTA FL245–FL460** (Class A, per the ASECNA/IVAO structure); the FIR's lower layers (GND–FL245) are published as predominantly **Class G (uncontrolled)** outside the Antananarivo TMA/CTR — confirm the exact class-letter bands against the current ASECNA eAIP. **Transition altitude 8,000 ft**; first usable flight level **FL090**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **E — Mauritius (FIMM).** SW Indian Ocean oceanic hand-off; see the [Mauritius FIR brief](mauritius-fimm.md).
  - **N — Seychelles (FSSS).** See the [Seychelles FIR brief](seychelles-fsss.md).
  - **NW — Dar es Salaam (HTDC, Tanzania).**
  - **SW/S — Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO, ATNS).** Large procedural SW Indian Ocean/South Atlantic OCA (per the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §3).
  - 🟧 Exact boundary azimuths/coordinates are tier-4 sourced here — confirm against the ASECNA ENR 6 chart.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 the FIR is worked as a **single Antananarivo Control sector** in the public/sim sources used here; no internal ACC sector split is evidenced — confirm against the current AIP/AIRAC.
- **🟧 Réunion delegation (the FIR's defining caveat):** **FMEE (Roland Garros) and FMEP (Pierrefonds)**, on the French island of **Réunion**, sit inside the Antananarivo FIR's published lateral boundary — but a **bilateral French–Malagasy technical arrangement** is reported to delegate air traffic service in the **La Réunion TMA/CTR** to French authorities (France's civil aviation administration maintains an Indian Ocean air-navigation office based at Réunion). In practice this means the **ATS provider and callsign inside the Réunion terminal area read as French/"Réunion", not "Antananarivo"**, even though the FIR ident on a flight plan remains **FMMM**. **This is a live 🟧 item: confirm the current designation, the exact delegated volume, and the citing AIP paragraph before relying on it operationally** (§12).

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **Antananarivo ACC ("Antananarivo Control")**, **ASECNA** — the multinational agency that also runs Dakar Oceanic/Terrestre, Niamey, N'Djamena and Brazzaville (see the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §3).
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 **SSR/primary radar** is reported centred on **Ivato (TNV VOR, 115.100 MHz)** with a quoted coverage of **≈250 NM at FL600** — but primary radar alone cannot resolve altitude/speed, and pilot position reports remain the controlling mechanism outside that terminal footprint. **Automatic Dependent Surveillance (ADS)** and mandatory position reporting cover the rest of the FIR, including the Réunion-bound oceanic segment. Treat surveillance as **terminal-only** and confirm live.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service outside radar cover is provided by Antananarivo Control; no separate upper-airspace unit is evidenced.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Antananarivo FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an **RVSM approval** is required to plan the AFI RVSM band. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute (AFI PBN baseline); terminal RNP at Ivato (FMMI) not confirmed here — check the current ASECNA eAIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not a mature FIR-wide capability.** Where VHF/radar is thin, the AFI **In-Flight Broadcast Procedure (IFBP)** — a listening watch on **126.9 MHz** with position self-broadcast — is the mitigation, not a FANS CPDLC service. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current AIP/NOTAM.
- **ETOPS / long over-water:** the Réunion/Mauritius-bound legs and the Mozambique Channel crossing are ETOPS/long-diversion segments — see [`OM E — ETOPS-EDTO Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/etops-edto.md).

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟩 Antananarivo is one of the ASECNA FIRs covered by **WACAF-style FRA at FL250 and above** (per the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §6) — confirm the current point set/conditions at planning.
- **Airway spine (where FRA not available / below floor):** the ASECNA fixed ATS-route network (A/B/G/L/M/N/R/U-prefixed airways) radiates from Ivato toward the Mauritius, Seychelles, Dar es Salaam and Johannesburg-Oceanic boundaries.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary fixes toward Mauritius/Seychelles (e.g. the **BERIL/NIBIS/SDG** family reported in the ASECNA route structure) and the Réunion/Mayotte feeder points; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string.
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Antananarivo — Ivato (FMMI, home hub)**; other Malagasy domestic fields (Toamasina FMMT, Antsiranana/Arrachart FMNA, Toliary FMST, Sainte-Marie FMMS and others); and, per §3, **Réunion (FMEE/FMEP)** and **Mayotte (FMCZ)** inside the FIR boundary. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Madagascar is mostly plateau and coastal lowland; the central highlands reach **Maromokotro ≈2,876 m (9,436 ft)**, the island's highest point — not a cruise-level factor at RVSM levels. The oceanic sectors, including the Réunion approach, have no enroute terrain of note (Réunion's own volcanic terrain — Piton des Neiges ≈3,069 m — is a terminal-approach consideration handled in the FMEE airport brief, not the enroute FIR).
- **Boundary terrain:** none of operational significance at the FIR's continental/oceanic boundaries.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint over most of the FIR; the binding constraint on the longer oceanic legs (Réunion/Mauritius) is diversion-field reach, not terrain.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a significant factor — tropical/subtropical climate.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/ASECNA IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance/filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 confirm the Madagascar/ASECNA refinement at the current AIP.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in the **non-radar (procedural) core**, apply the AFI/ICAO Doc 4444 weather-deviation and general in-flight contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **SW Indian Ocean tropical cyclones (≈ Nov–Apr, peak Jan–Mar)** — affect Madagascar's east coast, the Mozambique Channel and Réunion; long over-water legs with thin diversion coverage during the season. See [`OM E — Adverse Weather and Windshear`](../../../../flight-ops/adverse-weather-and-windshear.md).
  - **ITCZ convection** — seasonal embedded/towering CB across the wider AFI region (per the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §11).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, danger/restricted-area status, SIGMET/AIRMET and the **cyclone-season track/warning** picture (Météo France La Réunion RSMC is the regional tropical-cyclone warning centre for the SW Indian Ocean), the current **FRA point set**, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
