# Mauritius (FIMM) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — **one of the largest oceanic FIRs in the SW Indian Ocean**, covering the Republic of Mauritius (Mauritius island, Rodrigues, Agalega, Cargados Carajos/St Brandon) and a vast surrounding tract of open ocean bridging toward the Maldives, India and Australia oceanic boundaries · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Antananarivo (FMMM) W · Seychelles (FSSS) N · Mumbai Oceanic (VABF) NE · Male / Maldives (VRMF) NE · Melbourne Oceanic (YMMO) E/SE (remote) · Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO) SW
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR structure/adjacency confirmed against public sources, but exact boundary coordinates, the ANSP's precise name and the CPDLC-mandate claim are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Mauritius FIR is a **fully procedural oceanic FIR — no radar/SSR installation exists inside it** — surveillance and separation rest entirely on position reports and Automatic Dependent Surveillance (ADS). Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). This is a genuinely huge FIR by area — plan the oceanic legs with the same discipline as an Atlantic/Pacific crossing. Live restrictions and the SW Indian Ocean cyclone picture are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Mauritius FIR (**FIMM**) is one of the **largest FIRs in the SW Indian Ocean by area** — it covers not only the island of Mauritius but also **Rodrigues** (≈560 km ENE), **Agalega** and the **Cargados Carajos/St Brandon** banks, and then extends across a vast tract of open ocean toward the **Maldives/India** oceanic boundary and, remarkably, all the way to the edge of the **Melbourne (Australia) Oceanic FIR** to the east/south-east. 🟧 The exact boundary coordinates are not independently confirmed here, and whether the **Chagos Archipelago / Diego Garcia (FJDG, British Indian Ocean Territory)** falls inside FIMM or an adjacent FIR is an **open item** — verify against the ICAO AFI/ESAF Air Navigation Plan.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 upper **FIR FL245–FL460** (Class A); lower airspace (3,500 ft/FL245 down to the surface) is reported as predominantly **Class G (uncontrolled)** outside the Mauritius TMA (Class C, 3,500 ft–FL245) and CTR (Class C, GND–3,500 ft) around Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam. **Transition altitude 4,000 ft**; first usable flight level **FL060**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary, confirmed against a public adjacency table):**
  - **W — Antananarivo (FMMM, ASECNA/Madagascar).** See the [Antananarivo FIR brief](antananarivo-fmmm.md).
  - **N — Seychelles (FSSS, SCAA).** See the [Seychelles FIR brief](seychelles-fsss.md).
  - **NE — Mumbai Oceanic (VABF, India)** and **Male/Maldives (VRMF)** — the north-eastern oceanic reach of this FIR.
  - **E/SE — Melbourne Oceanic (YMMO, Australia)** — a remote boundary reflecting the FIR's genuinely large extent.
  - **SW — Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO, ATNS).**
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 the FIR is worked as a **single Mauritius Centre sector** in the sources used here; no internal split is evidenced.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟧 **Mauritius ACC ("Mauritius Centre")** — the ANSP's precise corporate/statutory name is not confirmed here; the national civil-aviation administration is the likely regulator/operator. Confirm against `civil-aviation.govmu.org` (the national AIP portal).
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟩 **no radar/SSR installation exists in the Mauritius FIR** — this is confirmed, not merely inferred. All ATC services are procedural, using **ADS** and mandatory position reporting.
- **FIS:** procedural service is provided throughout by Mauritius Centre; no separate upper-airspace unit is evidenced.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Mauritius FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an **RVSM approval** is required to plan the AFI RVSM band. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute (AFI baseline); terminal RNP at FIMP not confirmed here — check the current national AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 one public source states CPDLC is **available above FL195** and becomes the **primary means above FL245** in this FIR — **not independently corroborated here; verify** against the current AIP/AIC before relying on it. Where datalink is not available/serviceable, HF + the AFI **IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) is the fallback. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current AIP/NOTAM.
- **ETOPS / long over-water:** the legs toward the Maldives/India and Australia oceanic boundaries are the FIR's defining long-diversion segments — see [`OM E — ETOPS-EDTO Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/etops-edto.md) and §9.

## 6. Communications & frequencies

- **ACC sectors / frequencies:** a transit is worked by **Mauritius Centre** throughout; the exact frequencies are AIRAC/AIP-dependent — fly the current AIP, not a stored table.
- **HF & remote:** 🟧 **HF** and the **AFI IFBP (126.9 MHz listening watch)** cover the oceanic core, with no VHF beyond the terminal footprint around Mauritius. Exact HF kHz assignments are not confirmed here — verify against the national AIP ENR 3/GEN COM.
- **Language:** 🟩 **English** (ICAO); French in regional operational use.
- **Emergency / guard:** **121.5 MHz** guard (monitor); **123.45 MHz** air-to-air; **126.9 MHz** AFI IFBP.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 not established in this FIR per the sources used here — the **ESAF (Eastern & Southern Africa) FRA rollout** referenced in the [Africa area brief](../../africa.md) §6 has Mauritius as a candidate but no confirmed live status; confirm at planning.
- **Airway spine:** a fixed ATS-route network (Q/L/R/T/UM/UR/UT-prefixed airways) radiates from **Plaisance (PLS VOR/DME)** and **Grand Bay (GBY VOR/DME)** near the Mauritius terminal toward Madagascar/Antananarivo, Seychelles and the Indian-subcontinent/Australia oceanic tracks.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** boundary fixes toward Antananarivo, Seychelles and the Mumbai/Male oceanic hand-offs; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string.
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Intl (FIMP), Mauritius — the only served field in this brief's scope**; **Rodrigues — Plaine Corail (FIMR)**, a shorter domestic strip, sits within the same FIR but is out of scope here. Terminal detail for FIMP is in the airport briefing (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Mauritius and Rodrigues are low volcanic islands with modest peaks (Mauritius's highest point, Piton de la Petite Rivière Noire, is under 830 m) — **no cruise-level MORA/driftdown factor**. The rest of the FIR is open ocean.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** the binding constraint on the longer legs is **diversion-field reach, not terrain** — see §9.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a factor — tropical/subtropical climate.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO comms-failure rules (last clearance/filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 confirm the Mauritius-specific refinement at the current AIP.
- **Oceanic / procedural contingency:** 🟧 no SW-Indian-Ocean-specific contingency plan (analogous to the EUR/SAM Corridor's SAT Doc 002) is confirmed here — apply the standard **ICAO Doc 4444 general in-flight contingency and weather-deviation procedures** (offset, vertical-offset convention, broadcast on IFBP/HF) pending confirmation of a dedicated regional plan.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **SW Indian Ocean tropical cyclones (≈ Nov–Apr, peak Jan–Mar)** — Mauritius is one of the region's most frequently cyclone-affected islands; **Météo France La Réunion** is the WMO Regional Specialised Meteorological Centre (RSMC) for SW Indian Ocean tropical-cyclone warnings. See [`OM E — Adverse Weather and Windshear`](../../../../flight-ops/adverse-weather-and-windshear.md).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, SIGMET/AIRMET and the **RSMC La Réunion cyclone track/warning** picture during the season, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels, ETP and alternate weather for the long oceanic legs. Not stored here.
