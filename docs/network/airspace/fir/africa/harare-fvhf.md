# Harare (FVHF) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Zimbabwe** (FVHF is the country's **only** FIR), containing the **Robert Gabriel Mugabe Intl (FVRG, Harare)** capital gateway, **J.M. Nkomo Intl (FVJN, Bulawayo)** and the major tourism gateway of **Victoria Falls (FVFA)** · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia) NW · Beira (FQBE, Mozambique) E · Johannesburg (FAJA, South Africa) S · Gaborone (FBGR, Botswana) SW
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR structure/RVSM verified against public CAAZ/ICAO/OPS sources; **ATC infrastructure and comms-reliability concerns are a long-standing, publicly documented watch item** and several fields are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Harare FIR is a **mixed radar/procedural continental FIR** — surveillance concentrates around Harare and Bulawayo, with the wider interior worked procedurally, in line with ICAO AFI-region practice. **Public reporting (ATCAZ, OPSGROUP) has for years flagged ageing ATC equipment, air-to-ground comms blackouts and controller-fatigue concerns in Zimbabwe** — treat surveillance/comms as **not guaranteed** and confirm current serviceability before relying on it. Routings and levels here are planning context — file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Harare FIR (**FVHF**) is coterminous with **Zimbabwe** — it is the country's **only** FIR.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (terminal CTAs/CTRs and the domestic airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; the lower/upper handoff level is AIP-defined — confirm at **ENR 2.1**. RVSM applies **FL290–FL410**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia).** Note Lusaka has already been added to the AFI IFBP degraded-comms procedure list per IATA guidance — relevant precedent for FVHF (§11).
  - **E — Beira (FQBE, Mozambique).** The Eastern Highlands (Nyangani) sit astride this boundary (§8).
  - **S — Johannesburg (FAJA, South Africa).**
  - **SW — Gaborone (FBGR, Botswana).**
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 the transit-relevant fact is the single **Harare ACC/FIC** working the whole FIR; internal sector detail is AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **CAAZ — Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe** is both the regulator and the sole ANSP for FVHF, headquartered at Harare Intl (FVRG).
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟥 radar/surveillance is nominally provided around Harare and Bulawayo, but **ATCAZ (the controllers' professional association) has publicly and repeatedly flagged ageing equipment, air-to-ground comms blackouts (multiple full-blackout days documented) and controller fatigue/staffing shortfalls**. The Zimbabwean government has acknowledged the need for a full ATC/nav-aid/radar/AIM systems replacement (a reported ~US$22 million radar component) but implementation has historically been slow — **do not assume current-generation surveillance or reliable comms; confirm live serviceability at planning, every time.**
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by CAAZ outside radar cover.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Harare FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an RVSM approval from the State of Registry/Operator is required. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; terminal RNP where published — confirm the current Zimbabwe AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not a mature FIR-wide capability** — given the documented comms concerns, treat as unavailable and plan the **AFI IFBP (126.9 MHz listening watch)** as the degraded-comms mitigation; confirm current FVHF applicability. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current Zimbabwe AIP/NOTAM at planning.
- **High-elevation ops:** 🟧 **Harare (FVRG) and Bulawayo (FVJN)** are both high-elevation Highveld terminals — see [`OM E — High-Elevation Aerodrome Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/high-elevation-aerodrome-operations.md).

- **Airway spine:** the **fixed ATS-route network** feeds Harare, Bulawayo and Victoria Falls, and connects to the Lusaka, Beira, Johannesburg and Gaborone FIR boundaries.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the FIR-boundary reporting points toward the four adjacent continental FIRs; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 Zimbabwe's interior is a **Highveld plateau at ~1,200–1,500 m**, rising in the east to the **Eastern Highlands** on the Mozambique border, where **Mount Nyangani (~2,592 m / ~8,504 ft)** is the country's highest point. Victoria Falls, in the west along the Zambezi, sits at lower elevation (~900 m / ~2,900 ft).
- **Boundary terrain:** 🟧 the **Eastern Highlands** sit astride the **E boundary with Beira FIR (Mozambique)** — relevant to driftdown/escape for routings through the east, not for a Victoria Falls/western transit.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** relevant primarily to the Eastern Highlands crossing; the Highveld plateau's base elevation (not relief) is the operative factor for the rest of the FIR.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟧 applicable at the high-elevation Harare/Bulawayo terminals — see [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md) and the airport briefs.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Zimbabwe AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟥 **Given the documented history of upper-airspace comms blackouts in this FIR, brief and rehearse the comms-failure procedure as a live probability, not a remote contingency.** Confirm the Zimbabwe refinement at **AIP ENR 1.**
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** apply the standard ICAO Doc 4444 offset/broadcast technique where a radar-tactical clearance is not available or comms are degraded.
- **ATC infrastructure watch item (the dominant planning factor for this FIR):** 🟥 ATCAZ has publicly reported **complete air-to-ground radio blackouts on multiple days** in a single reporting year, alongside controller fatigue/staffing shortfalls; the government has acknowledged the need for a full radar/nav-aid/comms/AIM replacement programme (reported ~US$22m radar component) but implementation has historically been gradual. **Do not expect near-term resolution — confirm current serviceability every time.**
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Wet season convection (≈ Nov–Mar)** — thunderstorms across the Highveld interior; embedded CB, tactical avoidance.
  - **Victoria Falls spray/microclimate** — localised visibility reduction near the falls in high-water season; a terminal-area, not en-route, factor.

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs (incl. any **radar/comms-outage** notices — a recurring item in this FIR), danger/restricted-area status, SIGMET/AIRMET (wet-season convection), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
