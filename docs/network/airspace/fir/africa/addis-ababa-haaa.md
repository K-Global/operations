# Addis Ababa (HAAA) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Ethiopia**, including the **Addis Ababa (HAAB) Bole hub** · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Khartoum (HSSS) NW · Asmara (HHAA) N/NE 🟧 · Djibouti/Aden airspace NE 🟧 · Mogadishu (HCSM) SE · Nairobi (HKNA) S
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; single-FIR structure/RVSM/authority verified against public sources; exact ACC sectorisation, class letters per band and the live northern-Ethiopia security picture are flagged 🟧/🟥

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Addis Ababa FIR is Ethiopia's **only** FIR, in line with ICAO AFI/ESAF procedural-airspace practice, centred on the **Bole (HAAB)** hub — one of the highest-elevation major international airports in the world and Ethiopian aviation's primary long-haul gateway. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). **The northern Ethiopia (Tigray/Amhara) security picture is a live watch item** — pulled at planning (§11), not a standing closure but re-checked every operation. Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Addis Ababa FIR (**HAAA**) is coterminous with **Ethiopia** — the country's single FIR. Ethiopia borders Eritrea and Sudan to the north/northwest, Djibouti and Somalia to the east/southeast, Kenya to the south, and South Sudan to the west. 🟧 Precise coordinate-defined boundary points are AIP-published — confirm at **Ethiopia AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (terminal CTAs/CTRs and the airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; the lower/upper handoff level is AIP-defined — confirm at **ENR 2.1**. RVSM applies **FL290–FL410**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **NW — Khartoum (HSSS, Sudan)** — touches the Sudan conflict-zone closure owned by that FIR's brief/the Africa area brief.
  - **N / NE — Asmara (HHAA, Eritrea)** 🟧 — precise ident/boundary not independently verified in this build; treat as durable-context placeholder.
  - **NE — Djibouti / Aden (Yemen-side) airspace** 🟧 — the Red Sea/Gulf of Aden corridor risk that affects NE-Africa routing is cross-referenced from the Middle-East side; not detailed here.
  - **SE — Mogadishu (HCSM, Somalia)** — touches the Somalia conflict-zone risk (avoid below FL260 per EASA CZIB guidance).
  - **S — Nairobi (HKNA, Kenya)**.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 internal ECAA sector boundaries (Addis ACC sectorisation) are AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **ECAA — Ethiopian Civil Aviation Authority**, both the ANSP and safety regulator for the single Addis Ababa FIR.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/surveillance is expected around the **Addis Ababa (Bole/HAAB)** terminal area, one of the continent's busiest hubs; the wider FIR, especially the north (Tigray/Amhara) and border regions, is expected to be largely **procedural**. Confirm live serviceability — do not assume continuous coverage.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by ECAA outside radar cover; the **AFI IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) supports separation awareness where VHF/radar is thin.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Addis Ababa FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an **RVSM approval** from the State of Registry/Operator is required. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; terminal RNP where published at HAAB — confirm the current Ethiopia AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not a mature FIR-wide capability** in HAAA. Where VHF/radar is limited the **AFI IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) is the mitigation, **not** a FANS CPDLC service. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current Ethiopia AIP/NOTAM at planning.
- **High-elevation performance:** 🟥 **HAAB Bole (7,656 ft)** is one of the highest-elevation major international airports in the world — requires high-elevation/high-density-altitude performance planning (reduced climb gradient, longer take-off/landing distances, weight restriction on hot days). See the HAAB airport brief and [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md) for the companion high-elevation/temperature-correction topics.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 the **Ethiopian highlands** form a broad, high plateau across much of the country, with **Ras Dashen (~4,550 m / 14,928 ft)** in the Simien Mountains the highest point. **Addis Ababa/HAAB itself sits at 7,656 ft** — one of the highest-elevation major hubs in the world — making the terminal-performance and en-route MORA pictures unusually demanding for this FIR compared with the coastal East-African fields.
- **Boundary terrain:** high ground continues south toward the Kenyan highlands (Nairobi FIR) and east toward the Rift Valley escarpment; relevant to driftdown/escape planning across most of the FIR given the generally elevated terrain.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** 🟥 given the widespread high plateau, engine-out/decompression escape planning should account for higher minimum safe altitudes across much of Ethiopia, not just near named peaks — confirm grid MORA from current ENR charts.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟥 significant at **HAAB** given its elevation — see the airport brief and [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md).

## 10. Special-use airspace

- **Military / danger / restricted areas:** 🟧 danger and restricted areas are AIP/NOTAM-published across the FIR — pull the active list at planning.
- **Conflict-zone / security exposure:** 🟥 **northern Ethiopia (Tigray/Amhara)** carries a **periodic** security watch item — a historical NOTAM (HAAA A0220/22) prohibited flight at/below FL290 within the Mekele TMA and defined areas near the Sudan/Eritrea borders; the 2022 ceasefire has largely held, but a **February-2024 flare-up in Amhara** (including reported air strikes) shows the picture remains dynamic. This is **not a standing closure** but must be **re-checked live** before any routing that transits or overflies the north. The FIR also touches the Sudan (Khartoum, NW) and Somalia (Mogadishu, SE) conflict-zone boundaries.
- **Overflight-permit / diplomatic considerations:** 🟧 confirm current overflight/landing permit requirements for Ethiopia for the operation.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Ethiopia AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 Confirm the Ethiopia refinement at AIP ENR 1.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in non-radar (procedural) airspace, apply the AFI/ICAO weather-deviation and contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique, and coordinate as able. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Kiremt rains (≈ Jun–Sep)** — the main highland wet season, producing convective activity and reduced visibility over the plateau.
  - **Dry-season haze/dust** in the lowlands bordering Sudan/Somalia.
  - **Security wildcard** — the northern Tigray/Amhara watch item and the Sudan/Somalia boundary conflict zones are the strategic risk most likely to reshape a routing (§10).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs (incl. any northern-Ethiopia flight-level restriction notices), Safe Airspace / EASA CZIB / OPSGROUP advisories for northern Ethiopia and the Sudan/Somalia boundaries, SIGMET/AIRMET (Kiremt convection), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
