# Tunis (DTTC) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Tunisia** (DTTC is the country's **only** FIR/UIR), a small, low-terrain, largely coastal country between the Algerian and Libyan deserts and the central Mediterranean · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Algiers (DAAA) W · Tripoli (HLLL) SE/E 🟧 confirm exact land-border split · Malta / Valletta and Rome-area Mediterranean FIRs N/NE across the Sicilian Channel 🟧 confirm exact maritime boundary
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR ident and ANSP confirmed against OACA/public sources, but exact RVSM band dates, class letters, ACC sector split and the maritime boundary to Malta/Rome are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Tunis FIR is a **small, mostly radar-capable coastal FIR** — Tunisia's compact geography and dense coastal terminal network (Tunis, Zarzis, Hammamet, Monastir plus several other airports) put most of the country within or near terminal surveillance, with a lighter procedural residue toward the desert south-west. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Tunis FIR (**DTTC**) is coterminous with **Tunisia** — the country's **only** FIR/UIR, a compact coastal state on the central Mediterranean between Algeria and Libya.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (terminal CTAs/CTRs and the airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; exact lower/upper handoff level and RVSM implementation date are AIP-defined — confirm at **Tunisia AIP ENR 2.1 / 1.7**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **W — Algiers (DAAA, Algeria/ENNA)** — see the [Algiers (DAAA) FIR brief](algiers-daaa.md).
  - **SE / E — Tripoli (HLLL, Libya/LYCAA)** 🟧 confirm the exact land-border split — see the **[Tripoli (HLLL) FIR brief](tripoli-hlll.md)** for the standing conflict-zone caution immediately across this boundary.
  - **N / NE — Malta/Valletta and Rome-area Mediterranean FIRs**, across the Sicilian Channel 🟧 confirm exact maritime hand-off.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 **Tunis ACC/FIC** is the single ATSU for the FIR; internal sector detail is AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 not confirmed for Tunis FIR in public sources at this time — confirm current status at planning; assume the fixed airway network applies.
- **Airway spine:** the fixed ATS-route network links Tunis-Carthage with the coastal leisure destinations (Zarzis, Hammamet, Monastir) and connects to the Algiers and Tripoli boundaries.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** FIR-boundary reporting points toward Algiers, Tripoli and the Mediterranean maritime handoff; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Tunis-Carthage (DTTA)**, **Zarzis (DTTJ)**, **Enfidha-Hammamet (DTNH)** and **Monastir-Skanès (DTMB)** — all **K Global-served**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Tunisia is predominantly **low plains and coastal terrain**; the highest point, **Jebel Chambi (≈1,544 m / 5,066 ft)**, lies in the western interior near the Algerian border — well away from the coastal served destinations.
- **Boundary terrain:** no significant high terrain at the adjacent-FIR boundaries.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a constraint for the served coastal routings; the dense national airport network gives ready diversion options.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not a factor — Mediterranean climate.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Tunisia AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 confirm the Tunisia-specific refinement at AIP ENR 1.
- **Weather-deviation / in-flight contingency:** radar-tactical via ATC given the presumed surveillance coverage; apply standard ICAO Doc 4444 procedures if any procedural segment is encountered.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Sirocco / Ghibli** — hot, dust-laden southerly wind, most disruptive in spring; reduces visibility.
  - **Transitional-season convection** — spring/autumn thunderstorm activity along the coast.
  - **Boundary watch item** — the Tripoli FIR conflict-zone caution to the SE/E (§10).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, danger/restricted-area status, the current Tripoli-boundary advisory picture, SIGMET/AIRMET (sirocco dust, convection), and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
