# Gaborone (FBGR) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Botswana** (FBGR is the country's **only** FIR), plus a **delegated slice of Namibian airspace over the Caprivi Strip** from FL145 upward, containing **Maun Intl (FBMN)**, the Okavango-Delta safari gateway, plus Gaborone/Sir Seretse Khama and Kasane · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Windhoek (FYWF, Namibia) W · Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia) N · Harare (FVHF, Zimbabwe) NE/E · Johannesburg (FAJA, South Africa) S

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Gaborone FIR (**FBGR**) is coterminous with **Botswana** — it is the country's **only** FIR, from ground level to unlimited. 🟧 **Uniquely, CAAB's Air Navigation Services also provides ATC over a delegated portion of Namibian airspace above the Caprivi Strip, from FL145 upward** — an internationally agreed cross-border delegation (Namibia/NCAA retains sovereignty; CAAB provides the service). This is an unusual boundary geometry — confirm the exact delegated lateral extent and vertical floor (FL145) against the current Letter of Agreement/AIP before relying on it operationally.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (terminal CTAs/CTRs and the domestic airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; the lower/upper handoff level is AIP-defined — confirm at **ENR 2.1**. RVSM applies **FL290–FL410**. The Caprivi delegation itself begins at **FL145**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **W — Windhoek (FYWF, NCAA/Namibia).** Note the FL145+ Caprivi delegation runs the other way — CAAB works part of Namibia's own airspace here (§ above).
  - **N — Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia).**
  - **NE / E — Harare (FVHF, CAAZ/Zimbabwe).**
  - **S — Johannesburg (FAJA, ATNS/South Africa).**
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 the transit-relevant fact is the single **Gaborone ACC/FIC** working the whole FIR plus the Caprivi delegation; internal sector detail is AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **CAAB — Civil Aviation Authority of Botswana**, Air Navigation Services division, is both the regulator and the sole ANSP for FBGR (formed 2008; headquartered Gaborone).
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/surveillance concentrated around **Gaborone (FBSK)**; the Kalahari interior and the remote Caprivi delegation are worked more procedurally with HF backup. Confirm current serviceability at planning — do not assume continuous surveillance outside the Gaborone terminal area.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by CAAB outside radar cover, including across the delegated Caprivi airspace.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Gaborone FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; CAAB publishes an RVSM-approval process (per CAAB guidance material) — an RVSM approval from the State of Registry/Operator is required. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; terminal RNP where published — confirm the current Botswana AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not asserted as a mature FIR-wide capability** in FBGR — where VHF/radar is limited the AFI **In-Flight Broadcast Procedure (IFBP)** may apply; confirm current status. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current Botswana AIP/NOTAM at planning.

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 **not yet implemented** — Southern Africa (ESAF) FRA trials are the stated **2026** focus continent-wide; confirm current availability at planning.
- **Airway spine:** the **fixed ATS-route network** feeds Gaborone, Maun and Kasane and connects to the Windhoek, Lusaka, Harare and Johannesburg FIR boundaries; the Maun/Kasane corridor is a well-used tourism trunk (Okavango/Chobe safari traffic).
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** the airway boundary fixes toward the four adjacent continental FIRs and the Caprivi-delegation interface with Windhoek FIR; the SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Maun Intl (FBMN)** — the Okavango Delta safari gateway — plus **Sir Seretse Khama Intl (FBSK, Gaborone)** and **Kasane Intl (FBKE)**, the Chobe-area gateway. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟩 Botswana is overwhelmingly **flat Kalahari basin and savanna** — the country's highest point, **Otse Hill (~1,494 m / ~4,900 ft)**, is modest and near Gaborone in the south-east. The Okavango Delta (Maun) and Chobe (Kasane) areas in the north are low-lying wetland/savanna.
- **Boundary terrain:** none of operational significance — no high escarpment or mountain belt straddles the FBGR boundary.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** not a cruise-level constraint anywhere in the FIR; the operative constraint over the Kalahari interior is **diversion-field reach**, not terrain.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** not generally a factor — semi-arid/savanna climate; confirm at the airport briefs if relevant seasonally.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Botswana AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm the Botswana refinement at **AIP ENR 1.**
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in the procedural Kalahari interior and the Caprivi delegation, apply the standard ICAO Doc 4444 offset/broadcast technique where a radar-tactical clearance is not available.
