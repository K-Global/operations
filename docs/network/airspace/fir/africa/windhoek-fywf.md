# Windhoek (FYWF) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Namibia** (FYWF is the country's **only** FIR), containing the **Hosea Kutako Intl (FYWH)** capital gateway plus the Atlantic coastal port of Walvis Bay; the FIR also forms the **transit corridor between the continental route network and the Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO) entry/exit gates** on Namibia's Atlantic boundary · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Luanda (FNAN, Angola) N · Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia) NE · Gaborone (FBGR, Botswana) E (plus the Caprivi Strip is worked by CAAB above FL145 — see §3 🟧) · Johannesburg (FAJA, South Africa) SE · Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO, South Africa) W/SW (Atlantic)
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; FIR structure/RVSM/oceanic-gateway role verified against public NCAA/ICAO/OPS sources, but exact boundary coordinates and ACC sector detail are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Windhoek FIR is a **mixed radar/procedural continental FIR** — surveillance concentrates around Hosea Kutako/Windhoek, while much of the sparsely populated Namib/Kalahari interior is worked procedurally, in line with ICAO AFI-region practice. Namibia additionally functions as the **land bridge for South Atlantic oceanic entry/exit gates** feeding Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO) — a routing/planning feature specific to this FIR. Terminal procedures for aerodromes inside the FIR live in the airport briefings (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. Flag legend 🟥 hard · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Windhoek FIR (**FYWF**) is coterminous with **Namibia** — it is the country's **only** FIR. 🟧 **NCAA's own Area Control description places the FIR's controlled airspace between FL145 and FL460**, with lower/terminal airspace worked by aerodrome and approach units — confirm the precise lower-limit geometry against the current AIP, since public sources vary in how they express the floor.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 area control **FL145–FL460** per NCAA; RVSM applies **FL290–FL410** within that band. Confirm the exact lower-airspace/terminal handoff at **ENR 2.1**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N — Luanda (FNAN, Angola).**
  - **NE — Lusaka (FLFI, Zambia).**
  - **E — Gaborone (FBGR, Botswana).** 🟧 Note the reverse delegation: **CAAB (Botswana) provides ATC over Namibian airspace above the Caprivi Strip from FL145** — a Namibia-sovereignty, Botswana-serviced pocket inside the Windhoek FIR's nominal territory. Confirm the exact delegated extent against the current Letter of Agreement.
  - **SE — Johannesburg (FAJA, South Africa).**
  - **W / SW — Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO, South Africa, Atlantic).** 🟥 **Entry/exit gates exist on the FAJO boundary adjoining Windhoek FIR continental airspace**, with **fixed continental routes established transiting the Windhoek FIR** specifically to facilitate access to/from those oceanic gates — this makes FYWF a functional land-bridge for South Atlantic oceanic traffic, not merely a national FIR.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 the transit-relevant fact is the single **Windhoek ACC/FIC** working the whole FIR (less the Caprivi carve-out); internal sector detail is AIP/AIRAC-dependent — fly the current sectorisation, not a stored one.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **NCAA — Namibia Civil Aviation Authority**, Air Navigation Services department, is the ANSP and regulator for FYWF.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/surveillance concentrated around **Hosea Kutako/Windhoek**; the Namib/Kalahari interior and the Atlantic coastal corridor are worked more procedurally with HF backup. NCAA has publicly announced a large air-navigation-systems modernisation investment (reported ~N$652 million) — treat current surveillance/comms capability as improving but confirm live serviceability at planning.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by NCAA outside radar cover.

- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; terminal RNP where published — confirm the current Namibia AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).

## 7. Route structure & entry/exit

- **Free Route Airspace (FRA):** 🟧 **not yet implemented** — ESAF (Eastern & Southern Africa) FRA trials are the stated **2026** focus continent-wide; confirm current availability at planning.
- **Airway spine:** the **fixed ATS-route network** feeds Hosea Kutako/Windhoek and Walvis Bay, and connects to the Luanda, Lusaka, Gaborone and Johannesburg FIR boundaries.
- **Major fixes / entry-exit points:** 🟥 the FIR's most operationally distinctive feature — **fixed continental routes transit Windhoek FIR specifically to reach the entry/exit gates on the Johannesburg Oceanic (FAJO) boundary**, making FYWF the land-bridge segment for South Atlantic oceanic traffic to/from the South African oceanic structure. The SimBrief/current-AIRAC route provides the exact string and gate identity (planning context, not a clearance).
- **Hubs & aerodromes inside the FIR:** 🟩 **Hosea Kutako Intl (FYWH, capital gateway)** and **Walvis Bay Intl (FYWB, Atlantic coastal port)**. Terminal detail is in the airport briefings (§9).

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟧 Namibia is predominantly **desert and plateau** — the **Namib Desert** along the Atlantic coast (low, but frequently fog-affected) rising through a **central escarpment** to the interior highlands, where **Brandberg (~2,606 m / ~8,550 ft)** is the country's highest point. **Hosea Kutako Intl** itself sits at meaningful elevation on the central plateau — a high-elevation terminal consideration.
- **Boundary terrain:** none of major operational significance straddling the FYWF boundary itself; the escarpment is wholly domestic.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** relevant to the central escarpment/Brandberg belt; over the Namib coastal strip and the eastern Kalahari fringe the operative constraint is **diversion-field reach**, not terrain.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟧 applicable at the high-elevation Hosea Kutako terminal — see [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md) and the airport brief.

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Namibia AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency and 121.5. 🟧 Confirm the Namibia refinement at **AIP ENR 1.**
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in the procedural Namib/Kalahari interior, apply the standard ICAO Doc 4444 offset/broadcast technique where a radar-tactical clearance is not available.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **Coastal fog (Atlantic seaboard, incl. Walvis Bay)** — the cold Benguela current produces persistent advection fog along the coast, a recurring terminal-visibility factor.
  - **Interior heat/dust** — high density-altitude and dust/haze over the Namib/Kalahari in the dry season.
  - **ANS-modernisation programme (watch item):** NCAA's stated ~N$652m investment in navigation/surveillance systems is a live improvement programme — capability may change materially within the life of this document; re-verify at planning.
