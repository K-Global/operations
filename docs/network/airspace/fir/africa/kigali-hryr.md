# Kigali (HRYR) — FIR Briefing

**Scope:** single FIR — the **whole of Rwanda**; the FIR designator **shares the HRYR ident with Kigali International Airport** 🟧 · **Parent area brief:** [Africa](../../africa.md) 🟩 · **Adjacent FIRs:** Entebbe (HUEC) N/NE 🟧 · Kinshasa (FZZA) W (across the Virunga/Lake Kivu border belt) 🟧 · Bujumbura area S 🟧 · Dar es Salaam (HTDC) E/SE 🟧
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-26 · **Status** Draft — provisional; single-FIR structure and authority verified against public sources; exact adjacent-FIR boundary set, sectorisation and live radar serviceability are flagged 🟧

> **Read-me:** Strategic transit reference for the sim, not a chart or clearance. Kigali FIR is Rwanda's **only** FIR — a small, single-hub national airspace worked as one unit by the RCAA, in line with ICAO AFI/ESAF procedural-airspace practice. 🟧 **The FIR designator HRYR is the same ident as Kigali International Airport** — take care not to conflate the FIR-wide brief with the airport-specific one (§9) when referencing this document. Rwanda's hilly, high-elevation terrain and the **Virunga volcanic massif** on the northern DRC/Uganda border are the FIR's defining terrain features. Routings and levels here are planning context; file and fly the SimBrief/current-AIRAC-validated route and comply with tactical ATC clearances. Terminal procedures for the aerodrome inside the FIR live in the airport briefing (§9). Live restrictions are pulled at planning (§11). Open 🟧 items are in §12. ## 3. FIR structure & lateral/vertical boundaries

- **Lateral extent:** Kigali FIR (**HRYR**) is coterminous with **Rwanda** — the country's single, and smallest-in-this-network, FIR. Rwanda borders Uganda (N), Tanzania (E), Burundi (S) and the Democratic Republic of the Congo (W). 🟧 Precise coordinate-defined boundary points are AIP-published — confirm at **Rwanda AIP ENR 2.1**.
- **Vertical split:** 🟧 lower controlled airspace (the Kigali CTA/CTR and the airway network) beneath the RVSM upper band; the lower/upper handoff level is AIP-defined — confirm at **ENR 2.1**. RVSM applies **FL290–FL410**.
- **Adjacent FIRs (by boundary):**
  - **N / NE — Entebbe (HUEC, Uganda)** 🟧.
  - **W — Kinshasa (FZZA, DRC)** — the boundary runs through the **Virunga volcanic massif / Lake Kivu** border belt shared with Uganda and the DRC 🟧.
  - **S — Burundi airspace** 🟧 — precise FIR ident/ownership (Bujumbura area) not verified against AIP.
  - **E / SE — Dar es Salaam (HTDC, Tanzania)** 🟧.
- **Sectorisation:** 🟧 as a small single-airport FIR, Kigali is expected to be worked as a **single sector/unit** by RCAA rather than a multi-sector ACC split — confirm at the current AIP; this is a materially simpler structure than Kenya/Tanzania/Ethiopia's larger FIRs.

## 4. Control & ATSU

- **ATSU:** 🟩 **RCAA — Rwanda Civil Aviation Authority**, both the ANSP and safety regulator for the single Kigali FIR, headquartered at Kigali Intl.
- **Surveillance basis:** 🟧 radar/surveillance is expected around the **Kigali terminal area (HRYR)**; the wider FIR, including the Virunga border belt, is expected to be **procedural**. Confirm live serviceability.
- **FIS:** flight information / procedural service provided by RCAA outside radar cover; the **AFI IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) supports separation awareness where VHF/radar is thin.

## 5. Equipment & approvals

*Requirements below are DEFINED in OM E — Operations; this section states which apply in the Kigali FIR and references the OM E doc. It does not re-derive them.*

- **RVSM:** 🟥 **FL290–FL410** — **ICAO AFI RVSM**; an **RVSM approval** from the State of Registry/Operator is required. See [`OM E — RVSM Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/rvsm-operations.md).
- **PBN / RNP:** 🟧 **RNAV 5** enroute where the network is PBN-based; terminal RNP where published at HRYR — confirm the current Rwanda AIP. See [`OM E — PBN and RNP Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/pbn-and-rnp-operations.md).
- **8.33 kHz channel spacing:** 🟩 **not an AFI requirement** — standard 25 kHz VHF applies.
- **Datalink (CPDLC / ADS-C):** 🟧 **not a mature FIR-wide capability** in HRYR. Where VHF/radar is limited the **AFI IFBP** (listening watch on **126.9 MHz**) is the mitigation, **not** a FANS CPDLC service. See [`OM E — Datalink and Oceanic Procedures`](../../../../flight-ops/datalink-and-oceanic-procedures.md).
- **Transponder / ADS-B:** 🟥 **Mode C/S transponder required.** 🟧 Any ADS-B Out requirement is not asserted here — confirm the current Rwanda AIP/NOTAM at planning.
- **Terrain-critical note:** 🟥 the Virunga volcanic massif to the north demands terrain-aware routing and minimum safe altitudes near the DRC/Uganda border — see the HRYR airport brief for terminal-specific detail.

## 8. Terrain & MORA

- **Grid MORA / high terrain belts:** 🟥 the **Virunga Mountains** — a chain of volcanoes on the Rwanda/DRC/Uganda tripoint, north of Lake Kivu — are the FIR's dominant high terrain, with **Karisimbi (~4,507 m / 14,787 ft)** the highest peak; **Nyiragongo and Nyamuragira** (both across the border in the DRC) are active volcanoes (see also volcanic-ash note, §11). Rwanda's interior is broadly hilly and elevated — the country is known as the "land of a thousand hills" — with **Kigali Intl itself at 4,891 ft**.
- **Boundary terrain:** the highest ground sits **astride the northern boundary with Uganda (Entebbe FIR) and the DRC (Kinshasa FIR)** — relevant to driftdown/escape planning on any routing toward that boundary.
- **Driftdown / depressurisation escape:** relevant for routings toward the Virunga/northern border; Rwanda's generally elevated terrain (compared with the East-African coastal lowlands) means engine-out/decompression escape planning should account for higher minimum safe altitudes than a coastal FIR.
- **Cold-temperature altitude corrections:** 🟧 relevant at the high-elevation **HRYR** terminal — see the airport brief and [`OM E — Cold Weather Operations`](../../../../flight-ops/cold-weather-operations.md).

## 11. Contingency & seasonal / live-data pointer

- **Comms-failure:** squawk **7600**; continue per the ICAO/Rwanda AIP IFR comms-failure rules (last clearance / filed route and levels), attempt contact on the previous/adjacent frequency, 121.5 and the **126.9 MHz IFBP**. 🟧 Confirm the Rwanda refinement at AIP ENR 1.
- **Procedural / weather-deviation contingency:** in non-radar (procedural) airspace, apply the AFI/ICAO weather-deviation and contingency procedures — broadcast intentions on the IFBP/HF, offset per the standard technique, and coordinate as able. Squawk **7700** and declare for emergencies.
- **Seasonal hazards (durable):**
  - **ITCZ / equatorial convection** — Rwanda sits within the broader East-African rainfall belt; embedded/towering CB during the long/short rains seasons.
  - **Volcanic ash (Virunga)** — **Nyiragongo/Nyamuragira** (DRC side of the Virunga chain) are active volcanoes; ash advisories for the region come from **VAAC Toulouse** — see [`OM E — Volcanic Ash Avoidance`](../../../../flight-ops/volcanic-ash-avoidance.md).
  - **Security wildcard** — the eastern-DRC border belt is the strategic risk most likely to reshape a Rwanda-adjacent routing (§10).

> **Pull at planning (T-2h):** enroute NOTAMs, eastern-DRC/Virunga-border-belt advisories (Safe Airspace / state advisories), SIGMET/AIRMET (ITCZ convection), **VAAC Toulouse** volcanic-ash advisories, and the validated SimBrief route with assigned levels. Not stored here.
