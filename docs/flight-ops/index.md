# Flight Operations

Location-agnostic **capability and procedure policy** — the rules that read
identically network-wide, defined once here so the airspace and airport briefs
and the fleet packs can *apply* them to a place or a type without re-deriving
them.

> **Division of labour.** An airspace brief says *"RVSM FL290–410 and RNP4 apply
> in the NAT"*; a fleet dispatch page says *"the A339 is dispatched to the
> OFP-approved EDTO rule time"*. **The method itself lives here.**

## Procedures

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane-clock:{ .lg .middle } __ETOPS / EDTO__

    ---

    Extended Diversion Time Operations — threshold vs rule time, area of
    operations, en-route-alternate suitability and planning minima, the Critical
    Point / ETP and critical-fuel scenario, EDTO-significant systems and MEL, and
    the dispatch / in-flight go/no-go.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: ETOPS / EDTO](etops-edto.md)

-   :material-snowflake:{ .lg .middle } __Polar & High-Latitude Ops__

    ---

    High-latitude / near-polar operations — fuel freeze and cold soak,
    cold-weather diversion-field ops and altimetry, comms/datalink redundancy,
    space weather and cosmic radiation, and true/grid navigation.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Polar & High-Latitude Ops](polar.md)

</div>
