# A11 — Security · OM A

Company security policy covering the aircraft, crew, cargo, and information.

**Version** v1.0 · **Updated** 2026-07-25 · **Status** Verified

> Read-me: framework chapter. K Global is a VATSIM virtual airline and applies the **principles** of aviation security for realism — it operates no real aircraft and holds no security programme approval. Policy follows the ICAO Annex 17 framework. Flags: 🟥 restriction/hazard/hard requirement · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This chapter sets K Global's security policy for the aircraft, its crew, cargo/mail, and operational information. It follows the framework of **ICAO Annex 17 (Security — Safeguarding International Civil Aviation Against Acts of Unlawful Interference)** and the supporting guidance (Doc 8973, restricted). The aim of the security regime is to prevent **acts of unlawful interference** — hijacking, sabotage, seizure, and attacks against aviation. K Global mirrors the principles so that its simulated operation reflects real security discipline. 🟩

## 2. Security policy (framework)

- **Objective.** Safeguard the operation against acts of unlawful interference, consistent with the Annex 17 goal. 🟩
- **Layered principle.** Security is achieved in layers — access control, screening, aircraft protection, crew procedures, and information protection — no single layer is relied upon alone. 🟩
- **Baseline conformance.** Where a flight operates into or out of a real aerodrome, the operation respects that State's security measures as the governing regime; company policy supplements, never contradicts, them. 🟩

## 3. Flight-deck and aircraft security

- **Flight-deck access.** The flight-deck door policy follows the reinforced-door / controlled-access principle: the flight-deck is kept secure, and access is restricted to those with an operational need and proper authorisation. 🟥
- **Aircraft security search / check.** An aircraft **security search or check** is applied where the threat picture or the departure regime requires it, to confirm no prohibited item or unauthorised person is on board before departure. 🟩
- **Aircraft protection on the ground.** The aircraft is protected against unauthorised access during turnarounds and when unattended, consistent with the departure aerodrome's regime. 🟩

## 4. Crew security responsibilities and disruptive behaviour

- **Crew responsibility.** All crew hold a share of the security task — vigilance, control of flight-deck access, and correct response to a security concern. 🟩
- **Unruly / disruptive passengers.** Disruptive or unlawful passenger behaviour is managed under the safety-and-security authority of the Pilot-in-Command, escalating to a diversion and to reporting where warranted. Such events are **reportable occurrences** (see A12). 🟩
- **Prohibited items and weapons.** Weapons, munitions, and security items follow both the security regime and the dangerous-goods rules (A10); they are carried only under authorisation. 🟥

## 5. Cargo, mail, and supply-chain security

- **Screening principle.** Cargo and mail are subject to the **secure supply-chain / screening** principle before loading — cargo of unknown origin or security status is not carried on a passenger service without appropriate screening. 🟥
- **Known consignor / regulated agent** logic is represented as company policy where relevant (the secured-status concept), even though K Global maintains no real supply chain. 🟧 [supply-chain records not maintained — principle only]
- Interface with **A10** for dangerous-goods and special-load acceptance. 🟩

## 6. Information security

- Operational information (flight plans, schedules, crew and route data, and any member personal data) is treated as **confidential** and protected against loss or unauthorised disclosure. 🟩
- Access to operational systems and platforms (e.g. the VAMSYS platform) is controlled; credentials are not shared. 🟩
- Governance rule: OM content itself never publishes flight callsigns, personal names, or individual account usernames (see the content governance §1) — an information-security discipline as well as a governance one. 🟩

## 7. Threat and unlawful-interference response (principle)

- **Response principle.** On a credible threat or an act of unlawful interference, the crew's priorities are to **secure the flight-deck, land as soon as practicable at a suitable aerodrome, alert ATC (including the appropriate transponder code where able), and comply with the receiving State's instructions**. 🟥
- **Escalation and reporting.** Security events are escalated to operational control and are recorded as **occurrences** (A12), feeding the SMS hazard picture (A03). 🟩
- **Detail restricted.** Specific security search/response procedures are, in the real regime, restricted (Doc 8973); K Global holds only the public-principle level and does not attempt to reproduce sensitive detail. 🟧
