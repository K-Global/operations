# A12 — Occurrence Reporting and Investigation · OM A

Mandatory and voluntary occurrence reporting policy, and internal investigation.

**Version** v1.0 · **Updated** 2026-07-25 · **Status** Verified

> Read-me: framework chapter. K Global is a VATSIM virtual airline and applies the **principles** of occurrence reporting and just culture — it makes no report to a real regulator or investigation authority. Policy follows the ICAO Annex 13 / Annex 19 and EU 376/2014 framework. Flags: 🟥 restriction/hazard/hard requirement · 🟧 caution/unverified · 🟩 normal.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This chapter sets K Global's scheme for **reporting occurrences** and **investigating** them internally, and its **just-culture** foundation. It follows the framework of **ICAO Annex 13 (Aircraft Accident and Incident Investigation)**, **ICAO Annex 19 (Safety Management)**, and **Regulation (EU) 376/2014** on the reporting, analysis, and follow-up of occurrences in civil aviation. The sole purpose of reporting and investigation is **prevention and improvement — never blame**. The scheme feeds the SMS (A03). 🟩

## 2. The reporting scheme

K Global operates a **two-track** reporting scheme, mirroring the mandatory/voluntary split of EU 376/2014:

- **Mandatory occurrence reporting.** Certain occurrences **must** be reported. At VA scale these are the events that materially affected, or could have affected, the safety, integrity, or realism of a flight — for example: a loss of separation or airprox; a runway incursion; an unstable approach continued to land or a go-around from an abnormal state; a significant procedural or SOP breach; a security event (A11); a dangerous-goods/special-load event (A10); a dispatch outside capability or MEL condition (A09); or any event a member judges a real operator would be required to report. 🟥
- **Voluntary / confidential reporting.** Any member may **voluntarily** report a hazard, near-miss, error, or realism concern that is not caught by the mandatory list. Voluntary reports are welcomed and are the richest source of hazard data for the SMS. A **confidential** channel is available so a reporter's identity is protected. 🟩
- **When.** Report **promptly** — as soon as practicable after the flight or event, while detail is fresh. 🟩
- **What a report contains.** What happened, when, the phase/context, contributing factors as the reporter sees them, and any immediate action taken. Facts, not blame. 🟩

## 3. Just culture — the foundation

K Global's reporting scheme rests on **just culture**, the EU 376/2014 principle:

- **Protection.** A member is **not penalised** for honestly reporting an occurrence or error, or for information provided in good faith through the scheme. Reporting is encouraged, not punished. 🟥
- **The line.** Just culture is **not** blanket immunity. The recognised exceptions apply: **wilful misconduct, gross negligence, or a manifest and serious deliberate disregard** of an obvious risk fall outside protection. Honest error is protected; deliberate abuse is not. 🟥
- **Confidentiality.** The identity of a reporter, and of persons named in a report, is protected. Occurrence information is used for **safety and improvement only**, never as evidence in an unrelated dispute, and — consistent with OM governance — is de-identified before anything reaches OM content (the content governance §1). 🟥
- **Separation from discipline.** The occurrence scheme is **kept separate from any conduct/disciplinary process.** A safety report is not a disciplinary trigger; conduct matters are handled on their own track and must not be allowed to contaminate the openness of safety reporting. 🟩

## 4. Occurrence classification

Reports are classified so that effort is proportionate, following the accident / serious incident / incident gradation of Annex 13, scaled to the VA:

- **Accident (VA-scaled)** — an occurrence that, in a real operation, would meet the Annex 13 accident threshold (e.g. a simulated hull loss or a fatal-equivalent event). Rare; fullest review. 🟩
- **Serious incident** — an occurrence with a high probability of an accident (e.g. a near-collision/airprox, a serious loss of control recovered, a serious dispatch error). Full review. 🟥
- **Incident / minor occurrence** — an event of lower severity, reviewed proportionately and largely for trend value. 🟩
- **Hazard / observation** — no event yet occurred, but a latent risk was spotted; routed straight to the SMS risk register (A03 §3). 🟩

Classification sets the depth of the investigation and the priority of any follow-up.

## 5. Internal investigation

The investigation exists to **understand and prevent, not to attribute fault** (Annex 13 sole-objective principle). K Global's process — re-expressed for a just-culture occurrence rather than a conduct matter:

- **Resolve and clarify informally first.** Wherever the occurrence is minor, the facts are established through an informal, good-faith exchange with those involved. Most reports are resolved and closed at this level. 🟩
- **Impartial review for anything significant.** A serious incident or accident-level occurrence is reviewed by someone **not personally involved** in the event, to keep the review objective. The review establishes the sequence, the contributing factors, and the systemic lessons — it does **not** set out to assign personal blame. 🟩
- **Cooperation.** Those involved are asked to contribute their account openly. Within just culture this cooperation is protected and expected; the process is explicitly non-punitive for honest input. 🟩
- **Findings and safety actions.** The review produces **findings** and, where warranted, **safety recommendations** — an SOP change, a briefing or training update (OM D), a capability/MEL clarification (A09), or a policy note. Findings are recorded factually and de-identified. 🟩
- **Feedback to the reporter.** The reporter is told the outcome — closing the loop is what keeps a reporting culture alive. 🟩
- **Escalation / review of outcome.** If someone considers an outcome unfair or incomplete, it can be **escalated for a second look by a different, uninvolved reviewer** before it is finalised. This preserves fairness without turning the safety process into a grievance. 🟩
- **Conduct boundary.** If a review reveals that an event was **not** honest error but wilful misconduct or gross negligence (§3), it leaves the just-culture safety track and is handled separately as a conduct matter — it is not dealt with under this chapter. 🟥

## 6. Follow-up and the SMS feedback loop

- Every accepted finding produces a **safety action** tracked to closure, and the hazard is entered in the **SMS risk register** (A03 §3). 🟩
- **Trends** across occurrences — recurrence of the same issue — are monitored as a safety-assurance indicator (A03 §4); a recurring minor occurrence can matter more than a one-off serious one. 🟩
- The reporting-and-investigation loop is the **primary feed** into the K Global SMS. A03 owns the management system; A12 owns the reporting and investigation mechanics. 🟩
