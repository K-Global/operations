## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** the AD Simulations/DeltaWing CRJ-900 uses its own paint kit — a house scheme would need painting **separately from the CRJ-700 kit** (a different product, same developer) and would not be interchangeable 🟧.
- **Sub-type matching:** any house paint must be built for the **CRJ900** product — a CRJ-200 paint kit (JRollon's product line) would not fit this fleet's airframe.
- **Pending approval:** the entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (which add-on) is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
- **Common rating with CRJ7:** where practical, K Global's house scheme should be designed once and adapted across both the CRJ9 and CRJ7 packs, mirroring the shared crew rating (Intro §1) — see the [CRJ7 Livery](../crj7/livery.md) for the sibling pack.
