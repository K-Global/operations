## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** the JRollon CRJ-200 uses its own paint kit — a house scheme would need painting **separately from any CRJ700/900 kit** (a different product, different developer) and would not be interchangeable 🟧.
- **Sub-type matching:** any house paint must be built for the **CRJ-200** product — a CRJ700 or CRJ900 paint kit (the AD Simulations/DeltaWing product line) would not fit this fleet's airframe.
- **Pending approval:** the entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (which add-on) is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
- **Not a shared scheme with CRJ7/CRJ9:** unlike the CRJ700/900 pair (same developer, a "design once, adapt across both" approach is practical), the CRJ2 sits on a **different developer's** paint kit — a house scheme for this type must be designed and painted on its own terms, not assumed to port from the [CRJ7 Livery](../crj7/livery.md) or [CRJ9 Livery](../crj9/livery.md).
