# CRJ2 — Bombardier CRJ-200 · Normal Checklist

**CRJ2 — SEPARATE type rating from the CRJ700/900** · flown as JRollon CRJ-200 🟧 `[VERIFY — model]` · sim = X-Plane 11
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-25 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** K Global normal-ops flow for X-Plane. **The aircraft's own EICAS/central-warning display is authoritative** — where the sim model differs, the 🟧 note says so. This is a cockpit quick-reference, not a certified checklist, and re-expressed in K Global's own words (no verbatim OEM text). The CRJ-200 uses **Rockwell Collins Pro Line 4** integrated avionics with an EICAS-style caution/warning display. Flight controls are **conventional, cable/hydraulic-actuated through a yoke** — this is **not** a fly-by-wire aircraft; there is no envelope-protection law, and the pilot retains full, unassisted control authority throughout. **The wing has NO leading-edge slats (Intro §6) — a genuine hard wing** — this drives higher approach speeds and a stricter ice/contamination discipline than the slatted CRJ700/900; do not confuse the two. Flag legend 🟥 memory/critical · 🟧 model-dependent · 🟩 normal.

*Format: **CHALLENGE …… RESPONSE**. Generic CRJ-200 (Pro Line 4) flow. Model-dependent items are flagged 🟧. Verify each item against the flown model before line use `[VERIFY]`.*

## Preflight / Cockpit prep
- Documents / OFP / navlog …… ABOARD, CHECKED
- Circuit breakers / gear pins / covers …… STOWED / NORMAL
- Batteries (main / APU) …… ON
- External power / APU …… AS REQUIRED, POWER AVAIL
- EICAS status / message list …… REVIEWED, NO OPEN ITEM
- Fuel quantity / distribution …… CHECKED vs OFP (max fuel 6,489 kg — Intro §5)
- Flight controls (yoke, rudder, trim) …… CHECKED FULL & FREE
- FMC init (route, perf, fuel, ZFW/CG) …… SET & CROSS-CHECKED
- Takeoff data (V1/VR/V2, thrust rating, trim, flap setting) …… ENTERED, CROSS-CHECKED — **note the hard-wing type's higher scheduled approach/reference speeds carry through to takeoff-flap and trim planning** 🟥
- Baro / altimeters …… SET
- Departure brief …… COMPLETE, including icing/contamination discipline for a hard-wing type if conditions warrant 🟥

## Before start
- Cockpit prep …… COMPLETE
- Signs (seatbelts / no-smoking) …… ON / AUTO
- ATC clearance …… OBTAINED
- Doors / cargo …… CLOSED, EICAS DOORS INDICATION CLEAR
- Fuel boost pumps …… AUTO/ON per flow
- Beacon …… ON
- Thrust levers …… IDLE
- Parking brake …… SET
- Pushback / area …… CLEAR, CLEARANCE RECEIVED

## After start
- APU bleed / GPU (for start) …… AS REQUIRED
- Engine start sequence (No. 2 then No. 1 per flow, ignition AUTO/ON, engine parameters monitored) …… STARTED, PARAMETERS NORMAL 🟧 (start-sequencing detail is model-dependent)
- APU …… STOP once engines self-sustain (or AS REQUIRED)
- Anti-ice …… AS REQUIRED — **hard-wing type: confirm ground de-ice/anti-ice discipline before any dispatch in icing conditions** 🟥
- EICAS …… CHECKED, NO ABNORMAL MESSAGE
- Ground equipment / GPU …… DISCONNECTED, CLEAR

## Before taxi
- Flight controls …… CHECKED (full travel, yoke/rudder/trim) 🟧 (indication depth is model-dependent)
- Flaps …… SET FOR TAKEOFF (per takeoff data) — no leading-edge device to configure (hard wing, Intro §6)
- Pitch trim …… SET per takeoff data
- Rudder trim …… ZERO / AS REQUIRED
- Ground lift dumping (spoilers) / autobrake …… ARMED / AS REQUIRED per field
- Bleed / packs …… ON, AS REQUIRED
- EICAS TAKE OFF CONFIG message …… REVIEWED, NO CAUTION/WARNING
- Ground de-ice holdover time (if applicable) …… CHECKED, WITHIN LIMIT 🟥
- Signs / cabin …… CHECKED

## Before takeoff 🟥
- **Cabin …… SECURE**
- **Flaps …… CONFIRMED per takeoff data**
- **Trim …… CONFIRMED SET**
- **Flight controls …… FREE & CORRECT**
- **TAKE OFF CONFIG (EICAS message clear) …… CONFIRMED**
- **Transponder / TCAS …… TA/RA, ON**
- **Runway / heading …… CONFIRMED, LINED UP**
- **Landing lights / strobes …… ON**
- **Thrust rating (TO/flex) …… CONFIRMED, SET**
- **Anti-ice (if icing conditions) …… ON, CONFIRMED — hard-wing discipline**

## After takeoff / Climb
- Landing gear …… UP, DOORS CLOSED
- Flaps …… RETRACTED ON SCHEDULE
- Ground lift dumping …… DISARMED
- Climb thrust rating (CRZ transition) / autopilot …… SET, ENGAGED 🟧 (thrust-rating-mode transition logic is model-dependent — confirm CRZ mode engages correctly after TO)
- Packs / bleed …… ON
- Baro → STD (at transition) …… SET
- Landing lights …… OFF ABOVE 10,000 / AS REQUIRED

## Cruise
- Altitude / Mach / CI …… SET per OFP (normal cruise M0.74, high-speed M0.81 — Intro §6) `[VERIFY]`
- Fuel …… MONITORED vs OFP, crossfeed/boost pumps checked
- EICAS / systems …… NORMAL, no open message
- Route / FMC …… MONITORED, next-leg data cross-checked
- Anti-ice (if in icing conditions en route) …… AS REQUIRED, MONITORED — hard-wing type 🟥

## Descent / Approach
- Descent / approach brief …… COMPLETE — **include the hard-wing type's higher scheduled VREF/approach speed and its knock-on effect on landing distance** 🟥
- Baro (at transition level) …… SET
- Landing perf / VREF / flap setting …… CHECKED, SET — confirm VREF reflects the no-slat, hard-wing speed schedule, not a slatted-type figure
- Fuel …… SUFFICIENT, RESERVES CONFIRMED
- Signs …… ON
- EICAS / autoflight …… SET FOR APPROACH
- Minima …… SET
- Anti-ice …… AS REQUIRED

## Landing 🟥
- **Cabin …… SECURE**
- **Landing gear …… DOWN, 3 GREEN**
- **Flaps …… FULL / AS REQUIRED per perf**
- **Ground lift dumping (spoilers) …… ARMED**
- **Autobrake …… SET AS REQUIRED**
- **EICAS landing message …… CLEAR**
- **Approach speed (VREF, hard-wing schedule) …… CONFIRMED**
- **Go-around altitude / thrust …… SET**

## After landing
- Ground lift dumping / thrust reversers …… STOWED
- Flaps …… RETRACTED
- Autobrake …… DISARMED
- APU …… START AS REQUIRED
- Transponder …… AS REQUIRED (STBY/ground)
- Landing / strobe lights …… AS REQUIRED

## Shutdown / Secure
- Parking brake …… SET (or chocks in)
- Engines (both) …… OFF per shutdown flow
- Seatbelt signs …… OFF
- Beacon …… OFF
- Fuel boost pumps …… AUTO/OFF as required
- External power / APU …… AS REQUIRED
- EICAS status / doors …… CHECKED
- Aircraft …… SECURED (per secure-cockpit flow if last leg)
