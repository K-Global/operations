## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** the FlightFactor 777 uses its own paint kit and the kit **differs by variant** — a house scheme must be painted to the **777F freighter** template, not a passenger -200LR/-300ER kit with cargo titles added. Kits are not interchangeable across variants 🟥. This is a sharper constraint than on the B77W, since the freighter paint kit is the newer addition to the product line and may be less widely supported by third-party painters (§2).
- **Sim-model prerequisite 🟥:** do not commission or approve a house livery until the Sim-Model open item (whether the 777F variant genuinely exists on the flown XP11-generation product) is resolved — painting against the wrong or a non-existent kit wastes the work.
- **Night-lighting / PBR:** FlightFactor (XP11) lighting and PBR are of the XP11 generation — verify house-livery appearance on the actually flown build before approval 🟧 `[VERIFY]`.
- **Pending approval:** entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (FlightFactor 777, 777F variant confirmed + tested) is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
