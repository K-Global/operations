# CONC — Concorde · Normal Checklist

**CONC / Concorde** · flown as Colimata Concorde FXP (XP11 build) 🟧 `[VERIFY — model]` · sim = X-Plane 11
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-25 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** K Global normal-ops flow for X-Plane 11. **The aircraft's own checklist is authoritative** — where the sim model differs from the real aircraft, the 🟧 note says so. Concorde is a **three-crew** aircraft (Captain **CA**, First Officer **FO**, Flight Engineer **FE**); this flow keeps the **FE panel items distinct** so the crew split is trained even though one sim operator works all panels. This is a cockpit quick-reference, not a certified checklist, and re-expressed in K Global's own words (no verbatim text). Flag legend 🟥 memory/critical · 🟧 model-dependent · 🟩 normal.

---

*Format: **CHALLENGE …… RESPONSE** (crew station in brackets). Adapted for **four engines with reheat**, **no flaps/slats**, the **droop-nose schedule**, and the **transonic CG / fuel-transfer** discipline. Model-dependent items flagged 🟧 (confirm on the flown XP11 FXP build — Sim-Model §3–4). Verify figures `[VERIFY]`.*

## Preflight / Cockpit prep
- Documents / OFP / navlog *(CA/FO)* …… ABOARD, CHECKED
- Gear pins / covers / intake blanks *(FE)* …… REMOVED 🟧
- Batteries / ground power *(FE)* …… ON / CONNECTED, POWER AVAIL
- Hydraulics (Green / Blue / Yellow systems) *(FE)* …… CHECKED, PRESSURES NORMAL `[VERIFY]`
- Electrical panel / generators *(FE)* …… SET, ALL BUSES POWERED
- **Fuel panel — tank contents + transfer/trim tanks *(FE)*** …… CHECKED vs OFP, TRIM TANKS SET FOR DEPARTURE CG 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- Air conditioning / pressurisation / **ozone converters** *(FE)* …… SET, CONVERTERS ARMED 🟧 `[VERIFY]`
- **Cosmic-radiation meter** *(FE)* …… ON, CHECKED 🟧
- INS / nav alignment *(FO)* …… ALIGNING / ALIGNED, POSITION SET
- Analog instruments / altimeters / Machmeter *(CA/FO)* …… CHECKED, BARO SET
- **Droop nose / visor** *(CA)* …… SET **5° DOWN, VISOR DOWN** (taxi/subsonic) 🟥
- Takeoff data (V1 / VR / V2, CG, reheat) *(CA/FO)* …… ENTERED, CROSS-CHECKED
- Departure brief (incl. reheat + noise-abatement, subsonic/supersonic plan) …… COMPLETE

## Before start
- Cockpit prep …… COMPLETE
- Signs (seatbelts / no-smoking) *(FO)* …… ON
- ATC clearance *(FO)* …… OBTAINED
- Doors *(FE)* …… CLOSED & CHECKED
- **Fuel — pumps / crossfeed / transfer set for start & taxi *(FE)*** …… ON, CONFIGURED 🟥
- Beacon *(FO)* …… ON
- Throttles (four) *(CA)* …… CLOSED / IDLE
- Parking brake *(CA)* …… SET
- Area / pushback clearance …… CLEAR, RECEIVED

## After start
- Engine start — **four engines, per type sequence** *(FE runs, CA/FO monitor)* …… STARTED, PARAMETERS NORMAL ALL FOUR `[VERIFY]`
- Hydraulics / generators (on engine power) *(FE)* …… ONLINE, GROUND POWER OFF
- Flight controls (elevons / rudder — **no flaps/slats to set**) *(CA/FO)* …… CHECKED FULL & FREE, no split
- **Trim tanks / CG *(FE)*** …… CONFIRMED SET FOR TAKEOFF CG 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- Anti-ice *(FE)* …… AS REQUIRED
- Ground crew clearance …… SEEN/CONFIRMED

## Before taxi
- Flight instruments / INS *(CA/FO)* …… CROSS-CHECKED, NAV
- **Droop nose / visor** *(CA)* …… CONFIRM **5° DOWN, VISOR DOWN** 🟥
- Autobrake / anti-skid *(FE)* …… ARMED / CHECKED
- Fuel state & distribution *(FE)* …… CHECKED, TAKEOFF CG CONFIRMED
- Takeoff config *(all)* …… REVIEWED, NO ABNORMAL

## Before takeoff 🟥
- **Cabin *(FO)* …… SECURE, CREW ADVISED**
- **Droop nose / visor …… 5° DOWN, VISOR DOWN (takeoff) — CONFIRMED**
- **Trim / CG (trim tanks) …… CONFIRMED SET FOR TAKEOFF**
- **Flight controls (elevons/rudder) …… FREE & CORRECT**
- **Reheat …… ARMED / SELECTED for takeoff**
- **Transponder / squawk …… SET**
- **Runway / heading …… CONFIRMED, LINED UP**
- **Lights / strobes …… ON**
- **Takeoff thrust + reheat …… CONFIRMED**

## Takeoff / initial climb 🟥
- **Reheat …… LIT (all four) at takeoff thrust** 🟥
- Rotate at VR (~high-pitch delta rotation) …… POSITIVE CLIMB, GEAR UP
- **Reheat …… CANCEL on schedule** (noise-abatement / after initial climb) 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- **Droop nose …… RAISE to 0°/UP** on schedule as speed builds (visor up above the visor limit speed) 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- Climb thrust / power (four engines) …… SET, SYMMETRIC

## Transonic acceleration 🟥
- **Reheat …… RE-LIT for transonic acceleration (≈M0.95 → ~M1.7)** 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- **Fuel transfer — pump AFT to move CG aft as the aerodynamic centre moves aft** *(FE)* …… TRANSFERRING, **CG TRACKING WITHIN SUPERSONIC ENVELOPE** 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- Machmeter / CG / temperature …… MONITORED continuously
- **Reheat …… CANCEL above ~M1.7** (supersonic cruise without reheat) 🟥

## Supersonic cruise 🟥
- **Mach …… ≈M2.04, monitor Tmo / skin-temperature limit (~127 °C region)** 🟥
- **CG …… HELD AFT within the supersonic envelope by trim-tank fuel** *(FE)* 🟥
- Altitude …… CLIMB-CRUISE FL500 → ~FL600 as fuel burns off
- **Cosmic-radiation meter …… MONITORED; descend if limit approached** 🟧 `[VERIFY]`
- Ozone / air-conditioning …… NORMAL, CONVERTERS WORKING
- Fuel …… MONITORED vs OFP; transfer schedule tracking

## Deceleration / descent 🟥
- **Fuel transfer — pump FORWARD to move CG forward for deceleration** *(FE)* …… TRANSFERRING, **CG TRACKING** 🟥 `[VERIFY]`
- Decelerate below M1.0 …… SUBSONIC, MONITOR CG/ENVELOPE
- Descent / approach brief …… COMPLETE
- Baro (at transition level) …… SET

## Approach & landing 🟥
- **Droop nose …… 12.5° DOWN (visor down) for approach/landing** 🟥
- **Approach attitude …… HIGH NOSE-UP (delta vortex lift, no flaps/slats) — brief the sight picture** 🟥
- **Gear …… DOWN, THREE GREEN**
- **VAPP / speeds …… SET per perf `[VERIFY]`**
- **CG …… CONFIRMED WITHIN LANDING ENVELOPE (forward transfer complete)** 🟥
- **Cabin …… SECURE, CREW ADVISED**
- **Autobrake / anti-skid …… SET**
- **Go-around thrust (+reheat if required) …… SET/ARMED** `[VERIFY]`

## After landing
- Reheat …… OFF/SAFE
- **Droop nose …… 5° DOWN (taxi)** 🟥
- Reverse / braking …… AS REQUIRED (high-energy landing — mind brake/tyre temps) 🟧
- Transponder / lights …… AS REQUIRED
- Trim tanks / fuel *(FE)* …… SAFE FOR TAXI

## Shutdown / Secure
- Parking brake *(CA)* …… SET (or chocks in)
- Doors *(FE)* …… AS REQUIRED
- Throttles (four) …… CLOSED
- Fuel pumps / transfer *(FE)* …… OFF
- Generators / electrics *(FE)* …… GROUND POWER ON, then as required
- Ozone converters / cosmic meter *(FE)* …… OFF
- Engines (four) *(FE)* …… SHUT DOWN
- INS / nav *(FO)* …… OFF
- **Droop nose …… 5° DOWN or UP per parking standard** 🟧
- Batteries / electrics *(FE)* …… OFF
- Aircraft …… SECURED
