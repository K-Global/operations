# SF34 — Saab 340B · Normal Checklist

**SF34 · Saab 340B — TURBOPROP** · flown as Carenado Saab 340B 🟧 `[VERIFY — model]` · sim = X-Plane 11
**Version** v0.1 · **Updated** 2026-07-25 · **Status** Draft

> **Read-me:** K Global normal-ops flow for X-Plane. **The aircraft's own annunciator panel / master caution system is authoritative** — where the sim model differs, the 🟧 note says so. This is a cockpit quick-reference, not a certified checklist, and re-expressed in K Global's own words (no verbatim OEM text). **This is a TURBOPROP** — power is managed through **power levers, condition levers and propeller (RPM) levers**, not a single thrust lever, and the type carries **autofeather** as a takeoff/climb safety system. The flight deck uses early-generation **CRT-based EFIS** (EADI/EHSI) with conventional analog engine gauges — treat it as neither a modern glass cockpit nor a fully analog one. Flag legend 🟥 memory/critical · 🟧 model-dependent · 🟩 normal.

---

*Format: **CHALLENGE …… RESPONSE**. Generic Saab 340B flow. Model-dependent items are flagged 🟧. Verify each item against the flown model before line use `[VERIFY]`.*

## Preflight / Cockpit prep
- Documents / OFP / navlog …… ABOARD, CHECKED
- External inspection (props, engine inlets, de-ice boots/blade heat elements, pitot-static, gear) …… CHECKED, NO DAMAGE/CONTAMINATION
- Batteries …… ON
- External power / APU-equivalent GPU …… AS REQUIRED, POWER AVAIL
- Annunciator panel / caution lights …… REVIEWED, NO OPEN ITEM
- Fuel quantity / distribution …… CHECKED vs OFP
- Flight controls (yoke, rudder, trim) …… CHECKED FULL & FREE
- Power levers / condition levers / prop levers …… CHECKED FULL & FREE RANGE
- FMS init (route, perf, fuel, ZFW/CG) …… SET & CROSS-CHECKED 🟧 (FMS depth/behaviour is model-dependent)
- Takeoff data (V1/VR/V2, torque setting, trim, flap setting) …… ENTERED, CROSS-CHECKED
- Baro / altimeters …… SET
- Departure brief (incl. icing/terrain awareness given FL250 ceiling) …… COMPLETE

## Before start
- Cockpit prep …… COMPLETE
- Signs (seatbelts / no-smoking) …… ON / AUTO
- ATC clearance …… OBTAINED
- Doors / cargo …… CLOSED, DOOR-STATUS PANEL CHECKED CLEAR
- Fuel boost pumps …… AS REQUIRED per flow
- Beacon …… ON
- Power levers …… IDLE/GROUND IDLE detent
- Condition levers …… FUEL OFF / START per flow
- Parking brake …… SET
- Pushback / area …… CLEAR, CLEARANCE RECEIVED

## Engine start
- Battery/generator status …… CHECKED
- Ignition …… AS REQUIRED per flow
- Start sequence (No. 2 then No. 1, or per flow) …… STARTED, TORQUE/ITT/NG WITHIN LIMITS 🟧 (start-sequencing detail is model-dependent)
- Condition lever(s) …… ADVANCED PER FLOW ONCE STABLE
- Generators …… ON, LOAD SHARED
- Annunciator panel …… CHECKED, NO ABNORMAL CAUTION
- Ground equipment / GPU …… DISCONNECTED, CLEAR

## Before taxi
- Flight controls …… CHECKED (full travel, yoke/rudder/trim) 🟧 (indication depth is model-dependent)
- Flaps …… SET FOR TAKEOFF (per takeoff data)
- Pitch trim …… SET per takeoff data
- Rudder/aileron trim …… ZERO / AS REQUIRED
- **Autofeather …… ARMED per flow** (confirm arming logic/annunciation on the flown model 🟧)
- Prop levers …… SET FOR TAKEOFF RPM
- Ice protection (prop, windshield, airframe/tail) …… AS REQUIRED FOR CONDITIONS
- Bleed / pressurisation panel …… SET, AS REQUIRED
- Annunciator panel …… REVIEWED, NO CAUTION/WARNING
- Signs / cabin …… CHECKED

## Before takeoff 🟥
- **Cabin …… SECURE**
- **Flaps …… CONFIRMED per takeoff data**
- **Trim …… CONFIRMED SET**
- **Flight controls …… FREE & CORRECT**
- **Autofeather …… CONFIRMED ARMED**
- **Power levers / condition levers / prop levers …… SET FOR TAKEOFF**
- **Transponder …… ON**
- **Runway / heading …… CONFIRMED, LINED UP**
- **Landing lights / strobes …… ON**
- **Torque / takeoff power setting …… CONFIRMED, SET**

## After takeoff / Climb
- Landing gear …… UP, DOORS CLOSED
- Flaps …… RETRACTED ON SCHEDULE
- **Autofeather …… DISARMED per flow once above the applicable altitude/torque threshold** (confirm the flown model's disarm logic 🟧 `[VERIFY]`)
- Climb power / torque setting / autopilot …… SET, ENGAGED 🟧 (torque-setting-mode transition logic is model-dependent)
- Pressurisation …… CHECKED, CLIMBING NORMALLY
- Baro → STD (at transition) …… SET
- Ice protection …… AS REQUIRED FOR CONDITIONS
- Landing lights …… OFF ABOVE 10,000 / AS REQUIRED

## Cruise
- Altitude (max FL250) / torque / prop RPM …… SET per OFP `[VERIFY]`
- Fuel …… MONITORED vs OFP, crossfeed/boost pumps checked
- Ice protection …… MONITORED, SELECTED AS REQUIRED
- Annunciator panel / systems …… NORMAL, no open caution
- Route / FMS …… MONITORED, next-leg data cross-checked

## Descent / Approach
- Descent / approach brief (incl. icing/terrain awareness) …… COMPLETE
- Baro (at transition level) …… SET
- Landing perf / VREF / flap setting …… CHECKED, SET
- Ice protection …… AS REQUIRED FOR CONDITIONS
- Fuel …… SUFFICIENT, RESERVES CONFIRMED
- Signs …… ON
- **Autofeather …… ARMED per flow for approach/landing** (confirm arming point on the flown model 🟧)
- Minima …… SET

## Landing 🟥
- **Cabin …… SECURE**
- **Landing gear …… DOWN, 3 GREEN**
- **Flaps …… FULL / AS REQUIRED per perf**
- **Prop levers …… SET FOR LANDING RPM**
- **Autofeather …… CONFIRMED PER FLOW (armed or disarmed as procedure requires)**
- **Annunciator panel …… CLEAR**
- **Go-around torque / thrust …… SET**

## After landing
- Propellers …… GROUND RANGE / AS REQUIRED
- Flaps …… RETRACTED
- Ice protection …… AS REQUIRED
- Transponder …… AS REQUIRED (STBY/ground)
- Landing / strobe lights …… AS REQUIRED

## Shutdown / Secure
- Parking brake …… SET (or chocks in)
- Condition levers …… FUEL OFF per shutdown flow
- Power levers …… IDLE/GROUND IDLE prior to shutdown
- Seatbelt signs …… OFF
- Beacon …… OFF
- Fuel boost pumps …… AS REQUIRED
- External power …… AS REQUIRED
- Annunciator panel / doors …… CHECKED
- Aircraft …… SECURED (per secure-cockpit flow if last leg)
