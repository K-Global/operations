## 4. Notes
- **Model/edition matching:** Rotate ships the MD-11 as **separate Freighter and Passenger editions/products** — a house scheme must be painted to the **Freighter edition's** template, not a passenger kit with cargo titles added. This is a lower-severity constraint than the B77F pack's (where the freighter variant's very existence on the flown build generation was in question) but the match must still be confirmed. 🟥
- **Engine-variant paint detail:** engine nacelle/pylon paint detail can differ between the GE CF6-80C2D1F and PW4460/4462 cowling shapes — confirm K Global's actual engine variant (Intro §5, Dispatch §2) before finalising a paint kit, particularly around the tail-mounted #2 engine cowling and S-duct fairing.
- **Sim-model prerequisite 🟥:** do not commission or approve a house livery until the Sim-Model open items (Freighter-edition confirmation, engine-variant match) are resolved.
- **Night-lighting / PBR:** Rotate (XP11-generation) lighting and PBR — verify house-livery appearance on the actually flown build before approval 🟧 `[VERIFY]`.
- **Pending approval:** entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
