## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** Peters Aircraft and riviere ship their own separate paint kits for their own airframes — a house scheme would need painting **separately per model** and would not be interchangeable 🟧.
- **Fidelity caveat carried from Sim-Model:** neither current candidate is systems-authentic (Sim-Model §1–2); a house livery decision should not be read as endorsing either model's systems depth — livery and systems-approval are separate questions.
- **Pending approval:** entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (which add-on) is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
- **Code F restated 🟥:** regardless of livery/model, every A388 tail is bound by the **ICAO Code F** wingspan constraint and **RFF Cat 10** requirement (Intro §4, Dispatch §7) — confirm Code F stand/gate and Cat 10 RFF cover at both ends of every scheduled leg before dispatch. This is a network/ground-handling constraint, not a livery or sim-model matter, but it applies to every one of the 17 tails on this register without exception.
