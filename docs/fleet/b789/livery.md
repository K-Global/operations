## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** Magknight 787 and the default Laminar 787 each use their own 787-9 paint kit — a house scheme would need painting **separately per model** and would not be interchangeable 🟧.
- **Engine-option cosmetics 🟧:** GEnx-1B and Trent 1000 nacelles differ visually (GEnx chevron/serrated exhaust vs Trent 1000 profile) — a house paint should match the confirmed K Global engine fit (Sim-Model §2) so the model, nacelle and livery agree.
- **Night-lighting / PBR:** XP11 lighting is less refined than XP12; verify house-livery appearance on the actually flown build before approval 🟧 `[VERIFY]`.
- **Pending approval:** entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (which add-on) and engine fit are closed, so the paint targets the correct kit and nacelle.
