## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** the FlightFactor 777 uses its own paint kit and the kit **differs by variant** — a house scheme must be painted to the **-300ER** template, not the -200LR/F. Kits are not interchangeable across variants 🟥.
- **Night-lighting / PBR:** FlightFactor (XP11) lighting and PBR are of the XP11 generation — verify house-livery appearance on the actually flown build before approval 🟧 `[VERIFY]`.
- **Pending approval:** entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (FlightFactor 777, -300ER variant) is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
