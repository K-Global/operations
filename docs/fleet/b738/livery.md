## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** the Zibo mod is layered on the default XP11 737-800, so both candidate models share the **same underlying paint kit** — a house scheme painted for the default 737-800 should carry over to Zibo without a separate repaint, unlike the widebody packs where the lead/fallback models use different kits. 🟩
- **Night-lighting / PBR:** XP11 lighting is less refined than XP12; Zibo's own lighting tuning may still differ subtly from the stock default — verify house-livery appearance on the actually flown build before approval 🟧 `[VERIFY]`.
- **Pending approval:** entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (which candidate is flown) is closed.
