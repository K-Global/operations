## 4. Notes
- **Model/variant matching:** the X-Crafts E195 and the Flight Procedures Simulation E195 each use their own paint kit — a house scheme would need painting **separately per model** and would not be interchangeable 🟧.
- **Generation matching:** any house paint must be built for the **E1** product line (Sim-Model §2) — an E195-E2 paint kit from either developer's E2-generation product (if offered) would not fit this fleet's airframe.
- **Pending approval:** the entire house scheme is 🟧 pending; approve only after the Sim-Model decision (which add-on) is closed, so the paint targets the correct kit.
- **Common rating with E190:** where practical, K Global's house scheme should be designed once and adapted across both the E195 and E190 packs, mirroring the shared crew rating (Intro §1).
