# A339 — Airbus A330-900neo

Long-haul widebody twin and **EDTO backbone** for thin intercontinental trunks.
A330 family — common type rating **A330/A350**. This page is illustrated by the
worked flight **KAY09MA** (EDDF–KSFO, ~5,123 NM great-circle, ~11h33 block).

## Role in the network

Primary role is **thin-to-medium intercontinental trunks** where a 288-seat twin
sizes better than a quad or a larger widebody, and where EDTO reach opens
transoceanic / transpolar routings. Based out of the Frankfurt (EDDF) core, it
complements the larger A346 / A359 / A35K / A388 widebodies on trunks that don't
fill a bigger frame.

## Dimensions & ground footprint

- Length **63.66 m** (208 ft 10 in); wingspan **64.0 m** (210 ft); height
  **16.79 m** (55 ft 1 in).
- **ICAO Code E** — plans and parks only on Code E-capable stands/taxiways;
  watch wingspan-restricted taxiways at constrained hubs.
- Twin-aisle; LD3/LD6 container-capable belly plus bulk hold; GPU / air-start /
  de-ice footprint per widebody-twin standard.

## Performance

- Service ceiling **FL411**; typical cruise **M.80–.82** (up to M.86).
- KAY09MA step-climb: initial **FL310**, stepping **FL340 → FL360 → FL380** as
  weight burns down; average fuel flow **5,863 kg/hr**; trip burn ~**67.8 t**
  over ~11h33.
- **Cost index CI 10** on the reference OFP.
- **Field length:** ~3,000 m takeoff at MTOW, sea-level ISA, dry paved.
- **Hot-and-high:** high-elevation/high-temperature hubs (Johannesburg 5,558 ft,
  Addis Ababa ~7,600 ft, Nairobi ~5,300 ft) drive thrust-limited takeoffs —
  expect payload/fuel penalties and possibly a tech-stop or reduced ZFW on long
  legs out of those fields. Assess per field before scheduling.

- **Tankering:** the large tank (139,090 L) gives real headroom on short-turn

- **Payload & loading:** K Global config 288 pax + 20,000 kg belly; reference

## ETOPS / EDTO

- **Capability:** the A330neo is EDTO-certified **beyond 180 minutes, up to 285
  min**. K Global operates to the **OFP-approved rule time**, not the type
  maximum — the reference OFP dispatched at **ETOPS 120 min**.
- **Method** is defined once in [ETOPS / EDTO Procedures](../flight-ops/etops-edto.md); this
  page only records the type application.
- **En-route alternates (reference leg):** **BGSF** (Kangerlussuaq) and **CYZF**
  (Yellowknife) — the driftdown-adequate fields covering the near-polar
  Atlantic/Canada crossing.
- **Critical fuel:** the decompression + engine-out driftdown scenario at the
  ETOPS entry point to the limiting alternate must be checked against the fuel
  plan; on the reference sector the ~111 t tank leaves adequate margin over the
  ~67.8 t trip burn.
