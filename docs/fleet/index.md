# Fleet

K Global's fleet is organised by **ICAO aircraft type designator**. Where one
designator covers several variants (e.g. `A21N` spans the A321neo, A321LR and
A321XLR; `B748` covers the 747-8i passenger and the 747-8F freighter), the
variants share one type entry and are distinguished in the type's own page.

Figures below are **indicative** operational reference — seats and maximum cargo
vary by cabin fit and are confirmed on the dispatch release for each flight.

## Other types

Alongside the mainline Airbus and Boeing fleets, K Global operates the
following, grouped by role and business unit:

| Group | Types (ICAO) | Unit(s) | Tails |
|---|---|---|--:|
| **Supersonic** | Concorde (`CONC`) | Premier | 10 |
| **Freighter (tri-jet)** | MD-11F (`MD11`) | Cargo | 4 |
| **Regional jets** | E190/E195 (`E190`/`E195`), E170/175 (`E70`/`E75`), ERJ-145 (`E145`), CRJ700/900 (`CRJ7`/`CRJ9`), CRJ-200 (`CRJ2`) | City · Regional | ~40 |
| **Turboprops** | ATR 72-600 (`AT76`), ATR 42-600 (`AT46`), Dash 8 Q400 (`DH8D`), Saab 340B (`SF34`) | Regional | ~9 |
| **Business jets** | Gulfstream G650ER/G550 (`GLF6`/`GLF5`), Challenger 300 (`CL30`), Legacy 650 (`E35L`), Falcon 7X (`FA7X`), PC-12 (`PC12`) | Executive | ~28 |
| **Corporate (VIP) Airbus** | ACJ330neo / ACJ319 / ACJ321 | Executive (VIP) | 5 |

Taken together the fleet totals **around 421 aircraft**, spanning supersonic,
flagship wide-bodies, long-haul and narrow-body types, regional jets and
turboprops, business jets and freighters.
